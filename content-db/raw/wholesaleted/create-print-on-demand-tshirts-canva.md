# How to Create Print-On-Demand T-Shirts On Canva

## Overview

Thanks to Canva, anyone can create professional-looking t-shirt designs — even on a budget.

## What Are Variation Templates?

Templates that let you create multiple designs by modifying a single variable (image or text) while keeping the core design intact. Saves time — create 10-30 designs in a few hours.

**Canva benefits:**
- User-friendly interface
- Wide range of templates and elements
- Affordable pricing (free version available)
- Access to millions of stock images
- Convenient sharing options
- Consistent brand representation

## Step-by-Step: Recreating a Popular Retro Sunset Design

1. **Create free account** at canva.com
2. **Create design** — Custom canvas: 4500 x 5500 pixels
3. **Change background color** — Set to black (or white for white t-shirts)
4. **Add element** — Search "retro sunset" in elements, filter to "graphics"
5. **Add image** — Search your niche (e.g., "dachshund"), use "magic recommendations" for variations
6. **Add text** — Use "Add a heading" text block, change font color and size
7. **Change font** — Use font drop-down menu and letter spacing tool
8. **Add effects** — Select "curved" to wrap text around sunset
9. **Create duplicate text** — Drag below sunset, remove curve, add new text
10. **Save and download** — Share button, PNG format, transparent background, maximum size
11. **Create variation templates** — Duplicate and change text/images

## More Popular T-Shirt Designs

### Retro Vintage Text Design
Simple text box, vibrant colors, distressed-looking font — works well in many niches.

### "Things I Do In My Spare Time" Design
Visually striking, fun, creative — appeals to multiple niches from dog owners to fitness fanatics.

### Funny Retro Sunset Silhouette Design
Combines colors with humor — innocent animal + song lyrics creates funny jokes.

### Funny Slogan Design
Simple white text — simplicity adds more humor. Handwritten style feels more authentic.
