# How to Start Any Online Business With No Money

## Overview

Think you need money to start an online business? Think again! Thanks to free apps and websites, anyone can start an online business with $0.

## Free Traffic and Marketing

Social media platforms are ideal for driving traffic:
- **Facebook, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok** — reach thousands with video
- 82% of internet traffic comes from video streams
- Any creator shares a fair chance of succeeding with videos

**Examples:**
- Dropship store: Create product showcase videos, link in bio
- Digital products: Create educational Reels, link to course in caption

## Free Pictures, Videos, Music, and Sound Effects

**Sources:**
- Pixabay — free photos, illustrations, vectors, videos, music, sound effects
- Pexels
- Free-Imager

Most content is free for commercial use (with modifications required for some).

**Monetization:** Print images onto custom products (print-on-demand), create targeted ads.

## Free Selling Platforms

- **eBay** — 250 free listings/month, fees deducted after purchase
- **Etsy** — 40 free listings via referral link

**What to sell:**
- Digital products (stickers, coloring pages, posters) — 59,000+ sales example
- Physical custom products (t-shirts, mugs) via Printify/Printful integration

## Creating and Selling Digital Courses

Digital education market projected to reach $46.7 billion by 2026.

**Free platforms:** Thinkific and Teachable offer free plans for one course.

**Strategy:** Share free tips on Instagram, link to course in bio.

## Affiliate Marketing

Promote other companies' products for commission.

**Programs:**
- Impact — thousands of trusted brands (Sarah earned $237,000+ from Canva affiliate link alone)
- Amazon Associates — 353 million products to promote

## Freelancing

- **Fiverr** — gig bought every 4 seconds, 5.5% service charge deducted after payment
- **Upwork Project Catalog** — fewer services = opportunity for smart freelancers

## Graphic Design Apps

- **Photopea** — free Photoshop alternative
- **Canva** — user-friendly with vast free library

## Key Takeaways

Starting an online business with zero investment is possible. Leverage free apps, social media, marketplaces, and design tools to launch today.
