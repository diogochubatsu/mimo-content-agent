# 5 Print-On-Demand Apps to Create Profitable T-Shirt Designs

## Overview

T-shirt designs are easy to create when you have the right tools. These 5 apps help create money-making designs for print-on-demand businesses.

## Why Design Matters

Your design is the main "thing" that sells your t-shirt. Good design = more clicks = more sales.

## Recommended Apps

### 1. Canva
- Free graphic design platform
- Templates for non-designers
- Stock images and elements
- Easy export for print

### 2. Adobe Illustrator
- Professional vector design
- Industry standard
- More learning curve but powerful

### 3. GIMP
- Free Photoshop alternative
- Open-source
- Full photo editing capabilities

### 4. Placeit
- Mockup generator
- Design templates
- See designs on real products

### 5. Kittl
- AI-powered design tool
- Text-based design creation
- Trending design styles

## Design Tips

- Research trending designs on Etsy
- Use variation templates for efficiency
- Keep designs simple and impactful
- Test different niches and styles

## Key Takeaways

Choose tools that match your skill level. Canva is ideal for beginners, while Adobe Illustrator suits more advanced designers.
