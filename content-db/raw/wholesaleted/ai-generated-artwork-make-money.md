# How to Use AI-Generated Artwork to Make Money

## Overview

Thanks to AI, creating artwork to sell online has never been easier. Previously you needed artistic talent or had to hire designers — now AI tools can generate professional artwork.

## AI Art Tools

### Stable Diffusion
- Free and open-source
- Generate artwork from text descriptions
- Can be installed on computer or used via Google Colab

### Midjourney
- High-quality AI-generated images
- Subscription-based but produces stunning results

## Monetization Strategies

### Print-on-Demand Products
- Upload AI art to Printify/Printful
- Sell on t-shirts, mugs, posters, canvases
- Create unique designs that stand out

### Digital Products
- Sell as digital downloads on Etsy
- Printable art, wall art, stickers
- No inventory or shipping required

### Custom Merchandise
- Personalized products using AI art
- Pet portraits, custom illustrations
- Higher profit margins on personalized items

## Key Takeaways

AI art tools democratize design creation. Combine AI generation with print-on-demand or digital products for passive income streams.
