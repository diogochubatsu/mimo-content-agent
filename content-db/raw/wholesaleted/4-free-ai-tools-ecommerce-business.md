# 4 Free AI Tools to Grow Your Ecommerce Business

## Overview

AI has made it possible for anyone to create and sell custom products. These four free tools help you earn more money by creating and enhancing custom products.

## Tool #1: PatternedAI

Generates seamless patterns based on text inputs. Describe the pattern you want and the AI generates it. No image uploads required.

**Monetization ideas:**
- Digital paper packs for DIY crafts (sold on Etsy)
- Custom phone cases, hoodies, canvases
- Digital posters and stickers

## Tool #2: DeepAI

Recolorization tool that turns black-and-white images into color. Adds vibrant colors to old photos for a fresh, captivating look.

**Monetization ideas:**
- Sell recolorized vintage photos as digital posters
- Physical products like canvases
- Stand out from competitors selling the same photos in B&W

Similar tools: Colorize and Vance offer different artistic interpretations.

## Tool #3: AI Image Enlarger

Enlarges small images while preserving quality. AI-generated images are typically small (512x512 pixels) and appear stretched/pixelated on larger items.

**Use case:** Download images from PatternedAI or DeepAI, then enlarge for high-quality prints on any product size.

## Tool #4: Public Domain Directories

Photos in the public domain have no copyright protection and are free for commercial use.

**Sources:**
- Wikimedia Commons
- Free-Images

**Profitable niches:** Vintage photos (Prohibition era), Japanese art (Ukiyo-e), floral art.

Sell as digital products (posters, printable artwork) or physical products (cushions, canvases).

## Key Takeaways

These four tools create new possibilities for selling unique physical and digital products. Combine them for maximum impact — generate patterns, colorize vintage photos, and enlarge for quality prints.
