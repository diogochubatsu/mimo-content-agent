# 20 Free AI Tools to Grow Your Online Business

## Overview

Want to grow your online business faster? Steal these 20 free AI tools to fast-track your success.

Thanks to AI tools, there's never been an easier (and fun!) time to build an online business. But many AI tools have paid subscriptions costing over $200/month. After testing 108 free tools, these 20 were selected as all-time favorites — all truly free with no limited trials.

## The 20 Free AI Tools

### 1. Immerse
Scans the web to find similar royalty-free images that look like your upload. Upload any image, and the AI sources similar but different images. Great for finding product inspiration without copyright issues.

### 2. ToonTap
Mobile app that turns real-life photos into cartoons. Cartoon-style YouTube thumbnails tend to score higher click-through rates. Register for free, cancel the pro trial to stay on free account.

### 3. Watermark Remover
Removes watermarks from images. Just upload your image and get a clean, watermark-free result.

### 4. Lumen5
Transforms text into engaging videos using free stock videos. 88% of consumers feel more inclined to purchase after watching a video. Unlimited free videos — competitors charge $39/month for similar features.

### 5. Canva
Free graphic design app with AI Photo Editor, Magic Eraser (remove anything), and Magic Editing (replace objects). A must-have for every entrepreneur.

### 6. Erase
Removes image backgrounds with ease. No account registration required — free and instant access.

### 7. ClipChamp
Automatic video editor. Upload files, choose format, add music, and get a captivating video montage. Includes background remover, video filters, and auto-caption generation.

### 8. Character.ai
Chat with various characters including celebrities and fictional figures. Useful for interview practice, idea brainstorming, language learning, and storytelling.

### 9. Stable Diffusion
AI tool that generates artwork on command. Describe the image you want and it generates it. Requires installation on computer or Google Colab.

### 10. Replicate
AI upscaler that adds pixels to images to enhance resolution. Crucial for selling artwork on custom items where high resolution is required.

### 11. BrandCrowd
AI logo maker. Enter business details and get suitable brand logos in seconds.

### 12. BigSpeak AI
Converts text to speech with human-like voiceovers. Free account allows up to 8,000 characters.

### 13. DeepAI
Transforms black-and-white photos into vivid color. Great for selling vintage photos as posters and canvases. No sign-up required.

### 14. TinEye
Reverse image search engine. Upload an image to find where it's been used online. Useful for checking public domain status.

### 15. ChatGPT
Writes ads, emails, social media posts, and more. Can generate witty puns for text-based merch designs.

### 16. Quiz Maker (Fillout)
Build quizzes for lead generation. Paste information and get an engaging quiz in 60 seconds.

### 17. Text Classifier
Determines whether text was written by human or AI. Helps ensure writing authenticity.

### 18. GrammarChecker
Grammar and spelling checks, plus paraphrasing and summarizing tools.

### 19. Let's Foodie
AI recipe generator based on available kitchen ingredients.

### 20. Soundful
AI-generated music on command. Pick a genre and get a brand-new song. Modify tempo, pitch, and track name.

## Key Takeaways

AI tools can be truly life-changing but also expensive. These 20 free tools can perform a plethora of business tasks — create a brand name, edit photos, design artwork, convert text to speech, and more.
