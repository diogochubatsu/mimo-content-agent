# How to Sell Custom Gifts With ChatGPT and Canva

## Overview

Thousands of regular people are earning money online with custom gifts. Thanks to AI, what used to take weeks can now be achieved in minutes with ChatGPT and Canva.

## What is Print-On-Demand?

POD is printing designs on products like tees, mugs, and tote bags. Benefits:
- **Low overhead costs** — No large inventory upfront
- **Flexibility** — Diverse range of products with minimal effort
- **No risk of unsold inventory** — Only charged for products sold

Etsy partners with Printify which handles printing, packing, and shipping automatically.

## Profit Potential

- **Candles**: Sell for $20, source for $9.15 on Printify = $10.85 profit (42% margin)
- **Mugs**: Sell for $15, source for $4 on Printify = $11 profit (78% margin)

## How to Use ChatGPT for Funny Puns

1. Create a ChatGPT account
2. Ask for 10 funny puns about your niche
3. Find high-traffic niches using Etsy search
4. Look for underserved sub-niches (e.g., "candles for art teachers" vs. "candles for teachers")
5. Cross-niche: combine two interests (e.g., cats + coffee lovers)

## How to Create Designs With Canva

1. Go to Printify and select your product
2. Download the design template for exact printing dimensions
3. Create the design in Canva using:
   - Lines & shapes for borders
   - Text boxes for different-sized fonts
   - Popular fonts suggested by Canva
   - Line spacing tool
   - Graphics for extra touches
4. Export at maximum quality (3) with transparent background
5. Upload to Printify and connect to Etsy shop

## How to Set Up a Free Etsy Store

Etsy charges $0.20 per listing, but you can get 40 free listings:
1. Search "40 free listings" on Etsy
2. Click any dummy listing
3. Find the referral link in the description
4. Create your account and claim free listings

## Key Takeaways

Use ChatGPT for puns, Canva for design, Printify for fulfillment. Focus on high-demand, low-competition niches.
