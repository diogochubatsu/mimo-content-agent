# 10 Top Print-On-Demand Products to Sell

## Overview

Looking for product ideas? Here are 10 you can add to your Print-On-Demand store today. Go beyond t-shirts and mugs with these trendy, profitable products.

## Product #1: Stickers

Stickers are trending among millennials who use them to personalize books, laptops, phone cases, and water bottles. Cheap ($1-$3) so customers buy in multiples (10-15 per order), dramatically increasing profit per order.

**Tip:** Sell multiple designs to encourage bulk purchases and bump average order value.

## Product #2: Posters

Always a top seller. People use posters to showcase interests, express personality, and spice up room appearance.

## Product #3: All-Over Print T-Shirt

Graphic t-shirts experiencing huge surge in sales. Popularized by streetwear brands like Supreme. High perceived value allows premium pricing ($30-$100+).

## Product #4: Clutch Bag

Almost every woman has one. Popular collectors item. Check Printify and RedBubble for options.

## Product #5: Fleece Blanket

People love personalized blankets. Printify sells for $33, Etsy sells for $70-80 — 50%+ profit margin. Offer personalization options for higher conversions.

## Product #6: All-Over-Print Tote Bags

Previously limited to single design, now available with all-over prints. Pattern designs are popular and visually stunning.

## Product #7: Champion Hoodie

Higher quality than regular hoodies. Customizable through Printify. Stands out and wows customers.

## Product #8: All-Over-Print Yoga Pants

Always popular, but the new all-over-print look is trending. Pattern designs and one-legged designs are popular.

## Product #9: Crop Hoodie

Rare form of clothing that creates "novel factor" — customers feel they need to buy because it's unique and uncommon.

## Product #10: Biodegradable Phone Case

73% of millennials willing to spend more on sustainable goods. Eco-friendly cases are unlikely to go out of style. Available through Printful, Printify, and RedBubble.

## Key Takeaways

Offer wider product variety to reach more customers. Each product has unique profit potential and customer appeal.
