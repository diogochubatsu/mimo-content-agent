### Content Architecture (Pillar-Cluster Model)

```
PILLAR PAGE: Complete Guide to Importing from China (5000+ words)

├── Cluster: Best products to import from China
├── Cluster: China sourcing agent comparison
└── Cluster: Import from China to [country] (8 articles)
```

### On-Page SEO Checklist (per article)

- [ ] Title tag: primary keyword + year + modifier (e.g., "How to Buy from 1688 in 2026: Complete Guide")
- [ ] Meta description: 150 chars, includes keyword, has CTA
- [ ] H1: matches search intent exactly
- [ ] H2/H3: cover all subtopics competitors rank for
- [ ] First 100 words: include primary keyword naturally
- [ ] FAQ section: target question keywords (featured snippets)
- [ ] Comparison tables: structured data for rich results
- [ ] Internal links: 5-10 per article to related content
- [ ] External links: 3-5 to authoritative sources (1688, Alibaba)
- [ ] Images: original with alt text containing keyword
- [ ] Schema markup: Article, FAQPage, HowTo, Product
- [ ] Word count: 2000+ minimum, 4000+ for pillar pages

### Technical SEO (non-negotiable)

- [ ] Core Web Vitals pass (LCP <2.5s, FID <100ms, CLS <0.1)
- [ ] Mobile-first responsive design
- [ ] XML sitemap submitted to Google Search Console
- [ ] robots.txt allows all crawlers
- [ ] Canonical URLs on all pages
- [ ] hreflang tags for multi-language (PT/EN/ES/DE/PL/ZH/KO/JA)
- [ ] Schema.org structured data on every article
- [ ] Clean URL structure: /guide/buy-from-1688/
- [ ] Breadcrumb navigation
- [ ] 404 page with helpful links

### Link Building (Aggressive but Safe)

**Safe strategies (white-hat):**
1. **Content-driven links**: Create tools (calculator, directory) that people naturally link to
2. **Guest posts**: Write for e-commerce blogs (2-4 per month, high-quality only)
3. **Resource page link building**: Find "best import tools" pages, request inclusion
4. **HARO/Connectively**: Respond to journalist queries about import/dropshipping
5. **Broken link building**: Find broken links on competitor sites, offer your content as replacement
6. **Data-driven content**: Create original studies ("We analyzed 1000 products on 1688 — here's what we found")

**What to avoid (blacklist risk):**
- PBN (Private Blog Networks)
- Paid links without nofollow
- Mass directory submissions
- Comment spam
- Article spinning/rewriting at scale
- Keyword stuffing
- Cloaking
- Doorway pages

**Safe velocity:** 5-15 quality backlinks per month. Never spike above 30 in one month.

## 3. GOOGLE DISCOVERY (Discover Feed Optimization)

Google Discover shows content to users based on their interests, not searches. Key optimization:

1. **High-quality hero images**: 1200px wide, 16:9 ratio, no logos/text overlays
2. **E-E-A-T signals**: Author bylines, expert quotes, credentials
3. **Freshness**: Update articles within 30 days, re-publish with current date
4. **Engagement magnets**: Headlines that spark curiosity without clickbait
5. **Topic authority**: Cover your niche deeply — Google rewards topical expertise
6. **AMP-free but fast**: Page speed matters more than AMP now
7. **No paywalls**: Free content performs best in Discover
8. **Visual content**: Articles with original images get 2-3x more Discover traffic

## 4. AI SEARCH OPTIMIZATION (GEO + AEO)

This is the NEW frontier. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Claude are now showing AI-generated answers that cite sources. Getting cited = free traffic.

### Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

**What it is:** Optimizing content so AI models cite YOUR site when answering questions.

**How to rank in AI Overviews:**

1. **Structured answers**: Start articles with a direct, concise answer (2-3 sentences) that directly addresses the query. AI models extract this.
2. **Data tables**: AI loves pulling from structured tables. Include comparison tables, price tables, specification tables.
3. **FAQ sections**: Question-and-answer format maps directly to how AI generates responses.
4. **Clear definitions**: Define terms clearly in the first paragraph. "1688 is Alibaba's domestic Chinese marketplace..."
5. **Source citations**: Link to authoritative sources. AI models prefer content that cites data.
6. **Expert quotes**: "According to Jingsourcing..." — AI trusts attributed expertise.
7. **Step-by-step formats**: Numbered lists and processes are easily extractable.

### Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

**What it is:** Optimizing specifically for "Position Zero" and AI-generated answers.

**Target formats:**
- **Featured snippets**: Direct answers in 40-60 words at top of article
- **People Also Ask (PAA)**: FAQ sections targeting PAA questions
- **Knowledge panels**: Structured data about your brand/entity
- **AI Overviews**: Concise, factual paragraphs that AI can extract

### LLM Seeding

**What it is:** Ensuring AI language models have your content in their training/citation data.

**Actions:**
1. **Publish on authoritative platforms**: Your own site + guest posts on Shopify Blog, Jingsourcing, etc.
2. **Consistent entity mentions**: Your brand name + "import from China" in multiple contexts
3. **Reddit/Quora presence**: AI models heavily weight Reddit/Quora content
4. **GitHub**: If you have tools (like our calculator), publish on GitHub — AI cites code
5. **LLMs.txt file**: Create an LLMs.txt on your site (like robots.txt for AI)

### Entity SEO

**What it is:** Making your brand a recognized "entity" in Google's Knowledge Graph.

**Actions:**
1. **Google Knowledge Panel**: Claim and optimize your Knowledge Panel
2. **Consistent NAP**: Name, Address, Phone across all platforms
3. **Schema markup**: Organization, Person, WebSite schemas
4. **Wikipedia/Wikidata**: Create a Wikidata entry (if notable enough)
5. **Social profiles**: Consistent branding across all platforms
6. **Press mentions**: Get mentioned in news articles

## 6. ANTI-BLACKLIST SAFETY RULES

To stay aggressive without penalties:

1. **Content velocity**: Max 10-15 new articles per week. Spiking triggers manual review.
2. **Link velocity**: Max 15 new backlinks per month. 30+ is suspicious.
3. **Content quality floor**: Every article must be 1500+ words, unique, and genuinely useful.
4. **No thin content**: Every page must provide real value. No "AI slop."
5. **Diversify link sources**: Don't get 50 links from the same domain.
6. **Natural anchor text**: 60% branded, 20% URL, 15% generic, 5% keyword-rich.
7. **Update cadence**: Refresh old articles every 60-90 days (Google loves freshness).
8. **User signals**: Optimize for engagement (time on page, low bounce rate).
9. **EEAT**: Show expertise, experience, authority, trustworthiness in every article.
10. **Monitor**: Set up Google Search Console alerts for manual actions.
