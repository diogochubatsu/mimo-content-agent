## What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is optimizing content so AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) **cite your site** when answering questions. It's different from traditional SEO:

| Traditional SEO | GEO |
|----------------|-----|
| Rank on page 1 | Get cited in AI answer |
| Keywords + backlinks | Direct answers + structured data |
| One canonical page | Multiple quotable passages |
| Link authority | Information gain + source credibility |
| Click-based traffic | Citation-based traffic |

## Key GEO Strategies (from research)

### 1. Direct Answer Format
AI engines extract the first 2-3 sentences that answer a question. Start every section with a direct, factual answer.

**Bad:** "In this comprehensive guide, we'll explore the many facets of importing from China, covering everything from supplier selection to logistics..."

**Good:** "1688.com is 15-25% cheaper than Alibaba.com for identical products. It requires a Chinese-speaking agent for payments and export."

### 2. Structured Data Tables
AI models extract heavily from tables. Include comparison tables, price tables, specification tables in every article.

### 3. Source Citations
AI trusts content that cites authoritative sources. Always link to: official platform pages, research papers, government data.

### 4. FAQ Sections
Questions map directly to AI queries. FAQ format = AI citation format.

### 5. Information Gain
Content that provides NEW information (original data, case studies, unique insights) gets cited more than rehashed content.

### 6. Entity Optimization
Your brand/site needs to be a recognized "entity" in Google's Knowledge Graph.

## AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

AEO = optimizing for "Position Zero" and AI-generated answers.

**Target formats:**
- Featured snippets (40-60 word direct answers)
- People Also Ask (FAQ sections)
- AI Overviews (concise factual paragraphs)
