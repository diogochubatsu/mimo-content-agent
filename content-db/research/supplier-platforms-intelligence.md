## Supplier Verification Checklist (from Leeline)

1. Request business license — look for "manufacturing" in scope
2. Demand live video audit (not pre-recorded)
3. Check lot labels, housekeeping, calibration
4. Mandate inspections at 0%, 50%, and 100% production
5. Payment: 30/70 split (never 100% upfront)
6. Verify DDP shipping compliance
