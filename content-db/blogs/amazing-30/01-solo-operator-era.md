# I Was Wrong About the Solo Operator Era (And I've Never Been Happier About It)

*A few months ago, I told you that AI would enable one person to build a billion-dollar business. Turns out, it took two. Close enough.*

---

Back in September, when we launched the ASM AI edition, I stood in front of a camera and made a prediction that a lot of people thought was crazy.

I said we were entering the "Solo Operator Era" - a time when AI tools had become so powerful that a single person, working from a laptop, could build and run a business that previously would have required dozens or even hundreds of employees.

I wasn't alone in saying it. I played clips of Sam Altman, the CEO of OpenAI, predicting that AI would soon make "a one-person billion-dollar company" possible. And Mark Cuban went even further, saying the world's first trillionaire "could be just one dude in a basement."

At the time, plenty of people thought we were all exaggerating.

Well, yesterday the New York Times published a story that proved us right. Almost.

## Meet Matthew Gallagher - and His Brother

Matthew Gallagher is 41 years old. He lives in Los Angeles. He doesn't have a college degree. His previous company - a subscription watch business called Watch Gang - had 60 employees and never turned a profit.

In September 2024, Gallagher took $20,000 and a handful of AI tools and launched Medvi, a telehealth company that connects patients with doctors and pharmacies for GLP-1 weight-loss medications.

Two months. That's how long it took him to go from idea to live business.

In his first month, he had 300 customers. By the second month, 1,000 more. By the end of 2025 - his first full year - Medvi had generated **$401 million in revenue** with a **16.2% net profit margin**. That's roughly $65 million in profit.

In 2026, the company is on track to do **$1.8 billion in sales**. That's billion with a B.

His total number of employees? One. His younger brother, Elliot, who he brought on in April 2025.

## How He Actually Did It

Gallagher used more than a dozen AI tools to do the work that would normally require entire departments:

- **ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok** wrote the code that powers his platform
- **Midjourney and Runway** generated the images and videos for his website and ads
- **ElevenLabs** powered AI voice technology for the business
- He built custom AI agents to make his software systems talk to each other
- An AI chatbot handles customer service

He didn't raise a dime of venture capital. He didn't need it.

For context, let's compare him to Hims & Hers - a publicly traded telehealth company in a similar space. Hims has **2,442 employees** and generated $2.4 billion in revenue. Their net profit margin? Just 5.5%.

Gallagher is approaching the same revenue with **two people** and **triple the profit margin**.

## What This Means for You

The same AI tools that Matthew Gallagher used to build a $1.8 billion business are available to you right now, today, for the price of a monthly subscription.

ChatGPT. Claude. Midjourney. These aren't enterprise-only, Fortune 500 tools. They're the same tools we teach inside ASM AI. They're the same tools our members are using to research products, build brands, create listings, generate ad creatives, and handle customer service.

If AI can take one person from $20,000 to $1.8 billion in under two years, imagine what it can do for your ecommerce brand - even if your goal is $10,000 a month, $50,000 a month, or your first million-dollar year.

## The Gallagher Playbook

1. **He found a massive market** - GLP-1 weight-loss drugs are one of the biggest trends in healthcare
2. **He used existing infrastructure** - he didn't build a pharmacy or hire doctors
3. **He let AI handle everything else** - coding, design, copywriting, customer service
4. **He moved fast** - from idea to launch in 60 days
5. **He stayed lean** - no investors, no overhead, no bloated team
