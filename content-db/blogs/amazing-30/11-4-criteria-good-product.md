# The 4 Criteria of a Good Product

## 1. Big Market Potential

To ensure success, it's crucial to choose products that cater to a large and expanding market.

## 2. Differentiation Opportunity

Look for products where you can make improvements or add value compared to existing offerings.

## 3. Private Label Potential

Choose products that can be branded with your own label and marketed as your own brand.

## 4. Profitability

Ensure the product has good profit margins after accounting for all costs including Amazon fees, shipping, and manufacturing.
