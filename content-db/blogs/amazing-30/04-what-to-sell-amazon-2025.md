# What to Sell on Amazon: The 10 Best Profitable Products to Sell in 2025

Kicking off 2025, there's never been a better time to start a business on Amazon. With millions of shoppers and over 200 million Prime members across the globe, Amazon offers unmatched access to a thriving online marketplace.

## Why sell on Amazon?

- **Global reach and massive customer base** - 300+ million active customers
- **Trusted brand and built-in credibility** - customers trust Amazon
- **Low barriers to entry** - start with a few hundred dollars
- **Logistical ease with FBA** - Amazon handles storage, shipping, returns
- **Opportunities for passive income** - automate operations
- **Diverse selling models** - private label, wholesale, dropshipping, handmade

## How to choose a product

Key criteria: Market demand, Profitability, Competition analysis, Product size and weight, Seasonality.

## Top 10 product categories for 2025

1. Health and wellness products
2. Eco-friendly and sustainable products
3. Home office equipment
4. Smart home devices
5. Pet accessories
6. Baby products
7. Kitchen gadgets
8. Outdoor and fitness gear
9. Beauty and personal care
10. Home organization and storage
