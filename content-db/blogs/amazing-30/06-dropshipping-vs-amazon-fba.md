# Dropshipping vs Amazon FBA For Passive Income

Don't waste time building someone else's business. Learn about dropshipping vs Amazon FBA for passive income and take an in-depth look at two popular business models.

## How does dropshipping work?

Dropshipping is a simple e-commerce business model. The seller advertises a product that customers want. But when an order is placed, the dropshipper's supplier fulfills the order.

Popular dropshipping suppliers include Alibaba, AliExpress, or Oberlo.

**Drawbacks:**
1. You don't own the product you sell
2. You can't control the brand that you represent

Because you don't own the product, you don't get to set the price — the market and your competitors do.

## How is dropshipping different from Amazon FBA?

| Feature | Amazon FBA | Dropshipping |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| Sell online | Yes | Yes |
| Own product | Yes | No |
| Brand product | Yes | No |
| Profit margins | High | Low |
| Sell business | Yes | No |
| Scale easily | Easy | Hard |

## Key Differences

Amazon FBA sellers contact private label manufacturers to brand their own product. When ready, suppliers ship to Amazon. Amazon stores, packs, and ships on-demand.

The Amazon FBA business model saves sellers countless hours and leaves you with a real, valuable asset you can sell.
