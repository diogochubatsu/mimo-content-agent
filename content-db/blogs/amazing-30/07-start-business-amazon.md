# How to Start a Business on Amazon | Step-by-Step Guide to Amazon FBA

Starting your own business is fun, scary, and smart. Especially if you're using the proven Amazon FBA business model.

## Step 1: Set up your account

Set up your Amazon selling account and add Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA).

Required info:
- Business email address
- Government ID
- Tax information
- Phone number
- Bank account information

## Step 2: Pick your product

Research your market. The key is identifying a product that other sellers don't offer.

### The starter product checklist

1. **Good profit potential** – $10k to $25k each month
2. **Not too competitive** – fewer than 1k reviews on Amazon
3. **Low inventory investment** – sells for $17 to $100

### Find a supplier

Most Amazon FBA business owners work with suppliers in China. Use Alibaba, trade shows, or product sourcing agencies.

## Step 3: Launch your product

Come up with a clear name for your brand, a logo and product description. Get at least 5 to 10 reviews.

## Step 4: Grow your sales

Invest time and money in smarter advertising. Amazon shows you which keywords are wasting ad spend and which are making money.

## Step 5: Expand your brand

Add two additional products that'll skyrocket revenue: a growth product and a dream product.
