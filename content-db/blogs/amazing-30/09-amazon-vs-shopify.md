# Selling on Amazon vs Shopify: Which is a Better Business to Start?

If you want to start a business today, selling high-quality, physical products online is a great option.

## What is Amazon FBA?

Creating a custom-branded, private label product and selling it on Amazon using Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) to store and ship inventory.

## What is Shopify?

Shopify is an ecommerce software tool — like a website builder to sell products online. You create an online website for people to buy only your products.

## The Amazon FBA Opportunity

- About 50% of all online sales in the U.S. are on Amazon
- Sales grew by over $100 billion in the past year alone
- Pay 15% of sales + fulfillment fees

**Startup cost:** $2,000 to $5,000

**Best for:** Simple ecommerce business, low maintenance, don't worry about operations.

## The Shopify Opportunity

- DTC (Direct to Consumer) model
- You own the full customer experience
- Higher valuation multiples (1-2x revenue vs 3-5x profit for Amazon)

**Startup cost:** $500 to $10,000

**Best for:** Unique products, brand building, experienced marketers.

## Conclusion: Do both, but start with one

Start with Amazon FBA if you're new to ecommerce. Start with Shopify if you have a unique product idea or are an experienced marketer.
