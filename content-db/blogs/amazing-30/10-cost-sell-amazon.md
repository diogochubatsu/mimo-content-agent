# How Much Does it Cost to Sell on Amazon?

Before you make an investment, you want to know the total cost (risk) and possible return.

## Amazon Seller Central fee ($39.99/month)

Professional plan costs $39.99 per month. Individual plan is $0.99 per item but doesn't include all features.

## Amazon referral fee (15% of sales)

For most categories, you pay a 15% referral fee. For example, if you sell a $100 product, you pay $15 to Amazon.

## FBA fees (varies based on product size and weight)

- "Small and Light" under 4 oz: $1.97 per unit
- "Large standard" 2-3 lbs: $5.42 per unit

## Product cost ($1,500 to $7,500)

- 3 samples: ~$150
- 300 units at $5-$25 each: $1,500 to $7,500

## Shipping, freight forwarding, customs ($300 to $600)

Estimated $1.00 to $2.00 per unit for typical products.

## Other costs

- Inventory inspection: $200
- Brand (logo, social media): $50 to $350
- Product photos: $300
- Advertising: Run at break-even

## Summary

**Total upfront costs:** $2,350 to $8,950

**Recurring costs:** $39.99/month + 15% referral + FBA fees
