# How to Sell on Amazon FBA in 2025: The Ultimate Guide

Have you ever seen a product when browsing Amazon and said, "Hey, wait a minute – I could sell that"?

## Why Sell on Amazon?

- Trusted Platform with decades of experience
- Access to 180+ million Prime members in the US alone
- No need for inventory (Amazon handles fulfillment)
- International selling options

## Overview of Selling Plans

**Individual Plan:** $0.99 per item sold (up to 40 items)
**Professional Plan:** $39.99/month (unlimited items)

## The Best Way to Start

1. Create your Seller Account
2. Find a Product to Sell
3. Source from suppliers (Alibaba, wholesale, etc.)
4. Choose fulfillment (FBA recommended)
5. Launch and optimize your listing
6. Market your product

## FBA vs FBM

FBA: Amazon stores, picks, packs, ships. Access to Prime. Higher fees but more automation.
FBM: You handle everything. Lower fees but more work.

## Common Challenges

- Account suspensions - appeal with Plan of Action
- Intellectual property issues - use Brand Registry
