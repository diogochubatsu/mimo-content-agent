# What Business Should I Start?

If you want to build a profitable business you own as quickly as possible, it must meet five criteria.

## Criteria 1: Long-term Business

If you're building a business built for short-term cash but won't be viable months or years from now, you're wasting your time. You want a business that'll be around at least five years.

## Criteria 2: Profitable

You need to be making money as quickly as possible, ideally within the first six to 12 months.

## Criteria 3: Low Startup Cost

You want a business that can be started ideally with as little as a few thousand dollars, maybe at most five to 10 thousand dollars.

## Criteria 4: Business You Can Build in 1-2 Hours a Day

You don't want to have to quit your day job to start a business that may not work. Build a business that can be built in about an hour or two a day.

## Criteria 5: Cash Flow

You want a business that can be producing cash flow for you as quickly as possible, ideally within a few months, but at most six to 12 months. And a business that has real value that can be sold.
