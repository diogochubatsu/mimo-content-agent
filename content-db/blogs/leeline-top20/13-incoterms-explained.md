# 11 Incoterms Explained – Stop Overpaying in Shipping

Treating global trade rules as academic vocabulary costs you money.

## Complete Incoterms 2020 Overview

| Rule | Transport Mode | Seller's Risk Transfer | Best Used When |
|------|---------------|----------------------|----------------|
| EXW | Any | At seller's premises | Domestic-like purchases |
| FCA | Any | At seller's premises or carrier | Most common EXW replacement |
| CPT | Any | When handed to first carrier | Seller arranges freight, buyer wants early risk transfer |
| CIP | Any | When handed to first carrier | High-value goods needing insurance |
| DAP | Any | At named destination | Flexible door-to-door |
| DPU | Any | At named destination (unloaded) | Buyer wants seller to handle unloading |
| DDP | Any | At named destination (cleared) | Maximum convenience |
| FAS | Sea/Inland | Alongside ship | Bulk commodities |
| FOB | Sea/Inland | On board vessel | Classic ocean container shipments |
| CFR | Sea/Inland | On board vessel | Seller controls freight, buyer handles insurance |
| CIF | Sea/Inland | On board vessel | Traditional ocean with basic insurance |

## High-Frequency Comparison: EXW vs FOB vs CIF vs DAP vs DDP

| Aspect | EXW | FOB | CIF | DAP | DDP |
|--------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Risk Transfer | Factory gate | On board vessel | On board vessel | At destination | At destination (cleared) |
| Seller Control | Minimal | Medium | Medium-High | High | Maximum |
| Buyer Control | Maximum | High | Low | Medium | Minimal |
| Cost Visibility | Lowest | High | Medium | High | Highest |
| Best For | Very experienced buyers | Most ocean FCL | Traditional importers | Door delivery | Beginners |
| Recommendation | <5% | 55% | 10% | 15% | 15% |

## 6-Step Decision Process

### Step 1: Establish Your 2020 Rule Baseline
Global contracts operate under Incoterms 2020. No 'Incoterms 2026' exists.

### Step 2: Build Your Cargo Decision Profile
Map cargo value, fragility, shipment mode, frequency, LCL vs FCL.

### Step 3: Calculate Your True Landed Cost
Include: product cost, origin handling, main freight, insurance, destination charges, customs duties, broker fees.

### Step 4: Map Risk Transfer and Liability Points
Separate risk transfer, title of ownership, and payment release.

### Step 5: Translate Rule into Payment Triggers
Set 30/70 payment structure. Release final 70% only with clean Bill of Lading.

### Step 6: Build the Decision Matrix

| Scenario | Recommended Term | Top Hidden Risk |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| LCL Multi-Factory | FCA | Origin terminal fees |
| FCL Single-Supplier | FOB | Terminal forklift damage |
| High-Value Electronics | CIP | Seller under-insures |
| Low-Cost Textiles | DDP | Hidden unit markups |
| Direct-to-Amazon | DDP | IOR issues |

## People Also Ask

1. **Best Incoterm for international buyers?** FOB for experienced buyers moving ocean freight.
2. **FOB vs DDP difference?** FOB = buyer manages transit and duties. DDP = seller handles all.
3. **Who pays customs under EXW?** Buyer pays both export and import clearance.
