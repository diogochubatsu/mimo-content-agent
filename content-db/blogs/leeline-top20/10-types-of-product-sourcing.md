# Powerful 8 Types of Product Sourcing – Maximize ROI Easily

Most brands crash production by confusing sourcing with procurement. Sourcing is the strategic supplier-choice problem. Procurement is the execution problem.

## 8 Main Types

### 1. Direct Factory Sourcing
Removes trading middleman. Negotiate straight with assembly line. Fits brands with $100k+ annual revenue per SKU.
- IP Theft Risk: 7/10
- Lead-Time Volatility: 6/10
- Quality Drift: 8/10

### 2. Sourcing Through a Procurement Agency
Agency manages entire supply chain. Fits mid-market ecommerce brands.
- IP Theft Risk: 3/10
- Lead-Time Volatility: 2/10
- Quality Drift: 2/10

### 3. Wholesale Sourcing
Buy ready-made, non-custom goods. Speed at cost of margins.
- IP Theft Risk: 1/10
- Lead-Time Volatility: 2/10
- Quality Drift: 9/10

### 4. Dropshipping Sourcing
Sell before buying inventory. Perfect cash flow, low risk.
- IP Theft: 2/10
- Lead-Time Volatility: 9/10
- Quality Drift: 8/10

### 5. Private Label Manufacturing
Source existing core product with custom specification layer. Builds brand defensibility.
- IP Leakage: 5/10
- Quality Drift: 7/10
- Compliance Mistakes: 8/10

### 6. White Label Sourcing
Standard product with light branding. Fastest launch, weakest differentiation.
- IP Theft Risk: 2/10
- Competitive Sameness: 9/10
- Lead-Time Volatility: 3/10

### 7. Global vs Nearshore Sourcing
Geography as sourcing model. Balance cost with responsiveness.

### 8. Insourcing or Owned Production
Ultimate control but heavy capital intensity. Fits enterprise brands with predictable volumes.

## Selection Matrix

| SKU Complexity | Low ARR (<$100k) | Medium ARR ($100k-$1M) | High ARR (>$1M) |
|---------------|-------------------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Low | Dropshipping | Wholesale | White Label |
| Medium | Wholesale | Private Label | Agency-Led |
| High | Agency-Led | Direct Factory | Insourcing |

## People Also Ask

1. **Sourcing vs procurement?** Sourcing = finding factory. Procurement = buying process.
2. **Direct factory vs sourcing agent?** Direct maximizes profit but needs oversight. Agent charges fee but removes barriers.
3. **Dropshipping vs wholesale?** Wholesale scales better due to inventory control.
4. **Private label vs white label?** White label = logo on generic item. Private label = modified specifications.
5. **When to nearshore or insource?** Nearshore for fast replenishment. Insource when volume keeps machines running daily.
