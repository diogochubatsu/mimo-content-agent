# How to Import From China? Smart 10 Steps Guide to Big Wins

One hidden port fee can wipe out your profit margins. Learning **How To Import From China** is not a gamble. After conducting hundreds of Shenzhen and Guangzhou factory audits, I built this step-by-step operating system for e-commerce founders and Amazon FBA sellers.

You control supplier vetting, landed costs, and shipping terms in strict sequence. Experienced importers use these explicit checkpoints to source, verify, ship, clear customs, and receive inventory. According to U.S. Customs and Border Protection, simple documentation errors routinely cause severe shipment delays.

Treat sourcing as a rigid operational sequence, not a quick Alibaba transaction. Secure your quality before inventory leaves the port.

## Prerequisites: The Pre-Import Checklist

**Timeline:** 2–4 Weeks (Prep) | **Transit:** 7–10 days (Air), 30–45 days (Sea)

I audited hundreds of delayed shipments. I found most How To Import From China failures start before Step 1. Buyers ignore entity setup, compliance planning, and payment controls.

Complete this checklist before outreach:

- **Credentials:** Secure entity details, EIN, and Importer of Record access.
- **Targets:** Define product specs, target MOQ, landed-cost ceiling, and destination details.
- **Budget:** Include product costs, samples, tooling molds, inspection fees, freight, duties, customs brokerage, warehouse storage, and a contingency buffer.
- **Logistics:** Verify payment methods, document Amazon FBA prep rules, and contact a freight forwarder.

## Step 1: Filter Products for Business Viability

Stop browsing supplier directories for product ideas. In my experience, skipping business viability research guarantees disastrous test units.

Filter products by three strict criteria: market demand, manageable compliance risk, and wide margins after freight and duties.

Build a go/no-go framework. Define your target sale price, target landed cost, defect tolerance, reorder likelihood, and storage/shipping profile.

## Step 2: Select Your Sourcing Channel

Start your search online using platform filters and document verification. Add an agent or book a formal audit when your financial risk justifies it.

Compare the top three platforms:

| Platform | Best For | Factory Mix | Negotiation |
|----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Alibaba | Broad categories | Heavy trading companies | Fast, informal |
| Made-in-China | Industrial equipment | Direct factories | Formal, technical |
| Global Sources | Consumer electronics | Large-scale factories | Trade-show driven |

### Hire a Sourcing Agent

Hire a local agent for supplier sourcing if you are a new importer. Use agents to manage your complex product sourcing pipeline, including multi-SKU orders, custom molds, or severe time-zone barriers.

### Visit Wholesale Markets

Fly to China to compare suppliers directly. Attend the official Canton Fair for broad discovery. Walk the Yiwu market for cheap commodities.

### Verify Suppliers

1. Check the license: Request their Chinese Business License.
2. Verify payments: Ensure the Bank Account Name exactly matches the legal entity.
3. Demand visual proof: Request workshop videos, production-line photos.
4. Check certifications: Request ISO or BSCI certificates.
5. Verify experience: Ask for U.S. export records and references.

## Step 3: Compare Quotes and Incoterms

Force an apples-to-apples comparison using a strict Request for Quote (RFQ).

Evaluate these four liability models:
- **EXW:** You handle all pickup and export logistics.
- **FOB:** The supplier loads goods at their local port.
- **CIF:** The seller pays freight to your destination port.
- **DDP:** The simplest door-to-door model.

## Step 4: Order and Inspect the Golden Sample

Follow three stages:
1. Order an off-the-shelf sample to check baseline capabilities.
2. Request a revised sample with custom modifications.
3. Approve the exact golden sample before mass production.

## Step 5: Calculate Landed Costs and Verify Compliance

First, identify your product's HS Code. Next, verify your category compliance rules. Now, calculate your true landed cost.

## Step 6: Draft an Enforceable Purchase Order

Draft your Purchase Order (PO). List the exact product specs, approved sample reference, quantity, unit price, packaging, carton marks, lead times, and refund rules.

## Step 7: Select Your Shipping and Consolidation Strategy

| Shipping Method | Best For | Cost Profile | Transit Time |
|----------------|----------|--------------|--------------|
| Sea Freight | Bulk orders | Lowest unit cost | 30 to 45 days |
| Air Freight | Urgent replenishment | High unit cost | 7 to 10 days |
| Express Courier | Samples and small parcels | Highest unit cost | 3 to 5 days |

## Step 8: Manage Customs and Verify Documentation

Gather your core documents. Collect the Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and Bill of Lading.

## Step 9: Execute the Arrival and Receiving Checklist

1. Reconcile paperwork against the Commercial Invoice and Packing List.
2. Perform a sensory check: Inspect pallets for structural integrity.
3. Document issues: Separate transit damage from factory defects.
4. Enforce the 24-hour rule: Notify your supplier within 24 hours.

## Step 10: Review Performance and Scale Operations

Build a supplier scorecard. Track quality, communication speed, lead-time accuracy, defect rate, and landed-cost accuracy.

## People Also Ask

1. **Do I need a license to import from China?** No general import license needed for commercial goods into the US. Specific agencies require permits for regulated items.

2. **How much money do I need to start importing?** Between $1,000 and $5,000 for a viable test order.

3. **What is the cheapest shipping method from China?** Sea freight offers the lowest cost per unit for bulk orders exceeding two cubic meters.

4. **How do I pay Chinese suppliers safely?** Direct bank wire to a verified corporate account. Never use Western Union or personal accounts.
