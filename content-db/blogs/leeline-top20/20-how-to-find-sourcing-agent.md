# How To Find A Sourcing Agent in China - Get Reliable Fast

Slash your import costs instantly – master every step of how to find a sourcing agent in China with real vetting tricks that actually work.

## Why You Need a Sourcing Agent

- Language barrier navigation
- Factory verification on the ground
- Quality control and inspection
- Logistics coordination
- Cultural and business practice guidance

## How to Find the Right Agent

### Step 1: Define Your Needs
- Product category and complexity
- Order volume and frequency
- Budget and fee tolerance
- QC requirements
- Logistics needs

### Step 2: Research and Shortlist
- Industry referrals
- Trade show connections
- Online platforms and directories
- Industry associations

### Step 3: Verify Legitimacy
- Check Chinese business license
- Verify physical office address
- Confirm corporate bank account
- Request client references
- Check online reviews and reputation

### Step 4: Evaluate Fee Structure
| Fee Model | Description | Best For |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Percentage Commission | 3-10% of order value | Standard orders |
| Flat Fee | Fixed amount per project | Specific tasks |
| Hybrid | Base + commission | Complex projects |
| "Free" Agent | Markup on product cost | Avoid this model |

### Step 5: Test with Small Order
- Place trial order (50-100 units)
- Evaluate communication speed
- Check QC process
- Verify documentation quality

## Red Flags to Watch

1. **No Physical Office:** Agent operates from home or virtual office
2. **Refuses Factory Visits:** Won't show production floor
3. **Hidden Fees:** Markup built into unit price
4. **No Written Contracts:** Verbal agreements only
5. **Pressure to Pay Fast:** Rushing deposit without verification
6. **Generic Responses:** Copy-paste answers to specific questions
7. **No QC Process:** No inspection or quality control plan

## Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Verified Chinese business license
- [ ] Physical office address confirmed
- [ ] Corporate bank account matches license
- [ ] Client references provided and contacted
- [ ] Fee structure transparent and documented
- [ ] QC process clearly defined
- [ ] Communication channels established
- [ ] Contract terms reviewed
- [ ] Trial order completed successfully

## People Also Ask

1. **How much do sourcing agents charge?** 3-10% commission or flat fee. Never accept "free" agents with hidden markups.
2. **Sourcing agent vs freight forwarder?** Agent finds factories and controls quality. Forwarder moves cargo.
3. **Can agent help with Amazon FBA?** Yes, good agents handle entire e-commerce supply chain.
4. **How to verify agent?** Check business license, physical office, corporate bank account. Never send to personal account.
5. **When to hire agent?** When annual spend exceeds $200,000, product is complex, or you lack local presence.
