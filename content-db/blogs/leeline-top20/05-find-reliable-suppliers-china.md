# How to Find Reliable Suppliers China - Zero-Risk Strategy

When I walk a factory floor, I wait for a specific shift. The sales rep stops talking. The production manager hesitates over a precise CNC tolerance question. That hesitation separates operational truth from marketing fluff.

## 7-Step Procurement Workflow

### Step 1: Define Your Ideal Supplier Profile
Document target factory region, production process, exact order size, quality level, compliance exposure, and OEM/ODM support needs.

### Step 2: Source Candidates to Build a Comparable Shortlist
Search four channels: Referrals/Trade Fairs, Vetted Partners, B2B Marketplaces, Google/Baidu.

### Step 3: Execute the Legal and Financial Anti-Fraud Check
Verify Chinese business license against GSXT Registry. Match bank account name to verified entity.

### Step 4: Run a Structured Outreach Test
Ask six questions: revenue percentage, in-house operations, machinery models, subcontracting, live video capability, export volumes.

### Step 6: Validate Samples and Execute a Pilot Order
Order pre-production samples from three shortlisted suppliers. Select and approve one golden sample.

### Step 7: Finalize the Pre-Order Package and Logistics Controls
Build watertight PO. Tie final 70% payment to passed Pre-Shipment Inspection.

## Common China Sourcing Scams

1. **Bank Beneficiary Changes:** Freeze payment immediately. Call separate trusted contact.
2. **Pilot Batch Quality Plummets:** Compare pilot directly against approved sample.
3. **Factory Identity Remains Blurry:** Ask for live factory video, in-house process details.

## People Also Ask

1. **How do I verify if supplier is real factory?** Check business license scope. "Manufacturing" = factory. "Sales" = trading company.
2. **Safest payment method?** Direct wire (T/T) to verified corporate account. 30/70 split.
3. **How to protect product design?** Sign localized NNN agreement enforceable in Chinese courts.
4. **Can I negotiate MOQ?** Yes, during off-season or with slightly higher unit price.
5. **Do I need a sourcing agent?** Not for simple orders. Yes for complex assembly or compliance testing.
