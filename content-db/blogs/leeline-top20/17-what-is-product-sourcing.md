# What Is Product Sourcing? Essential Guide for Importers

Great marketing cannot save a bad sourcing decision. You can run brilliant ad campaigns, but if a factory cuts corners on the assembly line, you are simply paying for negative reviews.

## What is Product Sourcing?

Product sourcing is the strategic process of identifying, evaluating, and securing the right suppliers to manufacture your goods.

| Term | Core Function | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| Sourcing | Strategic supplier selection and setting terms | Negotiating $4.50 unit price and AQL 2.5 defect limit |
| Procurement | Operational purchasing, PO issuance, payment | Wiring 30% initial deposit |
| Supply Chain | Complete macro system | Sourcing → Production → QC → Shipping → Warehousing → Fulfillment |

## How the Sourcing Engine Works

### 1) Start with Product and Margin Targets
Define exact product requirements before contacting factory.

### 2) Choose the Right Sourcing Model
Match business stage to: dropshipping, private-label, wholesale, or mixed-model.

### 3) Find Suppliers Strategically
Match discovery channel to buyer's scale: digital platforms, local agents, trade shows.

### 4) Vet and Compare Suppliers
Demand verified documentation. Test communication latency. Require physical samples.

### 5) Lock Specifications Before Bulk Production
Build comprehensive tech pack. Enforce golden sample protocol.

### 6) Negotiate Commercial Terms and Incoterms
Choose between EXW, FOB, DDP. Lock terms in contract early.

### 7) Plan Quality Control, Logistics, and Inventory Flow
Pre-production checks, in-line audits, pre-shipment inspection.

## Key Benefits

- **Lowers Landed Costs:** Rolling five vendors into one protocol saved client 18%.
- **Guarantees Quality Consistency:** AQL 2.5 inspections catch defects before shipping.
- **Builds Inventory Resilience:** Dual sourcing prevents single points of failure.
- **Accelerates Brand Differentiation:** Custom OEM production escapes price wars.

## Key Risks

- **Scam Intermediaries and IP Leakage:** Sharing specs with unverified intermediaries invites theft.
- **Quality Drift Trap:** Suppliers swap components during bulk production.
- **Hidden Costs and Compliance Nightmares:** Tooling fees, compliance failures.
- **Lead-Time Delays and Stockouts:** Factories prioritize massive corporate buyers.
