# How to Import from China to Canada? Risk-Free Guide

I saw a client lose $5,000 last year. A simple missing invoice trapped their container in Vancouver for three weeks.

## Prerequisites

### 1. Importer Accounts (3-Day Setup)
- CRA & RM Account: Register via CRA Business Number registration
- CARM Portal: Link business in CBSA CARM Client Portal
- Logistics Data: Company contacts, destination province, delivery address

### 2. The Document Pack
- Specs & Financials: Material compositions, sample photos, supplier quotes
- Customs Prep: Commercial invoice, packing list templates, HS codes

### 3. Compliance Hard Stops
- Regulated Goods: Electrical, children's, food-contact items need product certification
- Forced Labor: Canada enforces strict bans
- Textiles & Bags: English/French labeling, fiber percentages, dealer identity

## Complete Guide

### Step 1: Define Your Product Admissibility
Write full commercial description. Verify country of origin. Consult CBSA import guidance.

### Step 2: Set Up CARM Client Portal and Broker Delegation
Activate RM account. Register via CARM portal. Delegate authority to customs broker. Configure RPP security.

### Step 3: Calculate Total Landed Cost
Product Cost + Sampling + Packaging + Factory Inland + Export Docs + Freight + Insurance + Brokerage + Duty + GST/HST + Exams + Port Fees + Final Delivery + Contingency

### Step 4: Lock Supplier Controls and Pre-Shipment Documents
Lock commercial terms in signed PI. Execute rigorous QC plan. Gather export documents.

### Step 5: Select Shipping Mode
- Express: Urgent samples under 50 kg
- Air Freight: Mid-weight, time-sensitive
- Sea Freight: Heavier goods
- LCL: Low volume
- FCL: Scale orders

### Step 6: Finalize Customs Release and Delivery
Submit entry data. Confirm HS classification. Pay duties and taxes. Monitor tracking.

## People Also Ask

1. **How much to import from China to Canada?** Total landed costs add 20%-40% to factory price.
2. **Need import license?** No general license. Must register for BN and RM account.
3. **How long shipping takes?** Air: 5-10 days. Sea: 25-45 days.
