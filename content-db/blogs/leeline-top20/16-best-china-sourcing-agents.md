## How to Hire the Right China Sourcing Agent

### Step 1: Separate Sourcing from Logistics
Sourcing agent finds factories and negotiates. Freight forwarder handles transport.

### Step 2: Audit the 2026 Fee Structure
Look for flat fees, commissions, or hybrid models. Watch for hidden markups.

### Step 3: Run the Final Buyer Checklist
- Require low MOQ experience
- Demand verified supplier process
- Verify product inspection capability
- Check private-label support
- Test compliance awareness

## People Also Ask

1. **How much do agents charge?** 5%-10% commission. Compare service inclusions, not just percentages.
2. **Sourcing agent vs freight forwarder?** Agent finds factories and controls quality. Forwarder moves cargo.
3. **Can agent help with Amazon FBA?** Yes. Good agents handle entire e-commerce supply chain.
4. **How to verify agent?** Check business license, physical office, corporate bank account. Never send to personal account.
