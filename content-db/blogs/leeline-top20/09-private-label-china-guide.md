# Private Label China Guide - Dominate with Expert Tips

Most sourcing advice relies on basic Alibaba searches. That guarantees three-day reply lags, sloppy sample finishes, and obvious generic goods.

## Prerequisites for the Factory Floor

### Strategic Baseline
- SKU & Volume: Define category, annual volume, first-order quantity
- Cost: Calculate landed DDP, not just ex-works
- Channels: Amazon FBA prep requirements differ from Walmart WFS
- Assets: Secure vector logos and packaging designs

### Q3 Telemetry Data
| Strategy | Avg. MOQ | Lead Time | Tooling Cost |
|----------|----------|-----------|--------------|
| White Label | 500 units | 15-20 days | $0 |
| Private Label | 2,000+ units | 45-60 days | $2,000+ |

## 7-Step Private Label Process

### Step 1: Select Your Manufacturing Route
Choose White Label for speed, Modified ODM for simple changes, True Private Label (OEM) for proprietary specs.

### Step 2: Build and Verify Your Supplier Shortlist
Pull from Alibaba, 1688, trade fairs, referrals. Filter to 3-5 candidates.

### Step 3: Freeze Product Specifications and Legal Agreements
Build bilingual RFQ, packaging brief, inspection checklist. Sign NNN agreement before revealing custom details.

### Step 4: Order, Score, and Negotiate the Golden Sample
Order from 3 suppliers simultaneously. Score function, material accuracy, finish, packaging.

### Step 5: Execute the Quality Control SOP
10-point QC checklist: golden sample verification, raw material check, first article inspection, logo/print check, function testing, packaging check, in-process checks, pre-shipment inspection, carton verification, CAPA closure.

### Step 6: Finalize Logistics, Prep, and Routing
Choose shipping method. Execute FBA prep. Confirm pallet rules.

### Step 7: Mature Supplier Relationship and Replenishment System
Hold post-order review. Build leverage with forecasts. Negotiate better terms.

## People Also Ask

1. **How much money to start private labeling?** At least $5,000-$10,000 for proper launch.
2. **Does Trade Assurance protect IP?** No. Trade Assurance only guards money. Need local NNN agreement.
3. **How to verify factory vs trading company?** Check business license scope on NECIPS. Demand live video.
