# 5 Steps Calculate True Product Costing: Price with Precision

A misleading quote looks perfect on paper. You see one clean unit price line without inland China freight, scrap rates, failed inspections, or tooling amortization.

## Prerequisites

- Finalized BOM with material grades in writing
- Standardized Incoterms (FOB baseline)
- Hard Factory Terms: MOQs, tooling, samples, payment
- Logistics Data: dimensions, weights, carton limits
- Quality & Scrap Metrics
- Verified Customs Data: exact HTS/HS codes

## 5-Step Process

### Step 1: Build the Quote Basis Sheet
Create spreadsheet with: SKU Name, Incoterm, Annual Forecast, Trial MOQ, Packaging Requirements, Compliance Requirements, Payment Terms.

### Step 2: Audit and Deconstruct the Bill of Materials

| Component | Spec/Grade | Supplier | Net Usage | Gross Usage | Scrap% | Unit Cost | Extended Cost |
|-----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Main Body | 600D Nylon | Apex Mills | 1.2m | 1.4m | 14% | $2.10 | $2.94 |
| Zipper | #5 YKK Brass | YKK | 1 unit | 1.05 unit | 5% | $0.45 | $0.47 |

### Step 3: Calculate True Manufacturing Cost Formula
Total manufacturing cost = Direct materials + Direct labor + Manufacturing overhead + Amortized tooling/sample cost + Factory margin

### Step 4: Build the Landed Cost Validation Table
Landed cost = FOB + Freight + Insurance + Duties + Handling/Drayage + Brokerage + Domestic delivery + QA/rework exposure + Service fee

### Step 5: Execute the Red-Team Quote Review
Compare should-cost calculation against supplier quote. Highlight unexplained spread.

## People Also Ask

1. **Total landed cost formula?** FOB + Freight + Duties + Insurance + Domestic trucking + QA costs.
2. **Why unit prices increase after sample?** Suppliers exclude packaging, compliance testing, carton specs from initial bids.
3. **How to negotiate lower MOQ?** Order during off-season or use standard raw materials.
