# 6 Steps to Safe Sourcing from China Cut Risk, Boost Control

Finding a supplier is easy. Surviving the volatile gap between quote approval and container arrival destroys margins.

## The Enterprise SOP

### Step 1: Build the Procurement-Grade Sourcing Brief
Create structured RFQ pack with material specs, dimensions, tolerances, BOM, packaging requirements, compliance standards, and target landed cost.

### Step 2: Identify and Filter Your Top Suppliers
Search Alibaba, Made-in-China, Global Sources, 1688. Filter down to 3-5 qualified candidates.

### Step 3: Execute the Forensic Anti-Fraud Verification
Collect Chinese company name, 18-digit USCC. Verify on NECIPS. Match bank beneficiary to legal entity.

### Step 4: Conduct the On-Site Factory Audit and Lock the Golden Sample
Execute Leeline Supplier Audit Checklist. Lock physical golden sample with signed approval tag.

### Step 5: Enforce Mass Production and Quality Control Cadence
Three-phase QC: Pre-Production, During-Production, Pre-Shipment. Use AQL 1.5 standards.

### Step 6: Finalize Commercial Terms and Execute the Shipment
Lock MOQ, unit price, packaging cost. Enforce 30/70 payment schedule. Build bulletproof shipment file.

## Emergency Playbook

- **Sample passed but bulk order worse:** Freeze payment. Verify materials against golden sample.
- **Payment details changed:** Stop payment. Audit documents via NECIPS.
- **Production slipped:** Escalate immediately. Split shipments with air freight.

## People Also Ask

1. **How to verify factory vs trading company?** Cross-reference export records and business license. Request live video on production floor.
2. **Standard MOQ?** 500-1,000 units per SKU. Negotiate during off-season (March-May).
3. **Can I sue Chinese supplier?** Yes with localized NNN agreement enforceable in Chinese courts.
4. **Best payment method?** Wire Transfer (T/T) to registered corporate account. 30/70 split.
