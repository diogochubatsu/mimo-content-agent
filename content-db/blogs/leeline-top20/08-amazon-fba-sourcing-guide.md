# Follow Amazon FBA Sourcing Guide - 6 Proven Ways to Scale

Bad sourcing decisions emerge months later as Q4 stockouts, rejected inbound shipments, and crushed margins.

## Prerequisites: The Readiness Gate

- BOM & Target ASIN: Lock down strict Bill of Materials
- Financial Floors: Define retail price and minimum gross-margin floor
- Carton Configurations: Finalize master packaging dimensions
- Category Compliance: Identify mandatory certifications (CPC, CE, FDA, RoHS)
- Protect IP: Sign China-enforceable NNN agreements via CNIPA
- Reject Blind DDP: Demand itemized freight route

## 6 Powerful Ways to Grow

### Step 1: Draft a Bulletproof Sourcing Brief
State exact measurements, target ex-factory cost, MOQ target, packaging method, compliance needs, production timeline.

### Step 2: Screen and Shortlist Your Suppliers
Search Alibaba, 1688, trade shows, referrals. Screen 10-15 suppliers, narrow to 4-5 finalists.

### Step 3: Audit Factory Capacity and Verify Production
Confirm legal entity, verify in-house production, inspect machine count, calculate daily output.

### Step 4: Calculate Total Landed Cost and Lock Negotiation Terms
**Example for 1,000 units:**
- Unit Cost: $5.00
- Tooling Amortization: $0.50
- Packaging: $0.20
- Ocean Freight: $1.10
- Customs Duty (25%): $1.25
- Total Landed Cost: $8.47

### Step 5: Execute Quality Control and AQL Inspection SOP
- Critical Defects (AQL 0): Unsafe flaws
- Major Defects (AQL 2.5): Functional failures
- Minor Defects (AQL 4.0): Cosmetic issues

### Step 6: Format Packaging and FBA Labels for Inbound Compliance
Generate FNSKU, assign labels, mask conflicting barcodes, enforce polybag rules.

## People Also Ask

1. **How to verify supplier is real factory?** Check business license on NECIPS. Request live video from production floor.
2. **Standard payment terms?** 30% deposit, 70% before shipment after passed AQL inspection.
3. **How long to source for FBA?** 8-12 weeks complete cycle.
