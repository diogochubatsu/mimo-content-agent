# 6 Hidden Sourcing Costs Revealed – Stop Overpaying Suppliers

Procurement teams often approve attractive EXW or FOB terms, only to watch margins vanish when hidden sourcing costs surface mid-production.

## 6 Hidden Cost Buckets

### 1. Factory Price vs. Landed Cost
| Category (20ft Container) | Average EXW Price | Real Landed Cost | Variance |
|--------------------------|-------------------|------------------|----------|
| Consumer Electronics | $12.50 | $16.15 | +29.2% |
| Cut-and-Sew Textiles | $4.20 | $6.45 | +53.5% |
| Molded Hardware | $2.80 | $4.10 | +46.4% |

### 2. Freight Quotes That Hide Port, Terminal, and Storage Charges
Cheap headline prices cover only origin handling and basic ocean freight. Destination fees hide.

### 3. Duties, Taxes, and Amazon FBA Fees
Wrong HS code ruins margins. Amazon prep fees add $0.20/unit for labeling and polybagging.

### 4. Quality Control Costs
$300 inspection prevents $4,000 rework bill. Standard man-day: $250-$350.

### 5. Sourcing Agent Fees and Hidden Markups
Honest agents charge 3%-7% and show real factory invoice. Bad agents hide 20% markup.

### 6. Customs Holds, Compliance Gaps, and Delay Fees
Missing invoices account for 75% of customs holds.

## How to Calculate Total Landed Cost

**Formula:** Total Landed Cost = Factory Price + Freight + Customs/Duties + Hidden Sourcing Costs

### Step 1: Set Up Spreadsheet Fields
Track: product cost, quantity, dimensions, Incoterm, freight mode, origin charges, duties, brokerage, Amazon prep, warehouse storage, contingency.

### Step 2: Run the Operating Sequence
Collect all quote inputs. Normalize Incoterms. Enter duty assumptions. Add admin overhead.

### Step 3: Audit Quotes Like an Expert
Compare two suppliers on strictly equal terms.

## People Also Ask

1. **Landed cost formula?** Factory Price + Freight + Customs/Duties + Hidden Costs.
2. **Which hidden fees missed most?** Destination port fees and currency conversion markups.
3. **EXW vs FOB vs DDP impact?** Yes. Switching EXW to FOB dropped landed cost by $0.40/unit.
4. **Agent fees cheaper than direct?** Transparent agent saves money by preventing defects.
5. **Budget for inspection and FBA prep?** $250-$350 man-day inspection. $0.20/unit FBA prep.
