# How to Simplify Shipping from China? Stress-Free Guide

**Shipping from China** usually operates like a black box. We want you to see the real costs of global shipping.

## Prerequisites

- Verified supplier/consignee legal details
- Exact product descriptions, quantities, weights, carton dimensions
- Importer of Record, Tax/IDs, broker contact
- Incoterms selection, service level, insurance, warehouse type

## How to Handle Shipping from China to the US

### Step 1: Lock the Import Structure and Incoterms
Name legal Importer of Record. Choose FOB or DDP.

### Step 2: Calculate the True Landed Cost
| Shipping Mode | Real Transit Time | Headline Freight | Hidden Destination Fees |
|--------------|-------------------|------------------|------------------------|
| Air Express | 5-7 Days | $6.50/kg | $0 (Included) |
| Standard Air | 10-15 Days | $4.20/kg | $150-$300 |
| Ocean FCL | 35-45 Days | $2,500/Box | $800-$1,500 |
| Ocean LCL | 40-55 Days | $120/CBM | $400-$900 |

### Step 3: Assign the Correct HS/HTS Classification
Search official USITC HTS Database. Never let supplier guess.

### Step 4: Verify the Commercial Document Stack
Match Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading.

### Step 5: Optimize Packaging and Compare Freight Quotes
Request quotes from 3-5 providers. Compare line by line.

### Step 6: Monitor the Freight Handoff and Transit Milestones
Track: Cargo Received, Export Released, ATD, ETA.

### Step 7: Clear U.S. Customs and Finalize Delivery
Wait for Arrival Notice. File entry documents. Pay duties.

## People Also Ask

1. **How much does shipping cost?** $6.50/kg air express to $2,500/container ocean freight.
2. **How long does shipping take?** 5 days air to 45 days ocean FCL.
3. **Cheapest way to ship?** Ocean freight (FCL) for high-volume cargo.
4. **Do I need import license?** No general license needed. Customs Bond for shipments over $2,500.
