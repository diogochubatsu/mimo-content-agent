# How to Import from China: Complete Guide (2026)

**Source:** Leeline Sourcing Blog
**URL:** https://leelinegroup.com/blog/
**Language:** en
**Date:** 2026-07-25

## Key Takeaways

1. **Platform Selection:** 1688 for lowest prices, Alibaba for easier English interface
2. **Supplier Verification:** Check Trade Assurance, years on platform, transaction history
3. **Payment Methods:** Alipay recommended for 1688, Trade Assurance for Alibaba
4. **Shipping Options:** Air (7-15 days), Sea (25-40 days), Express (5-7 days)
5. **Customs & Duties:** Vary by country - research before ordering

## Summary

Importing from China requires careful planning. Start with small sample orders, verify suppliers thoroughly, and understand your local customs requirements before placing bulk orders.
