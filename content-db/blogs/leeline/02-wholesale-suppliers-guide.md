**Source:** Leeline Sourcing Blog
**URL:** https://leelinegroup.com/blog/
**Language:** en
**Date:** 2026-07-25

## Key Takeaways

1. **Wholesale vs Retail:** Wholesale prices are 50-70% lower than retail
2. **MOQ Negotiation:** Most suppliers accept 50-70% of listed MOQ for first orders
3. **Quality Control:** Always order samples before bulk purchases
4. **Payment Protection:** Use Trade Assurance or escrow services
5. **Long-term Relationships:** Better prices with repeat business

## Summary

Finding reliable wholesale suppliers takes time but pays off. Focus on building relationships with 2-3 trusted suppliers rather than chasing the lowest price.
