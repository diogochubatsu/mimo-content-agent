Dropshippinghas been around for quite some time now. It is a low-risk eCommerce business model where you do not keep an inventory and all your orders are fulfilled directly by the supplier.
But by any means, launching a successful dropshipping store isn’t a walk in the park.
The competition is growing with each passing day, so it’s only natural to ask yourself, is dropshipping worth it nowadays? or is the market too saturated to break through?
To clear your confusion, in our Ultimate Guide we’re going to shed some light on whether dropshipping is worth your time and money.
Let’s begin!

## Is Dropshipping Worth It in 2023?

Dropshipping is worth it if you want a decent monthly income by starting a business on a low budget. But it cannot make you a ton of money in a short period of time.
Also, launching a successful dropshipping business requires patience, hard work, and resilience. Contrary to what most people believe, it isn’t an easy “money-making scheme”.

## What Makes Dropshipping Worth It?

Around33% of online storesuse the dropshipping fulfillment model, and here are a couple of reasons why:

### Low Investment to Start

In comparison to other eCommerce business models, dropshipping requires the lowest capital. That’s because a dropshipping store does not have an inventory. Every aspect of order fulfillment is handled by the supplier.
Most of your money will go towards website design and marketing. But platforms like Shopify also have plenty of custom themes, so this is another option that’s worth exploring, especially if you’re on a low budget.

### Easy to Manage

Dropshipping stores are easy to manage mainly because there’s no physical storefront and most of the work is handled by the supplier.
In fact, if youautomate your dropshipping business, then your orders can even be fulfilled while you’re asleep.

### Create Passive Income

Order fulfillment is one of the most time consuming aspects of an eCommerce store. But with dropshipping, this burden is not yours to carry.
That is the reason many entrepreneurs feel compelled to start a dropshipping store as a side hustle. If done right, it can help you earn a stable passive income.

### Improved Cash Flow

Dropshipping allows you more control over your funds. Traditional eCommerce stores always have to use a part of their profit for purchasing new inventory.
However, with a dropshipping store, you only pay for one unit at a time and don’t have to worry about any bulk investments.

### Minimal Risk

For most eCommerce businesses, a huge chunk of investment goes towards purchasing the products. If you’re supplying from overseas, then it’s ideally recommended to stock 3 months-worth of units in advance.
With so much inventory, if your store gets suspended or for some reason, the product loses its popularity, all your investment will be wasted. However, with dropshipping, you don’t have to worry about that either.

## What are the Drawbacks of Dropshipping?

Like any other business, dropshipping has its flaws and risks. So let’s walk you through some of its biggest drawbacks:

### High Competition

The fact that dropshipping can be started with a low investment also contributes to one of its biggest drawbacks, which is competition.
Unless you’re doing private label dropshipping, there’s a high chance that someone else will copy your listings, product descriptions, and image to start their own store.
To add, only10% of dropshipping storessucceed in their first year of operations.

### Quality Issues

Quality issues are quite common in a dropshipping business, especially if you don’t pick your supplier carefully. Ultimately, you don’t know what’s being shipped to your customers.
This is why product sampling is considered to be an essential part of starting a dropshipping store. Some stores offer free sampling, while others charge a certain fee.
But whether it’s free or not, it should always be your top priority.

### Supplier Reliability

The supplier you choose can make or break your dropshipping business. Even the slightest of negligence can lead to refunds and negative reviews.
And since you’re relying on your supplier’s stock, you don’t get the opportunity to double-check the product. So in case, there’s a delay or quality issue from the suppliers’ side, you’ll be the one bearing the losses.

### Less Profit

The dropshipping business model works by keeping a specific percentage ofprofit on each sale. Depending on the popularity of the product, that percentage can range anywhere from 10% to 40%,
This is why, unless you’redropshipping high ticket products, or in other words, expensive products, you won’t make too much profit on each sale.
Even if you’re keeping a 20% profit, a low ticket product priced at $20 will only make you a $4 profit. However, high ticket products start at $200, so at the very least, you’ll make $40 on each sale, which isn’t too bad.

### Poor Customer Support

As we mentioned, in dropshipping, your supplier handles every aspect of order fulfillment. So in case something goes wrong, it would be difficult for you to offer competent customer support.
First, you would have to get in touch with the supplier and wait for their response. Only then, can you offer a helpful resolution. And by then, the damage is usually done.

## Can Dropshipping be a Long-Term Business?

Dropshipping can be a long-term business, but like any other venture, there may be some ups and downs along the way. Here are a few things that could potentially get in the way of your dropshipping business:

### Outdated Products

The key to a thriving dropshipping business is to always search for what’s trending.
We’re not asking you to deviate from your brand, but it’s essential to know what people are searching for nowadays.
Most dropshipping stores often die because the store owners get too comfortable. They forget that trends change and so do the preferences and choices of people.
So you don’t have to sell multiple products in your store. Just make sure that the one product you have on your listings can be upsold.

### Inefficient Customer Service

We’ve also mentioned earlier that we don’t see dropshipping as a money-making scheme. Don’t only focus on making a profit on each sale, but also on customer satisfaction.
If you’re consistently getting complaints from your customers then try identifying the reason behind it.
Is it late shipping, or is the quality of the product? Regardless of what the reason is, rectify those problems even if you have to change your supplier.

### Inconsistency by Supplier

You can purely call this bad luck, but at the end of the day, as a dropshipper, you’re completely dependent on a third party to keep your business afloat.
If for some reason, your supplier disappears or runs out of stock, then that can completely halt your business operations. The only way around this is to always have a backup supplier to give you a helping hand.

## Why Do Most Dropshipping Stores Fail?

We mentioned earlier that 90% of dropshipping stores fail in their first year of operation, and here are the reasons behind it:

### Inadequate Planning

Going into any business unprepared is a recipe for disaster, and the same goes for dropshipping. In the past, setting up a copy-pasted dropshipping store may have worked due to lack of competition, but today, this isn’t the case.
If you’re setting up a dropshipping store without gaining any prior knowledge about how it works and how to promote it, then you’re bound to fail.

### Random Products

Product hunting has always been the most essential phase of starting an online store. And with so much competition, picking random products is the last thing you want to do as a dropshipper.
Just because your competitor is getting good results with a particular product, doesn’t mean you would as well.
They probably had a solid strategy and knew how to make it work. So you would have to find your own way with the product you choose.

### Lack of Investment

Dropshipping is a “low-investment” business model, not a “no-investment” business model. Unfortunately, most people think of it as the latter and try to save every dime that they can.
You don’t have to spend money on purchasing an inventory. But creating an aesthetic website is still essential – in fact, that’s the bare minimum you could do.

### Analyze and Plan Ahead

When you’re running any business, analyzing your competition and planning ahead is an ongoing process. You need to have a solid strategy both before and after your business becomes successful.
