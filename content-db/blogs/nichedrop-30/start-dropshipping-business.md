## What Is Dropshipping?

Dropshipping is an order fulfillment method that allows eCommerce stores to outsource the core operations of their business.
All the orders are sourced, packaged, and fulfilled directly by a third-party supplier – who may also be the manufacturer.
Simply put, dropshipping business owners work as an intermediary between the supplier and the customers.

## How Does the Dropshipping Business Model Work?

A dropshipping store works just like a regular eCommerce store.
First, you choose a product, then search for a supplier for the said product.
After that, all you need to do is set up an online storefront and generate sales.
Once you start receiving orders, forward the order information to your supplier, who will take care of the rest.
To make a profit, you will set the prices of the products higher than the sourcing price.

## How to Start a Dropshipping Business by Country

### How to Start a Dropshipping Business in the USA

You need an EIN and possibly an LLC. Most dropshippers register in Wyoming or Delaware for low fees and no state income tax.
- Business structure: LLC protects your personal assets. Sole proprietorship is free but risky.
- EIN: Apply free at irs.gov. Required to open a business bank account and file taxes.
- Sales tax: You must collect sales tax in states where you have “nexus” (inventory, employees, or high sales volume). Use TaxJar or Avalara to track automatically.
- Import duties: Shipments under $800 enter duty-free. Above that, customs duties apply.
- Business license: Most states don’t require a general business license for online-only dropshipping, but check your city/county rules.

### How to Start a Dropshipping Business in Canada

Register your business provincially or federally. You need a GST/HST number once you hit $30,000 CAD in revenue.
- Business registration: Federal incorporation ($200 CAD) protects your business name nationwide. Provincial registration is cheaper but limited.
- GST/HST: Register immediately if you expect to exceed $30,000 CAD in any 12-month period. Charge 5-15% depending on province.
- Import duties: Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) collects duties on goods over $20 CAD. Factor this into your pricing or use Canadian-based suppliers.
- Quebec requirements: If selling to Quebec, you need French-language customer support and website content under Bill 96.

### How to Start a Dropshipping Business in Australia

Get an ABN first. GST kicks in at $75,000 AUD. Import GST applies to all goods, even under $1,000.
- ABN/ACN: Apply free at abr.gov.au. An ABN lets you claim business expenses and register for GST.
- GST: Register once you reach $75,000 AUD in annual turnover. Charge 10% on all sales.
- Import GST: Since 2018, all imported goods face 10% GST regardless of value. Your supplier or freight forwarder usually handles this.
- ACCC compliance: Australian consumer law guarantees refunds for faulty products. You—not your supplier—are responsible to the customer.
- Restricted products: Check the TGA (Therapeutic Goods Administration) for health product rules and ACMA for electronics standards.

### How to Start a Dropshipping Business in Singapore

Register with ACRA. GST only applies after $1 million SGD revenue, but early registration builds credibility.
- ACRA registration: Incorporate online at bizfile.gov.sg. Cost starts at $315 SGD. Most foreigners use a local resident director or corporate service provider.
