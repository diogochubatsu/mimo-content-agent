### Are Made in China Products Bad?

There’s a common misconception, especially among new dropshippers, that products made in China are low quality.
That’s not true! This myth comes from the misunderstandings between dropshippers and suppliers that usually occur when ordering the correct clothing material or color.
Remember, as a dropshipper placing a product sourcing order, it’s your responsibility to give a precise product description so that the order fulfills your expectations.
For example, ordering a blue T-shirt is a typical rookie dropshipper mistake. Blue is very general. Instead, give your supplier the exact color code of the ‘blue’ you need.
Of course, we’re not saying that all products ordered from China will be of high quality. There are also many bad Chinese suppliers. To avoid this, always make sure to read the reviews of the supplier or product you’re ordering.

### What Percentage of Products in the US Are Made in China?

It depends on the category. Estimates suggest that roughly 60–80% of consumer goods sold in major US retailers—including Walmart, Amazon marketplace SKUs, and Target—are manufactured wholly or partially in China.
For electronics, toys, and textiles, the percentage often exceeds 80%. For food and heavy industrial machinery, it is significantly lower.
If you are sourcing for a US-based dropshipping store, assuming your competitors are also using Chinese suppliers is a safe bet.
Your advantage comes from how you source (agent-assisted, QC-verified, US-warehoused), not from avoiding China.

### Is Made-in-China.com a Legit Site for Dropshipping?

Yes. Made-in-China.com is a legitimate B2B platform operated by Focus Technology, a publicly listed Chinese company. It specializes in connecting international buyers with Chinese manufacturers.
However, it is primarily designed for bulk importers and wholesalers, not single-unit dropshippers. Minimum order quantities (MOQs) are typically higher than on AliExpress, and shipping logistics are buyer-managed.
If you want to use Made-in-China.com for dropshipping, you need either:
- A freight forwarder to consolidate and ship small batches; or
- A dropshipping agent who can place the MOQ order, store inventory, and fulfill single orders on your behalf.
