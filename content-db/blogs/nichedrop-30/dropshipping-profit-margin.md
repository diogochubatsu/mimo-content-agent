## What is Dropshipping Profit Margin?

Dropshipping profit margin refers to the percentage of your selling price that remains as profit after covering all costs involved in getting a product to your customer.
Put simply, it’s the gap between the price you sell the product for and the total expenses, including product costs, shipping, and other fees.
For instance, if you sell a pair of shoes for $100 but it costs $90 to source and deliver them, your profit margin is $10—or 10%.
Understanding your dropshipping profit margin is important for several reasons:
- Pricing:It helps you set prices that cover costs while still making a profit.
- Financial Health:Keeping track of your margins shows how well your business is doing.
- Growth:Knowing your margins can help you find ways to improve and grow your business.
In short, dropshipping profit margin tells youhow much moneyyou keep from sales after paying for everything. By calculating and understanding your margins, you can make better decisions for your business’s success.

## How to Calculate Your Dropshipping Profit?

Calculating your dropshipping profit is essential for understanding how much money you actually make from your sales. Here’s a simple breakdown of how to do it:
Before calculating profits, you need to know your costs, which include:
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): This is the price you pay to your supplier for the product, including shipping fees.
- Other Expenses: These can include marketing costs, transaction fees, website hosting, and any other operational expenses.
To calculate your profit margin, you can use the following formula:
Profit Margin (%) = [(Selling Price – Total Costs) / Selling Price] × 100
Let’s say you’re selling a product for $100. If your COGS is $70 and your other expenses total $10, your total costs would be $80.
You can apply this in the formula as:
Profit Margin (%) = [(100 – 80) / 100] * 100 = 20%
This is of course, a very simplified example. As your store grows, you’ll be playing with bigger numbers but the formula to calculate your profit margin will remain the same.

## How Much Dropshipping Profit Margin You Should Keep?

Deciding how much dropshipping profit margin one should keep depends on a variety of factors. Let’s discuss a couple of them below:

### When Was Your Store Created?

More than33% of online storesuse dropshipping as their fulfillment model. So it’s safe to say that the competition is tough.
If you’re keeping high product prices from day one, you’ll have a hard time surviving. Unless you’re bringing a unique product to the market.
So if your dropshipping business is still in its initial stages, then stick to a low or an average profit margin.

### What Is Your Seller Rating?

Your seller rating is determined by the number of positive reviews you get. This is what all the online stores are racing for nowadays.
The higher your seller rating, the higher the number of orders you’ll receive. A good seller rating is considered to be 4.3 or above. And a fantastic rating is 4.7 or above.
If your store is in the latter category, then you can consider going for a high dropshipping profit margin.

### How Many Sales Are You Getting?

Before you increase the prices for a higher profit margin, assess the number of regular sales you’re getting.
Increasing the prices of your products is always risky. If only a handful of customers are shopping from your store, then reconsider your decision for now.
In most cases, it will make you lose a couple of customers. However, if you’re already getting hundreds of monthly orders, then increasing the prices wouldn’t be as risky for you.

## When Should You Increase Your Dropshipping Profit Margin?

## How to Increase your Dropshipping Profit Margin?

Rather than randomly increasing the price of your products, here are some techniques you could follow for a higher margin:

### Offer Bundle Discounts

Bundle discounts can do wonders for your business. For instance, if you’re selling a product at a rate of $20 per piece, you could instead, sell a bundle of 3 at $55.
Now you might be thinking that you’re losing $5, so how is this profitable? Well, it’s simple – higher volume means higher profit.
Instead of making a meagre $20, you’re making $55 with a single sale.
Besides, discount offers are one of the best ways to attract more customers. So by doing this, not only will you get more sales, but also make faster profit.

### Free Shipping Strategy

There are two ways for you to adjust the shipping fee while running an online store.
To give an example, your first option is to set the product price to $45 with a $5 shipping fee. Alternatively, your product price can directly be set to $51 with a “free shipping” tag. That’s right, you can even earn an extra dollar or two this way.
An increasing number of stores have been adopting this strategy and trust us, it really does work.
Customers are often compelled to purchase from shops that offer “free shipping”.
While in reality, the shipping fee is already included in the product price and for that too, they’re charging slightly higher.

### Change your Supplier

The supplier controls the flow of a dropshipping store. Changing one might be all that you need to do to get a bigger profit margin.
But how to decide you shouldchange your supplier? Simple, browse through the product prices of your competitors.
If they’re selling the product at a much lower price, then this also means their sourcing price is far lesser than yours.
So now your only option is to source the product at a lower or a similar price. This will significantly help you increase your profit margin.

### Market yourself as a Brand

Your dropshipping store doesn’t have to be based on a copy-pasted theme like hundreds of others out there.
Spending a bit of time on branding and creating an aesthetic website can help you vamp up those profit margins.
Wondering how? Well, it’s simple – customers often feel more compelled to purchase from a brand, in comparison to a regular store.
The reason for that is, brands often look more credible. This is why you need to start marketing your dropshipping store as a brand.

### Improve Customer Service

Efficient customer service can be the ultimate game-changer for any online business.
Even if you’re keeping a higher margin than your competitors, customers would prefer purchasing the products from you.
The reason for that is your responsive customer service and your dedication to their satisfaction.

## 10 Key Factors That Can Affect Your Dropshipping Profit Margin

Sometimes, your dropshipping profit margin is tied to multiple factors than just its pricing. Here are to name a few:

### 1) Choice of Products

Some dropshipping products can naturally be sold at a higher price. This often includes gadgets and accessories.
So choosing a niche with more expensive products can both be a curse or a blessing in disguise.
A blessing because if the product is naturally expensive, you could sell it at an even higher price and keep a decent margin.
But if everyone knows the product is cheap, to begin with, then you don’t really have too much flexibility with its price.

### 2) Market Competition

Before you increase the prices of your products, don’t forget to analyze the competition.
If most of your competitors are selling the product at a lower price, then you might want to wait it out.
Showing haste may affect your sales and even drive some of your customers away.

### 3) Area of Operation

The location where you’re operating your dropshipping store can also significantly matter.
If a product is trending in one country, it doesn’t mean it is also in another.
This is why to secure the highest profit margins, before you launch your store, evaluate which demographics the product is performing the best in.

### 4) Sourcing Difficulty

The profit margin of the product also significantly depends on its difficulty to source.
If you’re dealing in regular phone accessories then you’ll have to be a bit careful while increasing the margin. There are hundreds of other stores your customers could purchase from.
However, if you’re dealing in a layered niche market that isn’t easy to source, then you naturally have the opportunity to keep more profit.

### 5) Shipping Costs

Dropshippers are recommended to search for suppliers who have warehouses in the countries they plan on operating.
The reason for that is not only timely fulfilment of orders but also, reducing shipping costs.
Importing products to another country can be a long process, and also impose a heavy fee on you. This, as a result, can affect your overall profit margin.

### 6) You Rely on the Same Supplier

In dropshipping, the supplier controls the overall flow of your business. The more they increase the product sourcing price, the more your profit margins drop.
This is why you shouldn’t put all your eggs in one basket.
Note that, we’re not suggesting that you should change your supplier too often, but there’s no harm in being on the lookout for better options.
Especially, if your supplier is increasing the product prices more frequently.

### 7) Your Products are Difficult to Ship

Dropshippers often avoid bulky products as they’re difficult to ship.
If you’re ready to take on the challenge, the real problem still stands: What if they get damaged?
And even with extra care to get them delivered to their destination, a large chunk of your money would go towards shipping.
This is why it’s normally recommended to choose products that are lightweight and easy to ship.

### 8) The Competition is Too Much

There’s also a chance that the product you’re trying to dropship might just have too much competition, to begin with.
Higher competition means a lower profit margin because you would have to keep your product prices similar to what other stores have.
So what can you do in such a situation? Go back to the drawing board.
Take your time to search for a low-competition product. Although difficult,it will be worth it, especially, when you see the profit coming in.

### 9) Your Choice of Platform

We often emphasize the importance of creating an attractive website for your dropshipping store.
However, you don’t always have to churn out big bucks for it.
Your choice of platform alone can make all the difference. To give you some perspective, there are hundreds offree themes on Shopifythat you could tweak to prepare your website.
Similarly, they also have numerous freeapps and pluginsthat you could use to further use toautomate your business.
So if all your money is going on website maintenance and design, then reconsider the platform you’re using.

### 10) Too Many Refund Requests

Refund requests are downright a nightmare for dropshipping store owners.
In most cases, buyers who ask for refund requests also leave a negative review. This not only puts a dent in your profit but also on your reputation.
So this isn’t a problem that you can simply ignore.
Try identifying the reasons behind the recurring refund requests, and rectify the problems, otherwise, it can even put your store at risk.

## 3 Golden Product Pricing Tips for a Higher Dropshipping Profit Margin

Dropshipping is a game of numbers. You have to strategically set and increase yourproduct pricesto earn more cash.
So here are 3 golden tips you could implement for your store:

### 1. Don’t Jump Too Quick

If your dropshipping store is performing well, it can be tempting to increase the prices.
But jumping the gun too early can do more bad than good for your business.
Instead of making a single big leap, take multiple small steps. Otherwise, it can even give your competitors a chance to break through the market.
Plan monthly increments to make it more digestible for your customers. This way, not only can you retain your existing customers, but also get closer to your desired profit margin.

### 2. Use Odd-Even Pricing

Nowadays almost every other store uses the “odd-even” pricing technique. The way it works is that, let’s say your desired price to sell a product is $50.
So instead of setting the price on your listings to $50, you’ll instead go $49.95 or a number closer to your desired price.
We know, there isn’t much difference. However, customers often end up rounding the number down and taking it as $49.
So although this pricing technique is mainstream, it’s still quite useful and can be a great way to make more profit while disguising it as less.

### 3. Display Discount Offers

Creating a sense of urgency with the “once-in-a-lifetime” discount offers is another way to capture the attention of your customers.
This can especially work well with both of the tips we’ve already discussed above.
You could slightly increase the price of your product, and then put up a “discount” to make the customers believe that they’re getting a better deal.
Keeping in mind how displaying discount offers also encourages impulsive purchases, it can be a great way to make more profit.
