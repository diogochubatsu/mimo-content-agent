# Swiss Entrepreneur Propounds Groundbreaking Business Model

E-commerce entrepreneur and business coach, Yomi Denzel, talks about his "unique" model adopted to grow his business.

## The internet and e-commerce revolution

The internet has changed the way businesses are done as well as the way people go about their daily lives. The emergence of e-commerce and other internet-related business solutions has helped the growth of businesses, allowing them to reach their target audience with relative ease.

## Yomi's entrepreneurial journey

Starting out, Yomi launched a social media management agency, where he was able to earn about $5,000. However, the difficulty in scaling his business was a major problem. His long hours of research into another profitable and scalable business led to the discovery of e-commerce.

## The dropshipping breakthrough

Yomi launched his first online store in September 2017 using a dropshipping model. The business almost immediately became a success, generating over $30,000 in sales revenue within the first month.

## The influencer marketing strategy

Yomi also developed a breakthrough marketing strategy, using influencers and story posts to generate sales. This became a key part of his business model.

## Entrepreneurship philosophy

According to Yomi Denzel, entrepreneurship is about solving problems all day long and the more difficult the problem is, the bigger the reward. He also places importance on timing, regardless of the business model adopted.
