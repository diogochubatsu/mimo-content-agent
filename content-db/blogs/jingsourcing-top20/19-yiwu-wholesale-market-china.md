# Yiwu Wholesale Market China: Complete Sourcing Guide for Importers

Yiwu Wholesale Market China is one of the world's largest wholesale sourcing hubs for importers, wholesalers, Amazon sellers, retailers, and eCommerce businesses. Located in Zhejiang Province, Yiwu International Trade Market contains more than 75,000 shops across 5 major districts.

## What Is Yiwu Wholesale Market China?

Yiwu Wholesale Market China, officially known as Yiwu International Trade Market, is the world's largest wholesale market for small commodities and consumer products.
