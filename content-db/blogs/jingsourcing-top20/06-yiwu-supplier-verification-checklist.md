# Yiwu Supplier Verification Checklist Before Paying Any Deposit

Before paying any Yiwu supplier, use a clear verification checklist to confirm who you are buying from, what stock they really have, how payment should work, and whether the products match your import requirements.

## Why Supplier Verification Matters in Yiwu Market

Unlike buying from one large factory, Yiwu buyers may place orders with 20, 50, or even more than 100 suppliers during one sourcing trip. Without a proper verification process, small mistakes can quickly become expensive.

## Yiwu Market Deposit vs Factory Deposit

| Item | Yiwu Market Supplier | Factory Supplier |
|------|---------------------|------------------|
| Common product type | Stock or semi-stock items | Made-to-order production |
| Typical first payment | RMB 500–1,000 reservation deposit | Often 30% production deposit |
| Buying style | Mixed items from many suppliers | One product from one factory |
| Main purpose of deposit | Hold stock before delivery plan | Start production |

## 10-Point Verification Checklist

1. **Confirm the Supplier's Real Business Identity** - Ask for booth number, company name, contact person, phone number, WeChat, and business license.

2. **Check Whether the Product Is Stock, Semi-Stock, or Custom-Made** - This affects deposit amount, delivery time, inspection timing, and risk level.

3. **Verify the Sample and Bulk Goods Standard** - Ask whether bulk goods will be exactly the same as the sample.

4. **Confirm the Real MOQ and Mixed Color Rules** - MOQ may be per item, per color, per carton, or per design.

5. **Confirm Price, Currency, and What Is Included** - Ask whether price includes inner packing, export carton, delivery, logo printing, barcode sticker.

6. **Confirm Delivery Time to Warehouse** - Ask for estimated delivery date to your Yiwu agent's warehouse.

7. **Verify the Supplier's Payment Method** - Confirm who will receive the payment and whether bank account matches business information.

8. **Confirm Packaging Requirements Before Ordering** - Packaging mistakes are one of the most common problems during consolidation.

9. **Plan Product Inspection Before Paying the Final Balance** - Arrange inspection before paying remaining balance.

10. **Keep Complete Order Records** - Create one folder for each supplier with all documentation.

## Final Verification Checklist

- ☐ Supplier identity confirmed
- ☐ Business information verified
- ☐ Stock availability confirmed
- ☐ Sample matches bulk specification
- ☐ MOQ confirmed
- ☐ Price confirmed
- ☐ Packaging confirmed
- ☐ Delivery schedule confirmed
- ☐ Inspection arranged
- ☐ Order records completed
