# How to Find Low-MOQ Products in Yiwu Without Sacrificing Quality

To find low MOQ products in Yiwu without sacrificing quality, choose products already made from standard materials, colours, components, and packaging. Compare several suppliers, approve a physical sample, write measurable specifications, and use a small pilot order with inspection before increasing volume.

## Why Low MOQ Products in Yiwu Are Possible

Yiwu Market serves wholesalers that stock or repeatedly produce broad product ranges. Many vendors can sell by carton, mixed colour assortment, or modest batch because they combine demand from several buyers.

## Stock Product, Standard Production, or Custom Order?

| Order Type | MOQ Flexibility | Quality Control Priority |
|------------|-----------------|-------------------------|
| Ready stock | Often lowest | Confirm batch consistency |
| Standard repeat production | Moderate | Retain approved sample |
| Light customization | Depends on suppliers | Approve artwork separately |
| Full customization | Usually highest | Use development milestones |

## Low MOQ Product Selection Checklist

- Uses standard materials and components already purchased by the supplier
- Relies on existing moulds, patterns, colours, and production methods
- Has simple packaging or can use a standard box with a compliant label
- Does not require expensive product-specific certification for a tiny batch
- Can be inspected with clear measurements and functional tests
- Has sufficient margin after unit price, inspection, consolidation, and freight

## Pilot-Order Process for Low MOQ Products in Yiwu

1. Shortlist standard products
2. Verify the supplier
3. Compare samples
4. Confirm the real MOQ
5. Calculate landed cost
6. Place a controlled order
7. Inspect before release
8. Review market results
