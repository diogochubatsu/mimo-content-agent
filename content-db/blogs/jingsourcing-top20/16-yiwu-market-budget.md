# Yiwu Market Budget: How Much Money Do You Need to Start?

If you are planning to import from Yiwu Market, the first thing you need to understand is your Yiwu Market budget. In most real cases, a practical Yiwu Market budget ranges from $3,000 to $10,000.

## Minimum Yiwu Market Budget: Understanding Product Costs

Typical product pricing structure:
- $0.1–$50 per product type
- 500–1000 pieces per item
- Orders usually spread across multiple suppliers

For wholesale, the initial Yiwu Market budget for products alone is usually between $10,000 and $50,000.

## Shipping Cost

Depending on your shipment size, shipping can represent 10% to 30% of your total Yiwu Market budget.

### Shipping Methods Comparison

- **Air Freight:** Fast delivery, higher cost ($5–$10/kg)
- **Sea LCL:** Lower cost, slower delivery
- **Full Container (FCL):** Best value for large orders

## Yiwu Agent Fees and Service Costs

Most Yiwu agents charge a commission between 5% and 10%.

## Hidden Costs

- Bank transfer fees
- Currency exchange differences
- Warehouse storage fees
- Inspection upgrades
- Customs duties and taxes

These costs can add an extra 5% to 15% to your total budget.

## Real Scenarios

### Beginner Buyer
- Products: $2,000
- Shipping: $800
- Agent Fee: $150
- **Total Budget: ~$3,000**

### Small Business Importer
- Products: $5,000
- Shipping: $2,000
- Agent Fee: $400
- **Total Budget: ~$7,500**

### Growing Importer
- Products: $10,000+
- Shipping: $3,000+
- **Total Budget: $13,000+**
