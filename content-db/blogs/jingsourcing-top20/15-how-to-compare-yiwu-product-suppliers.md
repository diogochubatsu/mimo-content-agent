# How to Compare Yiwu Product Suppliers Beyond Unit Price

If you want to know how to compare Yiwu product suppliers, do not start with the cheapest unit price. Start by comparing the full buying result: product quality, MOQ, packaging, lead time, supplier reliability, inspection risk, and final landed cost.

## Why Unit Price Alone Is Not Enough

Unit price is important, but it only shows one part of the buying cost. For example, Supplier A may quote $1.20 per piece, while Supplier B quotes $1.35 per piece. However, if Supplier A uses thinner material, weak cartons, unstable colors, or longer production time, the real risk becomes much higher.

## How to Compare Yiwu Product Suppliers Step by Step

### Step 1: Define the Product
Write down the exact product details including size, material, color, function, packaging, target market, and expected quantity.

### Step 2: Compare MOQ, Price, and Payment Terms Together
A lower price with a very high MOQ may not be suitable for a test order.

### Step 3: Check Quality Level
Check material thickness, finishing, smell, color consistency, function, weight, printing, stitching, accessories, and packaging strength.

### Step 4: Compare Lead Time and Delivery Stability
A supplier who quotes a good price but delays delivery can damage your shipping plan.

## Yiwu Supplier Comparison Table

| Comparison Item | Supplier A | Supplier B | Why It Matters |
|----------------|------------|------------|----------------|
| Unit Price | Lowest | Medium | Important, but not the only factor |
| MOQ | High | Flexible | Affects cash flow and test-order risk |
| Sample Quality | Average | Good | Shows real product level |
| Packaging | Weak carton | Export carton | Can reduce damage during shipping |
| Lead Time | Unclear | 15 days | Affects consolidation schedule |
| Communication | Slow | Clear | Important when problems happen |

## Calculate Landed Cost Before Choosing

The real cost includes packaging volume, inland delivery, warehouse handling, inspection, customs documents, sea freight, destination charges, tax, and possible product loss.
