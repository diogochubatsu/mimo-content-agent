# How to Start Sourcing Suppliers in China Successfully

Sourcing suppliers in China is one of the most effective ways to reduce product costs, increase profit margins, and access a massive range of wholesale products.

## Best Methods for Sourcing Suppliers in China

### Use B2B Supplier Platforms
Many beginners start through B2B websites like Alibaba, Made-in-China, and Global Sources.

### Visit Chinese Wholesale Markets
Wholesale markets remain one of the best ways to find suppliers directly.

### Attend Chinese Trade Fairs
Trade fairs are excellent for serious importers.

### Work With a Sourcing Agent
Many importers use sourcing agents to simplify the process.

## How to Verify Suppliers

### Check Business Licenses
Always request a Chinese business license before placing orders.

### Request Product Samples
Samples help buyers evaluate product quality and supplier reliability.

### Inspect Supplier Communication
Reliable suppliers usually reply clearly and consistently.

### Use Third-Party Inspection Services
Before shipping products, buyers should arrange inspections.

## Best Cities for Sourcing Suppliers in China

### Yiwu
Yiwu is famous for small commodities and mixed product sourcing.

### Shenzhen
Shenzhen specializes in electronics, gadgets, and technology products.

### Guangzhou
Guangzhou is well known for fashion, bags, cosmetics, and apparel sourcing.

## How to Negotiate With Chinese Suppliers

- Request Multiple Quotations
- Clarify Specifications Clearly
- Build Long-Term Relationships
