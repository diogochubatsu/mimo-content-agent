# How to Spot Fake Factory Videos From China Suppliers

If you import products from China, learning how to spot fake factory videos has become more important than ever. Today, many suppliers use polished factory walkthrough videos to attract overseas buyers. However, some videos are heavily edited, borrowed from other factories, created with AI tools, or completely unrelated to the real supplier.

## Why Fake Factory Videos Are Increasing

Modern editing software and AI tools now make it easier to create convincing factory content. Consequently, some dishonest suppliers use fake videos to appear larger, more professional, or more experienced than they actually are.

## How to Spot Fake Factory Videos Using Simple Checks

### Watch for Overly Perfect Production Lines
Real factories are usually busy and slightly messy. Workers move naturally, products stack unevenly, and production areas contain normal operational activity.

### Check Whether Workers Are Actually Working
In genuine factories, workers focus on tasks naturally, machines produce real operational sounds, and products move through different production stages.

### Look for Suspicious Editing Cuts
Heavy editing can hide important details. Pay attention to excessive transitions, fast cuts, dramatic music, or repeated scenes.

## How AI Technology Is Changing Supplier Videos

### AI Voiceovers and Artificial Narration
Some suppliers now use AI-generated voiceovers instead of real staff introductions.

### Unrealistic Lighting and Motion
AI-generated footage may contain unnatural lighting, distorted hands, inconsistent shadows, or unusual machine movement.

### Fake Factory Drone Footage
Some suppliers use stock drone footage from unrelated industrial parks.

## How to Verify Whether a Factory Is Real

### Request a Live Video Call
During the call, ask the supplier to walk through the factory continuously, show warehouse inventory, and display production lines in real time.

### Check Business Licenses Carefully
Always ask suppliers for their Chinese business license and verify company name, business scope, registered capital, and factory address.

### Use Third-Party Factory Inspections
Third-party inspections remain one of the safest sourcing methods.
