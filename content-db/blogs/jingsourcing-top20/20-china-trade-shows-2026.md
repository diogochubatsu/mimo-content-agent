# Top 10 China Trade Shows 2026: Complete Guide for Buyers

China trade shows 2026 are the most effective way for global buyers to find verified suppliers, compare products face-to-face, and secure better pricing.

## Why China Trade Shows 2026 Are Still Essential

Although digital sourcing platforms are convenient, they often come with risks such as fake suppliers, inconsistent quality, and communication issues.

## Top 10 China Trade Shows 2026

### 1. East China Fair (March)
📍 Shanghai | 📅 March 1–4, 2026
Focuses on textiles, garments, and consumer goods.

### 2. China International Furniture Fair – CIFF (March)
📍 Guangzhou / Shanghai | 📅 March 2026
One of the world's largest furniture exhibitions.

### 3. Canton Fair (April–May)
📍 Guangzhou | 📅 April 15 – May 5, 2026
The most comprehensive event covering nearly every product category.

### 4. CHINAPLAS (April)
📍 Shanghai | 📅 April 21–24, 2026
Focuses on plastics and industrial materials.

### 5. China Beauty Expo – CBE (May)
📍 Shanghai | 📅 May 2026
One of Asia's leading beauty trade shows.

### 6. China International Hardware Show (September)
📍 Shanghai | 📅 September 2026
Specializes in tools and hardware products.

### 7. China International Industry Fair – CIIF (October)
📍 Shanghai | 📅 October 2026
Focuses on industrial automation and robotics.

### 8. Yiwu International Commodities Fair (October)
📍 Yiwu | 📅 October 2026
Most practical for small and medium buyers.

### 9. China International Import Expo – CIIE (November)
📍 Shanghai | 📅 November 5–10, 2026
Focuses on global brands entering China.

### 10. Global Sources Exhibitions (April & October)
📍 Hong Kong | 📅 April & October 2026
Popular among electronics buyers and Amazon sellers.

## Cost Breakdown

- Flights: $800–$1500
- Hotels: $80–$300 per night

## FAQ

### What is the biggest trade show in China?
The Canton Fair is the largest and most comprehensive event.

### Which trade show is best for small buyers?
Yiwu Fair is ideal due to low MOQ requirements.

### When is the best time to visit?
April and October are peak seasons.
