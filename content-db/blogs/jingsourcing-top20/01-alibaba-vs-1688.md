# Alibaba vs 1688: Which Platform Is Better for Overseas Buyers?

If you are sourcing from China, you've probably asked: alibaba vs 1688 — which one is better? The short answer is simple. Alibaba is built for international buyers, while 1688 is designed for China's domestic market. However, the real difference is pricing, access, and difficulty. Choosing the wrong platform can cost you thousands or waste months.

## Quick Comparison

| Factor | Alibaba | 1688 |
|--------|---------|------|
| Target Market | International buyers | China domestic buyers |
| Language | English | Chinese only |
| Price | Higher | Much cheaper |
| MOQ | Lower | Higher |
| Shipping | International | China only |

## What Is Alibaba?

Alibaba is a global B2B marketplace operated by Alibaba Group. It is designed for overseas buyers who want to import from China. Because it targets international users, it offers English support, global payments, and shipping solutions. As a result, it is beginner-friendly. However, prices are higher because suppliers include export service costs.

## What Is 1688?

1688 (1688.com) is China's largest domestic wholesale marketplace. It is also owned by Alibaba Group, but it focuses on the domestic Chinese wholesale market. Because it serves local buyers, prices are much lower. In many cases, products are 20%–50% cheaper. However, it is harder to use because everything is in Chinese and suppliers rarely handle exports.

## Price Comparison: Alibaba vs 1688

Price is the biggest factor in alibaba vs 1688. For example, a product priced at $3 on Alibaba may cost only $1.8 on 1688. Therefore, large orders create big savings. However, you must include logistics and service costs. Even so, 1688 is usually cheaper overall.

## Problems Overseas Buyers Face on 1688

1688 offers better pricing, but it is not easy to use. First, the platform is in Chinese. Second, suppliers may not speak English. Third, payments are limited. Finally, shipping is domestic only. Therefore, many buyers cannot use 1688 directly.

## How to Buy from 1688 as an Overseas Buyer

Most overseas buyers cannot purchase directly from 1688 without support. This is because the platform is designed for the Chinese domestic market. As a result, suppliers usually do not offer English communication, international payment methods, or international shipping. Therefore, many buyers work with a sourcing agent in China.

In many cases, 1688 suppliers are not actual manufacturers. Instead, they source products from wholesale markets such as Yiwu and then resell them online. Because of this, the price you see on 1688 may already include a markup.

## Best Strategy: Use Alibaba and 1688 Together

The smartest approach is to use both platforms. Start with Alibaba to test products. Then switch to 1688 for better pricing. This reduces risk and increases profit.

## FAQ

### Which is better, Alibaba or 1688?
Alibaba is easier. 1688 is cheaper. The best choice depends on your needs.

### Is 1688 cheaper than Alibaba?
Yes. 1688 is usually much cheaper because it targets domestic buyers.

### Can foreigners use 1688?
Yes, but it is difficult without local support.

### Is Alibaba safer?
Yes. Alibaba offers better protection for international buyers.

## Final Verdict

Alibaba is easy and beginner-friendly. 1688 is cheaper but complex. Therefore, the best strategy is to use both based on your business stage. If you want to reduce cost and risk, working with a sourcing partner is the best option.
