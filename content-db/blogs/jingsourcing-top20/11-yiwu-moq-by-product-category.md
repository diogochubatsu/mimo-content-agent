# Yiwu MOQ by Product Category: What Minimum Order Quantities Should Buyers Expect?

Yiwu MOQ by Product Category is one of the first things importers should understand before buying from Yiwu Market. For many regular wholesale items, Yiwu MOQ can be as low as one carton, while customized products may require hundreds or thousands of pieces.

## Common Yiwu MOQ by Product Category

| Product Category | Typical MOQ | Notes |
|-----------------|-------------|-------|
| Small toys | 5 carton per item | Often flexible for stock items |
| Plush toys | 5–10 cartons per style | Custom design needs higher MOQ |
| Jewelry | 12–120 pieces per style | Small items can be mixed easily |
| Hair accessories | 5 carton or 500+ pieces | Depends on packing method |
| Bags | 300–500 pieces | Logo orders need higher MOQ |
| Apparel | 200–400 pieces per style | Size and color affect MOQ |
| Socks | 500–1,000 pairs | Custom patterns need higher MOQ |
| Home goods | 10 carton per item | Usually good for mixed orders |
| Kitchenware | 10 cartons per item | Heavy products may require carton MOQ |
| Electronics accessories | 100–500 pieces | Quality level affects price and MOQ |
| Packaging products | 1,000–5,000 pieces | Printing orders need higher MOQ |
| Festival goods | 10 carton per style | Seasonal stock changes quickly |

## How to Negotiate MOQ with Yiwu Suppliers

MOQ is often negotiable, especially for ready stock products. First, explain that you want to test the market. Then, ask whether the supplier can accept a trial order.

### Useful MOQ Questions to Ask Suppliers

- What is the MOQ for this item?
- Is the MOQ based on one style, one color, or one carton?
- How many pieces are in one carton?
- Can I mix colors or designs?
- What is the MOQ for logo printing?
- Do you have ready stock?
