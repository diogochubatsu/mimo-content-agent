# What a Useful Yiwu Product Inspection Report Should Include

A useful Yiwu inspection report should identify the inspected goods, explain the sampling method, record quantity and product checks, classify every defect, and provide clear photographs or measurements. It should also state what was not checked.

## Start With Inspection Identity and Scope

The first page should make the assignment traceable. It should record the buyer, supplier, factory or warehouse address, purchase-order number, product names, SKU codes, inspection date, inspector and report number.

## Show Order Status and Available Quantity

A Yiwu inspection report should compare ordered, finished, packed, presented and inspected quantities.

| Quantity Field | What It Tells the Buyer |
|----------------|------------------------|
| Ordered quantity | The contractual quantity by SKU or variation |
| Finished quantity | Units reported complete at inspection time |
| Packed quantity | Units already in retail or export packaging |
| Presented quantity | Goods actually made available to the inspector |
| Sample size | Units randomly selected and checked |

## Explain the Sampling and Acceptance Rules

The report should name the sampling standard or buyer-approved method, lot size, inspection level, sample size and acceptance limits for critical, major and minor defects.

## Include Product, Function and Workmanship Results

### Product Identity and Measurements
Confirm model, material, colour, dimensions, weight, components and accessories.

### Function and Assembly
Describe each functional test, sample size and outcome.

### Workmanship and Appearance
Record scratches, stains, loose parts, poor seams, sharp edges, deformation, colour differences and other relevant faults.

## Document Every Defect With Usable Evidence

For each defect type, the report should provide a description, classification, affected sample number, count and photographs showing both detail and product context.

## Verify Packaging, Labels and Shipping Marks

A Yiwu inspection report should cover retail packaging, inner packs, master cartons, carton dimensions, gross weight, closures, protection and shipping marks.

## How to Review a Yiwu Inspection Report

1. Confirm the supplier, location, order number, SKUs and inspection date
2. Check whether enough goods were complete and available for random sampling
3. Compare the sample size and defect limits with the approved plan
4. Review critical findings before looking at the overall result
5. Match defect totals to photographs, sample numbers and classifications
6. Verify functions, measurements, packaging, labels and carton results
7. Identify restrictions, missing evidence and tests not performed
8. Decide whether to pass, hold, correct, reinspect or reject the lot
