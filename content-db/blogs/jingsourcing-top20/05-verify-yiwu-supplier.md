# How to Verify Yiwu Supplier Before Sending Money

Many importers lose money because they trust suppliers too quickly. Therefore, if you plan to buy products from China, you must first learn how to verify Yiwu supplier credibility before placing an order.

## Why It Is Important to Verify Yiwu Supplier

When you verify Yiwu supplier properly, you can:
- Avoid scams and fake suppliers
- Reduce product quality risks
- Confirm factory legitimacy
- Check export experience
- Prevent shipment delays
- Protect your payment security

## How to Verify Yiwu Supplier Step by Step

### Check the Chinese Business License
A legitimate supplier should provide Chinese business license copy, company registration number, legal representative name, business address, and business scope.

### Verify Company Name and Bank Account
Before sending payment, always confirm the bank account matches the registered company name.

### Request Factory Photos and Videos
Ask suppliers to provide production line videos, warehouse photos, staff working videos, machine photos, and packaging process videos.

### Conduct a Video Call Inspection
During the video call, ask them to walk through the office, request warehouse demonstrations, check production areas, and confirm sample products.

### Order Samples Before Mass Production
Never place large orders without testing samples first.

## Warning Signs You Should Never Ignore

### Prices Too Cheap
If one supplier offers prices far below market level, there is usually a reason.

### Refusing Video Calls
If a supplier constantly refuses live video calls, you should investigate further.

### Pressure for Immediate Payment
Scammers often push buyers to transfer money urgently.

## Should You Use a Yiwu Sourcing Agent?

Many overseas buyers choose to work with professional sourcing agents because supplier verification in China can be difficult remotely.
