# China Sourcing Agent vs Yiwu Agent: Which One Is Better in 2026?

If you are planning to import products from China, you may be asking: china sourcing agent vs Yiwu agent — which one is better? The answer depends on your order size, sourcing goals, and business model.

## What Is a China Sourcing Agent?

A China sourcing agent helps international buyers source products directly from factories across China. They focus on large-scale production with higher minimum order quantities.

## What Is a Yiwu Agent?

A Yiwu agent operates inside Yiwu wholesale market, the largest small commodity market in the world. Instead of factory production, Yiwu sourcing focuses on ready-made products with low MOQ.

## Key Differences

### 1. MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)
China sourcing agents usually deal with factories requiring large orders. Yiwu agents allow small quantity purchases.

### 2. Product Variety
China sourcing agents work with selected factories. Yiwu market offers massive variety across multiple categories.

### 3. Price Structure
Factory prices may seem cheaper for large orders. For small and medium buyers, Yiwu prices are competitive.

### 4. Flexibility and Speed
Factory production takes time. Yiwu suppliers often have ready stock.

### 5. Buyer Type Suitability
- China sourcing agent: large companies, OEM production
- Yiwu agent: Amazon sellers, wholesalers, startups

## When Should You Choose a China Sourcing Agent?

Choose a China sourcing agent if you need custom products, private labeling, or mass production.

## When Should You Choose a Yiwu Agent?

Choose a Yiwu agent if you want low MOQ, multiple products, and faster sourcing.
