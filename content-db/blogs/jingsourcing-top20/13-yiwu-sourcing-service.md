# Yiwu Sourcing Service in 2026: Step-by-Step Process, Pricing & Common Mistakes

Yiwu sourcing sounds simple until buyers discover how many problems can happen between supplier selection and container delivery. This guide explains how a structured Yiwu sourcing service works in 2026.

## Step 1: Product Brief and Inquiry

The first step in any Yiwu sourcing project is the product brief. A strong brief should include product photos, dimensions, materials, packaging requirements, MOQ targets, destination country, and any certification requirements.

## Step 2: Booth Shortlist and Supplier Vetting

Supplier verification is one of the most important parts of Yiwu sourcing. Verification should include:
- Business license verification
- Factory or showroom address confirmation
- Bank account verification
- MOQ capability confirmation
- Packaging and compliance review

## Step 3: Quote Collection and Comparison

A structured Yiwu sourcing workflow normally collects multiple supplier quotations for each SKU. We recommend comparing at least three quotations whenever possible.

## Step 4: Sample Approval

Once quotations are shortlisted, the buyer usually approves supplier samples before production begins.

## Step 5: Order Placement and Deposit

Standard practice usually includes a deposit before production and balance payment before shipment.

## Step 6: Production Monitoring

During production, the sourcing partner monitors manufacturing progress and identifies delays.

## Step 7: Pre-Shipment Inspection

Pre-shipment inspection is one of the highest-value protection steps in any Yiwu sourcing workflow.

## Step 8: Consolidation, Shipping, and Export Documents

One major advantage of Yiwu sourcing is the ability to combine multiple suppliers into one shipment.

## Yiwu Sourcing Pricing Models

### Commission Model
The sourcing company charges a percentage of the shipment value.

### Flat Service Fee
A fixed project fee is charged regardless of shipment value.

### Hybrid Model
A smaller service fee plus reduced commission percentage.

## Common Mistakes Buyers Should Avoid

- Relying on only one supplier quotation
- Skipping inspection before shipment
- Paying deposits to personal accounts
- Assuming all suppliers are factories
- Ignoring packaging requirements early in sourcing
