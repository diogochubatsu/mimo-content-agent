# Critical, Major, and Minor Defects in Yiwu Product Inspections

Yiwu inspection defect classification separates faults into critical, major and minor categories according to risk and customer impact.

## Why Defect Classification Matters Before Shipment

An inspection report should support a commercial decision, not merely list observations. Classification helps the inspector count comparable defects, apply acceptance limits and explain why a lot should pass, remain on hold or fail.

## Critical Defects

A critical defect presents an unreasonable safety, regulatory or essential-function risk. Buyers commonly set an acceptance limit of zero.

Examples:
- Exposed electrical conductors or accessible sharp edges
- A safety guard, warning or required component is missing
- Contamination, mould or a prohibited material is found
- Incorrect compliance, age or hazard labeling creates legal risk

## Major Defects

A major defect is likely to make a customer reject the product, return it or experience a significant loss of function.

Examples:
- A switch that fails
- Incorrect dimensions that prevent assembly
- Severe colour variation
- Missing accessories
- Broken retail packaging

## Minor Defects

A minor defect is a departure from the approved standard that does not substantially reduce normal use or saleability.

Examples:
- A small mark in a hidden area
- A slight non-critical colour variation
- A neatness issue that most customers would tolerate

## Defect Classification Examples

| Finding | Possible Class | Reason |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| Accessible sharp metal edge on a children's item | Critical | Potential injury and compliance risk |
| Electronic product does not power on | Major | Primary function fails |
| Large scratch on the visible front surface | Major | Likely customer rejection |
| Small mark on a concealed underside | Minor | Limited cosmetic effect |

## Make Pass, Hold or Fail Decisions

1. **Pass:** Sample results remain within every agreed limit
2. **Hold:** Evidence is incomplete or defect needs technical review
3. **Fail:** One or more limits are exceeded or critical defect confirmed
