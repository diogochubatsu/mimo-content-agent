# 1688 Product Sourcing in 2026: Step-by-Step Guide for Overseas Importers

1688 product sourcing has become one of the most popular ways for overseas buyers to purchase low-cost wholesale products directly from China. Unlike Alibaba, 1688 mainly serves the Chinese domestic market, which means prices are often lower and supplier options are wider.

## What Is 1688 Product Sourcing?

1688 product sourcing refers to the process of purchasing wholesale products from suppliers on 1688.com, which is one of China's largest domestic B2B marketplaces owned by Alibaba Group.

## Why Overseas Buyers Use 1688 Product Sourcing

- Price competitiveness - products often 20-50% cheaper than Alibaba
- Larger supplier database
- More factory-direct products
- Better pricing for experienced buyers

## Common Problems with 1688 Product Sourcing

### Chinese Language Barrier
The platform is mainly written in Chinese.

### RMB Payments
Most suppliers prefer domestic Chinese payment methods.

### Supplier Verification Risks
Not every supplier is a real factory.

### Shipping and Consolidation Problems
Many suppliers only handle domestic Chinese shipping.

## Step-by-Step 1688 Product Sourcing Process

1. **Search Products on 1688** - Use Chinese keywords or sourcing agents
2. **Shortlist Suppliers** - Compare ratings, transaction history, certifications
3. **Request Samples** - Always test before bulk orders
4. **Negotiate MOQ and Pricing** - Some support low MOQ trial orders
5. **Consolidate Products** - Combine into one warehouse before export
6. **Product Inspection** - Verify quality before shipment
7. **Arrange International Shipping** - Sea freight most common for bulk

## 1688 Product Sourcing vs Alibaba

| Feature | 1688 | Alibaba |
|---------|------|---------|
| Price | Lower domestic pricing | Higher export pricing |
| Language | Chinese only | English platform |
| Payment | RMB only | International payments |
| Shipping | Domestic only | International |
