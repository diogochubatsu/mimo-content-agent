# What You NEED To Know Before Dropshipping

**Channel:** Wholesale Ted  
**Views:** 912K  
**Published:** September 2019

## Key Takeaways

- Dropshipping is a legitimate business model but requires realistic expectations
- Understanding profit margins and pricing strategies is crucial
- Customer service and order fulfillment quality matter significantly
- Marketing and traffic generation are essential skills to develop
- The business requires consistent effort and learning

## Topics Covered

1. Common dropshipping misconceptions
2. Realistic income expectations
3. Product research strategies
4. Supplier relationship management
5. Marketing fundamentals for dropshipping stores
