# AliExpress vs. Alibaba: How Do They Compare?

## Key differences

### AliExpress
- B2C marketplace
- Individual item orders
- No minimum order quantity
- Dropshipping-friendly
- Higher per-unit prices

### Alibaba
- B2B marketplace
- Bulk/wholesale orders
- Minimum order quantities
- Customization options
- Lower per-unit prices

## When to use AliExpress

- Testing products
- Dropshipping
- Small order quantities
- No customization needed

## When to use Alibaba

- Established products
- Custom branding/private label
- High volume orders
- Better margins needed

## Can you dropship from Alibaba?

Some suppliers offer dropshipping, but it's not the primary focus. Best for private label businesses.
