# 15 Best Automated Dropshipping Tools To Know in 2026

## Why automate dropshipping?

Automation helps you scale your business by reducing manual tasks like order processing, inventory updates, and product imports.

## Top automated dropshipping tools

### Supplier apps
1. Shopify Collective - Free for eligible merchants
2. DSers - AliExpress automation
3. DropCommerce - US/Canadian suppliers
4. Syncee - Global supplier network
5. AI Dropship - US/EU suppliers

### Order fulfillment
6. AutoDS - Full automation platform
7. CJDropshipping - China-based fulfillment
8. Zendrop - US warehouses

### Product research
9. Sell The Trend - Trending product finder
10. Niche Scraper - Product research tool

### Inventory management
11. Inventory Source - Multi-supplier sync
12. Ordoro - Inventory & order management

### Marketing automation
13. Klaviyo - Email marketing
14. Omnisend - Multi-channel marketing
15. AdRoll - Retargeting ads
