# How Does Alibaba Work? Buying and Safety Guide

## What is Alibaba?

Alibaba is the world's largest B2B marketplace connecting buyers with manufacturers and suppliers, primarily in China.

## How to use Alibaba safely

### Vetting suppliers
- Check Trade Assurance badge
- Review supplier history
- Request samples
- Verify business licenses

### Communication tips
- Ask detailed questions
- Request photos/videos
- Negotiate terms
- Get agreements in writing

### Payment safety
- Use Trade Assurance
- Avoid wire transfers to unknown suppliers
- Start with small orders
- Use escrow when possible

## Alibaba for dropshipping

Alibaba is not ideal for traditional dropshipping but works well for:
- Private label products
- Custom manufacturing
- Bulk purchasing for inventory
