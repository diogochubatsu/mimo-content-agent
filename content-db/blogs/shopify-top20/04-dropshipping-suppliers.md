## How to choose the right supplier

Red flags to avoid:
- Supplier subscription fees beyond normal
- "Wholesale prices" offered to consumers
- Minimum order sizes that conflict with dropshipping

## How to find a dropshipping supplier

Methods:
- Contact the manufacturer
- Search using Google
- Order from the competition
- Attend a trade show
