# 4 Real Dropshipping Success Stories + Beginner Tips

## What's the average dropshipping success rate?

The general consensus is that most dropshipping businesses fail. But it can often be hard to distinguish between businesses that have no prospects of success, and those that simply require more time and investment.

## 4 dropshipping success stories

### 1. Tze: $19,000 profit in 2 months
- Founded Subtle Asian Treats
- Sells bubble tea plush toys
- Success factors: trending niche, professional store, customer interactions

### 2. Cole: $2 million in just over a year
- Built ecommerce store making $2M in sales between March 2019 and May 2020
- Success factors: scaled Facebook ads, long-term brand, good customer service

### 3. Andreas and Alexander: $10 million per year
- Pet industry niche
- Made $500,000 per month in 2019
- Success factors: emotional connection, Facebook ads, invested time and energy

### 4. Sarah and Audrey: $1 million sales with influencer marketing
- Used Instagram influencers
- Over $1M in sales in just a few weeks
- Success factors: audience appeal, influencer power, Instagram Stories

## How to be a successful dropshipper

1. Add value through insights, information, and solutions
2. Focus on marketing and SEO (dedicate 75% of time for first 4-6 months)
3. Specialize in certain products
4. Have a long-term perspective (at least 1 year)
5. Offer outstanding customer service
6. Don't get hung up on details
