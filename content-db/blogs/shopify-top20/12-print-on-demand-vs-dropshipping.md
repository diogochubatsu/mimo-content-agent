# Print on Demand vs Dropshipping: Which Is Better?

## What is dropshipping?

Dropshipping is a retail fulfillment method that allows you to launch an ecommerce brand without having to manufacture, buy, store, or ship inventory yourself.

## What is print on demand?

Print on demand (POD) is a type of dropshipping business model that allows you to sell products without purchasing or managing inventory, but with a custom-designed element.

## Similarities

- No physical store or warehouse
- Limited inventory management
- Supplier reliability
- Low startup costs

## Differences

### Customization
- POD: Unique custom designs
- Dropshipping: No customization required

### Product selection
- Dropshipping: Wide variety of ready-to-ship products
- POD: Ready-to-customize products

### Competition
- Dropshipping: Multiple stores may have same products
- POD: Unique inventory with custom designs

### Branding
- POD: Branding is front and center
- Dropshipping: Fewer branding opportunities

### Return support
- Dropshipping: Standard return policies
- POD: Tricky returns due to custom nature

## Which is better?

- Print on demand: Better for custom products and brand-focused businesses
- Dropshipping: Better for sales-driven, marketing-focused retailers
