# Evergreen Products: How to Source and Dropship

## What are evergreen products?

Evergreen products have consistent demand year-round, not dependent on seasons or trends.

## Benefits of evergreen products

- Predictable revenue
- Less marketing pressure
- Stable inventory planning
- Lower competition in some niches

## Top evergreen product categories

1. Health and wellness
2. Home essentials
3. Personal care
4. Pet supplies
5. Office supplies
6. Kitchen essentials
7. Cleaning products
8. Basic clothing items

## How to source evergreen products

1. Research consistent demand using Google Trends
2. Find reliable suppliers
3. Test products before scaling
4. Build customer loyalty programs
