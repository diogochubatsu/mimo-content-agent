# Google Trends Products: 10 Trending Products To Sell in 2026

## What is Google Trends?

Google Trends is a free tool that tracks what people are searching for, how interest changes over time, and where searches happen. It indexes search volume on a scale of 0-100.

## How Google Trends helps retailers

- Discover product search volume
- Check seasonal demand
- Nail down customer vocabulary

## 10 trending products on Google Trends

### 1. Yoga mats
- Consistent year-round demand with seasonal spikes
- Part of $500B+ US wellness sector

### 2. Wrap skirts
- Spring fashion trend
- Breakout spring skirt search of 2025

### 3. Soap
- 23 million bars sold by Shopify merchants in 2024
- Evergreen category

### 4. Travel pants
- At all-time high interest
- Post-pandemic travel boom

### 5. Reusable water bottles
- $9.72B global market in 2024
- TikTok's #WaterTok has 2.5B views

### 6. Home decor
- Average household spends $2,752/year
- Instagram #homedecor has 174M posts

### 7. Pet supplies
- Consistently high year-round interest
- Pets-as-family trend growing

### 8. Blushes
- $2B global revenue (Rare Beauty alone)
- "Makeup's comeback kid"

### 9. Protein powder
- $114B global category
- 61% of consumers increased protein intake

### 10. Lego set
- Sharp holiday spikes
- Adult toy sales up 12% YoY
