# Import VAT in Germany Explained

## What is Import VAT?

Import VAT (Einfuhrumsatzsteuer or EUSt) is charged on all goods imported into Germany from non-EU countries, regardless of their value.

**Key fact:** It is calculated on the customs value PLUS customs duty — not on the goods alone.

## VAT Rates

| Category | Rate |
|----------|------|
| Standard rate | 19% |
| Reduced rate | 7% |

**Reduced rate (7%) applies to:**
- Books and printed publications
- Certain food products
- Cultural goods and artworks

## Can Import VAT be Reclaimed?

**For VAT-registered businesses:**
- Import VAT can typically be reclaimed as input tax on the German VAT return
- Makes import VAT primarily a cash-flow consideration

**For non-VAT-registered businesses:**
- Cannot reclaim import VAT
- Becomes a permanent cost

## IOSS (Import One Stop Shop)

For B2C eCommerce shipments up to €150:
- Seller collects and remits VAT at point of sale
- Goods receive exemption from Import VAT at border
- Requires IOSS number on customs declaration

## Cash Flow Optimization

- VAT deferral possible to 26th of the following month
- Register for German VAT to reclaim import VAT
- Use customs deferment account for duty payment

## Factors Affecting Import VAT

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Customs value (CIF) | Basis for VAT calculation |
| Tariff code | Determines duty rate |
| Country of origin | May affect preferential rates |
| VAT registration status | Determines reclaim eligibility |
| Trade agreements | May reduce or eliminate duty |
