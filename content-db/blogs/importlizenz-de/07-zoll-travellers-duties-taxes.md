# Duties and Taxes for Travellers Entering Germany

## Overview

Official guidance from German Customs (Zoll) for private individuals entering Germany from non-EU states.

## Key Topics

### Travellers' Allowances
If you are entering Germany from a non-EU state, the goods you are carrying and which do not exceed certain quantity and value limits are tax/duty-free under certain conditions.

### Exceeding Travellers' Allowances
If you enter Germany as a traveller from a non-EU state and the items you are bringing exceed the quantity and value limits, you must pay import duties.

### Restricted Travellers' Allowances
Special quantity and value limits apply for residents of border municipalities, cross-border workers, truck drivers, and tour guides.

### Tax-Free Shopping
If you are a traveller from a non-EU country, you can make purchases in Germany without paying turnover tax (VAT).

### Export of Motor Vehicles
Requirements for exporting motor vehicles purchased in the EU to non-EU countries for private use.

### Temporary Importation of Motor Vehicles
If you are normally resident outside the EU, you may import your non-EU registered vehicle under relief and use it in Germany.

### Baggage
If you are normally resident outside the EU, you may import your ordinary personal effects and sports equipment to Germany under relief without further formality.
