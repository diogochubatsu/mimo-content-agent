# Businesses - Import (Zoll Online)

## Overview

Because you must have all the goods that you import from a non-EU country cleared by customs, it is important to clarify a few points beforehand.

## Key Topics

### Restrictions
Cross-border movements of goods and services, the same as capital movements and payments, are generally free from restrictions. Nevertheless, the import of certain goods may be prohibited or restricted for a number of reasons.

### Duties and Taxes
Certain formalities are required if you intend to ship goods from a non-EU country into Germany. Moreover, excisable goods are subject to particular requirements.

### Procedures
Generally, all goods must be entered for a customs procedure. There are various ways to do that.

## About German Customs (Zoll)

The Zollverwaltung (Customs Administration) is the central authority for customs matters in Germany. They enforce EU customs legislation and national regulations at all major logistics hubs.
