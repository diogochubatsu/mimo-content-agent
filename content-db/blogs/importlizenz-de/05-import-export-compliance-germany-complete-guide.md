# Import & Export Compliance in Germany: Complete Guide

## Why Compliance Matters

Germany operates under EU customs law (Union Customs Code - UCC). Non-compliance can result in:
- Administrative fines
- Customs seizure of goods
- Retroactive duties and VAT demands
- Criminal prosecution in severe cases
- Suspension of import/export privileges

## Legal Framework

### EU-Level Regulations
- Union Customs Code (UCC)
- EU VAT Directive
- EU Dual-Use Regulation
- EU Sanctions Regulations

### German National Laws
- Foreign Trade and Payments Act (AWG)
- Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (AWV)
- German VAT Act (UStG)

## EORI Registration

**EORI (Economic Operators Registration and Identification)** number is mandatory for any business involved in customs activities within the EU.

**Without EORI you cannot:**
- File customs declarations
- Import/export goods
- Act as importer/exporter of record

**Registration Process:**
1. Submit application via German customs portal
2. Provide company registration documents
3. Provide VAT number (if applicable)
4. Processing time: 3–10 working days

## Customs Procedures

### Customs Declaration
- All goods from outside EU must be declared electronically via ATLAS
- Required: HS code, customs value, country of origin, Incoterms, importer details

### Import VAT
- Standard rate: 19%
- Reduced rate: 7%
- Calculated on: customs value + duties + transport/insurance costs
- Reclaimable through VAT returns

## Export Compliance

### Export Declaration Required When:
- Goods value exceeds €1,000 OR
- Weight exceeds 1,000 kg

### Export Control
- Military goods, dual-use goods, sanctioned destinations
- BAFA licenses required for controlled goods

## Required Documentation

- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
- Certificate of Origin
- Import/Export License (if required)
- CE Conformity Declaration
- Insurance Certificate

## AEO Status

Authorised Economic Operator status provides:
- Faster customs clearance
- Reduced inspections
- Priority treatment
- Enhanced credibility
