# Germany - Import Requirements and Documentation

## Overview

Germany Country Commercial Guide from the US International Trade Administration (ITA). Learn about market conditions, opportunities, regulations, and business conditions in Germany.

## Key Areas Covered

### Trade Barriers
Overview of trade barriers affecting imports into Germany.

### Import Tariffs
Detailed information on German import tariff structure and rates.

### Import Requirements and Documentation
Complete documentation requirements for importing goods into Germany.

### Labeling and Marking Requirements
German and EU labeling requirements for imported products.

### Prohibited and Restricted Imports
List of goods prohibited or restricted from import into Germany.

### Customs Regulations
Detailed customs procedures and regulatory framework.

### Standards for Trade
German and EU product standards applicable to imports.

### Trade Agreements
EU trade agreements affecting duty rates for German imports.
