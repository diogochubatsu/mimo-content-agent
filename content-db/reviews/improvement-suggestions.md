2. **Meta Descriptions**
   - 150-160 characters

   - Add call-to-action
   - Mention key benefit

3. **Header Structure**
   - H1: Primary keyword
   - H2: Secondary keywords
   - H3: Long-tail variations
   - Use question headers for FAQ schema

4. **Internal Linking**
   - 3-5 internal links per article

   - Create topic clusters
