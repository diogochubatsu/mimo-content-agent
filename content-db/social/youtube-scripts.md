**Check #1: Verify Business License** (1:30 - 2:30)

- Look for: 统一社会信用代码 (Unified Social Credit Code)
- Verify on: gsxt.gov.cn (National Enterprise Credit System)
- Red flag: No license or fake license number

**Check #2: Trade History Analysis** (2:30 - 3:30)
- Don't just look at total orders
- Look at RECENT orders (last 6 months)
- Check: Are orders consistent or spiked?
- Red flag: Sudden spike in orders (could be fake reviews)

**Check #3: Sample Order Test** (3:30 - 5:00)
- ALWAYS order samples first (even if it costs $50-100)
- Order from 3 different suppliers
- Test quality, packaging, shipping time
- Compare samples to bulk order photos
- Red flag: Supplier refuses samples or rushes you

**Check #4: Communication Test** (5:00 - 6:30)
- Test response time (within 24 hours = good)

**Check #5: Payment Safety** (6:30 - 7:30)

## Script 4: Import Taxes Explained

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:30)
"You just made your first $10,000 from importing... and the government wants $3,000! Import taxes can DESTROY your profit margins if you don't understand them. But here's the good news [pause] - there are LEGAL ways to reduce your tax bill by 50% or more! I'll show you exactly how!"

### MAIN CONTENT (0:30 - 9:00)

**Why Import Taxes Matter** (0:30 - 1:30)
- Most beginners forget about taxes
- Can reduce profit margins from 70% to 20%
- Different countries = different rules
- You MUST understand this before importing

**Country-by-Country Breakdown** (1:30 - 4:00)

**🇺🇸 United States:**
- Import Duty: 0-25% (depends on product)
- Merchandise Processing Fee: 0.3464%
- Harbor Maintenance Fee: 0.125%
- De minimis: $800 (below = no duty)
- Example: $10 phone case = $0 duty if under $800 total

**🇪🇺 European Union:**
- Import Duty: 0-17%
- VAT: 17-27% (varies by country)
- No de minimis for VAT
- Example: €10 product = €2.50 duty + €4.25 VAT

**🇧🇷 Brazil:**
- Import Duty: 14-35%
- ICMS: 17-25%
- PIS/COFINS: 9.25%
- TOTAL: Often 60-100% of product value!
- Example: $10 product = $16-20 in taxes

**🇯🇵 Japan:**
- Import Duty: 0-30%
- Consumption Tax: 10%
- De minimis: ¥10,000
- Example: ¥1,000 product = ¥100 duty + ¥100 tax

**How to Legally Reduce Taxes** (4:00 - 6:00)

**Strategy #1: De Minimis Thresholds**
- US: Keep orders under $800
- Split large orders into multiple shipments
- Example: $1,000 order = 2 shipments of $500 = $0 duty

**Strategy #2: Product Classification**
- Same product, different classification = different duty
- Example: "LED lights" (0%) vs "decorative lighting" (3.5%)
- Research HS codes thoroughly

**Strategy #3: Free Trade Zones**
- Import to bonded warehouse first
- Only pay taxes when goods leave for domestic sale
- Useful for testing market before committing

**Strategy #4: Import as Samples**
- Samples under $800 = duty free (US)
- Use for product validation
- Then bulk import once validated

**Strategy #5: Choose the Right Country**
- Some countries have lower duties
- Example: Import to US (0-25%) vs Brazil (60-100%)
- Consider where you'll sell

**Real Cost Calculation** (6:00 - 7:30)
"Let's say you're importing $10,000 of phone cases to the US:

Product cost: $8,000 (on 1688)
Shipping: $500

**Common Mistakes** (7:30 - 9:00)
1. Not declaring value honestly (illegal!)

3. Not factoring taxes into pricing
4. Using wrong HS codes
