## Pin Design Tips

### Visual Guidelines
- **Format:** 1000x1500px (2:3 ratio) - Pinterest optimal
- **Text Overlay:** Bold, readable, max 6-8 words
- **Colors:** Use brand palette (blue/orange for import theme)
- **Fonts:** Sans-serif, high contrast
- **Branding:** Logo watermark bottom-right corner

### Content Elements
- Price comparisons (before/after)
- Step-by-step infographics
- Product photos with pricing overlay
- Data visualizations (margins, costs)
- Before/after import scenarios

### Engagement Boosters
- Use numbers in titles ($0.30, 70%, 10 products)
- Create urgency (trending, 2026, limited)
- Ask questions in descriptions
- Include clear CTAs ("Read full guide")
