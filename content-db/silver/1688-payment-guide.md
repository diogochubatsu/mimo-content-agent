## Executive Summary

1688.com is Alibaba Group's domestic B2B marketplace, serving as the primary wholesale sourcing platform for businesses within China. Unlike its international counterpart Alibaba.com, 1688.com is designed exclusively for Chinese-speaking users and accepts only Chinese payment methods, creating a significant barrier for international buyers seeking competitive pricing and factory-direct products.

The payment landscape on 1688.com is fundamentally different from Western e-commerce platforms. The platform relies almost exclusively on Alipay, Alibaba's proprietary payment system, which serves as an escrow service protecting both buyers and sellers. International buyers must navigate currency conversion requirements, verification challenges, and platform restrictions that do not exist on Alibaba.com or AliExpress.

For international buyers, there are several viable approaches to completing payments on 1688.com. Direct Alipay registration is possible but requires a Chinese phone number or passport verification. Sourcing agents provide a turnkey solution by handling all payments on behalf of buyers, charging service fees ranging from 3-8% of order value. Third-party escrow services and specialized payment platforms also cater to international buyers, offering varying levels of convenience and cost.

Understanding the payment ecosystem is critical for successful sourcing on 1688.com. The platform processes over $50 billion in annual transactions, and payment method selection directly impacts buyer protection, transaction fees, and delivery timelines. This guide provides a comprehensive breakdown of all available payment options, their advantages and limitations, and step-by-step instructions for each method.

## Section 1: Understanding 1688.com Payment Landscape

1688.com operates within a payment ecosystem designed for domestic Chinese commerce. The platform integrates directly with Alipay, which functions as both a payment processor and buyer protection service. Unlike international platforms that accept credit cards or PayPal, 1688.com requires Chinese-issued payment instruments or third-party intermediaries.

### Subsection 1.1 Why 1688.com is Popular with International Buyers

1688.com offers prices typically 30-50% lower than Alibaba.com for identical products. This price advantage stems from the platform's domestic focus, reduced marketing overhead, and direct factory-to-business transactions. For international buyers willing to overcome payment barriers, the cost savings can be substantial.

The platform hosts over 100 million product listings across more than 40 industry categories, with new factories and manufacturers joining daily. Products available on 1688.com often appear on AliExpress and Alibaba.com at significantly marked-up prices, making 1688.com the preferred source for experienced importers.

### Subsection 1.2 Payment Challenges for International Buyers

The primary challenge is that 1688.com does not accept international credit cards, PayPal, or foreign bank accounts. Alipay, the platform's mandatory payment system, requires either a Chinese bank account or verified international identity. Additionally, all prices are displayed in Chinese Yuan (CNY), requiring currency conversion.

Language barriers compound payment difficulties. The entire payment interface is in Simplified Chinese, and customer support is available only in Chinese. Transaction disputes must be handled through the same Chinese-language system, creating additional complexity for non-Chinese speakers.

## Section 2: Alipay - The Primary Payment Method

Alipay (支付宝) is Alibaba's payment platform and the only direct payment method accepted on 1688.com. With over 1.3 billion active users, Alipay processes the majority of transactions on the platform. Understanding Alipay's requirements and capabilities is essential for any 1688.com buyer.

### Subsection 2.1 Alipay Registration Requirements

Registering an Alipay account requires either a Chinese mobile phone number or a verified international passport. Chinese mobile registration is straightforward but requires a China-based SIM card. International passport registration allows global users to create accounts but imposes certain limitations on transaction amounts and features.

For international users, the passport verification process requires uploading a clear photo of your passport's information page. Verification typically takes 24-48 hours, after which you can link an international credit card or bank account to your Alipay wallet.

### Subsection 2.2 Alipay Balance vs. Alipay Direct Payment

Alipay offers two primary payment modes: balance payment and direct card payment. Balance payment requires you to pre-fund your Alipay wallet from a linked bank account or card, then use the wallet balance for purchases. This method typically offers lower transaction fees and faster processing.

Direct card payment charges your linked credit or debit card at the time of purchase. While more convenient, this method may incur higher foreign transaction fees depending on your card issuer. International cards may also face lower transaction limits compared to Chinese bank accounts.

### Subsection 2.3 Alipay International Registration Process

Step 1: Download the Alipay app from your device's app store or visit alipay.com
Step 2: Select "Register" and choose your country/region
Step 3: Enter your mobile phone number (Chinese or international)
Step 4: Verify your identity using passport or Chinese ID
Step 5: Link a payment method (international credit card or Chinese bank account)
Step 6: Complete facial verification if prompted
Step 7: Set up your security questions and payment password

## Section 3: Bank Transfer Options

Bank transfers remain a popular payment method for large 1688.com orders due to their higher transaction limits and lower per-dollar costs. However, international bank transfers to Chinese accounts involve multiple intermediaries and fees.

### Subsection 3.1 Direct Wire Transfer to Chinese Suppliers

Some 1688.com sellers accept direct bank transfers, particularly for orders exceeding ¥50,000 (approximately $7,000 USD). This method bypasses Alipay entirely but eliminates the platform's buyer protection. Direct transfers are suitable for repeat buyers with established supplier relationships.

When using direct wire transfers, you'll need the supplier's bank details including the bank name, account number, SWIFT code, and account holder name. International wire transfers typically take 3-5 business days to clear and incur fees of $15-50 depending on your bank and the intermediary banks involved.

### Subsection 3.2 Chinese Bank Account Requirements

Opening a Chinese bank account as a foreigner is possible but requires significant documentation. You'll need your passport, proof of residence in China (hotel booking may suffice), and sometimes a Chinese phone number. Banks such as Bank of China, ICBC, and China Merchants Bank are most accommodating to foreign account holders.

Having a Chinese bank account enables direct Alipay top-ups and bank transfers without intermediary fees. The account also simplifies future transactions and provides access to Chinese financial services that can benefit ongoing sourcing operations.

### Subsection 3.3 Using Chinese Payment Platforms

Platforms like WeChat Pay and UnionPay offer alternative payment methods that some 1688.com sellers accept. WeChat Pay requires a Chinese bank account for verification, while UnionPay cards issued internationally can sometimes be used through compatible payment gateways. These alternatives may offer better exchange rates than direct Alipay international card payments.

## Section 4: Sourcing Agent Payments

Sourcing agents provide the most straightforward path for international buyers on 1688.com. These intermediaries handle the entire procurement process, including platform navigation, supplier communication, quality inspection, and payment processing. Buyers pay the agent in their preferred currency, and the agent handles all CNY transactions.

### Subsection 4.1 How Sourcing Agent Payments Work

The typical sourcing agent workflow involves five steps. First, you provide the agent with product links or specifications. Second, the agent sources quotes from multiple suppliers and presents options. Third, you select your preferred supplier and approve the order. Fourth, you pay the agent in your local currency (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.). Fifth, the agent places the order, monitors production, and arranges shipping.

Sourcing agents charge either a percentage-based fee (typically 3-8% of order value) or a fixed commission per order. Some agents charge additional fees for quality inspection, warehousing, and consolidation services. The total cost premium over direct payment is typically 5-12%, depending on the agent and service level.

### Subsection 4.2 Popular Sourcing Agent Platforms

Several established platforms connect international buyers with sourcing agents. CJ Dropshipping offers a user-friendly interface with integrated 1688.com product importing. SourcingNova provides dedicated account managers for high-volume buyers. Leelinesourcing specializes in quality inspection and supplier verification services.

Each platform offers different payment options, fee structures, and service levels. CJ Dropshipping accepts PayPal, credit cards, and bank transfers with fees starting at 0%. SourcingNova charges 5% commission and accepts international wire transfers and PayPal. Leelinesourcing offers tiered pricing based on order volume.

### Subsection 4.3 Evaluating Sourcing Agent Reliability

When selecting a sourcing agent, verify their business registration, check customer reviews on independent platforms, and request references from existing clients. Reputable agents will provide transparent fee structures, clear communication channels, and documented quality control processes. Avoid agents who require full payment upfront or refuse to provide documentation of their business operations.

## Section 5: Escrow and Buyer Protection Services

Escrow services provide payment security by holding funds until the buyer confirms satisfactory delivery of goods. While 1688.com's built-in Alipay escrow protects domestic transactions, international buyers may benefit from additional third-party escrow services.

### Subsection 5.1 1688.com Built-in Buyer Protection

Alipay's escrow system automatically holds payment until the buyer confirms receipt of goods. If a dispute arises, the platform mediates between buyer and seller, releasing funds only after resolution. This protection applies to all Alipay transactions on 1688.com and is one of the platform's strongest features for buyer security.

The protection period typically lasts 15 days after the seller marks an order as shipped. During this period, buyers can open disputes for non-delivery, defective products, or items not matching descriptions. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 3-5 business days.

### Subsection 5.2 Third-Party Escrow Services

For direct supplier payments outside Alipay, third-party escrow services add a layer of security. Services like Escrow.com and similar platforms hold funds in trust, releasing them only after both parties confirm the transaction terms are met. These services typically charge 1-3% of the transaction value.

Third-party escrow is particularly valuable for large orders, custom manufacturing agreements, and transactions with new suppliers. The additional cost is often justified by the protection against fraud and the documented transaction trail that escrow services provide.

### Subsection 5.3 Trade Assurance Programs

Some sourcing platforms offer trade assurance programs that guarantee payment protection. These programs typically require suppliers to meet certain verification criteria and may charge higher fees than standard escrow. Trade assurance is most commonly used on Alibaba.com but some platforms extend similar protections to 1688.com transactions.

## Section 6: Currency Conversion Strategies

Effective currency conversion can save international buyers 2-5% on each transaction. Understanding exchange rates, timing, and conversion methods is essential for cost optimization.

### Subsection 6.1 Understanding CNY Exchange Rates

The Chinese Yuan (CNY/RMB) exchange rate fluctuates daily against major currencies. The People's Bank of China (PBOC) sets a daily reference rate, and market rates typically trade within a 2% band of this reference. Monitoring exchange rate trends helps buyers time their purchases for optimal conversion rates.

For large orders, even small exchange rate movements can result in significant cost differences. A 1% improvement in exchange rate on a $10,000 order saves $100, while a 3% improvement on the same order saves $300. Strategic timing of currency conversion can meaningfully impact overall sourcing costs.

### Subsection 6.2 Alipay vs. Bank Conversion Rates

Alipay's international conversion rates include a built-in spread of approximately 1-2% above the interbank rate. This spread covers processing costs and currency risk. Direct bank transfers may offer better rates through specialized foreign exchange services, particularly for amounts exceeding $5,000.

Specialized FX providers like Wise (formerly TransferWise) and OFX often provide rates closer to the interbank rate with transparent fee structures. These services can be used to fund Alipay wallets or make direct supplier payments, potentially saving 1-3% compared to standard Alipay conversion.

### Subsection 6.3 Timing Your Purchases for Better Rates

Exchange rate volatility is influenced by economic indicators, trade policies, and geopolitical events. Monitoring these factors helps buyers identify favorable conversion windows. Historical patterns suggest that CNY tends to strengthen during periods of strong Chinese export growth and weaken during global economic uncertainty.

For regular buyers, setting up rate alerts through banking apps or FX services can notify you when favorable rates are available. Some services also allow you to lock in rates in advance through forward contracts, providing certainty for budget planning while protecting against adverse rate movements.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Set Up Your Alipay Account

Download the Alipay app from your device's app store or visit alipay.com. Select "Register" and choose your country/region from the dropdown menu. Enter your mobile phone number and verify with the SMS code you receive. If using international passport verification, upload a clear photo of your passport's information page and complete facial verification when prompted. Set up a payment password and security questions for account protection.

### Step 2: Link a Payment Method

Navigate to "Me" → "Bank Cards" in the Alipay app. Select "Add Bank Card" and enter your international credit or debit card details. Alipay accepts Visa, Mastercard, and JCB cards from most countries. Verify the card through the small charge verification process. For better exchange rates, consider using a card with no foreign transaction fees.

### Step 3: Navigate to 1688.com

Visit 1688.com using a web browser with translation capabilities (Google Translate or Chrome's built-in translator work well). Browse products using keywords or category navigation. Use the search filters to sort by price, sales volume, and supplier rating. Save promising product links for your sourcing agent or for direct payment.

### Step 4: Complete Your Purchase

Add selected products to your cart and proceed to checkout. Alipay will display the total in CNY, including any applicable fees. Review the exchange rate and total charge in your home currency before confirming. Complete payment using your linked card or Alipay wallet balance. Save your order confirmation and tracking information for future reference.

### Step 5: Track and Confirm Delivery

Monitor your shipment through the 1688.com order tracking system or your sourcing agent's portal. Upon delivery, inspect goods thoroughly before confirming receipt in Alipay. This confirmation releases payment to the supplier and starts the buyer protection countdown. Document any issues immediately and open a dispute if necessary.

## Security Tips for International Payments

1. **Use Strong Passwords**: Create unique passwords for Alipay, your email, and any sourcing agent accounts. Enable two-factor authentication wherever available.

2. **Verify Supplier Identity**: Check supplier ratings, transaction history, and business licenses before placing orders. Avoid suppliers with low ratings or incomplete profiles.

3. **Start with Small Orders**: Test new suppliers with small orders (¥500-1,000) before committing to larger purchases. This limits potential losses from fraud or quality issues.

4. **Document Everything**: Save screenshots of product listings, communications, and transaction confirmations. This documentation is essential for dispute resolution.

5. **Avoid Direct Payments**: Always use Alipay's escrow system or a reputable third-party service for payments. Direct transfers to suppliers eliminate buyer protection.

6. **Monitor Exchange Rates**: Check exchange rates before each transaction and consider using rate alert services to time your conversions optimally.

7. **Keep Software Updated**: Ensure your devices, browsers, and apps are updated with the latest security patches to protect against malware and phishing attacks.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I pay on 1688.com with a US credit card?**
A: You cannot pay directly with a US credit card on 1688.com. However, you can link your US credit card to an Alipay account and use it for payments. Alternatively, sourcing agents accept PayPal and credit card payments directly, eliminating the need for Alipay setup.

**Q: What is the minimum order amount on 1688.com?**
A: 1688.com itself has no minimum order amount. However, most suppliers set minimum order quantities (MOQs) ranging from 10-500 units depending on the product. Sourcing agents may also have minimum order requirements, typically ¥1,000-5,000 ($140-700).

**Q: How long does Alipay verification take for international users?**
A: Passport verification typically takes 24-48 hours. Chinese mobile phone verification is instant but requires a China-based SIM card. If additional documentation is requested, verification may take up to 5 business days.

**Q: Are there fees for using Alipay internationally?**
A: Yes, Alipay charges approximately 1-2% for currency conversion plus any applicable foreign transaction fees from your card issuer. Direct bank transfers to fund your Alipay wallet may have lower overall costs for larger transactions.

**Q: What happens if my 1688.com order never arrives?**
A: Alipay's buyer protection holds your payment until you confirm delivery. If your order doesn't arrive within the protection period, you can open a dispute and Alipay will refund your payment. For direct payments without escrow, recovery options are limited.

**Q: Can I get a refund if the products are defective?**
A: Yes, Alipay's dispute resolution system allows you to request refunds for defective products. You'll need to provide photos or video evidence of the defects. Refunds are typically processed within 3-5 business days after the dispute is resolved in your favor.

**Q: Is it safe to buy on 1688.com as a foreigner?**
A: 1688.com is safe when using proper payment methods with buyer protection. Use Alipay's escrow system, start with small test orders, verify supplier credentials, and document all communications. Avoid direct payments to suppliers without established relationships.

**Q: How do I convert my currency to CNY for 1688.com purchases?**
A: Currency conversion happens automatically when you pay with an international card through Alipay. For better rates, consider using specialized FX services like Wise or OFX to fund your Alipay wallet balance. Compare rates before each transaction to optimize costs.

**Q: Can I use cryptocurrency to pay on 1688.com?**
A: 1688.com does not accept cryptocurrency payments. The platform only accepts Alipay, which requires traditional banking instruments (credit cards, debit cards, or bank accounts). Some third-party services may offer crypto-to-CNY conversion, but these add complexity and fees.
