## Common China Sourcing Scams

### 1. Bank Beneficiary Changes

**How it works:** Hacker intercepts email, sends fake bank details.

**Prevention:**
- Freeze payment immediately if you receive change request
- Call separate trusted contact (not from same email thread)
- Verify new bank account through secondary channel

### 2. Pilot Batch Quality Plummets

**How it works:** Excellent samples, poor mass production quality.

**Prevention:**
- Compare pilot directly against approved sample
- Book independent pre-shipment inspection
- Hold 70% payment until inspection passes

### 3. Factory Identity Remains Blurry

**How it works:** Trading company poses as manufacturer.

**Prevention:**
- Ask for live factory video, in-house process details
- Verify business license scope on gsxt.gov.cn
- Check for manufacturing language in business scope

## Case Studies

### Case Study 1: First-Time Importer Success

**Challenge:** New Amazon FBA seller needed reliable kitchenware supplier.

**Process:**
1. Defined supplier profile (MOQ <1,000, FDA compliant)
2. Sourced 10 candidates from Alibaba
3. Verified 3 through NECIPS
4. Ordered samples from top 3
5. Approved golden sample from best supplier
6. Placed pilot order of 500 units
7. Booked pre-shipment inspection

**Results:**
- Landed cost: $8.50/unit (52% margin at $17.99 retail)
- Defect rate: 1.2% (below 2% target)
- Total profit: $4,250 on first order

### Case Study 2: Fraud Prevention Save

**Challenge:** Supplier requested bank account change before shipment.

**Detection:**
1. Email domain slightly different
2. Bank name didn't match business license
3. Beneficiary name was different

**Outcome:** Payment frozen. Verified through secondary contact. Scam prevented.

**Lesson:** Always verify bank changes through a separate channel.

## People Also Ask

1. **How do I verify if supplier is real factory?** Check business license scope on gsxt.gov.cn. "Manufacturing" = factory. "Sales" = trading company. See our [How to Verify Suppliers](/how-to-verify-suppliers/) guide.

2. **Safest payment method?** Direct wire (T/T) to verified corporate account. 30/70 split. Never use Western Union or personal accounts.

3. **How to protect product design?** Sign localized NNN agreement enforceable in Chinese courts. Consider trademark registration in China.

4. **Can I negotiate MOQ?** Yes, during off-season or with slightly higher unit price. See our [Supplier Price Negotiation](/supplier-price-negotiation/) guide.

5. **Do I need a sourcing agent?** Not for simple orders. Yes for complex assembly or compliance testing. See our [Best China Sourcing Agents](/best-china-sourcing-agents/) comparison.

6. **How do I find suppliers on Alibaba?** Use Trade Assurance filter, verify Gold Supplier status, check transaction history, request factory photos.

7. **What is a good MOQ for first order?** 100-500 units for most products. Lower MOQs often mean higher unit prices.

8. **How do I verify factory capacity?** Request production reports, check machinery count, verify worker numbers, review QC records.

9. **What documents should I request?** Business license, export license, ISO certificate, test reports, bank account proof.

10. **How do I handle language barriers?** Use Chrome auto-translate, hire Chinese-speaking agent, or work with sourcing partner.
