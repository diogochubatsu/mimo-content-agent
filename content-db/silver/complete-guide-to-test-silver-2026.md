4. **Factory Photos** — Request actual production facility images

6. **Sample Order** — Always order samples before bulk purchasing
7. **Certifications** — Verify CE, RoHS, and other relevant certifications

### Red Flags to Avoid

- Suppliers unwilling to provide business license
- No transaction history or very new accounts
- Prices significantly below market average (too good to be true)
- Requests for payment via personal bank accounts
- No factory photos or unwillingness to do video calls
- Pressure to order large quantities immediately

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between silver-plated and sterling silver products?**
A: Silver-plated items have a thin layer of silver over base metal (copper, brass, nickel), while sterling silver contains 92.5% pure silver. Plated items are cheaper but wear off over time. Sterling silver is more durable and valuable.

**Q: Do I need special certifications to import silver testing kits?**
A: Silver testing kits typically don't require specific certifications for most markets. However, for EU sales, ensure REACH compliance (no harmful chemicals in acid solutions). For US, general consumer product safety standards apply.

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity on 1688 for silver products?**
A: MOQs vary by supplier and product type. Testing kits: 10-50 units. Silver-plated jewelry: 50-200 pieces. Electronic testers: 5-20 units. Always negotiate lower MOQs for first orders.

**Q: How do I handle customs for silver products?**
A: Silver products typically fall under HTS codes 7113 (silver jewelry) or 7117 (imitation jewelry). Duties range from 5-15% depending on your country. Prepare commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin.

**Q: Can I sell silver testing kits on Amazon FBA?**
A: Yes, silver testing kits are allowed on Amazon. However, acid-based kits may require hazmat approval. Electronic testers face no restrictions. Always check Amazon's hazardous materials policy for acid kits.

**Q: What payment methods work best for 1688 silver orders?**
A: Alipay is the primary payment method on 1688. For international buyers, use a sourcing agent who can handle Alipay payments. For Alibaba, Trade Assurance with PayPal or T/T is recommended.

**Q: How do I prevent silver products from tarnishing during shipping?**
A: Request anti-tarnish packaging from suppliers: VCI (Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor) bags, silica gel packets, and airtight containers. For bulk orders, specify anti-tarnish requirements in your contract.

**Q: What's the average lead time for silver product orders from China?**
A: Standard production: 15-25 days. Custom/branded orders: 25-35 days. Sample orders: 5-10 days. Add 5-45 days for shipping depending on method (express vs. sea freight).

**Q: Are there restrictions on importing silver products to the US?**
A: Silver products are generally allowed, but be aware of: FTC guidelines on precious metal labeling (must disclose if plated), state-specific jewelry regulations, and CPSC requirements for children's jewelry (if applicable).

**Q: How can I verify silver purity on products from Chinese suppliers?**
A: Request assay certificates from suppliers. Use XRF testing (non-destructive) or acid testing (destructive). For high-value orders, hire third-party inspection services like SGS or Intertek.
