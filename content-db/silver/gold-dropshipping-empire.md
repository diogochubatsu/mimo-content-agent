2. **Quality over quantity** — 20 products > 200 products

4. **Data-driven decisions** — Track everything, optimize constantly
5. **Diversify channels** — Don't rely on one traffic source

## Section 3: Supplier Strategy

### Supplier Types

**1688 (Best for experienced sellers)**
- 30-50% cheaper than AliExpress
- Requires sourcing agent (3-8% fee)
- Quality varies — verification essential
- MOQ: 50-500 units

**AliExpress (Best for beginners)**
- No MOQ (1 unit minimum)
- Higher prices
- Buyer protection
- 15-30 day shipping

**CJ Dropshipping**
- Custom packaging available
- US/EU warehouses (3-7 day shipping)
- Quality inspection
- Higher than AliExpress, lower than domestic

### Supplier Evaluation Matrix

| Criteria | Weight | Score (1-5) |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Product quality | 30% | |
| Price competitiveness | 25% | |
| Shipping speed | 20% | |
| Communication | 15% | |
| Reliability | 10% | |

**Minimum threshold:** 3.5/5 overall

### Building Supplier Relationships

1. **Start small** — 20-50 unit test order
2. **Pay on time** — Builds trust for better terms
3. **Communicate clearly** — Expectations, timelines, quality standards
4. **Visit if possible** — Face-to-face builds stronger relationships
5. **Diversify** — Never rely on one supplier

## FAQ

**Q: How much money do I need to start dropshipping?**
A: Minimum $2,000-$3,000 (store setup + initial ad spend). Recommended: $5,000-$10,000 for 6 months of runway.

**Q: How long before I make my first sale?**
A: With paid ads: 1-7 days. With organic traffic: 1-3 months. Most stores see first sale within 2 weeks of launching ads.

**Q: What's the best platform for dropshipping?**
A: Shopify for beginners (easiest setup). WooCommerce for experienced sellers (more control, lower costs).

**Q: How do I handle returns?**
A: Have a clear return policy. For defective items, refund without requiring return (costs less than return shipping). For buyer's remorse, offer store credit.

**Q: Can I dropship from AliExpress to the US?**
A: Yes, but shipping takes 15-30 days. For faster shipping (3-7 days), use CJ Dropshipping or US-based suppliers.

**Q: How do I compete with Amazon?**
A: Don't compete on price or speed. Compete on: brand experience, curated selection, customer service, and niche expertise.

**Q: What's the best advertising platform?**
A: Facebook/Instagram for broad audiences. TikTok for younger demographics. Google Shopping for high-intent buyers. Start with one, master it, then expand.

**Q: How do I build a brand?**
A: Custom packaging, consistent visual identity, brand story, email sequences, social media presence, and quality products.

**Q: Is dropshipping saturated in 2026?**
A: Generic dropshipping is saturated. Brand-focused, quality-first dropshipping is growing. The opportunity shifted from "anyone can do it" to "those who do it well can win big."

**Q: What's the biggest mistake new dropshippers make?**
A: Choosing products based on price alone (cheap = high returns) instead of quality and customer demand. Second biggest: not building an email list.

## Glossary

- **AOV (Average Order Value):** Total revenue ÷ Number of orders
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Total marketing spend ÷ Number of customers
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Average revenue per customer over their lifetime
- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** Revenue ÷ Ad spend
- **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition):** Cost to acquire one customer
- **DSers/Oberlo:** Tools for automating AliExpress order fulfillment
- **Klaviyo:** Email marketing platform
- **Shopify:** E-commerce platform
- **COD (Cash on Delivery):** Payment method popular in some markets
- **ROI (Return on Investment):** Profit ÷ Investment × 100
