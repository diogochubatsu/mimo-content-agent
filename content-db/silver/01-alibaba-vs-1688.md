## What Is Alibaba?

Alibaba is a global B2B marketplace operated by Alibaba Group. It is designed for overseas buyers who want to import from China. Because it targets international users, it offers English support, global payments, and shipping solutions. As a result, it is beginner-friendly. However, prices are higher because suppliers include export service costs in their quotations.

### Key Features of Alibaba for Overseas Buyers

- **Trade Assurance**: Alibaba's buyer protection program holds payment in escrow until you confirm receipt of goods that match the agreed specifications. This is a major safety net for new importers.
- **English Interface**: The entire platform, including product listings, messaging, and support, is available in English.
- **International Payment Options**: You can pay via credit card, PayPal, wire transfer (T/T), or Letter of L/C for large orders.
- **Supplier Verification Badges**: Alibaba awards Gold Supplier, Verified Manufacturer, and Assessed Supplier badges that indicate third-party verification.
- **Logistics Integration**: Alibaba offers built-in logistics solutions through Cainiao, including sea freight, air freight, and express courier options.

## What Is 1688?

1688 ([1688.com](https://www.1688.com)) is China's largest domestic wholesale marketplace. It is also owned by Alibaba Group, but it focuses on the domestic Chinese wholesale market. Because it serves local buyers, prices are much lower. In many cases, products are 20%–50% cheaper. However, it is harder to use because everything is in Chinese and suppliers rarely handle exports.

### Key Features of 1688 for the Chinese Market

- **Lower Prices**: Since 1688 suppliers compete for domestic Chinese buyers, they do not include export markup, international shipping costs, or English service fees in their pricing.
- **Massive Supplier Base**: 1688 has over 100,000 active suppliers, many of which are direct factories or authorized distributors.
- **Real-Time Inventory**: Suppliers update stock levels in real time, which means you can see exactly how many units are available before ordering.
- **Trade Assurance (Chinese Version)**: 1688 has its own buyer protection program, but it operates entirely in Chinese and is designed for domestic dispute resolution.
- **Alipay Integration**: Payments are processed through Alipay, China's largest digital payment platform, which requires a Chinese bank account or verified Alipay account.

## Problems Overseas Buyers Face on 1688

1688 offers better pricing, but it is not easy to use. First, the platform is in Chinese. Second, suppliers may not speak English. Third, payments are limited. Finally, shipping is domestic only. Therefore, many buyers cannot use 1688 directly.

### Detailed Challenge Breakdown

1. **Language Barrier**: Every product listing, seller communication, and platform feature is in Mandarin Chinese. Google Translate can help with basic product descriptions, but technical specifications and negotiation require native-level fluency.

2. **Payment Limitations**: 1688 only accepts RMB payments through Alipay or Chinese bank transfers. Overseas buyers cannot use credit cards or PayPal directly. This requires either a Chinese bank account or a sourcing agent who can process payments on your behalf.

3. **Supplier Verification Difficulty**: While 1688 has a verification system for Chinese buyers, overseas buyers cannot easily access or understand business license information, factory audit reports, or transaction history in Chinese.

4. **No International Shipping**: 1688 suppliers ship within China only. You need a domestic warehouse or sourcing agent to receive, consolidate, and forward your goods internationally.

5. **Communication Gap**: Most 1688 suppliers communicate through Alibaba's Chinese messaging tool (AliWangWang) and may not respond to English messages. Negotiation, specification clarification, and dispute resolution all happen in Chinese.

## How to Buy from 1688 as an Overseas Buyer

Most overseas buyers cannot purchase directly from 1688 without support. This is because the platform is designed for the Chinese domestic market. As a result, suppliers usually do not offer English communication, international payment methods, or international shipping. Therefore, many buyers work with a sourcing agent in China.

In many cases, 1688 suppliers are not actual manufacturers. Instead, they source products from wholesale markets such as Yiwu and then resell them online. Because of this, the price you see on 1688 may already include a markup.

### Step-by-Step Process for 1688 Sourcing

1. **Identify Products**: Use Google, Amazon, or Alibaba to research products you want to source. Note specifications, target price, and required quantity.
2. **Find a Sourcing Agent**: Work with a verified Yiwu or China-based sourcing agent who can search 1688, communicate with suppliers, and handle logistics.
3. **Request Quotations**: Your agent will search 1688 using Chinese keywords and send you comparable supplier options with pricing.
4. **Order Samples**: Before committing to bulk orders, have your agent order samples from 2–3 suppliers for quality comparison.
5. **Negotiate and Place Order**: Your agent handles negotiation in Chinese, secures the best price and MOQ, and places the order.
6. **Consolidate and Ship**: Your agent receives goods at their warehouse, inspects quality, and consolidates multiple orders into one international shipment.

## FAQ

### Which is better, Alibaba or 1688?
Alibaba is easier and safer for beginners. 1688 is cheaper but requires more experience and typically a sourcing agent. The best choice depends on your order size, experience level, and whether you have a sourcing partner in China.

### Is 1688 cheaper than Alibaba?
Yes. 1688 is usually 20–50% cheaper because it targets domestic Chinese buyers and does not include export markup, English service fees, or international shipping costs in product pricing.

### Can foreigners use 1688?
Yes, but it is difficult without local support. The platform is entirely in Chinese, payments require RMB through Alipay, and suppliers generally do not offer international shipping. Most overseas buyers work with a sourcing agent to access 1688.

### Is Alibaba safer than 1688 for overseas buyers?
Yes. Alibaba offers Trade Assurance buyer protection, English interface, international payment options, and dispute resolution processes designed for international buyers. 1688's protection is designed for Chinese domestic buyers.

### How much can I save by using 1688 instead of Alibaba?
Typical savings range from 20–50% on product costs. However, after adding sourcing agent fees (5–10%), domestic shipping, consolidation, and international freight, net savings are usually 15–35%.

### Do I need a sourcing agent for 1688?
For most overseas buyers, yes. A sourcing agent handles Chinese-language communication, payment processing, supplier verification, quality inspection, and international shipping logistics.

### Can I use Google Translate to browse 1688?
Google Translate can help with basic product descriptions, but it is unreliable for technical specifications, negotiation, and understanding supplier terms. For serious sourcing, you need either Chinese language skills or a sourcing agent.

### What payment methods does 1688 accept?
1688 accepts Alipay (China's largest digital payment platform) and Chinese bank transfers. Overseas buyers cannot use credit cards or PayPal directly. A sourcing agent can process payments on your behalf.

### Are 1688 suppliers real factories?
Not always. Many 1688 suppliers are traders, distributors, or wholesale market vendors who source products from factories. This is similar to Alibaba. Always verify supplier identity and request factory documentation.

### What is the minimum order on 1688?
MOQ on 1688 varies by supplier and product. Stock items may have very low MOQs (1–5 pieces), while customized products typically require 100–1,000+ pieces. MOQ is often negotiable for repeat buyers.

### Can I combine Alibaba and 1688 orders in one shipment?
Yes, but you need a sourcing agent or freight forwarder who can receive goods from both platforms at a consolidation warehouse and ship them together.

### Which platform has better product variety?
1688 has a larger supplier base and often more product variety for Chinese domestic market goods. Alibaba has better variety for export-oriented and internationally compliant products.

## Final Verdict

Alibaba is easy and beginner-friendly. 1688 is cheaper but complex. Therefore, the best strategy is to use both based on your business stage. If you are just starting, begin with Alibaba to learn the sourcing process safely. Once you have validated products and built confidence, transition to 1688 with a reliable sourcing agent to maximize your profit margins. If you want to reduce cost and risk, working with a sourcing partner is the best option.
