## Executive Summary

AliExpress and Alibaba are both owned by Alibaba Group but serve different purposes. AliExpress is a B2C marketplace for individual item orders with no minimum order quantity (MOQ) — ideal for dropshipping and product testing. Alibaba is a B2B marketplace for bulk/wholesale orders with MOQs — ideal for private label and established products. Choose based on your stage: AliExpress for testing; Alibaba for scaling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Which is cheaper, AliExpress or Alibaba?
Alibaba is cheaper per unit (60–75% lower) but requires bulk orders (50–5,000+ units). AliExpress is more expensive per unit but has no minimum order. Use AliExpress for testing, Alibaba for scaling.

### 2. Can I dropship from Alibaba directly?
Not easily. Most Alibaba suppliers have MOQs and don't ship individual orders. However, some suppliers offer dropshipping services. Use AliExpress for traditional dropshipping.

### 3. When should I switch from AliExpress to Alibaba?
Switch when: (1) you have a validated winning product, (2) you need 100+ units, (3) you want custom branding, or (4) per-unit cost is eating your margins.

### 4. Is Alibaba safe for beginners?
Alibaba is safe with Trade Assurance, but requires more due diligence. Vet suppliers carefully, order samples, start with small orders, and use Trade Assurance for payment protection.

### 5. What is Trade Assurance?
Trade Assurance is Alibaba's escrow payment system. It protects buyers by holding payment until the order is confirmed delivered and meets quality standards. Always use it for bulk orders.

### 6. Can I use both platforms simultaneously?
Yes, and it's recommended. Use AliExpress for testing new products (no MOQ) and Alibaba for bulk orders of winning products (lowest prices). This hybrid approach maximizes profitability.

### 7. What's the minimum order on Alibaba?
Most suppliers require 50–500 units. Some offer lower MOQs (10–50 units) for sample orders or first-time buyers. Always negotiate MOQ with the supplier.

### 8. How do I communicate with Alibaba suppliers?
Most Alibaba suppliers have English-speaking sales representatives. Use the platform's messaging system. For complex negotiations, consider hiring a sourcing agent.

### 9. Can I get private label products from AliExpress?
No. AliExpress sells ready-made products. For private label (custom branding, packaging), you need Alibaba or a manufacturer directly.

### 10. Which platform has better buyer protection?
Both have strong protection. AliExpress offers standard escrow and refund policies. Alibaba's Trade Assurance is specifically designed for large B2B transactions. Both are reliable.

### 11. How do shipping costs compare?
AliExpress: $2–$8 per item (ePacket). Alibaba: $0.30–$2 per item (sea freight for bulk). Alibaba is cheaper per unit for shipping but requires bulk orders.

### 12. Which platform is better for building a brand?
Alibaba. Private label products, custom packaging, and custom branding are only available through Alibaba or direct manufacturer relationships.
