## Section 1: Understanding Amazon FBA

### What is FBA?

Amazon FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) allows you to send your products to Amazon's warehouses, and they handle storage, packing, shipping, and customer service. You focus on sourcing and marketing; Amazon handles logistics.

**FBA Benefits:**
- Prime eligibility (boosted visibility)
- Amazon handles returns and customer service
- Multi-channel fulfillment available
- Scalable without hiring staff
- Buy Box advantage over FBM

**FBA Drawbacks:**
- Fees eat into margins (25-40% of revenue)
- Storage fees (monthly + aged inventory surcharges)
- Less control over customer experience
- Risk of inventory stranding
- Competition from Amazon's own products

### FBA vs FBM in 2026

| Factor | FBA | FBM |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Prime Badge | Yes | No (unless SFP) |
| Shipping Speed | 1-2 days | 3-7 days |
| Customer Service | Amazon handles | You handle |
| Fees | 25-40% of revenue | 15% referral only |
| Buy Box Advantage | High | Low |
| Scalability | Easy | Requires infrastructure |
| Best For | Private label, high volume | Low volume, heavy items |

### Fee Structure Breakdown

**Referral Fee:** 8-15% (category dependent)
**Fulfillment Fee:** $3.07-$8.26+ (size/weight dependent)
**Monthly Storage:** $0.87-$2.40 per cubic foot
**Aged Inventory Surcharge:** $0.50-$6.90 per unit (after 181 days)
**Long-Term Storage:** $6.90+ per unit (after 365 days)

**Real Example:**
- Selling price: $29.99
- Referral fee (15%): $4.50
- Fulfillment fee: $5.40
- Storage fee: $0.35
- **Total Amazon fees: $10.25 (34%)**
- **Your revenue: $19.74**

## Section 3: Product Research

### The Golden Rules of Product Selection

1. **Price sweet spot:** $15-$50 (high enough for profit, low enough for impulse buys)
2. **Weight:** Under 2 lbs (lower fulfillment fees)
3. **Competition:** Fewer than 3 sellers with 100+ reviews
4. **Seasonality:** Year-round demand (check Google Trends)
5. **Margins:** 30%+ after all fees
6. **Differentiation potential:** Can you improve the product?

**Step 1: Niche Discovery**

**Step 3: Competition Analysis**

**Step 4: Supplier Verification**

```

```

**Optimization Process:**
1. Launch at 30-40% ACoS
2. Monitor for 2 weeks
3. Pause non-converting keywords
4. Increase bids on winners
5. Target 15-25% ACoS

### TACOS (Total Advertising Cost of Sales)

```
TACOS = Ad Spend ÷ Total Revenue
Target: Under 15%
```

## Section 7: Financial Management

### Profit Calculation Formula

```
Profit = Selling Price
       - Product Cost
       - Amazon Fees (Referral + Fulfillment + Storage)
       - Shipping to Amazon
       - PPC Cost
       - Returns (3-5%)
       - Defects (1-2%)
```

### Cash Flow Management

**Inventory Reorder Formula:**
```
Reorder Point = (Daily Sales × Lead Time) + Safety Stock
Example: (10 units/day × 30 days) + 100 units = 400 units
```

**Working Capital Calculation:**
```
Working Capital = (Inventory Cost × Turnover Days) + PPC Float + Operating Expenses
```

## FAQ

**Q: How much money do I need to start Amazon FBA?**
A: Minimum $3,000-$5,000 for private label (inventory + PPC + tools). Wholesale can start with $1,000-$2,000. Retail arbitrage can start with $500.

**Q: How long before I make my first sale?**
A: Private label: 4-8 weeks (after listing is live). Wholesale: 1-2 weeks. Retail arbitrage: 1-3 days.

**Q: What's the best product category for beginners?**
A: Home & Kitchen, Sports & Outdoors, or Pet Supplies. These have moderate competition, good margins, and year-round demand.

**Q: Do I need a trademark to start?**
A: No, but Brand Registry (requires trademark) unlocks A+ Content, Brand Analytics, and Sponsored Brands. File your trademark early.

**Q: How do I handle returns?**
A: Amazon handles FBA returns automatically. Budget 3-5% for returns. Monitor return reasons and address quality issues.

**Q: Can I sell internationally with FBA?**
A: Yes! Amazon has programs for selling in US, UK, DE, JP, and more. Use FBA Export or Multi-Channel Fulfillment.

**Q: What's the difference between private label and wholesale?**
A: Private label: You create your own brand. Higher margins, more work. Wholesale: You resell existing brands. Lower margins, less work.

**Q: How do I protect my product from hijackers?**
A: Register your brand, use Amazon Brand Registry, file complaints against hijackers, and consider transparent program.

**Q: What's the best time to launch a product?**
A: Avoid Q4 (October-December) for first launches — competition and costs spike. Best time: January-March or July-September.

**Q: How do I scale from $10K to $100K/month?**
A: Focus on: 1) Add 3-5 more SKUs, 2) Optimize PPC for profitability, 3) Expand to international markets, 4) Build email list for repeat buyers.

## Glossary

- **ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales):** Ad spend ÷ Ad revenue
- **TACOS (Total ACoS):** Ad spend ÷ Total revenue
- **BSR (Best Seller Rank):** Amazon's ranking of products within a category
- **IPI (Inventory Performance Index):** Score measuring inventory health (0-1000)
- **MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity):** Minimum units you must order from supplier
- **PPC (Pay-Per-Click):** Amazon's advertising model
- **ROI (Return on Investment):** Profit ÷ Investment × 100
- **SKU (Stock Keeping Unit):** Unique product identifier
- **FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon):** Amazon handles storage, shipping, and customer service
- **FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant):** You handle storage and shipping
