## What Is 1688 Product Sourcing?

1688 product sourcing refers to the process of purchasing wholesale products from suppliers on [1688.com](https://www.1688.com), which is one of China's largest domestic B2B marketplaces owned by Alibaba Group.

### Platform Overview

- **Founded**: 1999 (same year as Alibaba)
- **Owner**: Alibaba Group
- **Marketplace type**: B2B domestic wholesale
- **Active suppliers**: 100,000+
- **Product categories**: 40+ major categories
- **Daily transactions**: Billions of RMB
- **Primary language**: Chinese (Mandarin)
- **Primary currency**: RMB (Chinese Yuan)
- **Payment system**: Alipay, Chinese bank transfers

## Why Overseas Buyers Use 1688 Product Sourcing

### Price Competitiveness
Products on 1688 are often 20–50% cheaper than the same products on Alibaba. This is because 1688 suppliers do not include export service costs, English language support, or international shipping preparation in their pricing.

### Larger Supplier Database
1688 has over 100,000 active suppliers, many of which are direct factories or authorized distributors. This massive supplier base gives you more options and better pricing through competition.

### More Factory-Direct Products
Many 1688 suppliers are actual manufacturers who sell directly to domestic wholesalers. This eliminates middlemen and reduces costs significantly.

### Better Pricing for Experienced Buyers
If you have a sourcing agent in China and understand the platform, 1688 offers the best wholesale pricing available from Chinese suppliers.

## Common Problems with 1688 Product Sourcing

### Chinese Language Barrier
The platform is mainly written in Chinese. Product listings, seller communication, platform features, and help documentation are all in Mandarin Chinese. Google Translate can help with basic browsing, but technical specifications and negotiation require native-level fluency.

### RMB Payments
Most suppliers prefer domestic Chinese payment methods. Alipay is the primary payment platform, and it requires a Chinese bank account or verified Alipay account. Overseas buyers cannot use credit cards or PayPal directly on 1688.

### Supplier Verification Risks
Not every supplier is a real factory. Some are traders, distributors, or wholesale market vendors who source products from other suppliers. Verifying supplier legitimacy requires Chinese-language skills and access to Chinese business databases.

### Shipping and Consolidation Problems
Many suppliers only handle domestic Chinese shipping. You need a domestic warehouse or sourcing agent to receive, consolidate, and forward your goods internationally.

### Quality Control Challenges
Without physical presence in China, verifying product quality before shipment is difficult. Samples must be ordered and shipped internationally, adding time and cost to the sourcing process.

## Step-by-Step 1688 Product Sourcing Process

### Step 1: Search Products on 1688
Use Chinese keywords or sourcing agents to find products. The search function works best with Chinese keywords, so either learn basic product terms or use a sourcing agent who can search in Chinese.

**Search tips**:
- Use specific Chinese product names (e.g., "硅胶手机壳" for silicone phone cases)
- Filter by transaction volume to find popular suppliers
- Check supplier ratings and review history
- Compare multiple suppliers for the same product

### Step 2: Shortlist Suppliers
Compare ratings, transaction history, certifications, and product range. Focus on suppliers with:
- High transaction volumes
- Positive customer reviews
- Verified business licenses
- Factory or warehouse photos
- Responsive communication

### Step 3: Request Samples
Always test before bulk orders. Order samples from 2–3 shortlisted suppliers to compare quality, packaging, and pricing. Pay for samples to ensure you receive production-quality products.

### Step 4: Negotiate MOQ and Pricing
Some suppliers support low MOQ trial orders. Negotiate pricing based on order volume, payment terms, and long-term relationship potential. Use a sourcing agent to negotiate in Chinese for better results.

### Step 5: Consolidate Products
Combine into one warehouse before export. If you are ordering from multiple suppliers, use a consolidation warehouse to receive all goods, inspect them, and ship them together.

### Step 6: Product Inspection
Verify quality before shipment. Arrange pre-shipment inspection to ensure products match your specifications and quality standards.

### Step 7: Arrange International Shipping
Sea freight is most common for bulk orders. Air freight is faster but more expensive. Express courier is best for samples and urgent orders.

## FAQ

### What is 1688?
1688 is China's largest domestic B2B wholesale marketplace, owned by Alibaba Group. It primarily serves Chinese domestic buyers and offers lower prices than Alibaba because it does not include export service costs.

### Can overseas buyers purchase directly from 1688?
Technically yes, but it is extremely difficult without Chinese language skills, a Chinese bank account, and domestic shipping capabilities. Most overseas buyers work with a sourcing agent to access 1688.

### How much cheaper is 1688 than Alibaba?
Products on 1688 are typically 20–50% cheaper than the same products on Alibaba. However, after adding sourcing agent fees, domestic shipping, and international freight, net savings are usually 15–35%.

### What payment methods does 1688 accept?
1688 accepts Alipay and Chinese bank transfers. Overseas buyers cannot use credit cards or PayPal directly. A sourcing agent can process payments on your behalf using their Chinese bank account.

### Do I need to speak Chinese to use 1688?
While not impossible, using 1688 without Chinese language skills is extremely challenging. The entire platform is in Chinese, and supplier communication happens in Chinese. A sourcing agent is essential for non-Chinese speakers.

### How do I verify a 1688 supplier?
Verify the business license through the National Enterprise Credit Information system (gsxt.gov.cn), check transaction history and reviews, request samples, conduct video calls, and use a sourcing agent for in-person verification.

### What is the typical lead time for 1688 orders?
Stock items: 3–7 days for domestic shipping to consolidation warehouse. Custom production: 15–45 days. International shipping adds 15–45 days depending on method (sea, air, or express).

### Can I combine 1688 orders with other Chinese suppliers?
Yes, use a consolidation warehouse to combine goods from 1688, Yiwu Market, and other Chinese sources into one international shipment. Most sourcing agents offer consolidation services.

### What products are best sourced from 1688?
1688 is best for high-volume, standardized products where price is the primary factor. Electronics accessories, home goods, fashion accessories, toys, and general merchandise are popular categories.

### How do I handle quality control on 1688 orders?
Use a sourcing agent to order samples, verify quality, and arrange pre-shipment inspection. Never skip quality control, even for small orders.

### What are the biggest risks of 1688 sourcing?
The biggest risks are supplier fraud, quality inconsistencies, payment security, and communication barriers. These risks are mitigated by using a verified sourcing agent and following proper verification procedures.

## Final Verdict

1688 product sourcing offers significant cost advantages for overseas importers who are willing to navigate the platform's challenges. The key to success is working with a reliable sourcing agent, verifying suppliers thoroughly, ordering samples before committing to bulk, and calculating your complete landed cost. Start with small orders, build relationships with trusted agents and suppliers, and scale up as you gain experience and confidence.
