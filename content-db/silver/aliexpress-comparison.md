## FAQ

**Q: Can I use 1688 without speaking Chinese?**
A: Yes, but it's challenging. You'll need to use translation tools (Google Translate, DeepL) and ideally work with a Chinese-speaking agent. Many agents offer English support and can handle communications, negotiations, and quality checks for 5-10% of order value.

**Q: How do I pay on 1688 without Alipay?**
A: You have three options: 1) Use an agent who accepts PayPal/bank transfer, 2) Set up Alipay with a Chinese bank account (requires Chinese residency), 3) Use third-party payment services like WorldFirst or Payoneer that support Chinese merchants.

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity on 1688?**
A: Most 1688 suppliers require 50-500 units per order, depending on the product. Some suppliers offer lower MOQs (10-50 units) at slightly higher prices. Always negotiate MOQs, especially for first orders.

**Q: How do I handle returns from 1688?**
A: Returns are more complex than AliExpress. Options include: 1) Negotiate with supplier for partial refund, 2) Use agent's return service (additional fees), 3) Absorb the loss for small-value items, 4) Resell defective items locally at discount.

**Q: Is 1688 legal for international dropshipping?**
A: Yes, but you must comply with import regulations in your country. You're responsible for customs duties, taxes, and product compliance. Some products may require certifications (CE, FCC) for import.

**Q: How do I find reliable 1688 suppliers?**
A: Use these criteria: 1) Gold Supplier status (2+ years), 2) High transaction volume (1000+ monthly sales), 3) Positive reviews (4.5+ stars), 4) Factory verification badges, 5) Willing to provide samples and documentation.

**Q: Can I brand 1688 products for my business?**
A: Yes, many 1688 suppliers offer private labeling and customization services. Minimum orders for custom branding are typically 500-1000 units. Discuss options with suppliers directly or through your agent.

**Q: What's the best way to test products from 1688?**
A: Follow this process: 1) Order 10-20 samples, 2) Use quality inspection service, 3) Test product yourself, 4) Get feedback from beta customers, 5) Start with small bulk order (50-100 units) before scaling.

**Q: How do I calculate true costs when comparing platforms?**
A: Include all costs: product price + shipping + agent fees (5-10%) + quality inspection ($0.50-$2/item) + payment fees (1-3%) + customs duties + potential returns (5-10% of orders).

**Q: Are there any 1688 alternatives with English support?**
A: Yes, consider: 1) Alibaba.com (English B2B platform), 2) DHgate (English B2B/B2C), 3) Made-in-China.com (English B2B), 4) Global Sources (English B2B). These offer similar wholesale prices with English interfaces.
