## Fee Structures

### Common Fee Models

| Model | Description | Typical Range | Best For |
|-------|-------------|---------------|----------|
| Percentage of order | % of total order value | 5-10% | Large orders |
| Fixed fee per order | Flat rate per shipment | $200-1,000 | Small orders |
| Monthly retainer | Ongoing service fee | $500-2,000 | Continuous procurement |
| Project-based | Specific project fee | Varies | Complex projects |

## People Also Ask

1. **How do I find a sourcing agent?** Online search, trade shows, referrals, platforms. See our [How to Find a Sourcing Agent in China](/how-to-find-a-sourcing-agent-in-china/) guide.

2. **How much do sourcing agents cost?** 5-10% of order value, or $200-1,000 per order. Varies by service level.

3. **How do I verify an agent?** Check business license on gsxt.gov.cn, contact references, visit office if possible.

4. **When should I use a sourcing agent?** Complex products, first-time importers, compliance-heavy items, or large orders.

5. **Can I trust sourcing agents?** Most are reputable, but verify thoroughly. Check references, use contracts, start small.

6. **What's the difference between agent and trading company?** Agent works for you (buyer). Trading company works for themselves (seller).

7. **How do agents make money?** Commission on orders (5-10%), fixed fees, or monthly retainers.

8. **Should I use multiple agents?** Generally no. Build relationship with one good agent. Multiple agents create confusion.

9. **What's the biggest risk with agents?** Choosing wrong agent, hidden fees, poor communication, or fraud.

10. **How do I start with an agent?** Verify credentials, clarify fees, establish contract, test with small order.
