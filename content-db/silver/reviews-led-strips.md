## Import Tips

### 1. Start with Samples
Always order samples before bulk. Cost: $10-30 per product. Test brightness, color accuracy, and adhesive quality.

### 2. Check Certifications
- **US:** UL certification required for electrical products
- **Europe:** CE certification required
- **Japan:** PSE certification required

### 3. Calculate True Costs
