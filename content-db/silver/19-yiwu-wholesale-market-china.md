## How Wholesale from Yiwu Works

### Step 1: Product Research
Before visiting or contacting suppliers, research your target products, understand market demand, and set your budget.

### Step 2: Supplier Comparison
Compare multiple suppliers for each product. Check pricing, MOQ, quality, and reliability.

### Step 3: Sample Verification
Order samples from shortlisted suppliers to compare quality before committing to bulk orders.

### Step 4: Order Production
Place orders with selected suppliers. For stock items, this is immediate. For custom orders, production takes 15–45 days.

### Step 5: Product Inspection
Arrange pre-shipment inspection to verify quality before goods leave China.

### Step 6: Consolidation & Shipping
Combine orders from multiple suppliers into one international shipment through a consolidation warehouse.

## FAQ

### How many shops are in Yiwu Market?
Yiwu International Trade Market contains over 75,000 shops across 5 major districts, covering 5.5 million square meters of wholesale trading space.

### What products can I find in Yiwu Market?
Yiwu offers 2.1 million+ products across 40+ categories, including fashion accessories, toys, jewelry, kitchenware, home decoration, stationery, electronics, and general merchandise.

### What is the typical MOQ in Yiwu Market?
MOQ varies by product and supplier. For stock items, MOQ is often 1–5 cartons. For custom products, MOQ is typically 100–1,000+ units.

### Can I buy directly from Yiwu suppliers?
Yes, if you visit in person. For remote buying, you typically need a sourcing agent to handle communication, payment, and logistics.

### How do I find the right district for my product?
Each district specializes in different categories. District 1: toys, jewelry. District 2: hardware, electronics. District 3: office supplies, sports. District 4: textiles, apparel. District 5: imported goods.

### What are Yiwu Market's operating hours?
Yiwu Market is open Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Some shops may have different hours. Public holidays may affect availability.

### How do I get to Yiwu Market?
Yiwu is accessible by train from Shanghai (1.5 hours), Hangzhou (1 hour), and other major cities. Yiwu has its own airport and is connected by highway to major transportation hubs.

### Is it safe to buy from Yiwu Market?
Yiwu Market is generally safe for buyers. However, always verify suppliers, order samples, and arrange inspection. Use a sourcing agent for additional protection.

### Can I negotiate prices in Yiwu Market?
Yes, negotiation is expected and common. Start with 30–50% below the quoted price and work toward a mutually acceptable price.

### How do I handle shipping from Yiwu?
Use a sourcing agent or freight forwarder to arrange consolidation and international shipping. Yiwu has efficient logistics connections to Ningbo and Shanghai ports.
