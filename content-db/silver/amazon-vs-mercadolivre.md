## Executive Summary

Choosing between Amazon and Mercado Livre (Mercado Libre) is one of the most critical decisions for sellers entering or expanding in the Brazilian e-commerce market in 2026. After analyzing platform data, seller feedback, and transaction volumes from both marketplaces, we present a comprehensive comparison to help you make an informed decision.

- Sellers with products priced above R$150 (higher AOV offsets fees)

- Businesses needing maximum marketplace visibility
- Sellers wanting integrated payment solutions (Mercado Pago)
- Local Brazilian sellers with existing logistics infrastructure

## Step-by-Step Guide: Choosing Your Platform

### Step 1: Analyze Your Product Portfolio

Before committing to a platform, conduct a thorough product analysis:

- **Price point assessment:** Products above R$150 perform better on Amazon; below R$100 favor Mercado Livre
- **Category research:** Check which platform has stronger presence in your niche
- **Competition analysis:** Evaluate seller density and pricing pressure on both platforms
- **Margin calculation:** Run fee scenarios for your top 20 products on each platform

**Example:** If you sell electronics averaging R$350, Amazon's 15% fee equals R$52.50, but the higher conversion rate and AOV may yield better net profit than Mercado Livre's 13% fee (R$45.50) with lower conversion.

### Step 2: Evaluate Your Logistics Capabilities

Assess your current shipping infrastructure:

- **Do you have warehouse space in São Paulo?** Essential for FBA/MLF
- **Can you handle same-day or next-day shipping?** Required for Prime/Verified badges
- **What's your average order processing time?** Must be under 24 hours for both platforms
- **Do you have packaging materials meeting platform requirements?** Different specs for each

**Action Items:**
1. Contact FBA and MLF for onboarding requirements
2. Calculate shipping costs to major Brazilian cities
3. Determine if you need third-party logistics (3PL)

### Step 3: Consider Your Brand Positioning

Your brand strategy should influence platform choice:

- **Premium brands:** Amazon's curated experience and Prime badge signal quality
- **Value brands:** Mercado Livre's price-sensitive audience seeks deals
- **New brands:** Amazon's trust factor helps new entrants
- **Established local brands:** Mercado Livre's massive reach maximizes visibility

### Step 4: Plan Your Advertising Strategy

Both platforms require advertising investment for visibility:

- **Amazon:** Budget R$2,000-5,000/month minimum for Sponsored Products
- **Mercado Livre:** Budget R$1,000-3,000/month for sponsored listings
- **Timeline:** Allow 3-6 months for advertising optimization
- **Metrics:** Track ACOS (Amazon) and CPC (Mercado Livre) as primary KPIs

### Step 5: Start with One Platform, Then Expand

We recommend a phased approach:

1. **Month 1-3:** Launch on your primary platform (based on Steps 1-4)
2. **Month 4-6:** Optimize operations, advertising, and inventory
3. **Month 7-9:** Expand to second platform with proven products
4. **Month 10-12:** Analyze performance and allocate resources accordingly

## Case Study

### Background

TechGear Brasil, a consumer electronics accessories company based in São Paulo, was selling exclusively on Mercado Livre since 2020. The company specializes in phone cases, chargers, and audio accessories with an average price point of R$89.

### Challenge

By early 2025, TechGear faced several challenges:
- Increasing competition on Mercado Livre driving down margins
- Limited brand perception (seen as "budget" option)
- High advertising costs (CPC increased 45% YoY)
- Difficulty reaching premium customers

### Solution

In March 2025, TechGear implemented a dual-platform strategy:
- **Mercado Livre:** Maintained core catalog, focused on volume and Mercado Livre Fulfillment

### Key Takeaways

1. **Amazon's higher AOV compensated for lower volume** — fewer units but higher revenue per sale

## FAQ

**Q: Can I sell on both Amazon and Mercado Livre simultaneously?**
A: Yes, you can absolutely sell on both platforms simultaneously. In fact, many successful sellers in Brazil use a dual-platform strategy, allocating products based on price point and target audience. Amazon typically handles premium products while Mercado Livre drives volume with mid-range offerings.

**Q: Which platform has lower fees for sellers in 2026?**
A: Mercado Livre generally has lower fees (11-16%) compared to Amazon (12-17% including referral and closing fees). However, Amazon's higher average order value and conversion rates often offset the fee difference. Calculate your specific margins on both platforms before deciding.

**Q: How long does it take to start selling on each platform?**
A: Amazon Brazil typically takes 2-4 weeks for seller registration and product listing approval. Mercado Livre is faster, with most sellers operational within 3-7 days. Both platforms require business documentation (CNPJ) and bank account verification.

**Q: Which platform is better for international sellers entering Brazil?**
A: Amazon Brazil is generally better for international sellers due to its English-language seller support, established global seller onboarding processes, and FBA infrastructure. Mercado Livre requires more Portuguese-language navigation and local business knowledge, though they have improved international seller support in 2026.

**Q: What are the payment terms for sellers on each platform?**
A: Amazon Brazil pays sellers every 14 days via bank transfer. Mercado Livre offers instant payments through Mercado Pago, with funds available immediately after sale confirmation. This cash flow advantage is significant for sellers with tight working capital requirements.

**Q: Which platform provides better seller protection against fraud?**
A: Both platforms offer buyer and seller protection programs, but with different approaches. Amazon's A-to-Z Guarantee is more structured and favors buyers, while Mercado Livre's protection system is more balanced. Amazon has stricter account suspension policies, while Mercado Livre uses a gradual penalty system based on seller reputation scores.

**Q: Do I need a Brazilian company (CNPJ) to sell on these platforms?**
A: Yes, both Amazon Brazil and Mercado Livre require a Brazilian CNPJ for professional seller accounts. However, Amazon has recently introduced a simplified registration process for international sellers through their Global Selling program, which can help navigate Brazilian tax requirements.

**Q: How important is customer reviews on each platform?**
A: Reviews are critical on both platforms but function differently. Amazon's review system is more influential on conversion rates, with products needing 50+ reviews to gain significant visibility. Mercado Livre's reputation system (based on transaction history and feedback) is equally important, with sellers needing to maintain 95%+ positive ratings for premium placement.

**Q: Which platform offers better advertising tools?**
A: Amazon provides more sophisticated advertising options (Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, DSP) with detailed analytics. Mercado Livre's advertising is simpler but effective, focusing on sponsored listings and featured products. Amazon's tools are more complex but offer higher potential ROAS for experienced advertisers.
