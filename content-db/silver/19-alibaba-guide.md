## Executive Summary

Alibaba is the world's largest B2B marketplace connecting buyers with manufacturers and suppliers, primarily in China. With 10 million+ products and Trade Assurance buyer protection, Alibaba offers the lowest per-unit prices for bulk orders. This guide covers everything you need to safely source products: supplier vetting, Trade Assurance, payment safety, negotiation, and private label setup.

## What is Alibaba?

Alibaba is the world's largest B2B (business-to-business) marketplace. Founded in 1999, it connects international buyers with manufacturers and suppliers, primarily in China.

### Key Statistics

| Metric | Data |
|--------|------|
| Founded | 1999 |
| Products | 10 million+ |
| Suppliers | 200,000+ |
| Countries Served | 190+ |
| Annual GMV | $300B+ |
| Parent Company | Alibaba Group |

### Alibaba vs. AliExpress

| Feature | Alibaba | AliExpress |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Target | B2B (businesses) | B2C (consumers) |
| MOQ | 50–5,000+ units | None |
| Prices | Factory/wholesale | Retail/wholesale |
| Customization | Full (private label) | Limited |
| Best For | Bulk orders, private label | Testing, dropshipping |

## Supplier Vetting Process

### Step 1: Check Supplier Credentials

| Credential | What to Look For | Red Flag |
|-----------|------------------|---------|
| Trade Assurance badge | Gold/verified supplier | No badge |
| Years on platform | 3+ years | Less than 1 year |
| Transaction history | 100+ transactions | Less than 50 |
| Response rate | 90%+ | Below 80% |
| Repeat customer rate | 30%+ | Below 10% |

### Step 2: Verify Business License

- Request the supplier's business license
- Check that "manufacturing" is in the scope (not just "sales")
- Verify the license is current (not expired)
- Cross-reference with Chinese business databases

### Step 3: Request Samples

| Sample Order | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| 2–3 units of main product | Verify quality |
| Different color/size variants | Check consistency |
| Custom packaging sample | Test branding options |
| Rush order sample | Test production speed |

### Step 4: Video Audit

Request a live video walkthrough of the factory. Look for:
- Actual production equipment (not just a trading office)
- Quality control processes
- Inventory levels
- Worker conditions

## Payment Safety

### Payment Methods Comparison

| Method | Protection | Cost | Best For |
|--------|-----------|------|----------|
| Trade Assurance | Full escrow | Free | Most orders |
| Credit Card | Chargeback protection | 2.9% | Small orders |
| PayPal | Buyer protection | 3.5% + $0.30 | Small orders |
| Wire Transfer | None | Low | Established relationships |
| Escrow Service | Third-party protection | 1–3% | High-value orders |

### Payment Safety Rules

1. **Always use Trade Assurance** for orders over $500
2. **Never wire money** to unknown suppliers
3. **Start with small orders** ($100–$500) to test
4. **Use credit card** for sample orders (chargeback protection)
5. **Get payment terms in writing** before ordering

## Alibaba for Dropshipping

### Can You Dropship from Alibaba?

**Limited.** Most Alibaba suppliers have MOQs (50–5,000+ units) and don't ship individual orders. However, some scenarios work:

| Scenario | How It Works |
|----------|-------------|
| Hybrid model | Buy bulk from Alibaba, ship from your warehouse |
| CJDropshipping integration | Alibaba suppliers ship via CJDropshipping |
| Pre-stock bestsellers | Buy in bulk, ship yourself for bestsellers |
| Private label dropship | Custom products via CJDropshipping |

### When Alibaba + Dropshipping Works

- You have a validated winning product (tested on AliExpress)
- You want custom branding (private label)
- You need 100+ units of a bestseller
- You're willing to handle some fulfillment yourself

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is Alibaba safe to buy from?
Yes, when using Trade Assurance. Trade Assurance holds your payment in escrow until the order is confirmed delivered and meets quality standards. Always use it for orders over $500.

### 2. What is the minimum order on Alibaba?
Most suppliers require 50–5,000+ units. Some offer lower MOQs (10–50 units) for sample orders or first-time buyers. Always negotiate MOQ with the supplier.

### 3. How do I find a good Alibaba supplier?
Check: Trade Assurance badge, 3+ years on platform, 100+ transactions, 90%+ response rate, and verify business license. Request samples before bulk orders.

### 4. Can I use Alibaba for dropshipping?
Not easily. Most suppliers have MOQs and don't ship individual orders. Use AliExpress for traditional dropshipping. Alibaba works for bulk orders of validated products.

### 5. What is Trade Assurance?
Trade Assurance is Alibaba's escrow payment system. It protects buyers by holding payment until the order is confirmed delivered and meets quality standards. Always use it for large orders.

### 6. How do I negotiate on Alibaba?
Be specific about quantities, ask for volume discounts, compare multiple suppliers, and don't accept the first price. Start with 20–30% below asking price and negotiate from there.

### 7. Can I get private label products?
Yes. Alibaba is the best platform for private label. Find manufacturers (not trading companies), send your specifications, request samples with your branding, and place bulk orders.

### 8. How do I verify a supplier is legitimate?
Check: Trade Assurance badge, business license (verify "manufacturing" in scope), years on platform, transaction history, and request a live video audit of the factory.

### 9. What are common Alibaba scams?
Watch for: prices too good to be true, no Trade Assurance, requests for wire transfers to personal accounts, refusal to provide samples, and stock photos only.

### 10. How long does shipping from Alibaba take?
Sea freight: 25–45 days. Air freight: 5–10 days. Express courier: 3–5 days. Sea freight is cheapest for bulk; air freight for samples and urgent orders.

### 11. Do I need a sourcing agent?
For complex orders or first-time buyers, a sourcing agent ($100–$500) can help with: supplier vetting, quality inspection, negotiation, and logistics. For simple orders, handle directly.

### 12. How do I handle quality issues?
Document with photos, contact supplier immediately, request replacement or refund through Trade Assurance, and consider hiring SGS/Intertek for pre-shipment inspection.
