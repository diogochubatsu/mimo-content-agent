## Executive Summary

Print on demand (POD) companies handle the printing, packaging, and shipping of custom products after a customer places an order. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Which print on demand company has the best quality?
Printful, Gelato, and Apliiq consistently rank highest for print quality. Printful is the industry standard for quality assurance. Always order samples before committing.

### 2. What is the cheapest print on demand company?
Printify ($8.95 for a t-shirt), CustomCat ($8.50), and SPOD ($9.00) offer the lowest base costs. However, quality varies — always balance cost with quality.

### 3. Can I use multiple POD companies?
Yes. Many sellers use Printful for premium products and Printify for budget items. This maximizes margins while maintaining quality for key products.

### 4. Which POD company ships the fastest?
SPOD has the fastest production times (2–4 days on average). Printful and Gelato typically ship within 2–7 business days. Spring and Apliiq are slower (5–12 days).

### 5. Do I need to buy inventory with POD?
No. That's the core benefit of print on demand. Products are only printed and shipped after a customer places an order. Zero inventory risk.

### 6. Can I sell POD products on Amazon?
Yes. Printful, Printify, and Gooten integrate with Amazon. You can list custom products on Amazon and fulfill them through your POD provider.

### 7. How do I handle POD returns?
Most POD companies offer reprints or refunds for defective products (wrong print, damaged, etc.). For non-defective returns, your store policy should state that custom products are final sale.

### 8. Which POD company is best for beginners?
Printful is best for beginners — excellent documentation, easy Shopify integration, and reliable quality. Printify is a good budget alternative with more product options.

### 9. Can I add custom branding with POD?
Yes. Printful offers custom packaging, labels, and inserts. Apliiq specializes in premium branding (woven labels, patches, embroidery). Other providers offer limited branding options.

### 10. How much can I make with print on demand?
Average POD stores earn $1,000–$5,000/month in profit after 6–12 months. Top performers earn $10,000+/month. Profit margins range from 40–70% depending on product and provider.

### 11. What products can I sell with POD?
T-shirts, hoodies, mugs, posters, phone cases, tote bags, stickers, pillows, blankets, canvas prints, and more. Printful has 300+ products; Printify has 800+.

### 12. Do POD companies handle international shipping?
Yes. Most POD companies ship globally. Gelato excels at international shipping with local production in 30+ countries, reducing shipping times and costs.
