## Section 1: Understanding Product Profitability on 1688

### 1.1 What Makes a Product Profitable on 1688

Profitability on 1688 is determined by the relationship between three factors: the sourcing price, the total landed cost, and the achievable retail price. A product that looks cheap on 1688 may not be profitable once you add shipping, customs duties, platform fees, and marketing costs.

**The profitability equation:**

```
Profit = Retail Price - (Product Cost + Shipping + Duties + Platform Fees + Marketing)
Margin % = (Profit / Retail Price) × 100
```

**Minimum margin targets by sales channel:**

| Sales Channel | Minimum Margin Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon FBA | 30-40% | FBA fees consume 30-35% of revenue |
| Shopify / DTC | 40-60% | You bear all marketing costs |
| Wholesale / B2B | 20-30% | Volume compensates for lower margins |
| Retail (physical store) | 50-70% | Overhead costs are highest |
| eBay / Other marketplaces | 25-35% | Fees vary but typically 15-20% |

### 1.2 The 1688 Price Advantage

Products on 1688 are typically 30-50% cheaper than the same items on Alibaba.com and 50-70% cheaper than AliExpress. However, the raw product price is only one component of profitability. Understanding the full cost structure is essential.

**Cost breakdown example for a $25 retail product:**

| Cost Component | Amount | % of Retail |
|---|---|---|
| Product cost (1688) | $3.50 | 14% |
| Domestic shipping (China) | $0.50 | 2% |
| International shipping | $2.80 | 11.2% |
| Customs duties (8%) | $0.54 | 2.2% |
| Amazon FBA fees | $7.50 | 30% |
| Amazon referral fee (15%) | $3.75 | 15% |
| Marketing (PPC) | $2.50 | 10% |
| **Total cost** | **$21.09** | **84.4%** |
| **Profit** | **$3.91** | **15.6%** |

In this example, the product appears profitable, but the margin is thin. A small increase in shipping costs or advertising spend could eliminate profit entirely. This is why systematic research and validation are critical.

### 3.2 Landed Cost Calculator

Use this framework to calculate the true landed cost of any product sourced from 1688:

**Step 1: Base product cost**
- 1688 listed price × quantity = Raw product cost
- Account for MOQ pricing tiers

**Step 2: Add domestic costs**
- Domestic shipping within China: ¥5-50 per shipment
- Sourcing agent fee: 3-8% of order value

**Step 3: Add international shipping**
- Sea freight: $0.50-2.00 per kg
- Air freight: $4.00-8.00 per kg

**Step 4: Add import costs**
- Customs duties: 0-25% (varies by product and destination country)
- Import VAT/GST: 5-27% (varies by country)
- Broker fees: $50-150 per shipment

**Step 5: Add platform/fulfillment costs**
- Amazon referral fee: 15%
- Amazon FBA fee: $3-10 per unit (varies by size/weight)
- Shopify payment processing: 2.9% + $0.30

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Define Your Product Criteria

Before searching 1688, establish clear criteria for what you are looking for:

- **Target retail price:** $15-75 (sweet spot for margins after Amazon fees)
- **Target landed cost:** Under 40% of retail price
- **Weight limit:** Under 1kg (keeps shipping costs manageable)
- **Category focus:** Choose 1-2 categories you understand or are willing to learn
- **MOQ preference:** Under 500 units for initial testing
- **Seasonality preference:** Year-round products preferred for beginners

### Step 2: Research Demand on Amazon

Before sourcing from 1688, validate that demand exists on your target platform:

1. Search your product keyword on Amazon
2. Analyze the top 20 results: review counts, pricing, monthly revenue estimates
3. Check Amazon BSR in the product's main category
4. Use Jungle Scout or Helium 10 to estimate monthly sales volume
5. Note the average selling price and price range

**Demand validation criteria:** At least 5 sellers with 100+ monthly sales and average reviews between 50-500.

### Step 3: Search 1688 for Suppliers

Now search 1688 using the insights from your demand research:

1. Translate your product keywords into Chinese using Google Translate
2. Search on 1688 with the Chinese keywords
3. Filter by "实力商家" (Strength Merchant)
4. Sort by transaction volume
5. Identify 5-10 suppliers that match your product requirements
6. Record pricing, MOQ, and supplier ratings for each

### Step 4: Calculate Landed Costs

For each potential product, calculate the full landed cost:

1. Record the 1688 unit price at your target quantity
2. Add sourcing agent fee (3-8%)
3. Add domestic shipping costs
4. Add international shipping (based on weight and method)
5. Add customs duties and import taxes
6. Add platform fees (Amazon referral + FBA)
7. Compare total landed cost against retail price to verify margin

### Step 5: Validate and Order Samples

For the top 3-5 products from your analysis:

1. Contact suppliers and request samples
2. Order 2-3 units from 2-3 different suppliers per product
3. Evaluate samples against quality criteria
4. Test functionality and compare to listing descriptions
5. Select the winning supplier for each product
6. Place a test order of 50-200 units

### Step 6: Launch and Monitor

After receiving your test inventory:

1. Create optimized Amazon listings with professional photos
2. Launch with PPC advertising to generate initial sales
3. Monitor daily sales velocity and BSR
4. Track actual profit margins vs. projected margins
5. Collect customer reviews to identify improvement opportunities
6. Scale orders for products that meet your margin targets

### 1. How do I know if a product is profitable before ordering from 1688?

Calculate the full landed cost by adding the 1688 product price, sourcing agent fees (3-8%), domestic shipping, international shipping, customs duties, and platform fees. Compare the total landed cost against the average selling price on Amazon. If your landed cost is below 40% of the retail price, the product has strong profit potential. Always calculate margins using the pessimistic scenario (higher shipping costs, lower selling price) to ensure profitability even when things don't go perfectly.

### 2. What are the best product categories for beginners on 1688?

Home organization products, kitchen gadgets, pet accessories, and beauty tools are excellent categories for beginners. These categories have lightweight products (low shipping costs), moderate competition on Amazon, and strong repeat purchase potential. Avoid electronics, children's toys, and products requiring certifications until you have more sourcing experience.

### 3. How many suppliers should I evaluate before choosing one?

Evaluate at least 5-10 suppliers for each product before making a decision. Compare pricing, MOQs, transaction volumes, response rates, and years in operation. Order samples from 2-3 of the most promising suppliers to compare quality side by side. Never commit to a large order based on listing photos alone.

### 4. Can I source profitable products from 1688 with a small budget?

Yes. Many successful sellers start with $500-2,000 in initial capital. Focus on products with low MOQs (50-200 units), lightweight items to minimize shipping costs, and products in the $15-40 retail range where margins are highest relative to investment. A test order of 100-200 units is sufficient to validate demand before scaling.

### 5. What is the biggest mistake people make when researching products on 1688?

The biggest mistake is focusing only on the 1688 product price without calculating the true landed cost. A product that costs $1 on 1688 might cost $8 landed after shipping, duties, and fees. Always calculate the complete cost structure before making sourcing decisions. The second most common mistake is skipping sample orders, which leads to quality issues and returns.

### 6. How do I avoid products that are already oversaturated on Amazon?

Use Jungle Scout or Helium 10 to analyze the number of sellers, average review counts, and revenue distribution. Avoid products where the top seller has 10,000+ reviews or where more than 100 sellers are competing for the same keywords. Look for products with 10-50 active sellers where the average review count is 50-300—this indicates sufficient demand without oversaturation.

### 7. Should I use a sourcing agent or try to buy directly from 1688?

For your first 1-2 products, use a sourcing agent. They handle language barriers, payment processing, supplier communication, quality inspection, and shipping logistics. The 3-8% service fee is worth the reduced risk and time savings. As you gain experience and order volume, you can transition to direct purchasing through Alipay to reduce costs further.

### 8. How do I handle product customization or private labeling on 1688?

Search for "代加工" (contract manufacturing) or "OEM" combined with your product keyword. Contact suppliers directly to discuss custom packaging, branding, and product modifications. Most Strength Merchant suppliers offer private label services for orders of 500+ units. Always request samples of the customized product before placing a full order.
