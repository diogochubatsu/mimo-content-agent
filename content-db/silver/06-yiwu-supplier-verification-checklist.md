Before paying any Yiwu supplier, use a clear verification checklist to confirm who you are buying from, what stock they really have, how payment should work, and whether the products match your import requirements. This 10-point checklist is your protection against supplier scams, quality failures, and costly mistakes. Every step is designed to reduce risk before you commit your money.

## Why Supplier Verification Matters in Yiwu Market

Unlike buying from one large factory, Yiwu buyers may place orders with 20, 50, or even more than 100 suppliers during one sourcing trip. Without a proper verification process, small mistakes can quickly become expensive.

### The Scale of the Problem

- **75,000+ shops** operate in Yiwu International Trade Market
- **Not all suppliers are legitimate** factories or manufacturers
- **Quality varies dramatically** between suppliers for similar products
- **Scam risk increases** when buyers rush to close deals
- **Language barriers** make verification more challenging for overseas buyers

## Yiwu Market Deposit vs Factory Deposit

Understanding the difference between market deposits and factory deposits is critical for managing your cash flow and risk.

| Item | Yiwu Market Supplier | Factory Supplier |
|------|---------------------|------------------|
| Common product type | Stock or semi-stock items | Made-to-order production |
| Typical first payment | RMB 500–1,000 reservation deposit | Often 30% production deposit |
| Buying style | Mixed items from many suppliers | One product from one factory |
| Main purpose of deposit | Hold stock before delivery plan | Start production |
| Risk level | Lower (products already exist) | Higher (products not yet made) |
| Negotiation room | Limited (stock pricing) | More flexible (volume-based) |
| Lead time | 3–7 days for stock items | 15–45 days for production |
| Quality control | Verify sample from stock | Approve sample before production |

## 10-Point Verification Checklist

### 1. Confirm the Supplier's Real Business Identity

Ask for booth number, company name, contact person, phone number, WeChat, and business license.

**What to verify**:
- Booth number matches the location in Yiwu Market
- Company name is consistent across all documents
- Contact person is a real employee (verify via video call)
- Phone number is a Chinese mobile number (not a virtual number)
- WeChat account shows real activity and history
- Business license is current and matches company name

**Verification methods**:
- Visit the booth in person during market hours
- Use the Yiwu Market directory to confirm booth location
- Search the National Enterprise Credit Information system (gsxt.gov.cn)
- Request a video call to confirm the contact person

### 2. Check Whether the Product Is Stock, Semi-Stock, or Custom-Made

This affects deposit amount, delivery time, inspection timing, and risk level.

| Product Type | Characteristics | Risk Level | Deposit Expectation |
|-------------|----------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Ready stock | Already produced, stored in warehouse | Low | RMB 500–1,000 |
| Semi-stock | Basic product ready, customization pending | Medium | 20–30% of order value |
| Custom-made | Produced to your specifications | High | 30–50% of order value |
| OEM/ODM | Full custom development | Very high | 30–50% of order value |

**Key question**: "Is this product ready in your warehouse, or do you need to produce it after I order?"

### 3. Verify the Sample and Bulk Goods Standard

Ask whether bulk goods will be exactly the same as the sample. This is one of the most common sources of disputes.

**Verification steps**:
1. Request a physical sample before ordering
2. Pay for the sample (free samples may not represent actual quality)
3. Document sample specifications (photos, measurements, weight)
4. Ask for written confirmation that bulk goods match sample
5. Include sample-matching clause in your purchase agreement
6. Plan pre-shipment inspection to verify bulk quality

### 4. Confirm the Real MOQ and Mixed Color Rules

MOQ may be per item, per color, per carton, or per design. Understanding the exact MOQ structure prevents surprises.

**Questions to ask**:
- What is the MOQ for this specific item?
- Is MOQ per piece, per color, per design, or per carton?
- How many pieces come in one carton?
- Can I mix colors or designs to meet MOQ?
- What is the MOQ for adding my logo or custom packaging?
- Is there a different MOQ for stock items vs. custom orders?

**Common MOQ structures**:

| MOQ Type | Example | What It Means |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| Per item | 100 pieces | Minimum 100 units of one item |
| Per color | 50 pieces per color | Minimum 50 units in each color |
| Per carton | 5 cartons | Minimum order is 5 cartons |
| Per design | 200 pieces per design | Minimum 200 units of each design |
| Mixed MOQ | 100 pieces, 3 colors min | Minimum 300 total pieces |

### 5. Confirm Price, Currency, and What Is Included

Ask whether price includes inner packing, export carton, delivery, logo printing, barcode sticker.

**Price breakdown checklist**:

| Cost Element | Included? | Notes |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| Product unit price | ☐ | Base price per unit |
| Inner packaging | ☐ | Individual product packaging |
| Export carton | ☐ | Outer shipping carton |
| Logo printing | ☐ | Your brand on product/packaging |
| Barcode sticker | ☐ | UPC/EAN barcode |
| Product testing | ☐ | Safety/compliance testing |
| Loading charges | ☐ | Cost to load onto truck/container |
| Delivery to warehouse | ☐ | Transport to your agent's warehouse |

**Currency considerations**:
- Most Yiwu suppliers quote in RMB (Chinese Yuan)
- Some quote in USD for international buyers
- Exchange rate fluctuations can affect your costs
- Confirm whether the quoted price is fixed or subject to change

### 6. Confirm Delivery Time to Warehouse

Ask for estimated delivery date to your Yiwu agent's warehouse.

**Delivery time expectations**:

| Product Type | Typical Lead Time | Factors Affecting Lead Time |
|-------------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Ready stock | 3–7 days | Warehouse location, stock availability |
| Semi-stock | 7–15 days | Customization scope, stock levels |
| Custom production | 15–45 days | Order complexity, factory capacity |
| Large volume | 30–60 days | Production scheduling, material sourcing |

**Questions to ask**:
- When can you deliver to the warehouse?
- What happens if you cannot deliver on time?
- Is there a penalty for late delivery?
- Can I get a written delivery guarantee?

### 7. Verify the Supplier's Payment Method

Confirm who will receive the payment and whether bank account matches business information.

**Payment verification steps**:
1. Request corporate bank account details (not personal)
2. Verify account holder name matches company name
3. Confirm bank is in the same city as registered address
4. For international transfers, verify SWIFT code
5. Use escrow or Trade Assurance when possible
6. Never pay to a third party or personal account

**Payment methods and risk levels**:

| Payment Method | Risk Level | Buyer Protection |
|---------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Alibaba Trade Assurance | Low | Escrow protection |
| Letter of L/C | Low | Bank guarantee |
| Escrow service | Low | Third-party holding |
| Corporate T/T | Medium | Some traceability |
| Personal T/T | High | Minimal protection |
| WeChat/Alipay | High | No international protection |

### 8. Confirm Packaging Requirements Before Ordering

Packaging mistakes are one of the most common problems during consolidation. When multiple suppliers provide different packaging standards, consolidation becomes chaotic and expensive.

**Packaging specification checklist**:
- ☐ Retail packaging design approved
- ☐ Inner packaging materials specified
- ☐ Export carton dimensions confirmed
- ☐ Carton strength/strength grade specified
- ☐ Shipping marks and labels defined
- ☐ Barcode requirements confirmed
- ☐ Country of origin labeling confirmed
- ☐ Warning labels included (if applicable)

### 9. Plan Product Inspection Before Paying the Final Balance

Arrange inspection before paying remaining balance. This is your last chance to catch quality issues before goods leave China.

**Inspection timing**:
- **Pre-production**: Verify sample before bulk production starts
- **During production**: Monitor progress for large orders
- **Pre-shipment**: Final inspection before balance payment and shipping

**Inspection cost vs. risk**:

| Inspection Type | Cost | When to Use |
|----------------|------|------------|
| Self-inspection | $0 | Small orders, experienced buyers |
| Agent inspection | $100–200 | Most orders |
| Third-party inspection | $150–300 | Large orders, critical products |

### 10. Keep Complete Order Records

Create one folder for each supplier with all documentation. This protects you in case of disputes and helps you track your sourcing history.

**Documentation to maintain**:
- Business license copy
- Bank account details
- Product specifications and photos
- Sample approval records
- Purchase agreement/contract
- Payment receipts
- Communication records (WeChat, email)
- Inspection reports
- Shipping documents
- Quality certification copies

## Final Verification Checklist

## FAQ

### How long should I spend on supplier verification?
For a first-time order, spend 1–2 weeks on verification. This includes business license checks (1–2 days), video calls (1–2 days), sample ordering and evaluation (7–14 days), and documentation review (1–2 days). The time investment is minimal compared to the risk.

### Can I skip verification for small orders?
Even for small orders, basic verification is essential. At minimum, verify the business license and conduct a video call. The cost of losing $500 on a scam is the same as losing $5,000 — it's the percentage loss that hurts.

### What if the supplier refuses to provide their business license?
Refusal to provide a business license is a deal-breaker. Never order from a supplier who cannot prove their legal identity. This is the most basic verification step.

### Should I pay a deposit before or after receiving samples?
For stock items, you can pay after receiving samples. For custom production, you typically need to pay a deposit before production begins. Always pay samples separately and never combine sample payment with production deposit.

### How do I handle multiple suppliers in one order?
Maintain separate verification records for each supplier. Use a sourcing agent to manage multiple suppliers simultaneously. Consolidate orders at a single warehouse for efficient shipping.

### What is the best payment method for Yiwu suppliers?
For first-time orders, use Alibaba Trade Assurance or escrow services. For repeat orders from verified suppliers, corporate T/T is acceptable. Never use personal T/T, WeChat Pay, or Alipay for international transactions.

### Can I negotiate verification requirements?
Some verification steps are non-negotiable (business license, bank account verification). Others can be adjusted based on order size and risk level. For small orders, you may reduce the scope, but never eliminate verification entirely.

### What happens if I find issues during verification?
If you discover problems during verification, address them before placing the order. Common issues can often be resolved through negotiation. If the supplier cannot resolve fundamental issues (invalid license, personal bank account), find an alternative supplier.

### Should I document everything in writing?
Yes. Always get important commitments in writing (WeChat messages, email, or formal contracts). Verbal agreements are difficult to enforce in cross-border transactions.

### How do I verify a supplier I found on 1688?
1688 verification follows the same principles as Yiwu supplier verification. Check the business license, verify the bank account, request samples, and conduct video calls. Use a sourcing agent who can visit the supplier in person.

### Is it worth hiring a lawyer for verification?
For orders over $50,000 or complex OEM/ODM projects, hiring a Chinese lawyer for contract review and verification is worthwhile. For standard wholesale orders, the verification steps in this checklist are sufficient.
