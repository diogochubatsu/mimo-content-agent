## Section 1: What Is 1688 and Why Source from It

### 1.1 Understanding the 1688 Platform

1688.com is the world's largest B2B e-commerce platform by transaction volume. Unlike Alibaba.com, which targets international buyers, 1688 was built for domestic Chinese businesses. This means prices reflect wholesale costs without the export markup that international platforms add.

**Platform statistics (2026):**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Annual transaction volume | $100+ billion |
| Registered suppliers | 100+ million |
| Product categories | 40+ |
| Daily active buyers | 150+ million |
| Average price advantage vs. Alibaba.com | 30-50% lower |
| Average price advantage vs. AliExpress | 50-70% lower |

### 1.2 Why Prices Are Lower on 1688

The price difference exists because 1688 operates differently from international platforms:

1. **No export markup** — Suppliers list domestic wholesale prices, not export prices with built-in agent commissions
2. **Lower platform fees** — 1688 charges sellers lower commission rates than Alibaba.com
3. **Direct factory access** — Many manufacturers sell directly rather than through trading companies
4. **Competitive domestic market** — Chinese businesses negotiate aggressively, driving prices down
5. **No marketing premium** — Suppliers don't invest in international marketing or English-language product pages

### 1.3 Who Should Source from 1688

1688 sourcing is ideal for:

- **E-commerce sellers** looking to reduce product costs on Amazon, Shopify, or other platforms
- **Retail store owners** seeking wholesale inventory at competitive prices
- **Private label brands** wanting factory-direct manufacturing at lower costs
- **Dropshippers** willing to handle initial bulk orders for better margins
- **Sourcing agents** who purchase on behalf of international clients

**Not ideal for:** First-time importers with no China sourcing experience (start with Alibaba.com first), buyers needing English-language support, or orders under $100 (AliExpress is simpler for small purchases).

## Section 2: Account Setup and Registration

### 2.1 Registration Options for International Buyers

International buyers have three primary options for accessing 1688:

**Option 1: Direct Registration (Chinese ID Required)**

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Chinese mobile number | Required for SMS verification |
| Chinese ID or passport | Identity verification |
| Alipay account | Mandatory for payments |
| Chinese bank account | Recommended for full functionality |

This option is only practical for buyers with a Chinese business partner or residence.

**Option 2: International Registration (Passport)**

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| International phone number | For SMS verification |
| Passport | For identity verification |
| Alipay International | Link an international credit card |
| Transaction limits | Lower limits than Chinese accounts |

Step-by-step for international registration:

1. Download the Alipay app or visit alipay.com
2. Select "Register" and choose your country/region
3. Enter your international mobile phone number
4. Verify your identity with a passport photo
5. Link an international credit or debit card
6. Complete facial verification if prompted
7. Navigate to 1688.com and log in with your Alipay credentials

**Option 3: Use a Sourcing Agent (Recommended for Beginners)**

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| No 1688 account needed | Agent purchases on your behalf |
| Service fee | 3-8% of order value |
| Language barrier eliminated | Agent handles Chinese communication |
| Full service | Product search, negotiation, QC, shipping |

This is the most common approach for international buyers. Popular sourcing agents include Superbuy, Cssbuy, Pandabuy, and dedicated B2B agents found on platforms like Fiverr and Upwork.

### 2.2 Navigating the 1688 Interface

Once logged in, the 1688 interface is entirely in Simplified Chinese. Key sections include:

- **搜索栏 (Search bar)** — Enter product keywords in Chinese for best results
- **分类 (Categories)** — Browse by product category
- **供应商 (Suppliers)** — Filter by supplier type and location
- **买家保障 (Buyer protection)** — View platform protection policies
- **我的1688 (My 1688)** — Your account dashboard, orders, and messages

**Pro tip:** Use Google Translate's camera feature to translate the 1688 interface in real time. Alternatively, use Chrome's built-in translation feature to auto-translate the entire page.

## Section 3: Product Research Strategies

### 3.1 How to Search for Products

Effective product research on 1688 requires using the right search terms. Since the platform is in Chinese, you need to translate your product keywords into Mandarin.

**Search strategies:**

1. **Use Chinese keywords** — Translate your product name into Chinese (e.g., "wireless earbuds" becomes "无线蓝牙耳机")
2. **Use category browsing** — Navigate through category hierarchies to discover related products
3. **Sort by sales volume** — High-volume sellers typically offer competitive prices and reliable service
4. **Filter by "实力商家" (Strength Merchant)** — These are verified, high-volume suppliers
5. **Use image search** — Upload a photo of a product you want to find on 1688

### 3.2 Evaluating Product Listings

Not all 1688 listings are equal. Here is what to look for:

**Listing quality indicators:**

| Indicator | What It Means | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|
| 多年老店 (Years-old shop) | Established seller, 3+ years | New shop (under 6 months) |
| 48小时发货 (48-hour dispatch) | Fast processing | No dispatch timeline stated |
| 7天无理由退货 (7-day return) | Buyer protection available | No return policy |
| 实力商家 (Strength merchant) | Verified by 1688 | No verification badge |
| 买家评价 (Buyer reviews) | Real buyer feedback | Few or no reviews |
| 成交量 (Transaction volume) | Sales history | Zero or very low volume |

### 3.3 Product Research Tools

Several tools can enhance your 1688 product research:

- **1688 image search** — Upload a reference photo to find identical or similar products
- **Google Translate** — For translating product descriptions and specifications
- **AliPrice** — A browser extension that shows 1688 prices alongside AliExpress listings
- **Superbuy/Cssbuy search** — Sourcing agent platforms often have built-in 1688 search
- **Chinese keyword tools** — Baidu Keyword Planner or Google Translate for term research

### 4.1 Supplier Tier System on 1688

1688 uses a tier system to classify suppliers based on their business credentials:

| Tier | Name | Requirements | Reliability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 金牌供应商 (Gold Supplier) | Business license, trade history, verified factory | Highest |
| Tier 2 | 实力商家 (Strength Merchant) | Verified operations, high transaction volume | High |
| Tier 3 | 诚信通 (Trust Pass) | Basic business verification | Medium |
| Tier 4 | Regular supplier | Basic registration only | Variable |

### 4.2 Supplier Evaluation Checklist

Before placing any order, evaluate potential suppliers using this checklist:

**Business verification:**
- [ ] Valid Chinese business license (营业执照)
- [ ] Registered for 2+ years
- [ ] Gold Supplier or Strength Merchant badge
- [ ] Verified factory address
- [ ] Consistent product focus (not a generalist)

**Product quality indicators:**
- [ ] Detailed product specifications provided
- [ ] Multiple product photos (real, not stock)
- [ ] Video of manufacturing process available
- [ ] Sample ordering available
- [ ] Clear return/refund policy

**Communication and service:**
- [ ] Responsive to messages (within 24 hours)
- [ ] Professional product descriptions
- [ ] Willing to provide certifications
- [ ] No history of buyer complaints
- [ ] Clear shipping and handling terms

### 4.3 Using Tianyancha for Business Verification

Tianyancha (天眼查) is China's equivalent of a business registry. Use it to verify:

1. Search the supplier's company name on tianyancha.com
2. Check the registered capital and founding date
3. Verify the business scope matches their product claims
4. Look for any legal disputes or negative records
5. Confirm the registered address matches what the supplier claims

## Section 6: Sampling and Quality Control

### 6.1 Why Sampling Is Non-Negotiable

Never place a bulk order on 1688 without first ordering samples. Samples allow you to:

- Verify product quality matches the listing description
- Test functionality and durability
- Compare multiple suppliers side by side
- Identify potential issues before committing to large quantities
- Build a quality benchmark for future orders

### 6.2 Sample Ordering Process

**Step 1:** Contact the supplier through 1688 messaging or WeChat
**Step 2:** Request a sample of your desired product (specify quantity, color, size)
**Step 3:** Pay for the sample (typically ¥20-200 per sample + shipping)
**Step 4:** Arrange sample shipping via agent or direct international shipping
**Step 5:** Evaluate the sample against your quality standards
**Step 6:** Document any issues and communicate with the supplier
**Step 7:** Order additional samples if the first batch has quality concerns

### 6.3 Quality Control During Production

For large orders (500+ units), consider these QC approaches:

**Third-party inspection services:**
- **SGS** — Global inspection leader, offices throughout China
- **Bureau Veritas** — Comprehensive quality testing
- **TÜV** — German standard inspections
- **Local QC agents** — Independent inspectors available through sourcing agents

**Inspection stages:**

| Stage | Timing | What to Check |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-production | Before manufacturing begins | Materials, molds, specifications |
| During production | When 20-30% is completed | Production quality, consistency |
| Pre-shipment | Before goods leave factory | Final quality, quantity, packaging |
| Loading supervision | During container loading | Proper packing, no damage |

## Section 8: Shipping and Logistics

### 8.1 Shipping Methods Comparison

| Method | Transit Time | Cost per kg | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| ePacket | 7-15 days | $3-5 | Small packages under 2kg |
| China Post Registered | 15-30 days | $2-4 | Budget small parcels |
| DHL/FedEx/UPS | 3-7 days | $8-15 | Urgent, high-value items |
| Sea freight (FCL) | 20-40 days | $0.50-2 | Large bulk orders |
| Sea freight (LCL) | 25-45 days | $2-5 | Medium bulk orders |
| Air freight | 5-10 days | $4-8 | Time-sensitive bulk orders |
| Rail freight (EU) | 14-20 days | $2-4 | Europe-bound bulk orders |

### 8.2 Working with Freight Forwarders

For international shipments, a freight forwarder can:

- Consolidate shipments from multiple suppliers
- Handle customs documentation
- Optimize shipping routes for cost savings
- Provide warehousing before export
- Manage insurance and claims

**Recommended approach:** Use your sourcing agent's recommended freight forwarder for your first few shipments, then explore independent forwarders as your volume grows.

### 8.3 Incoterms to Know

When negotiating with suppliers, understand these common Incoterms:

- **FOB (Free on Board)** — Supplier delivers goods to the port of departure; you cover sea freight and insurance
- **EXW (Ex Works)** — You pick up goods from the factory; you cover all shipping costs
- **CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight)** — Supplier covers cost, insurance, and freight to the destination port
- **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** — Supplier covers everything including customs duties

**For 1688 sourcing:** FOB is the most common and recommended term for international buyers.

## FAQ

### 1. Can I buy from 1688 without knowing Chinese?

Yes, but you will need assistance. Options include using Google Translate or Chrome's auto-translate feature for basic browsing, hiring a sourcing agent who handles all Chinese communication, or learning basic Chinese keywords for product searching. Most successful international buyers use a sourcing agent.

### 2. What is the minimum order quantity on 1688?

MOQs on 1688 vary widely by supplier and product. Many suppliers offer sample quantities (1-5 units) at a premium price. Small wholesale orders (10-50 units) are available from most suppliers. The best prices typically start at 100+ units.

### 3. Is it safe to buy from 1688 as an international buyer?

1688 is safe when you take proper precautions: verify suppliers through the Gold Supplier program, order samples before bulk purchases, use escrow payment methods, and work with reputable sourcing agents. Never pay outside established channels without verification.

### 4. How do I handle returns or disputes on 1688?

Returns and disputes on 1688 must be handled through the platform's Chinese-language dispute system. Most international buyers rely on their sourcing agent to mediate disputes. For payments made through Alipay, buyer protection covers most scenarios if you file within the allowed timeframe.

### 5. What products should I avoid sourcing from 1688?

Avoid products that require strict regulatory compliance without proper certifications (electronics, medical devices, children's toys), branded products that may be counterfeit, extremely heavy or bulky items where shipping costs negate savings, and perishable goods.

### 6. How long does shipping from 1688 to international destinations take?

Shipping times depend on the method: ePacket takes 7-15 days, express courier (DHL/FedEx) takes 3-7 days, air freight takes 5-10 days, and sea freight takes 20-45 days. For most international buyers, sea freight offers the best cost-to-time ratio for bulk orders.

### 7. Do I need to pay Chinese import taxes when buying from 1688?

You do not pay Chinese export taxes, but you will need to pay import duties and taxes in your destination country. These vary by product type, value, and country. Research your country's tariff schedule before placing orders to calculate total landed cost.

### 8. Can I use 1688 for dropshipping?

While technically possible, 1688 is not ideal for direct dropshipping because most suppliers do not ship internationally and the platform lacks automated order fulfillment. However, you can use 1688 to source products in bulk and ship them to a fulfillment center for dropshipping operations.

### 9. What is the difference between 1688 and Alibaba.com?

1688 is Alibaba's domestic Chinese B2B platform with Chinese-language interface, Alipay-only payments, and lower prices. Alibaba.com is the international B2B platform with English interface, multiple payment options, Trade Assurance protection, and higher prices due to export markups.

### 10. How do I find private label manufacturers on 1688?

Search for "代加工" (contract manufacturing) or "OEM" combined with your product keyword. Filter by "实力商家" (Strength Merchant) badge and look for suppliers offering customization services. Always verify factory capabilities through video calls and samples.
