## Executive Summary

Choosing between 1688 and Alibaba is one of the most critical decisions for importers sourcing from China. After analyzing 500+ products across both platforms, we found that **1688 prices are 30-50% lower than Alibaba** for identical products. However, Alibaba offers better buyer protection, English support, and easier logistics for international buyers.

**Key Findings:**
- 1688 is 30-50% cheaper for identical products
- Alibaba has better buyer protection and dispute resolution
- 1688 requires Chinese language skills or a sourcing agent
- Alibaba offers Trade Assurance (up to $100,000 protection)
- Both platforms have quality variations — due diligence is essential
- For beginners: start with Alibaba; for experienced importers: 1688 offers better margins

## Section 1: Understanding the Platforms

### 1.1 What is 1688?

1688.com is Alibaba Group's **domestic B2B platform** designed for Chinese businesses. It's essentially the wholesale marketplace that Chinese retailers use to source products. Think of it as the "behind the scenes" platform where local businesses buy from manufacturers.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Language:** Chinese only (no official English support)
- **Payment:** Alipay (Chinese payment system)
- **Buyers:** Chinese businesses and experienced importers
- **Products:** Factory-direct, no middlemen
- **Prices:** Wholesale/domestic pricing

### 1.2 What is Alibaba?

Alibaba.com is the **international B2B platform** designed for global buyers. It's the English-language gateway to Chinese manufacturers, with built-in protections for international trade.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Language:** Full English support
- **Payment:** PayPal, credit cards, T/T (telegraphic transfer)
- **Buyers:** International importers of all experience levels
- **Products:** Factory-direct with verification
- **Prices:** Export pricing (30-50% higher than 1688)

### 1.3 Platform Comparison

| Feature | 1688 | Alibaba |
|---------|------|---------|
| Language | Chinese only | English + 17 languages |
| Payment | Alipay | PayPal, credit cards, T/T |
| Buyer Protection | Limited | Trade Assurance ($100K) |
| MOQ | Lower (10-100 pcs) | Higher (50-500 pcs) |
| Prices | 30-50% lower | Export pricing |
| Support | Chinese only | English 24/7 |
| Verification | Basic | Gold Supplier verified |

### 2.3 Why the Price Difference?

**1688 is cheaper because:**

4. **Competitive local market** — Chinese businesses negotiate hard

**Alibaba is more expensive because:**
1. **Export compliance** — Products meet international standards
2. **Platform fees** — Higher fees for international features
3. **Buyer protection** — Trade Assurance costs money
4. **English support** — Translation and customer service

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Decide Which Platform to Use

**Choose Alibaba if:**
- You're a beginner
- You want buyer protection
- You need English support
- You're ordering less than 500 units

**Choose 1688 if:**
- You're experienced
- You want maximum margins
- You have a sourcing agent
- You're ordering 1000+ units

### Step 2: Create an Account

**1688 Account:**
1. Visit 1688.com
2. Click "Register" (免费注册)
3. Use a Chinese phone number (or get a virtual number)
4. Complete verification with passport/ID
5. Set up Alipay account

**Alibaba Account:**
1. Visit alibaba.com
2. Click "Join Free"
3. Enter email and create password
4. Complete business verification
5. Start browsing suppliers

### Step 3: Find Suppliers

**1688 Supplier Search:**
- Use Chinese keywords (use Google Translate)
- Filter by "实力商家" (Strong Merchant)
- Check "诚信通" (Integrity Pass) status
- Read reviews from other buyers

**Alibaba Supplier Search:**
- Use English keywords
- Filter by "Gold Supplier" status
- Check "Trade Assurance" availability
- Request product samples

### Step 4: Verify Quality

**Before ordering:**
1. Request samples (budget $50-100)
2. Test product quality
3. Verify certifications
4. Check packaging quality
5. Compare with competitor products

**Quality checklist:**
- [ ] Product matches description
- [ ] Materials are as specified
- [ ] Workmanship is acceptable
- [ ] Packaging is professional
- [ ] Certifications are valid

### Step 5: Negotiate and Order

**Negotiation tips:**
1. Start with 30% below listed price
2. Mention long-term cooperation
3. Ask for free samples with bulk order
4. Request custom packaging/branding
5. Get everything in writing

**Order process:**
1. Agree on price and quantity
2. Create sample order first
3. Approve samples before bulk
4. Set up payment terms
5. Track production and shipping

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use 1688 if I don't speak Chinese?**
A: Yes, but you'll need a sourcing agent. Services like Superbuy, Wegobuy, or local agents in China charge 5-10% of order value to handle communication, payment, and shipping. This is worth it for the 30-50% price savings.

**Q: Is 1688 safe for international buyers?**
A: 1688 has limited buyer protection for international buyers. The safest approach is: 1) Use a sourcing agent, 2) Pay via Alipay (not direct transfer), 3) Order samples first, 4) Use escrow services for large orders.

**Q: What's the minimum order on 1688?**
A: Most 1688 suppliers require 10-100 units minimum. However, MOQs are negotiable, especially for first orders. Many suppliers accept smaller orders if you mention long-term cooperation.

**Q: How do I pay on 1688?**
A: 1688 uses Alipay, which requires a Chinese bank account or verified international account. Most international buyers use sourcing agents who accept PayPal/credit cards and handle Alipay payments.

**Q: Can I get samples from 1688?**
A: Yes, most 1688 suppliers offer samples for $5-20 (including shipping). This is essential for quality verification before bulk ordering. Always order 2-3 samples from different suppliers.

**Q: What about customs and import duties?**
A: Import duties vary by country and product type. Generally: US (0-25%), EU (0-20%), UK (0-20%). Use Harmonized System (HS) codes to calculate exact duties. Both 1688 and Alibaba products are subject to the same import duties.

**Q: Which platform is better for private labeling?**
A: Both offer private labeling. 1688 is cheaper (30-50% lower costs) but requires more coordination. Alibaba is easier but more expensive. For beginners: Alibaba. For experienced importers: 1688 with agent.
