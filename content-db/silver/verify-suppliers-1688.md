## Section 1: Why Supplier Verification Matters

### 1.1 The Scale of the Problem

1688.com is the world's largest B2B marketplace by volume, processing over $100 billion in transactions annually. However, this massive scale creates opportunities for fraudulent suppliers to hide among legitimate businesses.

**Fraud Statistics on 1688:**
- 12-18% of unverified suppliers have reported scam incidents
- Average loss per fraud incident: $2,500-$8,000
- 40% of first-time importers encounter at least one problematic supplier
- Only 25% of fraud victims recover their money

### 1.2 Common Types of 1688 Supplier Fraud

**Type 1: The Middleman Pretending to Be a Factory**
- Lists factory prices but adds 20-40% markup
- Uses stolen factory photos and videos
- Cannot provide production line access

**Type 2: The Sample Bait-and-Switch**
- Sends perfect samples that don't match bulk production
- Uses substandard materials in mass production
- Ships lower grade than what was approved

**Type 3: The Payment Disappearing Act**
- Accepts payment then goes silent
- Ships partial orders or nothing at all
- Creates new accounts after being flagged

**Type 4: The Quality Fraud**
- Misrepresents product specifications
- Uses counterfeit certifications
- Ships dangerous or non-compliant products

Understanding why 1688 requires extra caution helps frame the verification process:

## Section 2: Understanding 1688 Supplier Tiers

### 2.1 Supplier Badge System

1688 uses a badge system to indicate supplier trustworthiness. Understanding these badges is the first step in verification.

**[Screenshot Description: 1688 Supplier Profile Badge Area]**
Look at the top-right corner of any 1688 supplier page. You will see colored badges and text indicators. The most important ones are:

**Gold Supplier (金牌供应商) - Yellow Badge**
- Indicates annual revenue above ¥500,000 ($70,000)
- Paid annual membership fee to 1688 (¥6,688 or $940)
- Has been operating for 1+ years
- Passes basic business verification
- **Trust Level: Medium-High**

**Verified Manufacturer (认证工厂) - Green Badge**
- Has been verified as an actual factory
- Third-party audit conducted
- Physical premises inspected
- Production capacity confirmed
- **Trust Level: High**

**Super Supplier (超级供应商) - Red Badge**
- Top 5% of suppliers in their category
- Consistent high ratings over 12+ months
- Low dispute rate (under 0.5%)
- Fast shipping and good communication
- **Trust Level: Very High**

**New Supplier (新供应商) - Blue Badge**
- Operating for less than 1 year
- Limited transaction history
- Not yet fully evaluated
- **Trust Level: Low - Proceed with caution**

### 2.2 Rating Score Breakdown

**[Screenshot Description: Supplier Rating Section]**
Below the product listings, scroll to find the supplier's rating panel. Look for these specific metrics:

**Overall Rating (综合评分)**
- Scale: 1-5 diamonds (not stars like Alibaba)
- 5 diamonds = Excellent (top 10%)
- 4 diamonds = Good (top 30%)
- 3 diamonds = Average
- 2 diamonds = Below average
- 1 diamond = Poor (avoid)

**Sub-Ratings to Check:**
1. **Product Quality (产品质量)** - Most important metric
2. **Delivery Speed (发货速度)** - Affects your timeline
3. **Service Attitude (服务态度)** - Communication quality
4. **Shipping Accuracy (物流准确)** - Order fulfillment rate

**[Screenshot Description: Transaction Data Panel]**
Look for the "交易数据" (Transaction Data) section showing:
- Total transactions in past 30 days
- Number of repeat customers
- Average order value
- Dispute rate percentage

### 2.3 Membership Duration Analysis

**[Screenshot Description: Company Information Section]**
Scroll to "公司信息" (Company Information) to find:

- **注册时间 (Registration Date):** How long the company has existed
- **诚信通年限 (Chengxintong Years):** How long they've been a paying诚信通 member
- **年销售额 (Annual Sales):** Revenue range

**Rule of Thumb:**
- Less than 1 year: High risk, verify extensively
- 1-3 years: Moderate risk, standard verification
- 3-5 years: Low risk, basic verification
- 5+ years: Very low risk, still verify key points

## Section 3: The 10-Step Verification Process

### Step 1: Basic Profile Review (5 minutes)

**What to check:**
1. Company name consistency across the profile
2. Registration date and 诚信通 membership duration
3. Location matches product category (Shenzhen for electronics, Yiwu for small goods)
4. Number of products listed (too few = possible middleman)
5. Profile photo quality (professional = good investment in business)

**[Screenshot Description: Profile Header]**
At the very top of the supplier page, you'll see:
- Company name in large text (company-name class element)
- Badge icons to the right
- "X years" membership indicator
- Location with province and city

**Red Flags at This Stage:**
- Company name doesn't match across sections
- Located far from product manufacturing hub
- Very new membership with high claims
- No physical address listed

### Step 2: Product Listing Quality Analysis (10 minutes)

**What to look for in product listings:**

1. **Original Photos vs Stock Images**
   - Zoom in on product photos
   - Look for watermarks from other websites
   - Check if photos appear on multiple supplier pages
   - Original factory photos often have simple backgrounds and slight imperfections

2. **Description Depth**
   - Detailed specifications table
   - Multiple angles and close-ups
   - Real usage scenarios
   - Size charts and comparison photos

3. **Pricing Structure**
   - Clear tiered pricing (quantity discounts)
   - Realistic price range for the category
   - No suspiciously low prices (below material cost)

**[Screenshot Description: Product Listing Quality Indicators]**
Compare these two product listings:

**Good Listing:** Shows factory floor photos, detailed specification table in Chinese, multiple product angles, real customer photos in reviews, tiered pricing with 3+ quantity levels

**Bad Listing:** Stock photos with watermarks, vague description, single image, no reviews, one fixed price regardless of quantity

### Step 3: Review and Rating Analysis (10 minutes)

**How to read 1688 reviews properly:**

1. **Focus on Recent Reviews (最近评价)**
   - Filter by last 30 days
   - Look for patterns in complaints
   - Check if seller responds to negative reviews

2. **Review Translation Strategy**
   - Use browser translation for Chinese reviews
   - Look for keywords: 质量差 (poor quality), 假货 (fake), 推荐 (recommend)
   - Pay attention to photo reviews showing actual products received

3. **Volume vs Quality**
   - 500+ reviews with 4.8+ rating = strong indicator
   - 50 reviews with 4.9 rating = possible manipulation
   - New products with zero reviews = high risk

**[Screenshot Description: Review Section with Filters]**
The review section shows tabs at the top:
- 全部 (All) - Default view
- 好评 (Positive) - 4-5 star reviews
- 中评 (Medium) - 3 star reviews
- 差评 (Negative) - 1-2 star reviews - **ALWAYS check this tab**
- 有图 (With Photos) - Reviews with images

### Step 4: Business License Verification (10 minutes)

**Using Tianyancha (天眼查) for Verification:**

Tianyancha is China's equivalent of a business registry lookup tool. Here's how to use it:

1. Go to tianyancha.com
2. Enter the company name from 1688 (copy exactly from company information section)
3. Check these specific fields:

**[Screenshot Description: Tianyancha Search Results]**
- **统一社会信用代码 (Unified Social Credit Code):** Must match what supplier provides
- **法定代表人 (Legal Representative):** Should match the contact person
- **注册资本 (Registered Capital):** Minimum ¥100,000 for credibility
- **成立日期 (Establishment Date):** Should match 1688 profile
- **经营状态 (Business Status):** Must show "存续" (Active)
- **经营范围 (Business Scope):** Must include the products they sell

**Red Flags:**
- Business status shows "吊销" (Revoked) or "注销" (Cancelled)
- Registered capital under ¥50,000
- Business scope doesn't include their product category
- Multiple legal representative changes in short period

### Step 5: Factory Verification via Video Call (15 minutes)

**How to request and conduct a factory verification video call:**

1. **Request through 1688 chat (旺旺):**
   - Ask in Chinese: "您好，我想看下工厂，请问可以视频吗？" (Hello, I'd like to see the factory, can we do a video call?)
   - Request during Chinese business hours (9am-6pm CST)

2. **What to ask during the call:**
   - Show me the production floor
   - Can you show the machine that makes [specific product]?
   - How many workers are on this line?
   - Show me raw materials storage
   - Can you show me your quality inspection area?

3. **Red Flags during video call:**
   - Refuses to do video call entirely
   - Shows only office, not production area
   - Uses pre-recorded video instead of live
   - Background doesn't match claimed factory size
   - Unable to show specific equipment

**[Screenshot Description: Video Call Interface on 1688]**
The video call feature is accessed through the 旺旺 chat window. Click the video camera icon in the top-right of the chat. The supplier will accept or decline. During the call, request they walk around and show different areas.

### Step 6: Sample Order Process (7-14 days)

**The correct sample order process:**

1. **Order 3-5 samples from the same supplier**
   - Different color variants
   - Different sizes if applicable
   - Note exact specifications on order

2. **Track the sample quality:**
   - Packaging quality
   - Material thickness and feel
   - Print quality and color accuracy
   - Functionality testing

3. **Compare samples to listing photos:**
   - Side-by-side comparison
   - Note any discrepancies
   - Document with photos

**Sample Cost Expectations:**
- Typical sample cost: ¥50-200 ($7-28)
- Refundable upon bulk order (negotiate this upfront)
- Shipping cost: ¥50-150 ($7-21) via express

### Step 7: Communication Assessment (Ongoing)

**Evaluating supplier communication quality:**

1. **Response Time:**
   - Under 1 hour: Excellent
   - 1-4 hours: Good
   - 4-24 hours: Acceptable
   - Over 24 hours: Concerning

2. **Language Ability:**
   - Can they communicate basic English?
   - Do they understand technical specifications?
   - Are they willing to use translation tools?

3. **Problem-Solving Attitude:**
   - How do they handle sample issues?
   - Are they proactive or reactive?
   - Do they offer solutions or make excuses?

### Step 8: Payment Method Safety (5 minutes)

**Safe payment practices for 1688:**

1. **Always use Alipay (支付宝) escrow**
   - Never wire money directly to bank accounts
   - Use the 1688 order system for payment protection

2. **Escrow protection rules:**
   - Payment held until delivery confirmation
   - Dispute window of 15 days after shipping
   - Evidence-based refund process

3. **For large orders (over $5,000):**
   - Consider using 1688's trade assurance equivalent
   - Split payments into milestones
   - Never pay 100% upfront

### Step 9: Documentation Verification (10 minutes)

**Required documents to request from suppliers:**

1. **Business License (营业执照)**
   - High-resolution scan
   - Cross-reference with Tianyancha

2. **Product Certifications:**
   - CE certification (for EU market)
   - FCC certification (for US market)
   - CCC certification (for China market)
   - ISO 9001 quality management

3. **Export License:**
   - Confirms they can legally export
   - Needed for customs clearance

**[Screenshot Description: Document Verification Checklist]**
Create a verification checklist spreadsheet with columns:
- Document name
- Requested (date)
- Received (date)
- Verified against (Tianyancha/other source)
- Status (Pass/Fail/In Progress)

### Step 10: Trial Order and Evaluation (30-60 days)

**The final verification step:**

1. **Place a small trial order (10-50 units)**
   - Test actual production quality vs samples
   - Evaluate shipping time and packaging
   - Check quantity accuracy

2. **Quality inspection process:**
   - Open and inspect 100% of units
   - Document any defects with photos
   - Compare to approved sample

3. **Performance metrics to track:**
   - On-time shipping rate
   - Quality acceptance rate
   - Communication responsiveness
   - Packaging integrity

## Red Flags Checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate any 1688 supplier. If you check more than 3 red flags, consider finding an alternative supplier.

- [ ] No 诚信通 membership
- [ ] Profile photos look stock or generic
- [ ] Product photos appear on other websites
- [ ] Price significantly below market average

- [ ] Refuses video calls entirely
- [ ] Cannot show production facility
- [ ] Response time consistently over 24 hours

- [ ] Demands full payment before production

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Create a Verification Spreadsheet

Before you start researching suppliers, set up a tracking system:

Create a Google Sheets or Excel file with these columns:
- Supplier name (Chinese and English)
- 1688 URL
- Badge type
- Years of membership
- Tianyancha match (Yes/No/Pending)
- Video call completed (Yes/No)
- Sample ordered (Yes/No)
- Sample quality (1-5)
- Communication rating (1-5)
- Overall score
- Decision (Proceed/Caution/Reject)

### Step 2: Initial Screening (Per Supplier)

1. Open the supplier's 1688 page
2. Copy the company name to clipboard
3. Note the badge type and membership years
4. Check if the location matches product category
5. Review their product listing count (minimum 10 products for credibility)
6. Record findings in your spreadsheet

### Step 3: Deep Profile Analysis

1. Scroll to "公司信息" (Company Information)
2. Record: registration date, registered capital, legal representative name
3. Check "交易数据" (Transaction Data) for the past 90 days
4. Note the dispute rate (should be under 2%)
5. Review the rating breakdown by category
6. Screenshot the profile for your records

### Step 4: Tianyancha Verification

1. Go to tianyancha.com
2. Search for the exact company name from 1688
3. Verify: business status, registered capital, business scope
4. Check for any legal disputes or negative records
5. Screenshot the Tianyancha results
6. Compare all data points with 1688 profile

### Step 5: Product-Specific Research

1. Find the specific product you want to order
2. Check the product listing quality (photos, descriptions, specs)
3. Read recent reviews for this specific product
4. Look for photo reviews showing actual received products
5. Note any recurring complaints
6. Compare pricing with 3-5 other suppliers for the same product

### Step 6: Communication Test

1. Open 旺旺 chat with the supplier
2. Send a test message asking about product details
3. Note response time and quality
4. Ask if they can do a video call to see the factory
5. Request any certifications for the specific product
6. Evaluate their professionalism and helpfulness

### Step 7: Sample Order

1. Negotiate sample cost and shipping
2. Request refund policy for samples
3. Order 3-5 samples with different variants
4. Track shipping and delivery time
5. Document sample quality with photos
6. Compare to listing photos and description

### Step 8: Documentation Collection

1. Request business license scan
2. Request product certifications (CE, FCC, etc.)
3. Request export license if needed
4. Verify all documents against Tianyancha
5. Save copies in your verification folder
6. Note any discrepancies

### Step 9: Video Factory Verification

1. Schedule video call during Chinese business hours
2. Prepare a list of questions to ask
3. Ask to see: production line, raw materials, quality inspection
4. Look for consistency with claimed factory size
5. Take screenshots during the call
6. Record your observations

### Step 10: Trial Order and Final Decision

1. Place small trial order (10-50 units)
2. Track production time and communication
3. Inspect 100% of received units
4. Document quality with photos
5. Calculate actual vs promised delivery time
6. Make final decision based on all verification data

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the full verification process take?**
A: The complete 10-step process takes 3-4 hours per supplier. However, steps 1-3 (basic screening) only take 25 minutes and eliminate 50% of risky suppliers. For your first few suppliers, budget 1-2 days to complete verification for 3-5 candidates.

**Q: Is 1688 safe for international buyers?**
A: 1688 is designed for domestic Chinese buyers, so international buyers have limited protection. However, by using the verification process outlined in this guide and always paying through Alipay escrow, you can reduce risk to comparable levels with Alibaba. The key difference is you must do your own due diligence rather than relying on platform protections.

**Q: Can I verify suppliers without speaking Chinese?**
A: Yes, but it's more challenging. Use Google Chrome's built-in translation for 1688 pages. For Tianyancha, use the Google Translate extension. For video calls, prepare questions in advance using translation apps. Many 1688 suppliers have basic English ability through trading companies. Consider hiring a bilingual sourcing agent for your first few verifications.

**Q: What if a supplier refuses a video call?**
A: A refusal to do a video call is a significant red flag. Legitimate factories welcome verification as it builds trust. If they refuse, they are likely a middleman, trading company, or worse. Move on to another supplier. The only exception is if they offer to send a pre-recorded factory tour video AND can answer specific questions about their production process in detail.

**Q: How do I know if the product photos are real?**
A: Use reverse image search tools like TinEye or Google Images to check if photos appear elsewhere. Look for: watermarks from other sites, inconsistent backgrounds, professional stock-style photography, and photos that appear on multiple 1688 listings. Real factory photos often have: simple backgrounds, slight imperfections, workers visible in the background, and consistent lighting across listings.

**Q: What's the minimum order I should place after verification?**

Even after thorough verification, there's always a small risk of quality variation between samples and bulk production. However, even with an agent, you should independently verify key data points like Tianyancha records.

**Q: How often should I re-verify existing suppliers?**
A: Re-verify annually or if you notice: quality changes, communication delays, price increases without explanation, or if the supplier's 1688 profile shows significant changes. For ongoing relationships, maintain quarterly check-ins and annual full verification. Set calendar reminders for re-verification dates.

**Q: What payment method is safest on 1688?**
A: Always use Alipay through the 1688 order system. This provides escrow protection where your payment is held until you confirm delivery. Never: wire money directly to bank accounts, use Western Union, pay through unofficial channels, or pay 100% upfront for large orders. For orders over $5,000, negotiate milestone payments (30% upfront, 70% before shipping).

**Q: Can I get a refund if the supplier scams me?**
