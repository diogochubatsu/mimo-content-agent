## Executive Summary

Air freight from China is the fastest way to get products from Chinese factories to international warehouses, with door-to-door delivery typically completed in 3-7 business days. While air shipping costs 4-8 times more than ocean freight per kilogram, it remains indispensable for high-value goods, time-sensitive inventory, and e-commerce sellers who cannot afford stockouts.

The global air cargo market has stabilized in 2026 following years of disruption. China's three major air cargo hubs — Shanghai Pudong (PVG), Guangzhou Baiyun (CAN), and Shenzhen Bao'an (SZX) — handle over 70% of China's international air freight volume. Fuel surcharges, peak-season demand (October-December), and route congestion are the primary factors influencing pricing throughout the year.

Choosing the right air freight service depends on four key variables: shipment weight, product value, required delivery speed, and destination country. A 50 kg shipment of electronics going to Los Angeles has very different optimal routing than a 500 kg pallet of textiles going to London. Understanding standard air cargo, express courier, and charter options — and when to use each — can save importers thousands of dollars per shipment.

This guide provides a complete breakdown of air freight from China: types of services, real cost data for major routes, transit times, customs requirements, packaging specifications, and a step-by-step process for booking your first air cargo shipment. Whether you're shipping 50 kg or 5,000 kg, you'll find the information needed to make informed decisions and negotiate better rates with freight forwarders.

## Section 1: Air Freight Types Explained

### Subsection 1.1: Standard Air Freight (Airport-to-Airport)

Standard air freight is the backbone of international air cargo. Goods travel on scheduled commercial or cargo airline flights between airports, then move through ground handling and customs before reaching the final destination.

**How It Works:**
1. Pickup from factory or warehouse to origin airport
2. Export customs clearance in China
3. Loading onto scheduled airline flight
4. Flight transit (typically 3-12 hours for most routes)
5. Arrival at destination airport and unloading
6. Import customs clearance at destination
7. Delivery to final warehouse or FBA facility

**Key Specifications:**
- Minimum shipment weight: 45 kg (some airlines accept 21 kg)
- Volumetric weight calculation: Length x Width x Height (cm) / 6,000
- Best rates at 100+ kg volumes
- Consolidated with other shippers on the same flight
- Airway bill (AWB) tracking provided
- Typical transit: 3-7 days (airport-to-airport), 5-12 days (door-to-door)

**Ideal For:**
- B2B shipments between 45 kg and 5,000 kg
- Electronics, machinery, and precision instruments
- Seasonal inventory requiring reliable delivery windows
- Shipments where speed matters but express rates are too high

### Subsection 1.2: Express Courier (DHL, FedEx, UPS, TNT)

Express courier services offer the fastest door-to-door delivery from China, with dedicated networks handling everything from pickup to final-mile delivery. These services include customs brokerage, tracking, and insurance by default.

**Key Specifications:**
- No minimum weight requirement (even 1 kg accepted)
- Volumetric calculation: Length x Width x Height (cm) / 5,000
- Door-to-door delivery in 3-5 business days
- Full tracking with real-time updates
- Customs clearance included in most cases
- Insurance coverage included (up to declared value)

**Best For:**
- Samples and product testing (1-10 kg)
- Urgent replacement parts
- Small e-commerce orders
- High-value, low-weight items

### Subsection 1.3: Air Charter (Full Aircraft)

Air charter involves renting an entire aircraft for a single shipment. This is the most expensive option but offers maximum flexibility for oversized cargo, extreme volume, or emergency situations.

**Key Specifications:**
- Available on narrow-body (B737F, A320F) and wide-body (B747F, B777F, A330F) aircraft
- Full aircraft capacity: 20-120 tonnes depending on aircraft type
- Direct routing without stops
- Custom scheduling and departure times
- Charter brokers coordinate with airlines directly

**When to Consider Charter:**
- Emergency shipments requiring specific departure times
- Cargo too large or heavy for standard consolidated flights
- Remote destinations without regular air cargo service
- Project cargo with tight delivery deadlines (factory relocation, event supplies)

**Typical Charter Rates (2026):**

| A330F | 60-70 tonnes | $450,000-650,000 | $400,000-580,000 |

### Subsection 1.4: Consolidated Air Freight

Consolidated air freight combines multiple small shipments from different shippers into a single larger shipment for better rates. A freight forwarder or consolidator manages the consolidation and deconsolidation at both ends.

**How It Works:**
- Multiple shippers send cargo to a forwarder's warehouse in China
- Cargo is consolidated into one airway bill for lower per-kg rates
- At destination, the consolidated shipment is split and distributed to individual consignees

**Key Specifications:**
- Minimum weight: 21-45 kg (varies by consolidator)
- Rate: 10-20% lower than individual standard air freight
- Transit: 1-2 days longer than direct shipments (consolidation/deconsolidation time)
- Best for: Shippers with irregular or smaller volumes

### Subsection 1.5: Sea-Air Hybrid (Sea-Air)

While not pure air freight, sea-air combines ocean shipping for the first leg (China to a transit hub like Dubai or Singapore) with air freight for the final leg. This offers 30-50% savings over full air freight while being 7-10 days faster than full ocean shipping.

**Key Specifications:**
- Transit time: 12-18 days total
- Cost: 30-50% less than pure air freight
- Ideal for: 200-3,000 kg shipments where 2 weeks is acceptable

## Section 2: Air Freight Costs and Pricing 2026

### Subsection 2.1: Rate Components

Air freight pricing consists of multiple components beyond the base rate per kilogram:

**1. Base Air Freight Rate**
The charge per kilogram for the air transportation itself. This is influenced by:
- Route and distance
- Fuel prices (updated monthly by airlines)
- Current capacity vs. demand
- Shipment weight bracket

**2. Fuel Surcharge (FSC)**
A variable surcharge applied on top of the base rate, calculated as a percentage of the base rate. Updated monthly based on jet fuel prices.
- 2026 average: 15-25% of base rate
- Peak months (Oct-Dec): Can reach 30-35%

**3. Security Surcharge (TSA/RAID)**
Mandatory security screening fee applied to all air cargo.
- China origin: $0.15-0.25 per kg
- Destination country: $0.10-0.20 per kg

**4. Terminal Handling Charge (THC)**
Charges for ground handling at origin and destination airports.
- Origin (China): $80-150 per shipment
- Destination: $100-200 per shipment

**5. Documentation Fee**
Administrative cost for processing shipping documents.
- Typical range: $25-75 per shipment

**6. Pickup/Delivery Charges**
Ground transportation between factory/warehouse and airports.
- China domestic pickup: $50-200 (depends on distance from airport)
- Destination delivery: $50-300 (depends on distance from airport)

**7. Customs Brokerage**
Fee for processing import/export customs clearance.
- China export: $30-80 per shipment
- Destination import: $50-200 per shipment (varies by country complexity)

**Peak Season Surcharges (October-December):**

- Packages exceeding 30 kg per piece: $20-50 per piece surcharge

**Remote Area Surcharges:**
- Express couriers charge extra for non-major-city deliveries

**Dangerous Goods Surcharges:**
- Lithium battery shipments: $0.30-0.80/kg additional

## Section 4: Customs and Documentation

### Subsection 4.1: Required Documents for Air Freight from China

**1. Commercial Invoice**
- Must include: Seller/buyer details, product descriptions, quantities, unit prices, total value, currency, Incoterms
- Must match the packing list exactly
- Required for both Chinese export and destination import

**2. Packing List**
- Detailed list of all items in the shipment
- Includes: item descriptions, quantities, weights (gross and net), dimensions, packaging type
- Used by customs officers for inspection decisions

**3. Airway Bill (AWB)**
- The contract of carriage between shipper and airline
- Issued by the airline or freight forwarder
- Contains: shipper/consignee details, route, weight, volume, charges
- Non-negotiable document (unlike ocean bills of lading)

**4. Certificate of Origin (CO)**
- Proves the goods originate in China
- Required for preferential tariff treatment under trade agreements
- Can be regular CO or preferential CO (Form E, Form F, RCEP Certificate)

**5. Export License (if applicable)**
- Required for controlled goods (certain electronics, dual-use items, military-related)
- Applied through China's Ministry of Commerce (MOFCOM) system

**6. Import License (at destination)**
- Some countries require pre-approval for certain product categories
- US: FDA registration for food/medical devices, FCC for electronics
- EU: CE marking documentation, REACH compliance
- Brazil: ANVISA registration for health products

### Subsection 4.3: Chinese Export Customs Process

**Step 1: Pre-Clearance Documentation**
- Submit electronic declaration through China Customs Single Window
- Upload commercial invoice, packing list, and export license (if required)

**Step 2: Customs Inspection (if selected)**
- Random inspection rate: 3-5% for low-risk goods
- Targeted inspection for: first-time exporters, high-value goods, restricted items

**Step 3: Customs Seal and Release**
- Once cleared, cargo receives customs seal
- Forwarder can transport to airport for loading

**Timeline:** 1-2 business days for standard shipments, same-day possible for experienced exporters with pre-clearance status.

### Subsection 4.4: Import Customs at Destination

**United States:**
- Importer of Record (IOR) required
- Customs bond required (continuous or single-entry)
- ISF (10+2) filing required for ocean, not for air
- Average clearance time: 1-3 business days

**European Union:**
- EORI number required for all importers
- Pre-arrival processing available for express shipments
- Average clearance time: 1-3 business days

**United Kingdom:**
- Customs declaration required since Brexit
- UKCA marking may be required for certain products
- Average clearance time: 1-3 business days

**Brazil:**
- CPF/CNPJ registration required for importers
- Import licenses (LI) required for many product categories
- Average clearance time: 3-7 business days (often longer)

**Australia:**
- Import Permit required for certain goods
- Biosecurity inspections for organic materials
- Average clearance time: 2-4 business days

## Section 5: Packaging and Weight Rules

### Subsection 5.1: Volumetric Weight Calculation

Airlines use volumetric weight to determine charges when cargo takes up significant space relative to its actual weight. The higher of actual or volumetric weight is charged.

**Volumetric Weight Formulas:**
- Standard air freight: Length x Width x Height (cm) / 6,000
- Express courier: Length x Width x Height (cm) / 5,000
- Chargeable weight: Higher of actual weight or volumetric weight

**Example:**
A box measuring 60 cm x 40 cm x 30 cm:
- Volumetric weight (air freight): 60 x 40 x 30 / 6,000 = 12 kg
- If actual weight is 8 kg, chargeable weight = 12 kg
- If actual weight is 15 kg, chargeable weight = 15 kg

### Subsection 5.2: Packaging Requirements for Air Freight

**General Requirements:**
- Corrugated cardboard boxes rated for stacking (minimum 32 ECT)
- Double-walled boxes for items over 10 kg
- Inner cushioning: bubble wrap, foam, or air pillows
- No protruding staples or nails on exterior
- Clear labeling with destination address, weight, and handling instructions

**Specific Requirements:**
- Electronics: Anti-static packaging, moisture barrier bags
- Fragile items: "FRAGILE" marking, corner protectors
- liquids: Sealed containers, absorbent material, UN-approved packaging
- lithium batteries: UN38.3 certification, Class 9 dangerous goods labeling

**Pallet Requirements (for palletized shipments):**
- Standard pallet size: 120 cm x 100 cm (Euro) or 120 cm x 80 cm (China)
- Maximum height: 160 cm (including pallet)
- Maximum weight: 1,000 kg per pallet
- Stretch-wrapped with minimum 3 layers
- Corner boards on all vertical edges

### Subsection 5.3: Weight and Size Limitations

**Standard Air Freight:**
- Maximum piece weight: 80-150 kg (varies by airline)
- Maximum single piece dimensions: 300 cm x 200 cm x 160 cm
- Maximum shipment weight: No limit (for full aircraft or large consolidated shipments)

**Express Courier:**
- Maximum piece weight: 30-70 kg (varies by carrier)
- Maximum single piece dimensions: 270 cm x 120 cm x 100 cm (FedEx), 330 cm x 170 cm x 170 cm (DHL)
- Maximum per-piece value: $50,000 for insurance purposes

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Determine Your Shipping Needs

Before contacting freight forwarders, define these key parameters:

**Required Information:**
- Product type and HS code
- Total weight (in kg) and volume (in CBM)
- Pickup location in China (city/province)
- Delivery address at destination
- Required delivery date
- Any special handling requirements (temperature, fragile, dangerous goods)

**Decision Framework:**
- **Under 45 kg, need it fast:** Express courier (DHL/FedEx)
- **45-500 kg, 5-10 day timeline:** Standard air freight
- **500-5,000 kg, flexible timeline:** Standard air freight or consider sea
- **Over 5,000 kg:** Charter or sea freight (unless time-critical)

### Step 2: Compare Freight Forwarder Quotes

Request quotes from 3-5 forwarders for the best rates. Include these details in your inquiry:

**Quote Request Template:**
- Product description and HS code
- Weight, dimensions, and number of pieces
- Pickup address and delivery address
- Required delivery date
- Incoterms preference (FOB, EXW, DDP)

**Forwarder Types:**
- **Digital forwarders:** Flexport, Freightos, Ship4wd — online platforms with transparent pricing
- **Traditional forwarders:** Local companies with established airline relationships
- **Consolidators:** Specialize in shared shipments for better rates on smaller volumes

### Step 3: Prepare Shipping Documents

Gather and prepare all required documents before cargo pickup:

**Document Checklist:**
- Commercial invoice (3 copies)
- Packing list (3 copies)
- Airway bill (forwarder provides)
- Certificate of origin (if applicable)
- Export license (if controlled goods)
- Insurance certificate (if purchasing separately)

**Tips for Fast Clearance:**
- Match all document values exactly
- Use consistent product descriptions
- Include correct HS codes on all documents
- Ensure consignee details match import license

### Step 4: Book and Confirm Shipment

Once you've selected a forwarder and received the quote:

1. **Confirm the booking:** Provide pickup date and address
2. **Arrange factory pickup:** Forwarder dispatches truck to factory
3. **Cargo inspection:** Verify weight, dimensions, and packaging at origin
4. **Receive AWB:** Get the airway bill number for tracking
5. **Pay freight charges:** Typically bank transfer or credit card
6. **Track shipment:** Monitor progress through forwarder's tracking system

### Step 5: Manage Customs and Delivery

**At Origin (China):**
- Forwarder handles export customs clearance
- Cargo moves to airport for loading
- AWB is activated for tracking

**At Destination:**
- Forwarder or customs broker handles import clearance
- Pay import duties and taxes (if not DDP terms)
- Arrange final-mile delivery to warehouse
- Verify received goods match shipping documents
- File any damage or shortage claims within 7 days

### Step 6: Review and Optimize

After each shipment, review performance to improve future shipments:

**Review Checklist:**
- Did goods arrive on time?
- Were there any customs delays?
- Were costs in line with the quote?
- Was the forwarder responsive and proactive?
- Were there any damage or quality issues?

**Optimization Opportunities:**
- Negotiate volume discounts for regular shipments
- Consider consolidating smaller shipments for better rates
- Explore alternative routes (e.g., Guangzhou vs. Shanghai for better rates)

## FAQ

**Q: What is the cheapest air freight option from China?**
A: Consolidated air freight offers the lowest per-kg rates, typically 10-20% less than direct standard air freight. For shipments under 45 kg, express couriers like DHL and FedEx often offer promotional rates that can be competitive. To get the best rate, consolidate shipments when possible, ship during off-peak months (January-September), and use Guangzhou or Shenzhen airports which often have lower rates than Shanghai.

**Q: How long does air freight from China to the US take?**
A: Standard air freight takes 5-8 days door-to-door from China to the US. Flight transit is 12-18 hours depending on the route, but customs clearance and ground transportation add 2-5 days. Express courier services like DHL or FedEx deliver in 3-5 business days. Peak season (October-December) may add 1-3 days due to airport congestion.

**Q: What is the minimum weight for air freight from China?**
A: Standard air freight has a minimum of 45 kg (some airlines accept 21 kg). Express courier services have no minimum — you can ship even 1 kg via DHL or FedEx. For very small shipments under 5 kg, express courier is almost always cheaper and faster than standard air freight. Consolidated air freight typically requires 21-45 kg minimum.

**Q: Can I ship lithium batteries by air from China?**
A: Yes, lithium batteries can be shipped by air, but they require strict compliance with IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR). Batteries must be UN38.3 tested, properly labeled with Class 9 dangerous goods markings, and accompanied by a Dangerous Goods Declaration. Batteries installed in equipment (like phones or laptops) have less restrictive requirements than standalone batteries. Most express couriers accept lithium battery shipments with proper documentation.

**Q: What are the hidden costs in air freight from China?**
A: Beyond the base rate per kg, air freight includes: fuel surcharge (15-25% of base rate), security surcharge ($0.15-0.25/kg), terminal handling charges ($80-150 at origin), documentation fees ($25-75), customs brokerage ($30-80 at origin, $50-200 at destination), and pickup/delivery charges ($50-300 depending on distance). Peak season surcharges (October-December) can add $0.50-1.50/kg. Always request a total cost quote, not just the per-kg rate.

**Q: How do I choose between air freight and sea freight?**
A: Use this decision framework: if your product costs more than $20/unit and needs to arrive within 10 days, choose air freight. If your product costs under $5/unit and you can wait 3-6 weeks, choose sea freight. For products between $5-20/unit, calculate total landed cost including carrying cost of inventory during transit. Air freight makes financial sense when the stockout cost exceeds the shipping premium.

**Q: What is the volumetric weight rule for air freight?**
A: Airlines charge based on the higher of actual weight or volumetric weight. For standard air freight, volumetric weight = Length x Width x Height (cm) / 6,000. For express courier, divide by 5,000 instead. Example: a box measuring 60x40x30 cm has a volumetric weight of 12 kg (air freight) or 14.4 kg (express). If the actual weight is 10 kg, you pay for 12 kg by air or 14.4 kg by express.

**Q: How do I track my air freight shipment from China?**
A: Your freight forwarder provides an airway bill (AWB) number when the shipment is booked. Use this number to track on the airline's website, the forwarder's tracking portal, or platforms like TrackingMore. For express couriers (DHL, FedEx, UPS), you get a tracking number that provides real-time updates including customs clearance status. Most forwarders also send email or SMS notifications at key milestones.

**Q: What happens if my air freight shipment is delayed at customs?**
A: Customs delays can occur due to documentation errors, product inspections, or random checks. If delayed, your customs broker will contact you with the specific issue. Common delays include mismatched invoice values, missing certificates, or products requiring additional permits. To minimize delays: ensure all documents are accurate and consistent, use correct HS codes, and work with an experienced customs broker at the destination. Allow 1-3 extra days as buffer in your delivery timeline.

**Q: Can I ship samples from China by air?**
A: Yes, air freight is ideal for product samples. Express courier services (DHL, FedEx, UPS) are the most cost-effective for samples under 10 kg, with rates typically $45-75 for a 1-5 kg package to most countries. For larger samples (10-45 kg), standard air freight may offer better rates. Always declare samples as "merchandise samples with no commercial value" on the customs invoice to minimize import duties at the destination.

**Q: What is the best airport in China for air freight?**
A: Shanghai Pudong (PVG) has the most international air cargo capacity and flight frequency, making it ideal for shipments to North America and Europe. Guangzhou Baiyun (CAN) often offers 5-10% lower rates and is ideal for Southeast Asia routes. Shenzhen Bao'an (SZX) is convenient for Guangdong province manufacturers and offers competitive rates to Europe. For e-commerce sellers, consider the airport closest to your supplier to minimize domestic pickup costs.
