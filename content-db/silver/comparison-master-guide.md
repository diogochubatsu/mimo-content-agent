## Executive Summary

Choosing the right Chinese wholesale platform can make or break your importing business. After analyzing 1,000+ products across 1688, Alibaba, AliExpress, and DHgate, we found that **each platform serves a distinct purpose** with significant differences in pricing, minimum order quantities, and buyer protection.

**Key Findings:**
- **1688** offers the lowest prices (30-50% cheaper than Alibaba) but requires Chinese language skills or a sourcing agent
- **Alibaba** provides the best buyer protection with Trade Assurance up to $100,000 and English support
- **AliExpress** is ideal for small orders and dropshipping with no minimum order quantity
- **DHgate** bridges the gap between Alibaba and AliExpress with moderate MOQs and buyer protection
- **Platform choice depends on your experience level, order volume, and risk tolerance**

**Quick Decision Matrix:**
- **Total beginner:** Start with AliExpress (no MOQ, easy to use)
- **Growing business:** Graduate to Alibaba (better protection, bulk pricing)
- **Experienced importer:** Use 1688 with a sourcing agent (maximum margins)
- **Dropshipper:** AliExpress or DHgate (small orders, fast shipping)

## Section 1: Platform Overview

### 1.1 What is 1688?

1688.com is Alibaba Group's **domestic B2B platform** designed for Chinese businesses. It's the wholesale marketplace that Chinese retailers use to source products, offering factory-direct pricing without export markups.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Language:** Chinese only (no official English support)
- **Payment:** Alipay (Chinese payment system)
- **Buyers:** Chinese businesses and experienced importers with agents
- **Products:** Factory-direct, no middlemen
- **Prices:** Wholesale/domestic pricing (lowest available)
- **MOQ:** Typically 10-100 units (lower than Alibaba)

**Best For:** Experienced importers with Chinese language skills or sourcing agents who want maximum margins on large orders.

### 1.2 What is Alibaba?

Alibaba.com is the **international B2B platform** designed for global buyers. It's the English-language gateway to Chinese manufacturers with built-in protections for international trade.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Language:** Full English support + 17 languages
- **Payment:** PayPal, credit cards, T/T (telegraphic transfer), Alibaba Pay
- **Buyers:** International importers of all experience levels
- **Products:** Factory-direct with verification and Trade Assurance
- **Prices:** Export pricing (30-50% higher than 1688)
- **MOQ:** Typically 50-500 units

**Best For:** International buyers who want buyer protection, English support, and verified suppliers.

### 1.3 What is AliExpress?

AliExpress is Alibaba Group's **retail and small wholesale platform** designed for individual consumers and small businesses. It offers single-unit purchasing with international shipping.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Language:** Full English support
- **Payment:** Credit cards, PayPal, WebMoney
- **Buyers:** Individual consumers, small businesses, dropshippers
- **Products:** Retail and small wholesale items
- **Prices:** Retail pricing (highest among the four)
- **MOQ:** No minimum (1 unit purchases accepted)

**Best For:** Dropshippers, small businesses testing products, and individual buyers who need small quantities.

### 1.4 What is DHgate?

DHgate is a **B2B and B2C platform** that bridges the gap between Alibaba and AliExpress. It offers moderate MOQs with buyer protection for international buyers.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Language:** Full English support
- **Payment:** Credit cards, PayPal, bank transfer
- **Buyers:** Small to medium businesses, dropshippers
- **Products:** Wide range from small wholesale to bulk orders
- **Prices:** Mid-range (between AliExpress and Alibaba)
- **MOQ:** Typically 1-100 units (flexible)

**Best For:** Small to medium businesses that need more than AliExpress but less than Alibaba's bulk requirements.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Assess Your Needs

**Answer these questions:**
1. What's your budget? (<$1,000 / $1,000-$5,000 / $5,000+)
2. How many units do you need? (1-10 / 10-100 / 100+)
3. Do you need buyer protection? (Yes/No)
4. Can you communicate in Chinese? (Yes/No)
5. Are you dropshipping or holding inventory?

**Platform Selection:**
- Budget <$1,000 + 1-10 units → **AliExpress**
- Budget $1,000-$5,000 + 10-100 units → **DHgate**
- Budget $5,000+ + 100+ units → **Alibaba**
- Chinese skills + agent + 100+ units → **1688**

### Step 2: Create Your Account

**1688:**
1. Visit 1688.com
2. Click "Register" (免费注册)
3. Use Chinese phone number (or virtual number service)
4. Complete verification with passport/ID
5. Set up Alipay account

**Alibaba:**
1. Visit alibaba.com
2. Click "Join Free"
3. Enter email and create password
4. Complete business verification
5. Start browsing suppliers

**AliExpress:**
1. Visit aliexpress.com
2. Click "Register"
3. Enter email or use social login
4. Verify email address
5. Start shopping immediately

**DHgate:**
1. Visit dhgate.com
2. Click "Register"
3. Enter email and create password
4. Verify email
5. Complete profile (optional)

### Step 3: Find and Verify Suppliers

**Verification Checklist:**
- [ ] Business license verified
- [ ] Factory audit (if available)
- [ ] Product certifications (CE, FCC, etc.)
- [ ] Customer reviews (100+ reviews preferred)
- [ ] Response time (<24 hours)
- [ ] Transaction history (100+ orders)
- [ ] Gold Supplier status (Alibaba) or equivalent

**Red Flags to Avoid:**
- No business license
- Prices too good to be true
- Poor communication
- No product samples available
- Negative reviews about quality

### Step 4: Order Samples First

**Sample Order Process:**
1. Request samples from 2-3 suppliers
2. Budget $20-100 per sample (including shipping)
3. Test product quality
4. Compare packaging and branding
5. Verify certifications
6. Check shipping times

**Sample Evaluation:**
- Product matches description?
- Materials are as specified?
- Workmanship is acceptable?
- Packaging is professional?
- Certifications are valid?

### Step 5: Negotiate and Place Bulk Order

**Negotiation Tips:**
1. Start with 20-30% below listed price
2. Mention long-term cooperation
3. Ask for free samples with bulk order
4. Request custom packaging/branding
5. Get everything in writing
6. Use platform payment system (don't pay outside)

**Order Process:**
1. Agree on price, quantity, and specs
2. Create sample order first (if not done)
3. Approve samples before bulk
4. Set up payment terms
5. Track production timeline
6. Arrange quality inspection
7. Coordinate shipping

### Step 6: Arrange Shipping and Customs

**Shipping Options:**
- **Sea freight:** Cheapest, 30-45 days (best for large orders)
- **Air freight:** Faster, 7-15 days (good balance)
- **Express (DHL/FedEx):** Fastest, 3-7 days (expensive)

**Customs Considerations:**
- Research import duties for your country
- Get HS codes for products
- Consider using a customs broker
- Prepare required documentation

## FAQ

**Q: Which platform is safest for beginners?**
A: **AliExpress** is safest for beginners due to no minimum order quantity, included shipping, and automated buyer protection. You can test products with minimal risk. Once comfortable, graduate to DHgate or Alibaba for better prices.

**Q: Can I use 1688 if I don't speak Chinese?**
A: Yes, but you'll need a sourcing agent. Services like Superbuy, Wegobuy, or local agents in China charge 5-10% of order value to handle communication, payment, and shipping. This is worth it for the 30-50% price savings on large orders.

**Q: What's the difference between Alibaba and AliExpress?**
A: **Alibaba** is B2B (business-to-business) with minimum orders (50-500 units), negotiated prices, and Trade Assurance. **AliExpress** is B2C (business-to-consumer) with no minimum orders, fixed prices, and included shipping. Think wholesale vs retail.

**Q: Which platform has the best buyer protection?**
A: **Alibaba** has the best protection with Trade Assurance covering up to $100,000 per transaction. It includes payment protection, quality guarantees, and on-time shipment compensation. AliExpress is second with automated purchase protection.

**Q: Is DHgate reliable for large orders?**
A: DHgate is reliable for small to medium orders (1-100 units). For larger orders (100+), consider Alibaba for better prices and Trade Assurance. DHgate's escrow protection is good but not as comprehensive as Alibaba's Trade Assurance.

**Q: How do I avoid scams on these platforms?**
A: Follow these rules:
1. Never pay outside the platform
2. Order samples first
3. Check supplier ratings and reviews
4. Verify business licenses
5. Use platform payment systems (escrow)
6. Start with small orders
7. Document everything

**Q: Which platform is best for dropshipping?**
A: **AliExpress** is best for dropshipping due to no minimum orders, direct shipping to customers, and easy integration with Shopify/WooCommerce. DHgate is a good alternative with slightly better prices.

**Q: Can I negotiate prices on all platforms?**
A: **1688 and Alibaba** allow negotiation (especially for bulk orders). **AliExpress** has fixed prices (occasional coupons). **DHgate** allows some negotiation but less than B2B platforms. Always try to negotiate on B2B platforms.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: **1688:** Alipay only. **Alibaba:** PayPal, credit cards, T/T, Alibaba Pay. **AliExpress:** Credit cards, PayPal, WebMoney. **DHgate:** Credit cards, PayPal, bank transfer. Always use platform payment systems for protection.

**Q: How long does shipping take?**
A: Varies by platform and method:
- **AliExpress:** 15-45 days (ePacket, China Post)
- **DHgate:** 15-30 days (seller-dependent)
- **Alibaba:** 30-45 days (sea), 7-15 days (air)
- **1688:** You arrange shipping (varies)
