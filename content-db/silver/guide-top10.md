## FAQ

**Q: Which product has the highest margin?**
A: Phone cases have the highest margin at 97%. With costs as low as $0.35 on 1688 and retail prices of $12.99 on Amazon, the profit potential is enormous.

**Q: Which product is easiest for beginners?**
A: LED strip lights or resistance bands. Both have low MOQs, consistent demand, and manageable quality control requirements.

**Q: How much should I invest in my first product?**
A: Start with $1,000-2,000 total. This covers samples ($100), first order ($500-1,000), shipping ($200-500), and marketing ($200-500).

**Q: Should I focus on one product or multiple?**
A: Start with one product. Master it, then expand. Spreading too thin early on leads to poor quality control and customer service.

**Q: How do I handle seasonal demand?**
A: Focus on evergreen products that sell year-round. If seasonal, order inventory 2-3 months before peak season.

**Q: What about product liability?**
A: Essential for all products. Budget $500-2,000/year for product liability insurance. This protects you if products cause injury or damage.

**Q: Can I import these products to any country?**
A: Most products can be imported globally, but check local regulations. Some countries have specific requirements for electronics, cosmetics, or fitness equipment.
