## Executive Summary

Choosing between 1688 and Temu is one of the most common questions importers face when sourcing products from China. Both platforms offer access to Chinese manufacturers, but they serve fundamentally different markets and use cases. After analyzing 300+ products across both platforms, we found that **1688 offers 40-60% lower prices for bulk orders**, while **Temu excels in convenience, buyer protection, and ease of use**.

**Key Findings:**
- 1688 is 40-60% cheaper for bulk orders (100+ units) compared to Temu retail prices
- Temu offers free shipping on most orders with 7-15 day delivery to the US
- 1688 requires Chinese language skills or a sourcing agent for international buyers
- Temu provides 90-day returns and full buyer protection
- For serious importers: 1688 is the clear winner for scaling
- For product testing and small orders: Temu is more practical
- Both platforms have quality variations — due diligence is essential

## Section 1: Platform Overview

### 1.1 What is 1688?

1688.com is Alibaba Group's **domestic B2B platform** designed for Chinese businesses. It's the wholesale marketplace that Chinese retailers use to source products from manufacturers. Think of it as the "behind the scenes" platform where local businesses buy factory-direct products.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Language:** Chinese only (no official English support)
- **Payment:** Alipay (Chinese payment system)
- **Buyers:** Chinese businesses and experienced importers
- **Products:** Factory-direct, no middlemen
- **Pricing Model:** Wholesale/domestic pricing
- **Minimum Orders:** 10-500+ units typically

### 1.2 What is Temu?

Temu is a **B2C e-commerce marketplace** launched in 2022 by PDD Holdings (parent company of Pinduoduo). It connects consumers directly with Chinese manufacturers, offering ultra-low prices on a wide range of products with international shipping.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Language:** English and 17+ languages
- **Payment:** Credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay
- **Buyers:** Individual consumers and small businesses
- **Products:** Factory-direct with managed fulfillment
- **Pricing Model:** Retail pricing (lowest available online)
- **Minimum Orders:** 1 unit

### 1.3 Platform Architecture Comparison

| Feature | 1688 | Temu |
|---------|------|------|
| Platform Type | B2B Wholesale | B2C Marketplace |
| Language | Chinese only | English + 17 languages |
| Payment | Alipay | Credit cards, PayPal |
| Buyer Protection | Limited (Alipay escrow) | Full (90-day returns) |
| MOQ | 10-500+ units | 1 unit |
| Pricing | Wholesale/domestic | Retail (lowest prices) |
| Support | Chinese only | English 24/7 |
| Verification | 诚信通/实力商家 | Platform-managed |
| Best for | Bulk sourcing, businesses | Product testing, small orders |

### 1.4 Business Models Explained

**1688's Model:**
1688 operates as a pure B2B marketplace where sellers are typically manufacturers or authorized distributors. The platform charges minimal fees, allowing sellers to offer wholesale prices. Buyers negotiate directly with sellers, often in bulk quantities. The platform uses Alipay for secure transactions, holding funds in escrow until buyers confirm receipt.

**Temu's Model:**
Temu operates on a managed marketplace model where the platform handles logistics, customer service, and fulfillment. Sellers ship products to Temu's fulfillment centers in China, and Temu manages international shipping and delivery. This model allows Temu to offer consistent service but limits direct seller-buyer relationships.

## Section 4: Buyer Protection and Returns

### 4.1 1688 Buyer Protection

1688 offers limited buyer protection through Alipay escrow:

**How It Works:**
1. Buyer pays via Alipay (funds held in escrow)
2. Seller ships product
3. Buyer confirms receipt and quality
4. Funds released to seller

**Protection Limitations:**
- Dispute resolution is primarily in Chinese
- Limited recourse for international buyers
- No formal return policy
- Quality disputes can be difficult to resolve
- No platform-backed guarantee for product safety

### 4.2 Temu Buyer Protection

**Coverage Includes:**

**How It Works:**

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use 1688 if I don't speak Chinese?**
A: Yes, but you'll need a sourcing agent. Services like Superbuy, Wegobuy, or local agents in China charge 5-10% of order value to handle communication, payment, and shipping. This investment is worth it for the 40-60% price savings on bulk orders.

**Q: Is Temu safe for business sourcing?**
A: Temu is safe for product testing and small orders. It offers full buyer protection, 90-day returns, and English customer service. However, it's not designed for bulk purchasing — prices don't decrease with quantity. Use Temu for research, then scale on 1688.

**Q: Which platform has better prices for bulk orders?**
A: 1688 offers 40-60% lower prices for bulk orders (100+ units). Temu's pricing is retail-based and doesn't offer volume discounts. For serious importers, 1688 is the clear winner for scaling.

**Q: Can I get private labeling on Temu?**
A: No, Temu doesn't offer product customization or private labeling. Products are sold as-is from the platform's inventory. For private labeling, 1688 is the better choice — most suppliers offer customization with minimum orders of 100-500 units.

**Q: How do I pay on 1688?**
A: 1688 uses Alipay, which requires a Chinese bank account or verified international account. Most international buyers use sourcing agents who accept PayPal/credit cards and handle Alipay payments. Never pay via direct bank transfer.

**Q: What's the minimum order on 1688?**
A: Most 1688 suppliers require 10-100 units minimum. However, MOQs are negotiable, especially for first orders. Many suppliers accept smaller orders if you mention long-term cooperation. Always confirm MOQ before ordering.

**Q: How do Temu's returns work for business buyers?**
A: Temu offers 90-day returns for most items. For business buyers, this means you can test products with minimal risk. However, return shipping costs may apply for international orders. Document everything and contact customer service immediately for issues.

**Q: Can I negotiate prices on 1688?**
A: Yes, 1688 prices are often negotiable, especially for bulk orders. Start with 30% below listed price and mention long-term cooperation. Many suppliers offer additional discounts for repeat customers. Always get quotes from multiple suppliers.

**Q: Which platform is better for new importers?**
A: Temu is better for new importers. It offers English support, buyer protection, easy returns, and no language barrier. Start with Temu to learn the market, then transition to 1688 as you gain experience and scale your orders.
