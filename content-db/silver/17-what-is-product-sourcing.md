## What is Product Sourcing?

### Definition

Product sourcing is the process of identifying, evaluating, and acquiring products from suppliers for resale or business use. It encompasses everything from finding suppliers to negotiating prices, managing quality, and coordinating logistics.

### Detailed Model Breakdown

#### Direct Factory Sourcing

**Definition:** Buying directly from manufacturers without intermediaries.

**Advantages:**
- Lowest unit cost
- Full customization control
- Direct communication
- Build long-term relationships

**Disadvantages:**
- Higher MOQs required
- Requires sourcing expertise
- More time-consuming
- Higher risk for beginners

**Best for:**
- High-volume importers
- Experienced buyers
- Custom product requirements

#### Trading Companies

**Definition:** Intermediaries that source from multiple factories.

**Advantages:**
- Lower MOQs
- Multiple factory options
- Quality verification
- Export documentation

**Disadvantages:**
- Higher unit cost (10-30% markup)
- Less direct control
- Potential communication gaps

**Best for:**
- Small to medium orders
- First-time importers
- Multi-SKU orders

#### Sourcing Agents

**Definition:** Professional intermediaries that manage the entire sourcing process.

**Advantages:**
- Expert negotiation
- Factory verification
- Quality control
- Language/culture bridge

**Disadvantages:**
- Agent fees (5-15%)
- Communication layer
- Potential conflicts of interest

**Best for:**
- Complex product requirements
- First-time importers
- High-value orders

#### Dropshipping

**Definition:** Supplier ships directly to customer, no inventory holding.

**Advantages:**
- No inventory risk
- Low startup cost
- Test products easily
- Scalable

**Disadvantages:**
- Lower margins (20-35%)
- Less quality control
- Longer shipping times

**Best for:**
- Product testing
- Low capital start
- Market validation

## The Sourcing Process

### 7-Step Sourcing Process

| Step | Activities | Timeline |
|------|------------|----------|
| 1. Research | Market analysis, product selection | 1-2 weeks |
| 2. Supplier identification | Find potential suppliers | 1-2 weeks |
| 3. Supplier evaluation | Verify capabilities, compare quotes | 2-3 weeks |
| 4. Sampling | Order samples, evaluate quality | 2-4 weeks |
| 5. Negotiation | Price, terms, MOQ | 1-2 weeks |
| 6. Order placement | PO, payment, production | 1 week |
| 7. Quality & delivery | Inspection, shipping, receiving | 4-6 weeks |

## People Also Ask

1. **What is product sourcing?** The process of finding and acquiring products for resale. Includes supplier identification, evaluation, negotiation, and quality control.

2. **How do I start product sourcing?** Research market, identify suppliers, verify capabilities, order samples, negotiate terms, place orders. See our [Product Sourcing](/product-sourcing/) guide.

3. **What's the best sourcing model?** Depends on your needs. Trading company for beginners, direct factory for volume, agent for complex orders.

4. **How do I find suppliers?** Online platforms (Alibaba, 1688), trade shows, referrals, sourcing agents.

5. **How do I verify suppliers?** Check business license on gsxt.gov.cn, request references, order samples, conduct audits.

6. **What's landed cost?** Total cost including product, freight, duty, insurance, and fees. See our [Hidden Sourcing Costs](/hidden-sourcing-costs/) guide.

7. **How do I protect my IP?** NNN agreements, trademarks, patents. See our [Product Sourcing](/product-sourcing/) guide for details.

8. **What's the cheapest sourcing method?** Direct factory (lowest unit cost) or dropshipping (no inventory).

9. **How do I ensure quality?** Implement QC protocols: samples, pre-shipment inspection, third-party audits.

10. **How do I scale sourcing?** Build supplier relationships, negotiate volume discounts, implement automation.
