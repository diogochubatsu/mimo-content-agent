## Case Studies

### Case Study 1: Alibaba Success

**Challenge:** First-time importer with limited budget.

**Strategy:**
1. Used Alibaba Trade Assurance
2. Filtered by Gold Supplier (3+ years)
3. Verified on gsxt.gov.cn
4. Ordered samples before bulk

**Results:**
- Found supplier in 1 week
- Price: 15% lower than Global Sources
- MOQ: 500 units (vs 1,000)
- Total cost: $6,000
- **Saved: $1,200**

**Results:**
- Found verified manufacturer
- Quality: Consistent (0.3% defect rate)
- Lead time: 35 days (on schedule)
- Relationship: Strategic partnership

## People Also Ask

1. **Which platform is better for beginners?** Alibaba for lower MOQs and prices. Global Sources for higher verification. See our [China Wholesale Websites](/china-wholesale-websites/) guide.

2. **Is Alibaba safe?** Yes, with Trade Assurance. Always verify suppliers independently on gsxt.gov.cn.

3. **Is Global Sources worth the higher prices?** Yes for high-value orders, compliance-heavy products, or when factory verification is critical.

4. **Can I use both platforms?** Yes. Many experienced importers use both for different product categories.

5. **Which has better buyer protection?** Global Sources has higher verification, but Alibaba's Trade Assurance provides payment protection.

6. **What's the typical MOQ difference?** Alibaba: 500-5,000 units. Global Sources: 2,000-10,000 units.

7. **How do I verify suppliers on Alibaba?** Use Trade Assurance, verify on gsxt.gov.cn, order samples, request factory tour.

8. **How do I verify suppliers on Global Sources?** Check ISO verification, attend trade shows, request due diligence.

9. **Which platform has more suppliers?** Alibaba: 100,000+. Global Sources: 10,000+.

10. **Which is better for electronics?** Both good. Global Sources for verified manufacturers, Alibaba for wider selection.
