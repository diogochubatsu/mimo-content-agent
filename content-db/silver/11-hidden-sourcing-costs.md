## The 15 Hidden Costs

### 1. Freight Surcharges

**What it is:** Additional fees beyond base freight rate.

**Common surcharges:**
- Fuel surcharge: 10-20%
- Peak season surcharge: 15-30%
- Emergency surcharge: 5-15%
- BAF (Bunker Adjustment Fuel): 5-10%

**Calculation:**
```
Total Freight = Base Rate × (1 + Fuel Surcharge + Peak Surcharge + Other)
```

**Example:**
- Base rate: $3,000
- Fuel surcharge: 15%
- Peak surcharge: 20%
- Total: $3,000 × 1.35 = $4,050

### 2. Customs Brokerage Fees

**What it is:** Fees for customs clearance services.

**Typical range:** $150-300 per shipment

**Breakdown:**
- Entry preparation: $50-100
- Document processing: $50-100
- Bond fees: $25-50
- FDA/USDA filing: $25-50

### 3. Duty Calculations

**What it is:** Import tariffs based on HS code and country of origin.

**Calculation:**
```
Duty = Declared Value × Duty Rate
```

**Example:**
- Declared value: $15,000
- Duty rate: 8%
- Duty: $1,200

### 4. Port Storage Fees

**What it is:** Charges for container storage at port.

**Typical rates:**
- Free time: 3-5 days
- Storage: $10-25 per pallet/day
- Demurrage: $50-100 per container/day

### 5. Inland Transportation

**What it is:** Trucking from port to warehouse.

**Typical range:** $300-800 per shipment

**Factors:**
- Distance from port
- Container type
- Fuel prices
- Driver availability

### 6. Inspection Costs

**What it is:** Third-party quality inspection fees.

**Typical range:** $200-500 per inspection

**Types:**
- Pre-production: $200-300
- During production: $300-400
- Pre-shipment: $250-500
- Container loading: $150-300

### 7. Documentation Fees

**What it is:** Costs for required shipping documents.

**Common fees:**
- Bill of Lading: $50-100
- Commercial Invoice: $25-50
- Packing List: $25-50
- Certificate of Origin: $50-100
- Fumigation certificate: $50-150

### 8. Currency Conversion

**What it is:** Fees for international money transfers.

**Typical range:** 1-3% of transaction

**Ways to minimize:**
- Use forward contracts
- Negotiate better rates
- Use multi-currency accounts
- Time transfers strategically

### 9. Payment Processing

**What it is:** Fees for wire transfers and payment methods.

**Typical range:** $25-50 per transaction

**Methods:**
- T/T wire: $25-50
- Letter of Credit: 1-2% of value
- PayPal: 3-4%
- Trade Assurance: 0-3%

### 10. Insurance Premiums

**What it is:** Cargo insurance coverage.

**Typical range:** 0.3-1% of declared value

**Calculation:**
```
Insurance = Declared Value × Rate
```

**Example:**
- Declared value: $15,000
- Rate: 0.5%
- Insurance: $75

### 11. Compliance Testing

**What it is:** Product testing for regulatory compliance.

**Typical range:** $500-5,000 per product

**Common tests:**
- FCC: $500-1,500
- FDA: $500-2,000
- CE: $500-1,500
- UL: $1,000-3,000
- CPSIA: $500-1,000

### 12. Quality Failures

**What it is:** Costs from defective products.

**Typical range:** 5-20% of order value

**Types:**
- Rework costs
- Replacement costs
- Return shipping
- Customer refunds
- Lost sales

### 13. Storage Fees

**What it is:** Warehouse storage costs.

**Typical range:** $10-25 per pallet/day

**Factors:**
- Duration
- Space required
- Climate control
- Security

### 14. Labeling/Marking Fees

**What it is:** Costs for product labeling.

**Typical range:** $0.10-0.50 per unit

**Types:**
- FNSKU labels: $0.05-0.10
- UPC labels: $0.05-0.10
- Custom labels: $0.10-0.25
- Hang tags: $0.15-0.30

### 15. Returns Processing

**What it is:** Costs for handling returns.

**Typical range:** 2-5% of revenue

**Costs:**
- Return shipping
- Inspection
- Restocking
- Refund processing
- Customer service

## People Also Ask

1. **What's the biggest hidden cost?** Freight surcharges and quality failures are typically largest. See our [How to Import From China](/how-to-import-from-china/) guide.

2. **How do I calculate total landed cost?** Product + freight + insurance + duty + fees + storage + compliance. Use our calculator above.

3. **How can I reduce hidden costs?** Negotiate volume discounts, optimize shipping, improve quality, hedge currency.

4. **What's a typical quality allowance?** 3-5% of order value for consumer goods. Higher for complex products.

5. **How do I minimize port storage?** Plan delivery windows, arrange inland transport in advance, avoid peak seasons.

7. **How do I reduce compliance costs?** Choose products with fewer requirements, test early, maintain certifications.

8. **What's the typical customs brokerage fee?** $150-300 per shipment. Negotiate flat rates for regular shipments.

9. **How do I handle currency risk?** Use forward contracts, multi-currency accounts, time transfers strategically.
