## Step 1: Lock Down the Complete Legal Identity Packet

Request: Chinese business license, company name in Chinese and English, 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code, registered address, legal representative name, bank beneficiary name, ISO/BSCI certificates.

### Required Documents Checklist

| Document | Purpose | Verification Method | Red Flag |
|----------|---------|---------------------|----------|
| Chinese Business License | Legal entity proof | Check on gsxt.gov.cn | Name mismatch |
| Export License | Export capability | Local commerce bureau | Missing or expired |
| ISO 9001 Certificate | Quality management | Verify certificate number | Invalid certificate |
| BSCI Certificate | Social compliance | Check amfori.org | Not registered |
| Bank Account Proof | Payment identity | Match to business license | Personal account |
| Test Reports | Product compliance | Verify lab accreditation | Unknown lab |

### Document Verification Protocol

1. **Request high-resolution scans** of all documents
2. **Zoom 400%** on red seals and stamps
3. **Verify Chinese characters** match English translations
4. **Check expiration dates** on all certificates
5. **Cross-reference** company name across all documents

## Step 2: Cross-Check the Official Mainland Registry

Check all documents against the NECIPS database at gsxt.gov.cn.

### Inspector Wang's 15-Point Cross-Check Checklist:

1. Exact Chinese legal name
2. English trade name
3. 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code
4. Registration status (must be Active)
5. Registered capital amount
6. Incorporation date
7. Legal representative name
8. Registered operating address
9. Operating term limits
10. Exact business scope wording
11. Company type
12. Manufacturing language in scope
13. Importing/exporting language in scope
14. Pro forma invoice payee match
15. Exporter name consistency

### NECIPS Verification Process

| Step | Action | What to Look For |
|------|--------|------------------|
| 1. Access gsxt.gov.cn | Enter company name or credit code | Official government site |
| 2. Verify status | Must show "Active" (存续) | No abnormal listings |
| 3. Check business scope | Must include "manufacturing" | Trading companies say "sales" |
| 4. Verify legal rep | Name must match documents | Different name = red flag |
| 5. Check capital | Must be reasonable for industry | Too low = suspicious |

### Case Study: Fake Factory Detection

**Discovery:** Supplier claimed to be electronics manufacturer with 10 years experience.

**NECIPS Findings:**
- Business scope: "Wholesale and retail of electronic products"
- No manufacturing language
- Registered capital: ¥50,000 (too low for factory)
- Status: "Active" but with "abnormal listings"

**Outcome:** Lost $2,000 deposit. Learned to verify NECIPS before any payment.

## Step 3: Validate Certificates, Export Capability, and Payment Identity

Three tracks: Certification Validity, Export Capability, Payment Identity.

### Certification Validation Matrix

| Certificate | Verification Method | Common Issues |
|-------------|---------------------|---------------|
| ISO 9001 | Check certificate number online | Expired, wrong scope |
| ISO 14001 | Verify issuing body | Fake certificates |
| BSCI | Check amfori.org database | Not registered |
| CE Marking | Verify notified body | Self-declared without testing |
| FCC | Check FCC ID database | Invalid ID numbers |

### Export Capability Verification

| Check | Method | Pass Criteria |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| Export license | Request copy | Valid, matches entity |
| Export history | Ask for records | 3+ years of exports |
| Shipping documents | Request examples | Recent, consistent |
| Customs records | Verify with broker | Clean history |

### Payment Identity Verification

| Requirement | Verification | Risk if Failed |
|-------------|--------------|----------------|
| Bank name matches entity | Compare documents | Payment fraud |
| Account type is corporate | Not personal account | No recourse |
| SWIFT code verified | Check with bank | Wire failure |
| Beneficiary name exact | Character-for-character match | Payment lost |

## Step 4: Run a Live Trader-vs-Factory Video Challenge

Force supplier into live, unscripted environment. Start outside front gate. Walk without cuts to production line.

### Video Audit Protocol

| Phase | Duration | What to Observe |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| 1. Exterior | 2-3 min | Building size, signage, address match |
| 2. Entrance | 1-2 min | Reception, staff presence |
| 3. Production floor | 5-10 min | Machinery, workers, activity |
| 4. QC area | 3-5 min | Inspection stations, documentation |
| 5. Warehouse | 3-5 min | Inventory, organization |
| 6. Q&A | 5-10 min | Technical questions |

### Red Flags During Video Audit

| Observation | What It Means | Action |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| No production during call | Not a real factory | Request unannounced call |
| Only sales staff visible | Trading company | Verify manufacturing scope |
| Workers can't answer questions | Staffing issues | Ask detailed product questions |
| No QC documentation | Quality gaps | Request QC records |
| Dirty/disorganized floor | Management issues | Consider alternatives |
| Pre-recorded video | Hiding something | Demand live only |

### Technical Questions to Ask

1. What is your current capacity utilization?
2. How many production lines do you operate?
3. What is your defect rate for the last 3 months?
4. Can you show me the raw material storage area?
5. What is your lead time for a 1,000-unit order?

## Step 5: Audit Physical Production Capacity and Quality Controls

Execute strict on-site audit. Count machines, inspect condition, verify worker counts, review QC checkpoints.

| Capacity | Daily/weekly output | Production reports |

| Inventory | Raw materials, WIP | Stock reports, turnover rate |

### Quality Control Verification

| QC Stage | What to Check | Pass Criteria |
|----------|---------------|---------------|
| Incoming materials | Inspection process | Documented, consistent |
| In-process | Checkpoints, standards | AQL 2.5 for critical defects |
| Final inspection | Sampling, documentation | Complete records |
| Packaging | FBA compliance | Labels, protection correct |

## Step 6: Execute a Controlled Pilot Order and Inspection

Place controlled pilot order. Book independent pre-shipment inspection. Review all defect evidence before paying balance.

### Pilot Order Protocol

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| 1. Sample order | 50-100 units | Week 1-2 |
| 2. Golden sample approval | Compare to specs | Week 3 |
| 3. Pre-production sample | 50 units | Week 4-5 |
| 4. Mass production | 500-1,000 units | Week 6-10 |
| 5. Pre-shipment inspection | Independent QC | Week 11 |
| 6. Balance payment | After inspection passes | Week 12 |

| Check | Method | Pass Criteria |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| Quantity | Count cartons | ±2% of PO |
| Visual | Inspect 10% of units | AQL 2.5 |
| Function | Test 5% of units | 100% pass |

## Real-World Fraud Cases

### Case Study 1: Bank Account Change Fraud

**Scenario:** Supplier emailed requesting payment to new bank account.

**Detection:**
1. Email came from slightly different domain
2. Bank name didn't match business license
3. Beneficiary name was different

**Outcome:** Payment frozen. Verified through secondary contact. Scam prevented.

**Lesson:** Always verify bank changes through a separate channel.

### Case Study 2: Quality Bait-and-Switch

**Scenario:** Approved golden sample was excellent. Mass production quality was poor.

**Discovery:**
1. Pre-shipment inspection found 15% defect rate
2. Material quality significantly lower than sample
3. Factory had used cheaper materials to increase margin

**Outcome:** Payment held. Supplier agreed to rework at their cost.

**Lesson:** Always book independent pre-shipment inspection.

## People Also Ask

1. **Can I verify using only an Alibaba badge?** No. Badge only proves paid membership. Cross-reference on NECIPS. See our [China Wholesale Websites](/china-wholesale-websites/) guide.

2. **How do I check if a business license is fake?** Verify 18-digit code on gsxt.gov.cn. Zoom 400% on red seal. Check company name matches across all documents.

3. **What is a safe deposit amount?** Never pay more than 30% upfront. Hold 70% until passed inspection. See our [How to Import From China](/how-to-import-from-china/) guide.

4. **How do I verify a Chinese business address?** Check on Baidu Maps or Google Maps. Request photos of building exterior. Visit if possible.

5. **What is the NECIPS database?** National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. Official Chinese government database for business verification.

6. **How do I verify ISO certificates?** Check certificate number on certification body website. Verify issuing organization is accredited.

7. **What are common supplier fraud tactics?** Bank account changes, fake licenses, quality bait-and-switch, trading companies posing as factories, IP theft.

8. **How do I protect my product design?** Sign NNN agreement enforceable in Chinese courts. Consider trademark registration in China.

9. **What is BSCI certification?** Business Social Compliance Initiative. Social audit standard for factories. Check amfori.org for verification.

10. **How do I verify factory capacity?** Request production reports, check machinery count, verify worker numbers, review QC records.

11. **What payment methods are safest?** T/T bank wire to verified corporate account (30/70 split). Alibaba Trade Assurance for added protection.

12. **How do I verify export capability?** Request export license, check export history, verify with freight forwarder, review shipping documents.
