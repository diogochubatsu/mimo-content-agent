## Supplier Verification Checklist

Before placing any order, verify your supplier with these critical checks:

### Required Verification Steps

1. **Business License** — Confirm the company is registered and active
2. **Platform Status** — Check for 诚信通 (Integrity Pass) or 诚信通 (Strong Merchant) on 1688
3. **Transaction History** — Review past 12 months of sales data
4. **Factory Photos** — Request actual production facility images
5. **Video Audit** — Conduct live video call to verify operations
6. **Sample Order** — Always order samples before bulk purchasing
7. **Certifications** — Verify FDA, LFGB, and other relevant certifications

### Red Flags to Avoid

- Suppliers unwilling to provide business license
- No transaction history or very new accounts
- Prices significantly below market average (too good to be true)
- Requests for payment via personal bank accounts
- No factory photos or unwillingness to do video calls
- Pressure to order large quantities immediately

## FAQ

**Q: What certifications do kitchen gadgets need?**
A: Kitchen gadgets require food-safe certifications: FDA (US), LFGB (EU), and sometimes BPA-free certification. Always request certificates from suppliers and verify authenticity.

**Q: How do I test kitchen gadget quality before ordering?**
A: Order 2-3 samples. Test: durability (drop test), material quality (food-safe smell), functionality (use as intended), and packaging quality. Check certifications match actual product.

**Q: What's the best shipping method for kitchen gadgets?**
A: Sea freight is most cost-effective for bulk orders (100+ units). Air freight works for medium orders (50-100 units) with faster delivery. Express shipping is best for samples and urgent orders.

**Q: Can I create a private label brand for kitchen gadgets?**
A: Absolutely. Private label requires higher MOQs (500+ units) but allows 2-3x higher margins. Include custom packaging, brand logo, and instruction manual. Start with white-label products to test market.

**Q: How do I handle quality issues with kitchen gadgets?**
A: Prevention is key: order samples first, use quality inspection services, get photos before shipping. For defective products, work with supplier to negotiate replacement or refund. Keep documentation for disputes.

**Q: Are there seasonal trends for kitchen gadget sales?**
A: Yes, sales peak during holidays (Christmas, Mother's Day, Father's Day) and wedding season (May-September). Plan inventory 2 months ahead. Summer sees higher demand for grilling accessories.

**Q: What's the average lead time for kitchen gadget orders from China?**
A: Standard production: 10-20 days. Custom/branded orders: 20-30 days. Sample orders: 5-10 days. Add 5-45 days for shipping depending on method (express vs. sea freight).

**Q: Can I sell kitchen gadgets on multiple platforms?**
A: Yes, but consider platform-specific strategies. Amazon for volume, Etsy for handmade/artisanal, Shopify for brand building, Walmart for mainstream reach. Each platform has different fee structures.

**Q: How do I protect my kitchen gadget brand?**
A: Register trademarks in target markets, use Amazon Brand Registry, create unique packaging designs, and consider design patents for innovative products. Monitor competitors for copycats.

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity for kitchen gadgets on 1688?**
A: MOQs vary by product and supplier. Basic items: 50-100 units. Complex gadgets: 100-500 units. Always negotiate lower MOQs for first orders to test market demand.
