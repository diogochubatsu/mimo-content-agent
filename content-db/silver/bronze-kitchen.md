## Tips

1. **Solve a problem** — The best kitchen gadgets solve specific problems. Vegetable choppers save time, silicone spatulas don't scratch pans, magnetic jars save space. Focus on problem-solving products.

2. **Viral potential** — Products that look satisfying in videos sell better. Think: self-stirring pots, one-click egg separators, automatic soap dispensers. These go viral on TikTok/Instagram.

3. **Material safety is critical** — Kitchen products must be food-safe. Always request FDA/LFGB certification for silicone products. Non-certified products can be recalled.

4. **Bundle for value** — Single gadgets sell for $10-15. Bundles (3-5 pieces) sell for $25-35 with higher margins. Include a carrying case or stand.

5. **Seasonal gifting** — Kitchen gadgets peak during holidays (Christmas, Mother's Day). Plan inventory 2 months ahead for these seasons.

## Detailed Import Process

Importing from China involves several key steps:

1. **Product Research** — Validate demand using Google Trends, Amazon BSR, TikTok trends
2. **Supplier Discovery** — Use 1688 (cheapest), Alibaba (safest), or Global Sources (verified)
3. **Sample Evaluation** — Order 2-3 samples ($10-50 each) before bulk
4. **Supplier Verification** — Check business license, years on platform, reviews, live video audit
5. **Negotiation** — Use competitive quotes to negotiate 10-20% price reduction
6. **Payment** — 30/70 split via Trade Assurance. Never 100% upfront

## FAQ

**Q: What kitchen gadgets sell best on Amazon?**
A: Top sellers include: vegetable choppers, silicone spatula sets, magnetic spice jars, automatic soap dispensers, and egg cookers. Check Amazon Best Sellers in "Kitchen Gadgets" for current trends.

**Q: How do I ensure food safety compliance?**
A: Request FDA (US) or LFGB (EU) certification from suppliers. For silicone products, ensure they're BPA-free and heat-resistant up to 450°F. Most quality suppliers provide these certificates.

**Q: What's the best way to photograph kitchen gadgets?**
A: Use lifestyle shots (product in use) rather than white background only. Show the problem it solves. Include size reference (hand, common kitchen items). Video demonstrations convert 3x better.

**Q: How do I handle product recalls?**
A: Keep records of all certifications and supplier agreements. For Amazon, enroll in Product Liability Insurance ($500-1000/year). This protects you if products are recalled.

**Q: Can I create my own brand?**
A: Yes! Private labeling is common. Most suppliers offer custom branding for 100+ units. Create a brand name, logo, and custom packaging. This allows 2-3x price increase over generic products.
