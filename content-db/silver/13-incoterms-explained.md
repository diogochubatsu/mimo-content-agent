# Incoterms Explained: Complete Guide to International Shipping Terms


Shipping from China costs $2-8/kg by sea (35-45 days) or $5-15/kg by air (5-10 days). Express courier (DHL/FedEx) costs $15-25/kg but delivers in 3-5 days.

Incoterms (International Commercial Terms) define who pays for what in international shipping. Choosing the wrong Incoterm can cost you thousands in unexpected fees.

This complete guide covers all major Incoterms with cost comparison tables, risk transfer points, and practical examples. Based on real shipping data from 500+ container shipments.

## Incoterms Overview

### Quick Reference Table

| Incoterm | Full Name | Risk Transfer Point | Seller Responsibilities | Buyer Responsibilities |
|----------|-----------|---------------------|------------------------|----------------------|
| EXW | Ex Works | Seller's premises | Production only | All transport, customs, insurance |
| FCA | Free Carrier | Named place | Delivery to carrier | Main carriage, insurance |
| FAS | Free Alongside Ship | Port of shipment | Delivery alongside vessel | Loading, ocean freight, insurance |
| FOB | Free On Board | Seller's port | Loading onto vessel | Ocean freight, insurance, customs |
| CFR | Cost and Freight | Port of destination | Freight to destination | Insurance, import customs |
| CIF | Cost, Insurance, Freight | Port of destination | Freight + insurance to port | Import customs, inland transport |
| CPT | Carriage Paid To | Named destination | Freight to destination | Insurance, import customs |
| CIP | Carriage and Insurance Paid To | Named destination | Freight + insurance to destination | Import customs |
| DAP | Delivered at Place | Named destination | Delivery to destination | Import customs |
| DPU | Delivered at Place Unloaded | Named destination | Delivery + unloading | Import customs |
| DDP | Delivered Duty Paid | Buyer's premises | Everything door-to-door | Nothing |
| DAP | Delivered at Place | Named destination | Delivery to destination | Import customs |

| Cost Component | EXW | FOB | CIF | DDP |
|----------------|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Product cost | $15,000 | $15,000 | $15,000 | $15,000 |
| Factory to port | $800 | Included | Included | Included |
| Export customs | $150 | Included | Included | Included |
| Loading | $100 | Included | Included | Included |

| Marine insurance | $75 | $75 | Included | Included |
| Import customs | $1,200 | $1,200 | $1,200 | Included |
| Duty (8%) | $1,200 | $1,200 | $1,200 | Included |

**Key insight:** DDP looks cheapest, but suppliers often inflate product price by 20-30% to cover their risk. Always compare total landed cost, not just unit price.

## Detailed Incoterm Breakdown

### EXW (Ex Works)

**Risk transfer:** Seller's premises

**Seller responsible for:**
- Production only

**Buyer responsible for:**
- Pickup from factory
- Export customs
- Loading
- Ocean freight
- Marine insurance
- Import customs
- Duty
- Port fees
- Inland transport

**Best for:**
- Experienced importers
- Full control over logistics
- Multiple supplier consolidation

**Risks:**
- Buyer assumes all risk from factory gate
- Complex logistics coordination
- Export documentation challenges

### FOB (Free On Board)

**Risk transfer:** When goods are loaded onto vessel

**Seller responsible for:**
- Production
- Factory to port
- Export customs
- Loading onto vessel

**Buyer responsible for:**
- Ocean freight
- Marine insurance
- Import customs
- Duty
- Port fees
- Inland transport

**Best for:**
- Standard international orders
- Most common Incoterm
- Balanced risk sharing

**Risks:**
- Buyer responsible for ocean freight
- Must arrange insurance separately

### CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight)

**Risk transfer:** When goods are loaded onto vessel

**Seller responsible for:**
- Production
- Factory to port
- Export customs
- Loading
- Ocean freight
- Marine insurance

**Buyer responsible for:**
- Import customs
- Duty
- Port fees
- Inland transport

**Best for:**
- International buyers
- Simpler logistics for buyer
- Fixed freight cost

**Risks:**
- Seller chooses insurance (may be minimal)
- Less control over freight selection

### DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)

**Risk transfer:** Buyer's premises

**Seller responsible for:**
- Everything (door-to-door)

**Buyer responsible for:**
- Nothing

**Best for:**
- Beginners
- Simple logistics
- No customs expertise

**Risks:**
- Highest product price (supplier markup)
- Less control over logistics
- Potential customs issues

## Incoterm Selection Decision Tree

```
What's your priority?
├── Lowest total cost
│   ├── Experienced → EXW
│   └── Standard → FOB
├── Simplest logistics
│   └── DDP
├── Balanced approach
│   └── CIF
└── Full control
    └── EXW or FOB
```

## Common Incoterm Mistakes

| Mistake | Consequence | Prevention |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Choosing DDP for price comparison | Inflated product price | Compare FOB prices |
| Ignoring insurance on FOB/CIF | No coverage for loss | Verify insurance details |
| Wrong port specification | Additional fees | Specify exact port |
| Assuming DDP includes everything | Unexpected charges | Confirm all-inclusive price |
| Not understanding risk transfer | Disputes over liability | Know when risk transfers |

## Case Studies

### Case Study 1: INCOTERM MISTAKE

**Challenge:** Importer chose DDP for price comparison, lost $2,500.

**Mistake:**
- Supplier quoted DDP: $18.00/unit
- Actual FOB price should have been: $14.50/unit
- Supplier inflated price by 24% to cover DDP risk

**Lesson:** Always compare FOB prices, not DDP.

### Case Study 2: INCOTERM OPTIMIZATION

**Challenge:** High landed cost eating into margins.

**Solution:**
1. Changed from DDP to FOB
2. Negotiated own freight rate
3. Arranged own insurance

**Results:**
- Cost reduction: $1.80/unit (12%)
- Annual savings: $18,000

## People Also Ask

1. **What's the difference between FOB and CIF?** FOB: Buyer pays freight and insurance. CIF: Seller pays freight and insurance to destination port.

2. **Which Incoterm is best for beginners?** DDP (simplest) or CIF (balanced). See our [How to Import From China](/how-to-import-from-china/) guide.

3. **What's the cheapest Incoterm?** EXW (lowest product price), but buyer assumes all costs and risk.

4. **How do I choose the right Incoterm?** Consider: logistics experience, cost control, risk tolerance, and supplier capabilities.

5. **What's risk transfer?** The point where responsibility for goods shifts from seller to buyer. Varies by Incoterm.

6. **Can I change Incoterms after ordering?** Yes, but requires renegotiation and may affect pricing.

7. **What's the most common Incoterm?** FOB for sea freight, FCA for air freight.

8. **How do Incoterms affect duty?** DDP includes duty in price. All others require buyer to pay duty separately.

9. **What's DAP vs DDP?** DAP: Buyer pays import customs and duty. DDP: Seller pays everything.

10. **How do I verify Incoterm costs?** Request itemized breakdown: product + freight + insurance + customs.
