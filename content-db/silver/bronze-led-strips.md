## Supplier Verification Checklist

Before placing any order, verify your supplier with these critical checks:

### Required Verification Steps

1. **Business License** — Confirm the company is registered and active
2. **Platform Status** — Check for 诚信通 (Integrity Pass) or 诚信通 (Strong Merchant) on 1688
3. **Transaction History** — Review past 12 months of sales data
4. **Factory Photos** — Request actual production facility images
5. **Video Audit** — Conduct live video call to verify operations
6. **Sample Order** — Always order samples before bulk purchasing
7. **Certifications** — Verify CE, UL, FCC, and other relevant certifications

### Red Flags to Avoid

- Suppliers unwilling to provide business license
- No transaction history or very new accounts
- Prices significantly below market average (too good to be true)
- Requests for payment via personal bank accounts
- No factory photos or unwillingness to do video calls
- Pressure to order large quantities immediately

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Ordering without samples** — Always test brightness and quality before committing to bulk orders
2. **Ignoring certifications** — CE, UL, and FCC certifications are required for US/EU markets
3. **Using unsecured payments** — Never pay via personal bank transfers without escrow
4. **Skipping quality inspection** — Pre-shipment inspection saves money on returns
5. **Ignoring power supply quality** — Cheap power supplies cause fires and returns
6. **Over-ordering inventory** — Start with 50-100 rolls to test market demand
7. **Poor packaging** — LED strips need proper packaging to prevent damage

## Tips for Success

1. **Request samples first** — Always order 2-3 samples before committing to bulk. Test brightness, color accuracy, and adhesive quality.

2. **Check certifications** — For US/EU markets, ensure products have CE, UL, or FCC certification. Non-certified products may be seized at customs.

3. **Waterproof vs non-waterproof** — IP65 (silicone coated) costs 20-30% more but opens outdoor markets. IP20 (non-waterproof) is fine for indoor use.

4. **Power supply matters** — Cheap power supplies are the #1 cause of returns. Source quality 12V/24V adapters separately if needed.

5. **Color temperature options** — Warm white (3000K) sells better in home decor. Cool white (6000K) is preferred for commercial/office use.

## FAQ

**Q: What certifications do LED strip lights need?**
A: For US markets: UL certification is required. For EU: CE marking required. FCC certification is recommended for electromagnetic compliance. Budget $300-800 for certifications.

**Q: How do I test LED strip quality before ordering?**
A: Order 2-3 samples. Test: brightness (lumens), color accuracy, adhesive quality, and flexibility. Check certifications match actual product. Test with actual power supply.

**Q: What's the best shipping method for LED strips?**
A: Sea freight is most cost-effective for bulk orders (100+ rolls). Air freight works for medium orders (50-100 rolls). Express shipping is best for samples and urgent orders.

**Q: Can I create a private label brand for LED strips?**
A: Absolutely. Private label requires higher MOQs (200+ rolls) but allows 2-3x higher margins. Include custom packaging, brand logo, and installation guide. Start with white-label products to test market.

**Q: How do I handle quality issues with LED strips?**
A: Prevention is key: order samples first, use quality inspection services, get photos before shipping. For defective products, work with supplier to negotiate replacement or refund. Keep documentation for disputes.

**Q: Are there seasonal trends for LED strip sales?**
A: Yes, sales peak during holiday season (Nov-Dec) and back-to-school (Aug-Sep). Summer sees steady demand for outdoor/patio lighting. Plan inventory 2 months ahead.

**Q: What's the average lead time for LED strip orders from China?**
A: Standard production: 10-20 days. Custom/branded orders: 20-30 days. Sample orders: 5-10 days. Add 5-45 days for shipping depending on method.

**Q: Can I sell LED strips on multiple platforms?**
A: Yes, but consider platform-specific strategies. Amazon for volume, Home Depot for premium, Shopify for brand building, Walmart for mainstream reach. Each platform has different requirements.

**Q: How do I protect my LED strip brand?**
A: Register trademarks in target markets, use Amazon Brand Registry, create unique packaging designs, and monitor competitors for copycats. Design patents protect innovative features.

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity for LED strips on 1688?**
A: MOQs vary by product and supplier. Standard RGB: 50-100 rolls. Smart LED strips: 20-50 rolls. Waterproof strips: 50-100 rolls. Always negotiate lower MOQs for first orders.
