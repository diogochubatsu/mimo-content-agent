## Tips

1. **Request samples first** — Always order 2-3 samples before committing to bulk. Test brightness, color accuracy, and adhesive quality.

2. **Check certifications** — For US/EU markets, ensure products have CE, UL, or FCC certification. Non-certified products may be seized at customs.

3. **Waterproof vs non-waterproof** — IP65 (silicone coated) costs 20-30% more but opens outdoor markets. IP20 (non-waterproof) is fine for indoor use.

4. **Power supply matters** — Cheap power supplies are the #1 cause of returns. Source quality 12V/24V adapters separately if needed.

5. **Color temperature options** — Warm white (3000K) sells better in home decor. Cool white (6000K) is preferred for commercial/office use.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between SMD 5050 and SMD 2835?**
A: SMD 5050 chips are larger (5x5mm) and brighter, ideal for RGB and accent lighting. SMD 2835 chips (2.8x3.5mm) are more energy-efficient and cheaper, perfect for general照明. SMD 5050 costs 30-40% more but offers better margins in premium markets.

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity on 1688?**
A: Most 1688 suppliers require 50-200 rolls minimum. However, you can negotiate MOQ by ordering mixed colors/styles. Some suppliers accept 10-20 rolls for first-time buyers if you mention long-term cooperation.

**Q: How do I calculate shipping costs from China?**
A: Sea freight costs $0.50-1.00/kg for LED strips (typically 0.3-0.5kg per roll). Air freight is $3-5/kg but 5-7x faster. For 100+ rolls, sea freight is more economical. Use freight calculators on 1688 or contact suppliers for accurate quotes.

**Q: Are LED strips safe to import?**
A: Yes, but ensure compliance with destination country regulations. US requires UL certification, EU requires CE marking. Most quality 1688 suppliers can provide these certificates for an additional $50-100 per batch.

**Q: What's the return rate for LED strips?**
A: Quality suppliers show 2-5% return rates. Main issues are: defective LEDs (test before shipping), poor adhesive (use 3M backing), and wrong color temperature (specify clearly in orders).
