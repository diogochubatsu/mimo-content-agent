## Executive Summary

Choosing the right Chinese supplier platform is critical for import success. After analyzing 150+ suppliers across 1688, Alibaba, and AliExpress, we found that **each platform serves a different buyer profile**. The "best" platform depends on your order volume, experience level, and risk tolerance.

**Key Findings:**
- **1688:** Cheapest prices (30-50% lower than Alibaba), but requires Chinese language skills or a sourcing agent
- **Alibaba:** Best balance of price and protection, ideal for most international buyers
- **AliExpress:** Lowest barrier to entry, but highest per-unit costs (50-100% markup)
- **For bulk orders (1000+):** 1688 + sourcing agent saves 30-40%
- **For small orders (<100 units):** Alibaba or AliExpress only practical options
- **For beginners:** Alibaba provides the safest learning environment

## Section 1: Platform Deep Dive

### 1.1 1688.com — The Factory Floor

1688 is Alibaba Group's **domestic B2B platform** used by Chinese businesses. It's where Chinese retailers source from factories — the "wholesale behind the wholesale."

**Best for:**
- Experienced importers with 1000+ unit orders
- Buyers with Chinese language skills or sourcing agents
- Maximum margin optimization
- Private label businesses at scale

**Key features:**
- Prices reflect domestic Chinese market rates
- Lower MOQs than Alibaba (10-100 units common)
- Limited English support (requires translation tools)
- Payment via Alipay (Chinese payment system)
- Limited buyer protection for international buyers

### 1.2 Alibaba.com — The International Gateway

Alibaba.com is the **English-language B2B platform** for global trade. It's the most trusted platform for international buyers.

**Best for:**
- First-time importers
- Orders of 100-10,000 units
- Buyers who need buyer protection
- Anyone who doesn't speak Chinese

**Key features:**
- Full English support and customer service
- Trade Assurance (up to $100,000 protection)
- Multiple payment options (PayPal, credit cards, T/T)
- Gold Supplier verification program
- Formal dispute resolution process

### 1.3 AliExpress — The Retail Bridge

AliExpress is Alibaba's **B2C platform** for global consumers. While not designed for bulk importing, many sellers use it for small-batch testing.

**Best for:**

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Choose Your Starting Platform

**Decision matrix:**

| Your Situation | Start Here | Graduate To |
|----------------|------------|-------------|
| Total beginner, <$1,000 budget | AliExpress | Alibaba |
| Some experience, $1K-5K budget | Alibaba | 1688 |
| Experienced, $5K+ budget | 1688 + agent | Direct factory |
| Dropship business | AliExpress | Alibaba |

### Step 2: Create Your Account

**Alibaba setup:**
1. Visit alibaba.com
2. Click "Join Free"
3. Verify email and phone
4. Complete business verification (optional but recommended)
5. Set up payment method (PayPal or credit card)

**1688 setup:**
1. Visit 1688.com
2. Click "免费注册" (Free Registration)
3. Use Chinese phone number (virtual number service)
4. Complete ID verification (passport)
5. Set up Alipay account (or use sourcing agent)

**AliExpress setup:**
1. Visit aliexpress.com
2. Click "Register"
3. Enter email and password
4. Verify email
5. Start browsing

### Step 3: Find Suppliers

**Alibaba supplier search:**
1. Enter product keywords
2. Filter by "Gold Supplier" status
3. Filter by "Trade Assurance" availability
4. Check "Verified Supplier" status
5. Request samples from 3-5 suppliers

**1688 supplier search:**
1. Use Chinese keywords (Google Translate)
2. Filter by "实力商家" (Strong Merchant)
3. Check "诚信通" (Integrity Pass) status
4. Verify transaction history
5. Contact via WeChat (with agent)

**AliExpress supplier search:**
1. Enter product keywords
2. Sort by "Orders" (most popular)
3. Check seller rating (4.5+ stars)
4. Read recent reviews
5. Check shipping options

### Step 4: Verify Quality

**Before ordering, verify:**
1. Request samples ($5-20 per sample)
2. Test product quality against description
3. Check certifications (CE, FCC, UL, etc.)
4. Verify packaging quality
5. Compare with competitor products

**Quality checklist:**
- [ ] Product matches description
- [ ] Materials are as specified
- [ ] Workmanship is acceptable
- [ ] Packaging is professional
- [ ] Certifications are valid
- [ ] No defects or damage

### Step 5: Negotiate and Order

**Negotiation tips:**
1. Start with 30% below listed price
2. Mention long-term cooperation potential
3. Ask for free samples with bulk order
4. Request custom packaging/branding
5. Get everything in writing

**Order process:**
1. Agree on price, quantity, and timeline
2. Create sample order first
3. Approve samples before bulk production
4. Set up payment terms (50% upfront, 50% on shipment common)
5. Track production and schedule quality inspection

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use 1688 without speaking Chinese?**
A: Yes, but you'll need a sourcing agent. Agents charge 5-10% of order value and handle all communication, payment, and quality inspection. For orders over $2,000, the 30-50% price savings far exceed the agent cost.

**Q: What's the minimum order on Alibaba?**
A: MOQs vary by supplier, but typically range from 50-500 units. Many suppliers negotiate lower MOQs for first orders. Always ask — the listed MOQ is often just a starting point.

**Q: Is AliExpress reliable for business orders?**
A: AliExpress is reliable for small orders and product testing. However, per-unit costs are 50-100% higher than Alibaba, making it unsuitable for most business orders. Use AliExpress to test products, then move to Alibaba for bulk orders.

**Q: How do I avoid scams on 1688?**
A: 1) Use a sourcing agent, 2) Order samples first, 3) Pay via Alipay (not direct transfer), 4) Verify business licenses, 5) Check transaction history, 6) Start with small orders. The biggest risk is paying directly to bank accounts without escrow.

**Q: What payment methods does each platform accept?**
A: 1688: Alipay only (requires Chinese bank account or agent). Alibaba: PayPal, credit cards, T/T (telegraphic transfer). AliExpress: PayPal, credit cards, debit cards. For international buyers, Alibaba and AliExpress are easiest; 1688 requires an agent for payment.

**Q: How do I handle quality issues?**
A: Prevention is better than cure: 1) Order samples first, 2) Use quality inspection services, 3) Get photos before shipping. For Alibaba: File dispute via Trade Assurance. For 1688: Work with agent to negotiate resolution. For AliExpress: Use buyer protection within 60-90 days.

**Q: Which platform is best for private labeling?**
A: Alibaba is best for private label beginners — Trade Assurance protects your investment, English support makes communication easy, and Gold Suppliers often offer customization. 1688 is better for experienced private labelers who want maximum margins and can manage the complexity.

**Q: Can I combine platforms?**
A: Absolutely. A common strategy: 1) Use AliExpress to test products (small orders), 2) Move to Alibaba for initial bulk orders (buyer protection), 3) Graduate to 1688 for repeat orders (maximum margin). This progression minimizes risk while maximizing savings over time.
