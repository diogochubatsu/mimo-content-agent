## Tips

1. **Niche down** — Don't sell generic cases. Focus on specific niches: eco-friendly materials, MagSafe compatible, luxury leather, or sports cases.

2. **Customization is king** — Cases with custom prints or personalization sell for 2-3x more. Find suppliers who offer low-MOQ printing.

3. **Material matters** — TPU is cheapest but perceived as low quality. Silicone and leather command premium prices. Hybrid cases (TPU + PC) offer best value.

4. **Check compatibility** — Always verify exact phone model compatibility. iPhone cases sell 3x more than Android cases on Amazon US.

5. **Seasonal demand** — Phone case demand spikes 2 weeks before new iPhone launches (September). Plan inventory accordingly.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the best material for phone cases?**
A: It depends on your target market. TPU ($0.30-0.50) is cheapest but feels cheap. Silicone ($0.80-1.20) offers better grip. Leather ($2-4) commands premium prices. For maximum profit, focus on silicone or leather cases.

**Q: How do I get custom designs printed?**
A: Most 1688 suppliers offer custom printing for 100+ pieces. Upload your design, choose placement (back, sides, or full wrap), and pay $0.20-0.50 extra per unit for printing. Use Canva or hire a designer on Fiverr for $5-20.

**Q: What phone models should I target?**
A: Focus on the latest iPhone models (iPhone 15/16 series) — they have 3x higher case demand than Android. Samsung Galaxy is second. Always check Amazon Best Sellers for current top models.

**Q: How do I handle returns?**
A: Phone case returns average 5-8%. Main reasons: wrong size, poor fit, color mismatch. Always order samples first, verify exact dimensions, and use accurate product photos.

**Q: Can I sell cases without a brand?**
A: Yes, but branded cases sell better. Create a simple brand name and logo (use Canva), add it to packaging. This allows you to build customer loyalty and charge premium prices.
