## Supplier Verification Checklist

Before placing any order, verify your supplier with these critical checks:

### Required Verification Steps

1. **Business License** — Confirm the company is registered and active
2. **Platform Status** — Check for 诚信通 (Integrity Pass) or 诚信通 (Strong Merchant) on 1688
3. **Transaction History** — Review past 12 months of sales data
4. **Factory Photos** — Request actual production facility images
5. **Video Audit** — Conduct live video call to verify operations
6. **Sample Order** — Always order samples before bulk purchasing
7. **Certifications** — Verify quality certifications and material safety

### Red Flags to Avoid

- Suppliers unwilling to provide business license
- No transaction history or very new accounts
- Prices significantly below market average (too good to be true)
- Requests for payment via personal bank accounts
- No factory photos or unwillingness to do video calls
- Pressure to order large quantities immediately

## FAQ

**Q: What materials are best for phone cases?**
A: TPU is cheapest but least premium. Silicone offers better grip and feel. Leather provides luxury appeal. Hybrid (TPU + PC) offers best protection. MagSafe-compatible materials are trending.

**Q: How do I ensure phone case fit quality?**
A: Order samples for specific phone models. Test fit with actual devices. Check button alignment, camera cutouts, and port access. Poor fit is the #1 reason for returns.

**Q: What's the best shipping method for phone cases?**
A: Sea freight is most cost-effective for bulk orders (500+ units). Air freight works for medium orders (100-500 units). Express shipping is best for samples and urgent orders.

**Q: Can I create a custom design phone case brand?**
A: Absolutely. Custom design requires lower MOQs (50-100 units) but allows 2-3x higher margins. Include unique artwork, brand logo, and premium packaging.

**Q: How do I handle quality issues with phone cases?**
A: Prevention is key: order samples first, use quality inspection services, get photos before shipping. For defective products, work with supplier to negotiate replacement or refund.

**Q: Are there seasonal trends for phone case sales?**
A: Yes, sales peak during iPhone launch season (September-October) and holiday season (November-December). Plan inventory 2 months ahead. Android launches create smaller peaks.

**Q: What's the average lead time for phone case orders from China?**
A: Standard production: 7-15 days. Custom/branded orders: 15-25 days. Sample orders: 3-7 days. Add 5-45 days for shipping depending on method.

**Q: Can I sell phone cases for multiple brands?**
A: Yes, but focus on high-volume brands first. iPhone cases sell 3x more than Android on Amazon US. Samsung and Google Pixel cases have growing markets.

**Q: How do I protect my phone case brand?**
A: Register trademarks in target markets, use Amazon Brand Registry, create unique packaging designs, and monitor competitors for copycats. Design patents protect unique shapes.

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity for phone cases on 1688?**
A: MOQs vary by product and supplier. Basic TPU: 100-500 units. Custom design: 50-100 units. Premium leather: 50-200 units. Always negotiate lower MOQs for first orders.
