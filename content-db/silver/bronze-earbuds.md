## Tips

1. **Quality control is critical** — Always request samples and test: sound quality, Bluetooth connectivity, battery life, and build quality. Cheap earbuds have high return rates.

2. **Certifications required** — For US/EU markets, earbuds need FCC, CE, and sometimes Bluetooth SIG certification. Budget $200-500 for certifications.

3. **Battery life matters** — Market earbuds with 4-6 hours battery life minimum. Include charging case specs (20-30 hours total). This is a key selling point.

4. **ANC is premium** — Active Noise Cancellation earbuds sell for 2-3x more. Source ANC models if targeting premium market.

5. **Branding opportunity** — Custom packaging and logo printing adds $0.50-1.00 per unit but allows 3-5x price increase.

## Detailed Import Process

Importing from China involves several key steps:

1. **Product Research** — Validate demand using Google Trends, Amazon BSR, TikTok trends
2. **Supplier Discovery** — Use 1688 (cheapest), Alibaba (safest), or Global Sources (verified)
3. **Sample Evaluation** — Order 2-3 samples ($10-50 each) before bulk
4. **Supplier Verification** — Check business license, years on platform, reviews, live video audit
5. **Negotiation** — Use competitive quotes to negotiate 10-20% price reduction
6. **Payment** — 30/70 split via Trade Assurance. Never 100% upfront
7. **Quality Control** — Pre-shipment inspection (SGS/Intertek, $100-300)
8. **Shipping** — Sea: $2-8/kg (35-45d), Air: $5-15/kg (5-10d), Express: $15-25/kg (3-5d)
9. **Customs** — Check HTS codes, prepare commercial invoice + packing list
10. **Quality on Arrival** — Inspect 100% on first order, file claims if needed

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between TWS and regular Bluetooth earbuds?**
A: TWS (True Wireless Stereo) means no wires between earpieces. Regular Bluetooth earbuds have a wire connecting them. TWS is more popular and commands higher prices. Always source TWS for better margins.

**Q: How do I ensure quality from Chinese suppliers?**
A: 1) Order samples first. 2) Request certifications (FCC, CE). 3) Use Alibaba Trade Assurance. 4) Start with small orders (50-100 pcs). 5) Consider hiring a QC inspector for large orders ($100-200).

**Q: What's the minimum order for custom branding?**
A: Most suppliers offer custom branding (logo, packaging) for 100+ units. Some accept 50 units for logo printing. Full custom packaging usually requires 500+ units.

**Q: How do I handle warranty claims?**
A: Budget 3-5% for warranty claims. Most Chinese suppliers offer 6-12 month warranty. Keep spare units for replacements. For Amazon, FBA handles returns automatically.

**Q: Are there any legal restrictions on importing earbuds?**
A: Yes. Earbuds with Bluetooth must comply with FCC (US), CE (EU), and local regulations. Some countries require specific Bluetooth version certifications. Check import regulations before ordering.
