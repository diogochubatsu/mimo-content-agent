## Supplier Verification Checklist

Before placing any order, verify your supplier with these critical checks:

### Required Verification Steps

1. **Business License** — Confirm the company is registered and active
2. **Platform Status** — Check for 诚信通 (Integrity Pass) or 诚信通 (Strong Merchant) on 1688
3. **Transaction History** — Review past 12 months of sales data
4. **Factory Photos** — Request actual production facility images
5. **Video Audit** — Conduct live video call to verify operations
6. **Sample Order** — Always order samples before bulk purchasing
7. **Certifications** — Verify CE, RoHS, and other relevant certifications

### Red Flags to Avoid

- Suppliers unwilling to provide business license
- No transaction history or very new accounts
- Prices significantly below market average (too good to be true)
- Requests for payment via personal bank accounts
- No factory photos or unwillingness to do video calls
- Pressure to order large quantities immediately

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between latex and fabric resistance bands?**
A: Latex bands are cheaper ($0.30-0.80) but can snap and cause injuries. Fabric bands ($1.50-3.50) are non-slip, more durable, and safer. For premium markets, always choose fabric.

**Q: What resistance levels should I include in a set?**
A: Standard 5-band sets include: X-Light (5-10 lbs), Light (10-20 lbs), Medium (20-30 lbs), Heavy (30-40 lbs), X-Heavy (40-50 lbs). This covers all fitness levels and exercises.

**Q: How do I test quality before bulk ordering?**
A: Order 2-3 sample sets. Test: stretch recovery (should return to original shape), snap resistance (stretch 3x length), and color fastness (wash 5 times). Poor quality bands lose elasticity quickly.

**Q: What certifications are needed for resistance bands?**
A: Resistance bands don't require specific certifications for most markets. However, for EU, ensure compliance with REACH regulations (no harmful chemicals). For US, general product safety standards apply. Children's products require CPSIA compliance.

**Q: Can I sell resistance bands as "fitness equipment"?**
A: Yes, but be specific. Use keywords like "resistance bands for exercise," "workout bands," "physical therapy bands." Different audiences: fitness enthusiasts, physical therapy patients, home gym owners.

**Q: What's the best shipping method for resistance bands?**
A: Sea freight is most cost-effective for bulk orders (100+ sets). Air freight works for medium orders (50-100 sets) with faster delivery. Express shipping is best for samples and urgent orders.

**Q: How do I handle quality issues with resistance bands?**
A: Prevention is key: order samples first, use quality inspection services, get photos before shipping. For defective products, work with supplier to negotiate replacement or refund. Keep documentation for disputes.

**Q: Are there seasonal trends for resistance band sales?**
A: Yes, sales peak in January (New Year resolutions) and March (summer body prep). Plan inventory 2 months ahead. Summer and fall have steady but lower demand. Holiday season (Nov-Dec) sees gift purchases.

**Q: Can I create a private label brand for resistance bands?**
A: Absolutely. Private label requires higher MOQs (500+ units) but allows 2-3x higher margins. Include custom packaging, brand logo, and exercise guide. Start with white-label products to test market.

**Q: What's the average lead time for resistance band orders from China?**
A: Standard production: 10-20 days. Custom/branded orders: 20-30 days. Sample orders: 5-10 days. Add 5-45 days for shipping depending on method (express vs. sea freight).
