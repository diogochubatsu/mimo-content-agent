## Section 1: Supply Chain Fundamentals

### What is Landed Cost?

**Landed Cost = Product Cost + Shipping + Customs + Insurance + Other Fees**

This is the TRUE cost of your product. Many importers only look at product cost and miss 30-50% of their actual expenses.

**Landed Cost Breakdown:**

| Component | Typical % of Landed Cost |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| Product cost (FOB) | 40-60% |
| Shipping | 15-25% |
| Customs duties | 5-15% |
| Insurance | 1-3% |
| Customs broker fees | 2-5% |
| Other fees | 3-8% |

**Example:**
- FOB price: $5.00
- Shipping: $1.50
- Customs (10%): $0.50
- Insurance: $0.10
- Broker: $0.25
- **Landed cost: $7.35** (47% higher than FOB)

### The Supply Chain Funnel

```
Supplier Selection → Negotiation → Order → Manufacturing → QC → Shipping → Customs → Warehouse → Customer
     ↓                  ↓          ↓           ↓          ↓        ↓          ↓           ↓          ↓
  15-25%            10-15%       5%          3-5%       2-5%    15-25%     5-15%       5-10%      100%
  savings          savings      savings     savings    savings  savings    savings     savings    revenue
```

Each stage offers cost reduction opportunities. Top importers optimize every stage.

### The True Cost Calculator

**Use this formula to calculate your true landed cost:**

```
Landed Cost per Unit = 
  (Product Cost × Quantity)
  + (Shipping Cost per Unit × Quantity)
  + (Customs Duty Rate × (Product + Shipping))
  + (Insurance: 0.5-1% of total value)
  + (Customs Broker: $100-300 per shipment ÷ Quantity)
  + (Inland Transport: $0.20-0.50 per unit)
  + (Warehousing: $0.10-0.30 per unit per month)
  ÷ Quantity
```

## Section 3: Shipping Optimization

### Shipping Method Comparison

| Method | Cost/kg | Time | Best For |
|--------|---------|------|----------|
| Sea FCL | $0.50-1.50 | 25-40 days | 500+ kg |
| Sea LCL | $1.50-3.00 | 30-45 days | 50-500 kg |
| Air Freight | $4.00-8.00 | 5-10 days | 50-500 kg |
| Express | $10.00-20.00 | 3-5 days | < 50 kg |
| Rail (EU) | $2.00-4.00 | 15-25 days | 100+ kg |

### The Hybrid Shipping Strategy

**Most importers use one method. Top importers use a combination:**

```
80% Sea Freight (bulk, non-urgent)
+ 15% Air Freight (replenishment)
+ 5% Express (samples, urgent orders)
= 30-40% shipping cost reduction vs. 100% air
```

**Case Study:**
- Before: 100% air freight, $6.50/kg
- After: 80/15/5 hybrid, $2.80/kg
- **Savings: 57% on shipping costs**

### Container Optimization

**Full Container Load (FCL):**
- 20ft container: 28 CBM, 22 tons
- 40ft container: 58 CBM, 26 tons
- 40ft HC: 68 CBM, 26 tons

**Cost per unit reduction:**
- LCL: $3.00/kg
- FCL (partial): $1.50/kg

- **FCL saves 40-70% vs. LCL**

**Optimization tips:**
1. **Consolidate orders** — Combine multiple products in one container
2. **Mix heavy and light** — Maximize weight AND volume
3. **Plan ahead** — Book containers 2-3 weeks in advance
4. **Share containers** — Partner with other importers

### Shipping Incoterms Explained

**Recommendation:** Use FOB for most orders. You control shipping costs and can negotiate better rates.

## Section 4: Customs and Compliance

### Duty Calculation

**Formula:**
```
Duty = (Product Value + Shipping Cost) × Duty Rate
```

**Common duty rates (US):**
- Electronics: 0-5%
- Clothing: 10-25%
- Home goods: 0-10%
- Toys: 0-10%
- Automotive parts: 2.5-10%

### Duty Reduction Strategies

1. **Correct HS code classification** — Wrong code = overpaying
2. **Free Trade Agreements** — Some countries have reduced rates
3. **Bonded warehousing** — Defer duties until goods sell
4. **Foreign Trade Zones** — Duty-free processing
5. **De minimis threshold** — Under $800 (US) = duty-free

### Customs Documentation

**Required documents:**
- Commercial invoice
- Packing list
- Bill of lading/airway bill
- Certificate of origin
- Product certifications (CE, FCC, etc.)

### Common Customs Mistakes

| Mistake | Cost | Prevention |
|---------|------|------------|
| Wrong HS code | 10-30% overpayment | Hire customs broker |
| Undervaluing | Seizure + fines | Accurate invoices |
| Missing certificates | Shipment held | Verify before shipping |
| Incorrect origin | Wrong duty rate | Get certificate of origin |

## FAQ

**Q: How much can I save by optimizing my supply chain?**
A: Average savings: 45%. Top importers save 50-60%. Biggest savings come from: shipping optimization (30-50%), supplier negotiation (15-25%), and customs optimization (10-15%).

**Q: What's the biggest hidden cost in importing?**
A: Shipping. Most importers underestimate shipping costs by 30-50%. Always calculate volumetric weight, not just actual weight.

**Q: How do I find reliable suppliers on 1688?**
A: 1) Verify business license on Tianyancha, 2) Order samples first, 3) Check transaction history, 4) Use sourcing agents for first orders, 5) Build relationships over time.

**Q: What's the best shipping method for beginners?**
A: Start with sea freight (LCL) for cost savings. Use air freight for urgent orders. Express for samples. As volume grows, move to FCL.

**Q: How do I calculate duty rates?**
A: 1) Find correct HS code, 2) Check duty rate for your country, 3) Calculate customs value (product + shipping), 4) Multiply by duty rate. Use online calculators or hire a customs broker.

**Q: Can I reduce customs duties legally?**
A: Yes! 1) Use correct HS codes, 2) Leverage Free Trade Agreements, 3) Use bonded warehousing, 4) Apply for Foreign Trade Zone status, 5) Use de minimis threshold (under $800 in US).

**Q: What's the minimum order quantity for cost savings?**
A: Significant savings start at 500+ units. Major savings at 1,000+ units. Below 100 units, costs are 30-50% higher than volume pricing.

**Q: How do I handle currency fluctuations?**
A: 1) Negotiate prices in USD, 2) Use forward contracts to lock rates, 3) Build 5-10% buffer into pricing, 4) Consider multi-currency accounts.

**Q: What's the difference between FOB and CIF?**
A: FOB (Free On Board): You pay shipping from port. CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight): Seller pays shipping to destination port. FOB gives you more control and often better rates.

**Q: How do I scale my supply chain?**
A: 1) Build supplier relationships (2-3 per product), 2) Consolidate shipments, 3) Use FCL containers, 4) Hire customs broker, 5) Implement inventory management system.

## Glossary

- **FOB (Free On Board):** Seller delivers goods to port; buyer pays shipping
- **CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight):** Seller pays shipping to destination
- **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid):** Seller pays all costs to delivery
- **HS Code (Harmonized System):** International product classification
- **LCL (Less than Container Load):** Shared container space
- **FCL (Full Container Load):** Exclusive container
- **Landed Cost:** Total cost including shipping, customs, and fees
- **MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity):** Minimum units per order
- **FTA (Free Trade Agreement):** Reduced duty rates between countries
- **Incoterms:** International commercial terms defining responsibilities
