## Top 10 China Trade Shows 2026

### 1. East China Fair (March)
**Location**: Shanghai
**Date**: March 1–4, 2026
**Focus**: Textiles, garments, and consumer goods

**Key Details**:
- One of China's largest trade fairs for textiles and garments
- Attracts 20,000+ buyers from 100+ countries
- Features 5,000+ exhibitors
- Free admission for registered buyers

**Best for**: Fashion retailers, textile importers, garment buyers
**Product categories**: Fabrics, clothing, home textiles, fashion accessories

### 2. China International Furniture Fair – CIFF (March)
**Location**: Guangzhou / Shanghai
**Date**: March 2026
**Focus**: One of the world's largest furniture exhibitions

**Key Details**:
- Split between Guangzhou (Phase 1) and Shanghai (Phase 2)
- Features 4,000+ exhibitors
- Covers all furniture categories
- International buyer registration available

**Best for**: Furniture retailers, home decor importers, interior designers
**Product categories**: Home furniture, office furniture, outdoor furniture, lighting

### 3. Canton Fair (April–May)
**Location**: Guangzhou
**Date**: April 15 – May 5, 2026
**Focus**: The most comprehensive event covering nearly every product category

**Key Details**:
- China's oldest and largest trade fair (since 1957)
- Two phases covering different product categories
- 25,000+ exhibitors
- 200,000+ buyers from 210+ countries
- Free admission for registered buyers

**Phase 1 (April 15–19)**: Electronics, machinery, vehicles, lighting
**Phase 2 (April 23–27)**: Consumer goods, gifts, home decor
**Phase 3 (May 1–5)**: Textiles, clothing, sports, medical

**Best for**: All importers, especially those seeking diverse product categories
**Product categories**: Everything from electronics to textiles

### 4. CHINAPLAS (April)
**Location**: Shanghai
**Date**: April 21–24, 2026
**Focus**: Plastics and industrial materials

**Key Details**:
- Asia's largest plastics and rubber trade fair
- Features 3,500+ exhibitors
- Covers raw materials, machinery, and finished products
- International buyer program available

**Best for**: Packaging manufacturers, plastics processors, industrial buyers
**Product categories**: Plastic products, rubber materials, packaging machinery

### 5. China Beauty Expo – CBE (May)

**Date**: May 2026

- Features 2,500+ exhibitors
- Covers cosmetics, beauty tools, packaging, and ingredients
- Attracts 40,000+ professional buyers
- International buyer registration available

**Best for**: Beauty retailers, cosmetic importers, beauty tool buyers
**Product categories**: Cosmetics, skincare, beauty tools, packaging, ingredients

### 6. China International Hardware Show (September)

- Features 3,000+ exhibitors
- Covers hand tools, power tools, fasteners, and hardware

## How to Prepare for China Trade Shows

### Before the Show

1. **Research exhibitors**: Review exhibitor lists and identify targets
2. **Register early**: Most shows offer free pre-registration
3. **Prepare product briefs**: Bring detailed specifications for products you need
4. **Book accommodation**: Hotels near venues fill up quickly
5. **Arrange visa**: Check visa requirements for your country
6. **Bring business cards**: Essential for networking
7. **Download show apps**: Most shows have official apps with exhibitor maps

### During the Show

1. **Arrive early**: Beat the crowds for better supplier attention
2. **Follow a plan**: Visit target exhibitors first, then explore
3. **Take notes**: Record key information about each supplier
4. **Collect samples**: Take product samples for later evaluation
5. **Take photos**: Document products, booths, and contacts
6. **Negotiate pricing**: Trade shows often offer show specials
7. **Verify suppliers**: Check business licenses and certifications

### After the Show

1. **Organize contacts**: Sort collected business cards and information
2. **Follow up**: Contact promising suppliers within 1 week
3. **Compare options**: Evaluate all collected information
4. **Order samples**: Request samples from shortlisted suppliers
5. **Verify credentials**: Check business licenses online
6. **Plan next steps**: Decide which suppliers to work with

## Common Trade Show Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No preparation**: Arriving without a plan wastes time
2. **Ignoring MOQ**: Not understanding minimum orders
3. **Skipping verification**: Not checking supplier credentials
4. **Poor follow-up**: Not contacting suppliers after the show
5. **Over-committing**: Placing orders too quickly without comparison
6. **Ignoring logistics**: Not planning shipping and consolidation
7. **Missing trends**: Focusing only on current products, not new trends

## FAQ

### What is the biggest trade show in China?
The Canton Fair (China Import and Export Fair) is the largest and most comprehensive trade show in China, held twice annually in Guangzhou. It covers virtually every product category and attracts over 200,000 buyers from 210+ countries.

### Which trade show is best for small buyers?
Yiwu Fair is ideal for small and medium buyers due to its focus on small commodities and low MOQ requirements. Canton Fair is also accessible to small buyers with proper preparation.

### When is the best time to visit?
April and October are peak trade show seasons in China. April features Canton Fair Phase 1 and 2, while October features Canton Fair Phase 3 and Yiwu Fair.

### Do I need a visa to attend trade shows in China?
Most countries require a visa to visit China. Check the Chinese embassy or consulate in your country for specific requirements. Some trade shows offer special visa assistance for registered buyers.

### How do I register for trade shows?
Most trade shows offer free online pre-registration through their official websites. Register at least 2–4 weeks before the show to ensure admission and receive buyer badges.

### Can I place orders directly at trade shows?
Yes, many buyers place orders or make deposits at trade shows. However, it's recommended to order samples first and complete verification before committing to large orders.

### What should I bring to a trade show?
Essential items: business cards, product briefs (with photos and specifications), notebook/tablet, comfortable shoes, power bank, and appropriate business attire.

### How do I follow up after a trade show?
Contact promising suppliers within 1 week via email or WeChat. Reference your meeting, request samples if needed, and continue negotiations. Maintain organized records of all contacts.

### Are trade show prices better than online prices?
Trade show prices are often 5–15% lower than online prices because suppliers offer show specials and there's room for direct negotiation. However, always compare with online pricing.

### Can I attend multiple trade shows in one trip?
Yes, many buyers combine visits to multiple shows. For example, attend Canton Fair in Guangzhou, then visit Yiwu Market (2 hours by train). Plan your itinerary efficiently.

### What if I don't speak Chinese?
Most major trade shows have English-speaking staff and translation services. Bringing a bilingual sourcing agent can also help with communication and negotiation.
