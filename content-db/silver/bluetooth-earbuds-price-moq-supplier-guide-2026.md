## Section 3: MOQ Requirements by Supplier

### Understanding MOQ

MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) is the smallest number of units a supplier will produce or sell. Lower MOQs mean higher per-unit costs but lower upfront investment.

### MOQ Tiers

| MOQ Tier | Quantity | Price Impact | Best For |
|----------|---------|-------------|---------|
| Sample | 1-5 units | +200-300% | Testing quality |
| Small batch | 10-50 units | +50-100% | New product testing |
| Standard | 50-200 units | Base price | Regular orders |
| Bulk | 200-500 units | -10-20% | Established sellers |
| Volume | 500-1000+ units | -20-35% | High-volume sellers |

### Negotiation Tips for Lower MOQ

1. **Start with samples** - Order 5-10 units first to test quality before committing to larger orders
2. **Combine orders** - If you're sourcing multiple products, negotiate a combined MOQ
3. **Build relationship** - Consistent orders over time lead to lower MOQs
4. **Pay premium initially** - Accept higher per-unit cost for small MOQ, then negotiate down as volume grows
5. **Use platforms wisely** - Alibaba's Trade Assurance often has lower MOQs than direct factory orders
