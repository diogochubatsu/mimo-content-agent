## Platform Deep Dive: Alibaba vs 1688

### Alibaba Overview

**Strengths:**
- Largest English-language B2B platform
- Trade Assurance payment protection
- Wide product categories
- Global shipping options

**Weaknesses:**
- Many trading companies pose as factories
- Variable quality verification
- Higher prices than 1688

**Best for:** First-time importers, English speakers, small to medium orders

### 1688 Overview

**Strengths:**
- Direct factory pricing (20-40% cheaper than Alibaba)
- Real transaction invoices available
- Domestic Chinese market focus
- Higher factory verification rate

**Weaknesses:**
- Chinese language only
- No export support
- Payment requires Chinese bank account

**Best for:** Experienced importers, Chinese speakers, bulk orders

## How to Verify Suppliers on China Wholesale Websites

### Step 1: Request the Pre-Call Document Pack
Demand business license, export license, and ISO 9001 certificates.

**Required Documents Checklist:**

| Document | Purpose | Verification Method |
|----------|---------|---------------------|
| Chinese Business License | Legal entity verification | Check on gsxt.gov.cn |
| Export License | Export capability | Verify with local commerce bureau |
| ISO 9001 Certificate | Quality management | Verify certificate number online |
| BSCI Certificate | Social compliance | Check amfori.org database |
| Test Reports | Product compliance | Verify lab accreditation |

### Step 2: Demand a Live Video Audit Path
Require real-time walkthrough. Never accept pre-recorded video.

**Video Audit Protocol:**
1. Request unannounced video call during business hours
2. Start outside front gate (verify address matches license)
3. Walk inside without cuts or pauses
4. Show production floor, machinery, and workers
5. Ask production manager technical questions
6. Request close-ups of raw materials and finished goods

### Step 3: Spot Pattern Deviations on the Floor
Check traceability, organization, calibration, product flaws, and staff knowledge.

**Red Flags to Watch For:**

| Red Flag | What It Means | Action |
|----------|---------------|--------|
| No production during call | Not a real factory | Request factory tour |
| Workers can't answer technical questions | Staffing issues | Ask detailed product questions |
| No QC documentation | Quality control gaps | Request QC records |
| Dirty/disorganized floor | Management issues | Consider alternative suppliers |
| Only sales staff visible | Trading company | Verify manufacturing scope |

### Step 4: Close the Procurement Loop
Score supplier based on transparency. Request physical pre-production samples.

## Platform-Specific Sourcing Strategies

### Alibaba Strategy

1. **Filter by Gold Supplier** (3+ years membership)
2. **Use Trade Assurance** for all payments
3. **Request factory photos** with recent timestamps
4. **Negotiate samples** before bulk orders

## People Also Ask

1. **Which website is best?** Alibaba for fast supplier discovery. 1688 for lowest prices with local support. Global Sources for factory-verified suppliers.

2. **How do I spot a real factory?** Check Chinese business license on gsxt.gov.cn. Real factory lists "manufacturing" in business scope. Request live video tour.

3. **Is DDP shipping safe?** Only if you trust your forwarder completely. Demand pristine Commercial Invoice. Always verify insurance coverage.

4. **When should I hire a sourcing partner?** When annual procurement exceeds $200,000 or needs strict QC. See our [Best China Sourcing Agents](/best-china-sourcing-agents/) comparison.

5. **How do I verify a Chinese business license?** Check 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code on gsxt.gov.cn. Match company name, legal representative, and business scope.

6. **What's the difference between Alibaba and 1688?** Alibaba targets international buyers with English support. 1688 is domestic Chinese market with lower prices but no export support.

7. **Can I use PayPal on Chinese platforms?** Most Chinese platforms don't accept PayPal. Use T/T bank wire or platform payment systems (Alibaba Trade Assurance).

8. **How do I calculate total cost from Chinese suppliers?** Include: product cost + freight + insurance + duty + customs fees + port fees + inland transport. See our [Hidden Sourcing Costs](/hidden-sourcing-costs/) guide.

9. **What MOQ should I expect?** Varies by platform and product. Alibaba: $500-5,000. 1688: $200-1,000. Global Sources: $2,000-10,000.

10. **How do I protect my product design?** Sign NNN agreement enforceable in Chinese courts. Consider trademark registration. See our [Product Sourcing](/product-sourcing/) guide for IP protection.

11. **What payment methods are safest?** T/T bank wire to verified corporate account (30/70 split). Alibaba Trade Assurance for added protection. Never use Western Union or personal accounts.

12. **How do I handle language barriers on 1688?** Use Chrome auto-translate, hire Chinese-speaking agent, or use 1688's official export service.
