## Case Studies

### Case Study 1: Global Sourcing Success

**Challenge:** High product cost eating into margins.

**Solution:**
1. Moved production from USA to China
2. Established local QC team
3. Maintained local warehouse for distribution

**Results:**
- Cost reduction: 40%
- Lead time increase: +4 weeks (planned)
- Quality maintained: Same standards
- Profit margin improvement: 25%

### Case Study 2: Local Sourcing Win

**Challenge:** Stockouts during peak season, global supply chain disrupted.

**Solution:**
1. Added local supplier as backup
2. Maintained 50% local inventory
3. Used local for urgent replenishment

**Results:**
- Stockout reduction: 90%
- Lead time: 1 week vs 6 weeks
- Cost increase: 15% (worth it)
- Revenue preservation: $50,000

## People Also Ask

1. **What's the difference between global and local sourcing?** Global: Lower cost, longer lead time, higher risk. Local: Higher cost, shorter lead time, lower risk.

2. **When should I use global sourcing?** High-volume, cost-sensitive products with predictable demand. See our [Sourcing From China](/sourcing-from-china/) guide.

3. **When should I use local sourcing?** Urgent needs, high-quality requirements, or risk-averse situations.

4. **What's nearshoring?** Sourcing from nearby countries (e.g., Mexico for US). Balances cost and lead time.

5. **How do I choose between global and local?** Consider: cost priorities, lead time requirements, quality standards, and risk tolerance.

6. **Can I use both global and local?** Yes, hybrid strategy. Use global for bulk, local for urgent needs.

7. **What's the biggest risk of global sourcing?** Supply chain disruption, quality issues, and longer lead times.

8. **How do I mitigate global sourcing risks?** Multiple suppliers, safety stock, insurance, local QC team.

9. **What's the cost difference?** Global: 30-60% lower product cost. Local: 50-80% lower freight.

10. **How do I start with global sourcing?** Begin with small test orders, verify suppliers, establish QC protocols.
