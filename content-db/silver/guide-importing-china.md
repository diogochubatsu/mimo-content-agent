## Section 4: Negotiation and Ordering

### 4.1 Negotiation Strategies

**Price Negotiation:**
1. Start with 30-40% below listed price
2. Mention long-term cooperation potential
3. Ask for volume discounts
4. Request free samples with bulk order
5. Get everything in writing

**Payment Terms:**
- **T/T (Telegraphic Transfer):** Most common, 30% deposit + 70% before shipping
- **Alipay:** For 1688, buyer protection
- **PayPal:** For small orders, buyer protection
- **Letter of Credit:** For large orders ($10,000+)

### 4.2 Sample Ordering

**Sample Order Process:**
1. Request 2-3 samples from different suppliers
2. Budget $5-20 per sample + shipping
3. Test quality, packaging, and accuracy
4. Compare with competitor products
5. Document issues before bulk order

**Sample Evaluation Checklist:**
- [ ] Product matches description
- [ ] Materials are as specified
- [ ] Workmanship is acceptable
- [ ] Packaging is professional
- [ ] Certifications are valid
- [ ] Size/dimensions correct
- [ ] Colors accurate

### 4.3 Bulk Ordering

**Order Process:**
1. Finalize product specifications
2. Agree on price and quantity
3. Sign purchase agreement
4. Pay deposit (30-50%)
5. Monitor production
6. Pay remaining balance
7. Arrange shipping
8. Track delivery

## Section 5: Shipping and Logistics

### 5.1 Shipping Methods

| Method | Time | Cost | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| Air Freight | 5-7 days | $4-8/kg | Small, urgent orders |
| Sea Freight | 25-35 days | $0.50-2/kg | Large, non-urgent orders |
| Express (DHL/FedEx) | 3-5 days | $10-20/kg | Samples, small orders |
| Rail (China-Europe) | 15-20 days | $2-4/kg | Medium orders to EU |

### 5.2 Customs and Duties

**Import Duty Rates (US):**
- Electronics: 0-5%
- Clothing: 10-25%
- Home & Garden: 0-10%
- Toys: 0-10%

**How to Calculate Duties:**
1. Find HS code for your product
2. Check duty rate for your country
3. Calculate: (Product Cost + Shipping) × Duty Rate
4. Add to total landed cost

### 5.3 Shipping Tips

1. **Start with air freight** — Faster delivery, easier to track
2. **Use freight forwarders** — They handle customs and logistics
3. **Get insurance** — 2-3% of shipment value
4. **Track everything** — Use shipment tracking tools
5. **Keep records** — For customs and tax purposes

## FAQ

**Q: How much money do I need to start importing from China?**
A: Minimum $1,000-2,000 for a viable first order. This covers: samples ($100), first bulk order ($500-1,000), shipping ($200-500), and marketing ($200-500). Start small and reinvest profits.

**Q: Do I need a business license to import?**
A: For personal use, no. For business resale, yes — you'll need an LLC or corporation. This also helps with customs, taxes, and liability protection.

**Q: How do I avoid getting scammed?**
A: 1) Use Trade Assurance on Alibaba, 2) Order samples first, 3) Verify business licenses, 4) Never pay via Western Union, 5) Start with small orders, 6) Use escrow services for large orders.

**Q: What products should I avoid importing?**
A: Avoid: 1) Products requiring complex certifications (medical devices), 2) Fragile items (high shipping damage), 3) Heavy items (high shipping costs), 4) Trademarked products (legal issues), 5) Perishable goods.

**Q: How long does it take from order to delivery?**
A: Typically 4-6 weeks: 1-2 weeks production + 2-4 weeks shipping. Air freight is faster (1-2 weeks total) but 3-5x more expensive.

**Q: What about product liability insurance?**
A: Essential for selling in US/EU. Costs $500-2,000/year depending on product type and sales volume. Protects you if products cause injury or damage.

**Q: Can I import for personal use only?**
A: Yes, but customs duties still apply. Most countries allow personal imports up to $800 duty-free (US). Above that, you'll pay import duties.
