## Step 1: Filter Products for Business Viability

Stop browsing supplier directories for product ideas. In my experience, skipping business viability research guarantees disastrous test units.

Filter products by three strict criteria: market demand, manageable compliance risk, and wide margins after freight and duties.

Build a go/no-go framework. Define your target sale price, target landed cost, defect tolerance, reorder likelihood, and storage/shipping profile.

|----------|--------|-----------------|------------|

**Minimum viable score: 6.5/10**

## Step 3: Compare Quotes and Incoterms

Force an apples-to-apples comparison using a strict Request for Quote (RFQ).

Evaluate these four liability models:
- **EXW:** You handle all pickup and export logistics.
- **FOB:** The supplier loads goods at their local port.
- **CIF:** The seller pays freight to your destination port.
- **DDP:** The simplest door-to-door model.

### Incoterms Comparison Table

| Incoterm | Risk Transfer Point | Seller Responsibilities | Buyer Responsibilities | Best For |
|----------|---------------------|------------------------|----------------------|----------|
| EXW | Seller's premises | Production only | All transport, customs, insurance | Experienced importers |
| FOB | Seller's port | Loading onto vessel | Ocean freight, insurance, customs | Standard orders |
| CIF | Buyer's port | Freight + insurance to port | Import customs, inland transport | International buyers |
| DDP | Buyer's premises | Everything door-to-door | Nothing | Beginners, Amazon FBA |

### Cost Comparison Example: 1,000 Units @ $15/unit

| Cost Component | EXW | FOB | CIF | DDP |
|----------------|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Product cost | $15,000 | $15,000 | $15,000 | $15,000 |
| Factory to port (truck) | $800 | Included | Included | Included |
| Export customs | $150 | Included | Included | Included |
| Ocean freight | $2,200 | $2,200 | Included | Included |
| Marine insurance | $75 | $75 | Included | Included |
| Import customs | $1,200 | $1,200 | $1,200 | Included |
| Duty (8%) | $1,200 | $1,200 | $1,200 | Included |
| Port to warehouse | $400 | $400 | $400 | Included |
| **Total landed cost** | **$21,025** | **$20,075** | **$17,800** | **$15,000** |
| **Per unit landed** | **$21.03** | **$20.08** | **$17.80** | **$15.00** |

**Key insight:** DDP looks cheapest, but suppliers often inflate product price by 20-30% to cover their risk. Always compare total landed cost, not just unit price.

## Step 4: Order and Inspect the Golden Sample

Follow three stages:
1. Order an off-the-shelf sample to check baseline capabilities.
2. Request a revised sample with custom modifications.
3. Approve the exact golden sample before mass production.

### Sample Inspection Protocol

| Stage | Purpose | Timeline | Cost | Pass Criteria |
|-------|---------|----------|------|---------------|
| 1. Off-the-shelf | Baseline quality | 3-5 days | $20-50 | Functional, no defects |
| 2. Revised sample | Custom modifications | 7-14 days | $50-200 | Matches specs exactly |
| 3. Golden sample | Production standard | 3-5 days | $0 (free) | Identical to final product |

### Golden Sample Checklist

- [ ] Dimensions match ±2mm tolerance
- [ ] Colors match Pantone reference
- [ ] Materials match specification
- [ ] Packaging meets Amazon FBA requirements
- [ ] Labels are correct and readable
- [ ] All components present and functional
- [ ] Weight within 5% of target

## Step 5: Calculate Landed Costs and Verify Compliance

First, identify your product's HS Code. Next, verify your category compliance rules. Now, calculate your true landed cost.

### Landed Cost Formula

```
Landed Cost = Product Cost + Freight + Insurance + Customs Duty + Customs Brokerage + Port Fees + Inland Transport + Storage
```

### Complete Landed Cost Calculator

| Cost Component | Percentage of Landed Cost | Typical Range |
|----------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| Product cost | 50-65% | $5-50/unit |
| Ocean freight | 10-15% | $2,000-4,000/container |
| Customs duty | 8-15% | 0-25% of value |
| Customs brokerage | 3-5% | $150-300/shipment |
| Insurance | 0.5-1% | 0.3-1% of value |
| Port fees | 2-4% | $200-600 |
| Inland transport | 3-6% | $300-800 |
| Storage | 2-5% | $10-25/pallet/day |

## People Also Ask

1. **Do I need a license to import from China?** No general import license needed for commercial goods into the US. Specific agencies require permits for regulated items.

2. **How much money do I need to start importing?** Between $1,000 and $5,000 for a viable test order. Budget 3x landed cost for working capital.

3. **What is the cheapest shipping method from China?** Sea freight offers the lowest cost per unit for bulk orders exceeding two cubic meters.

4. **How do I pay Chinese suppliers safely?** Direct bank wire to a verified corporate account. Never use Western Union or personal accounts. Use 30/70 payment split.

5. **How long does shipping from China take?** Sea freight: 30-45 days. Air freight: 7-10 days. Express courier: 3-5 days.

6. **What is FOB vs CIF vs DDP?** FOB: supplier loads at port. CIF: seller pays freight to destination. DDP: door-to-door, no buyer customs responsibility.

7. **How do I calculate landed cost?** Landed Cost = Product Cost + Freight + Insurance + Duty + Customs Fees + Port Fees + Inland Transport.

8. **What is AQL in quality inspection?** Acceptable Quality Limit: the maximum percentage of defective units considered acceptable. Standard is AQL 2.5 for consumer goods.

9. **Do I need a customs broker?** Yes, for first-time importers. Brokers handle documentation, duty calculation, and customs clearance. Cost: $150-300 per shipment.

10. **How do I protect my product design?** Sign an NNN agreement (Non-disclosure, Non-use, Non-circumvention) enforceable in Chinese courts. Consider trademark registration in China.

11. **What are common customs delays?** Missing documents, HS code disputes, random inspections, unpaid duties, and compliance issues with regulated products.

12. **Can I import samples before bulk orders?** Yes, use express courier for samples. Cost: $15-30/kg. Timeline: 3-5 days. Always test samples before committing to bulk orders.

13. **How do I find reliable freight forwarders?** Get quotes from 3+ forwarders, check reviews, verify insurance coverage, and test with a small shipment before committing to large orders.

14. **What is Amazon FBA prep?** Includes labeling (FNSKU), packaging (poly bags, bubble wrap), and shipping to Amazon warehouse. See our [Amazon FBA Prep Services](/amazon-fba-prep-services/) guide.

15. **How do I handle customs duty refunds?** File for duty drawback within 5 years of import. Requires proof of export or destruction of imported goods.
