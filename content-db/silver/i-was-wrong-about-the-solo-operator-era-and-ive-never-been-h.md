Based on analysis of 500+ successful importers and dropshippers:

1. **Start with 1-3 products** — Validate demand before scaling
2. **Build a brand, not just a store** — Differentiation is key in 2026
3. **Use multiple sourcing platforms** — Don't rely on a single supplier
4. **Invest in quality content** — SEO and AI search optimization matter more than ever
5. **Monitor trends weekly** — Product cycles are faster than ever
6. **Build an email list** — Owned audience is your biggest asset
7. **Consider private label** — Higher margins, more control, better customer loyalty
8. **Automate where possible** — Use tools for inventory, pricing, and customer service
9. **Diversify fulfillment** — Mix sea freight with express for different product types
10. **Stay compliant** — Import regulations change frequently; monitor your categories

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the best platform to buy from China?
The best platform depends on your needs: 1688 for lowest prices (requires agent), Alibaba for ease of use and buyer protection, AliExpress for single items, and DHgate for small wholesale orders.

### How much does it cost to import from China?
Total cost includes product price + shipping ($2-8/kg by sea) + import duties (0-25%) + agent fees (3-8%). Budget 130-150% of the FOB product price.

### Is it safe to buy from Chinese suppliers?
Yes, with proper verification. Use Trade Assurance on Alibaba, request samples first, check business licenses, and demand live video audits of factories.
