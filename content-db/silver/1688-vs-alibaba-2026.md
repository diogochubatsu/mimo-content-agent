## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Assess Your Readiness for 1688

**Before choosing 1688, ensure you have:**
- [ ] Access to a reliable Chinese-speaking agent
- [ ] Ability to use Alipay or agent payment services
- [ ] Experience importing from China (at least 2-3 Alibaba orders)
- [ ] Time to build supplier relationships (3-6 months)
- [ ] Budget for samples ($200-500 initially)

**If you answered NO to any of these, start with Alibaba.**

### Step 2: Find and Vet 1688 Suppliers

**Supplier Search Process:**
1. Use Chinese keywords (Google Translate or agent assistance)
2. Filter by "实力商家" (Strong Merchant) status
3. Check "诚信通" (Integrity Pass) membership duration (longer = better)
4. Review transaction history and buyer ratings
5. Request business license and factory photos
6. Order samples from 3-5 suppliers before committing

**Red Flags to Avoid:**
- New accounts (< 1 year)
- No transaction history
- Prices significantly below market average
- Refusal to provide factory photos
- No "诚信通" membership

### Step 3: Set Up Payment System

**Option A: Direct Alipay (Advanced)**
1. Open international Alipay account
2. Link to Chinese bank card or international card
3. Complete identity verification
4. Fund account for purchases

**Option B: Sourcing Agent (Recommended)**
1. Research reputable agents (Superbuy, Wegobuy, CSSBuy, or private agents)
2. Create account and verify identity
3. Fund agent account via PayPal/credit card
4. Agent handles all 1688 payments

### Step 4: Negotiate and Place Orders

**Negotiation Strategy:**
1. Start with 20-30% below listed price
2. Mention long-term cooperation potential
3. Ask for free samples with first bulk order
4. Request custom packaging/branding quotes
5. Get all terms in writing (Chinese and English)

**Order Process:**
1. Agree on price, quantity, and specifications
2. Place sample order first (2-5 units)
3. Approve samples before bulk production
4. Set up production timeline and milestones
5. Arrange quality inspection before shipping

### Step 5: Quality Control and Shipping

**Quality Control Checklist:**
- [ ] Product matches approved samples
- [ ] Materials are as specified
- [ ] Workmanship meets standards
- [ ] Packaging is professional
- [ ] All certifications are valid
- [ ] Quantity is correct

**Shipping Options:**
1. **Sea Freight:** 30-45 days, cheapest ($2-4/kg)
2. **Air Freight:** 7-15 days, moderate ($6-10/kg)
3. **Express (DHL/FedEx):** 3-7 days, expensive ($12-20/kg)
4. **Agent Consolidation:** Combines multiple orders for better rates

### Step 6: Customs and Import

**Documentation Required:**
- Commercial invoice
- Packing list
- Bill of lading/airway bill
- Certificate of origin (if required)
- Product certifications (CE, FCC, etc.)

**Duty Calculation:**

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use 1688 if I don't speak Chinese?**
A: Yes, but you'll need a sourcing agent. Services like Superbuy, Wegobuy, or private agents charge 5-10% of order value to handle communication, payment, and shipping. This fee is worth it for the 30-50% price savings. Many agents now offer real-time translation tools to help you communicate directly with suppliers.

**Q: Is 1688 safe for international buyers in 2026?**
A: 1688 has limited buyer protection for international buyers compared to Alibaba's Trade Assurance. The safest approach is: 1) Use a reputable sourcing agent, 2) Always pay via Alipay (not direct transfer), 3) Order samples first, 4) Use escrow services for large orders over $5,000, 5) Start with small test orders.

**Q: What's the minimum order on 1688 in 2026?**
A: Most 1688 suppliers require 10-100 units minimum, which is lower than Alibaba's typical 50-500 unit MOQs. However, MOQs are negotiable, especially for first orders. Many suppliers accept smaller orders if you mention long-term cooperation. In 2026, average MOQs have decreased by 20% as suppliers compete for smaller orders.

**Q: How do I pay on 1688 as an international buyer?**
A: The most common methods are: 1) Through a sourcing agent (recommended for beginners), 2) Direct Alipay with international verification (requires Chinese bank or verified international account), 3) WeChat Pay (limited international support). Never pay via direct bank transfer to suppliers - this offers no protection.

**Q: Can I get samples from 1688 before ordering?**
A: Yes, most 1688 suppliers offer samples for $5-20 (including shipping via your agent). Always order 2-3 samples from different suppliers before committing to a bulk order. Some suppliers now offer free samples for orders over 500 units (new policy in 2026). Budget $100-200 for comprehensive sampling.

**Q: What about customs and import duties when buying from 1688 vs Alibaba?**
A: Import duties are identical regardless of platform - they're based on the product, not the source. Generally: US (0-25%, average 8-12%), EU (0-20%, average 5-15%), Brazil (10-60%, average 20-35%), UK (0-20%, average 5-15%). Use Harmonized System (HS) codes to calculate exact duties for your products.

**Q: Which platform is better for private labeling in 2026?**
A: Both offer private labeling, but with different trade-offs. 1688 is 30-50% cheaper but requires more coordination through an agent. Alibaba is easier with English support and Trade Assurance coverage for custom orders. For beginners: Alibaba. For experienced importers with agents: 1688. Private label costs on 1688 average $0.50-2.00 per unit vs $1.00-3.50 on Alibaba.

**Q: How do I handle quality control when sourcing from 1688?**
A: Quality control requires extra diligence on 1688: 1) Order samples first, 2) Request video tours of production facilities, 3) Use third-party inspection services (SGS, Bureau Veritas), 4) Include quality specifications in writing, 5) Conduct pre-shipment inspections, 6) Start with small orders to test quality. Many sourcing agents offer quality control as part of their services.

**Q: Can I use 1688 for dropshipping?**
A: 1688 can work for dropshipping but requires careful setup: 1) Find suppliers willing to ship individual units, 2) Set up automated order forwarding through your agent, 3) Ensure packaging is neutral (no 1688 branding), 4) Negotiate competitive shipping rates. However, most 1688 suppliers prefer bulk orders. For pure dropshipping, AliExpress or CJDropshipping may be more practical.

**Q: What are the payment risks on 1688 vs Alibaba?**
A: Alibaba's Trade Assurance provides escrow protection for payments up to $150,000. 1688's Alipay offers domestic escrow but limited international recourse. Key risk mitigation: 1) Never pay via direct bank transfer on 1688, 2) Use agent payment services, 3) Break large orders into milestone payments, 4) Get payment receipts in Chinese and English, 5) Verify supplier business licenses before paying.
