## Cost Component Breakdown

### Direct Costs

| Cost Component | Definition | Typical Range |
|----------------|------------|---------------|
| Product cost | Manufacturing price | 50-65% of landed |
| Tooling/molds | Custom equipment | 5-15% (amortized) |
| Packaging | Product packaging | 3-8% |
| Labels/branding | Product labels | 1-3% |

### Indirect Costs

| Cost Component | Definition | Typical Range |
|----------------|------------|---------------|
| Freight | Shipping charges | 10-20% |
| Insurance | Cargo protection | 0.3-1% |
| Duty | Import tariffs | 5-15% |
| Customs fees | Brokerage, storage | 2-5% |
| Compliance | Testing, certification | 1-5% |
| Quality control | Inspection fees | 1-3% |

## Landed Cost Calculator

### Formula

```
Landed Cost = Product Cost + Freight + Insurance + Duty + Customs Fees + Port Fees + Inland Transport + Storage + Compliance
```

**Product: Silicone kitchen utensils (1,000 units)**

| Ocean freight | Container rate | $2,200 | $2.20 |

## Pricing Strategy Framework

### Cost-Plus Pricing

```
Selling Price = Landed Cost × (1 + Desired Margin)
```

**Example:**
- Landed cost: $13.69
- Desired margin: 50%
- Selling price: $13.69 × 1.5 = $20.54
- **Recommended Amazon price: $22.99**

### Competitive Pricing

| Strategy | Description | Best For |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Below market | 10-20% below competitors | Market penetration |
| At market | Match competitor prices | Established products |
| Above market | 10-20% premium | Premium branding |
| Value pricing | Price based on value delivered | Unique products |

### Break-Even Analysis

```
Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs / (Selling Price - Variable Cost per Unit)
```

**Example:**
- Fixed costs: $5,000 (tooling, samples, setup)
- Selling price: $22.99
- Variable cost: $13.69
- Break-even: $5,000 / ($22.99 - $13.69) = 538 units

## People Also Ask

1. **What's the formula for landed cost?** Product + freight + insurance + duty + fees + storage + compliance. See our [Hidden Sourcing Costs](/hidden-sourcing-costs/) guide.

2. **How do I calculate profit margin?** (Selling Price - Landed Cost) / Selling Price × 100

3. **What's a good profit margin?** 40-60% for Amazon FBA. Minimum 30% for sustainable business.

4. **How do I reduce landed cost?** Negotiate discounts, optimize freight, improve quality, minimize fees.

5. **What's the biggest cost component?** Product cost (50-65%) and freight (10-20%) are typically largest.

6. **How do I price my products?** Use cost-plus (2-3x landed cost) or competitive pricing (match market).

7. **What's break-even analysis?** Fixed costs / (Price - Variable cost). Tells you minimum units to sell.

8. **How do I optimize margins?** Volume discounts, supplier negotiation, freight optimization, quality improvement.

9. **What's the typical customs duty rate?** 5-15% for most consumer goods. Check HTS code for exact rate.

10. **How do I calculate Amazon fees?** Use Amazon's FBA Revenue Calculator. Includes referral (15-30%) and fulfillment ($3-10/unit).

## Detailed Import Process

Importing from China involves several key steps:

1. **Product Research** — Validate demand using Google Trends, Amazon BSR, TikTok trends
2. **Supplier Discovery** — Use 1688 (cheapest), Alibaba (safest), or Global Sources (verified)
3. **Sample Evaluation** — Order 2-3 samples ($10-50 each) before bulk
4. **Supplier Verification** — Check business license, years on platform, reviews, live video audit
5. **Negotiation** — Use competitive quotes to negotiate 10-20% price reduction
6. **Payment** — 30/70 split via Trade Assurance. Never 100% upfront
7. **Quality Control** — Pre-shipment inspection (SGS/Intertek, $100-300)
8. **Shipping** — Sea: $2-8/kg (35-45d), Air: $5-15/kg (5-10d), Express: $15-25/kg (3-5d)
9. **Customs** — Check HTS codes, prepare commercial invoice + packing list
10. **Quality on Arrival** — Inspect 100% on first order, file claims if needed
