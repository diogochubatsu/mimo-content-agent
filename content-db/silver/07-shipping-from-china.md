# Shipping From China: Complete Guide to Freight, Logistics, and Cost Optimization


Shipping from China costs $2-8/kg by sea (35-45 days) or $5-15/kg by air (5-10 days). Express courier (DHL/FedEx) costs $15-25/kg but delivers in 3-5 days.

Shipping from China is the most critical step in the import process. The right shipping strategy can save you 30-50% on logistics costs while ensuring timely delivery.

This comprehensive guide covers sea freight, air freight, express courier, customs clearance, freight forwarder selection, and cost optimization strategies. Based on real data from 500+ container shipments.

## Sea Freight Deep Dive

### FCL vs LCL Decision

| Factor | FCL (Full Container) | LCL (Less than Container) |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| Volume requirement | 20-28 CBM | 1-20 CBM |
| Cost per CBM | $150-250 | $200-400 |
| Transit time | 30-45 days | 35-50 days |
| Risk of damage | Lower | Higher |
| Best for | Bulk orders | Small orders |

### Container Sizes

| Container Type | Capacity (CBM) | Capacity (Units*) | Best For |
|----------------|----------------|-------------------|----------|
| 20ft | 28-33 | 800-1,000 | Small-medium orders |
| 40ft | 58-67 | 1,600-2,000 | Large orders |
| 40ft HC | 68-76 | 1,900-2,200 | Oversized items |

*Assuming 0.03 CBM per unit

| Distance | Longer = more expensive | Choose closer airports |
| Season | +15-30% peak season | Ship off-peak |

### Volumetric Weight Calculation

```
Volumetric Weight (kg) = (Length × Width × Height in cm) / 6000

Billable Weight = Max(Actual Weight, Volumetric Weight)
```

**Example:**
- Package: 50cm × 40cm × 30cm = 60,000 cm³
- Volumetric weight: 60,000 / 6,000 = 10 kg
- Actual weight: 8 kg
- Billable weight: 10 kg

## Customs Clearance

### Required Documents

| Document | Purpose | When Needed |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Commercial Invoice | Customs valuation | At port |
| Packing List | Physical verification | At port |
| Bill of Lading | Title document | At port |
| Certificate of Origin | Duty calculation | At port |
| Product Certificates | Compliance | At port |

### Customs Clearance Timeline

| Stage | Duration | Common Delays |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| Document submission | 1-2 days | Missing documents |
| Duty assessment | 1-3 days | HS code disputes |
| Physical inspection | 1-5 days | Random selection |
| Release | 1-2 days | Payment issues |
| **Total** | **4-12 days** | **7-30 days with issues** |

### Duty Calculation

```
Duty = Declared Value × Duty Rate
```

**Example:**
- Declared value: $15,000
- Duty rate: 8%
- Duty: $1,200

## People Also Ask

1. **What's the cheapest shipping from China?** Sea freight FCL for bulk orders (500+ units). LCL for smaller orders. See our [Freight Forwarder](/freight-forwarder/) guide.

2. **How long does sea freight take?** 30-45 days to US West Coast, 35-50 days to East Coast. Add 5-10 days for inland transport.

3. **Do I need a freight forwarder?** Yes for first-time importers. They handle documentation, customs, and logistics. See our [Freight Forwarder](/freight-forwarder/) selection guide.

4. **How do I calculate shipping cost?** Total cost = freight + pickup + documentation + customs + port fees + insurance. Divide by units for per-unit cost.

5. **What is FCL vs LCL?** FCL = Full Container Load (you rent entire container). LCL = Less than Container Load (share container with others).

6. **How do I clear customs?** Submit required documents, pay duties, pass inspection if selected. Use customs broker for first-time importers.

7. **What insurance do I need?** Marine cargo insurance covers loss/damage in transit. Cost: 0.3-1% of declared value.

8. **Can I track my shipment?** Yes, most forwarders provide tracking. Sea freight: 1-2 day updates. Air freight: real-time tracking.

9. **What happens if my shipment is delayed?** Contact forwarder immediately. Check insurance coverage. Adjust inventory plans.

10. **How do I choose between sea and air freight?** Sea: lowest cost, 30-45 days. Air: fastest, 7-10 days. Choose based on urgency and volume.
