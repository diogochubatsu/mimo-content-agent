## Model Deep Dives

### 1. Direct Factory Sourcing

**Definition:** Buying directly from manufacturers without intermediaries.

**Advantages:**
- Lowest unit cost
- Full customization control
- Direct communication
- Build long-term relationships

**Disadvantages:**
- Higher MOQs required
- Requires sourcing expertise
- More time-consuming
- Higher risk for beginners

**Best For:**
- High-volume importers
- Experienced buyers
- Custom product requirements
- Long-term partnerships

**Cost Structure:**

| Cost Component | Percentage |
|----------------|------------|
| Product cost | 60-70% |
| Freight | 10-15% |
| Duty | 5-10% |
| Other fees | 5-10% |

### 2. Trading Companies

**Definition:** Intermediaries that source from multiple factories.

**Advantages:**
- Lower MOQs
- Multiple factory options
- Quality verification
- Export documentation

**Disadvantages:**
- Higher unit cost (10-30% markup)
- Less direct control
- Potential communication gaps
- Limited customization

**Best For:**
- Small to medium orders
- First-time importers
- Multi-SKU orders
- Quick turnaround

**Cost Structure:**

| Cost Component | Percentage |
|----------------|------------|
| Product cost | 55-65% |
| Trading company margin | 10-30% |
| Freight | 8-12% |
| Duty | 5-10% |
| Other fees | 5-10% |

### 3. Sourcing Agents

**Definition:** Professional intermediaries that manage the entire sourcing process.

**Advantages:**
- Expert negotiation
- Factory verification
- Quality control
- Language/culture bridge

**Disadvantages:**
- Agent fees (5-15%)
- Communication layer
- Potential conflicts of interest
- Variable quality

**Best For:**
- Complex product requirements
- First-time importers
- High-value orders
- Compliance-heavy products

**Cost Structure:**

| Cost Component | Percentage |
|----------------|------------|
| Product cost | 55-65% |
| Agent fee | 5-15% |
| Freight | 8-12% |
| Duty | 5-10% |
| Other fees | 5-10% |

### 4. Dropshipping

**Definition:** Supplier ships directly to customer, no inventory holding.

**Advantages:**
- No inventory risk
- Low startup cost
- Test products easily
- Scalable

**Disadvantages:**
- Lower margins (20-35%)
- Less quality control
- Longer shipping times
- Customer service challenges

**Best For:**
- Product testing
- Low capital start
- Market validation
- Passive income

**Cost Structure:**

| Cost Component | Percentage |
|----------------|------------|
| Product cost | 40-50% |
| Supplier margin | 15-25% |
| Shipping | 10-20% |
| Platform fees | 5-10% |

### 5. Wholesale Sourcing

**Definition:** Buying pre-made products in bulk from distributors.

**Advantages:**
- Quick to market
- No customization needed
- Established products
- Lower risk

**Disadvantages:**
- Higher unit cost
- Less differentiation
- Competition from other sellers
- Limited branding

**Best For:**
- Quick revenue generation
- Established product categories
- Limited sourcing expertise
- Test before private label

**Cost Structure:**

| Cost Component | Percentage |
|----------------|------------|
| Product cost | 50-60% |
| Distributor margin | 15-25% |
| Shipping | 5-10% |
| Other fees | 5-10% |

### 6. Private Label

**Definition:** Branding existing products with your own label.

**Advantages:**
- Brand ownership
- Higher margins (50-70%)
- Less competition
- Customer loyalty

**Disadvantages:**
- Higher upfront cost
- Longer time to market
- IP protection needed
- Marketing investment

**Best For:**
- Brand building
- Long-term business
- Amazon FBA
- E-commerce

**Cost Structure:**

| Cost Component | Percentage |
|----------------|------------|
| Product cost | 55-65% |
| Branding/packaging | 5-10% |
| Freight | 8-12% |
| Duty | 5-10% |
| Other fees | 5-10% |

### 7. OEM Manufacturing

**Definition:** Manufacturer builds your custom design.

**Advantages:**
- Full customization
- IP ownership
- Unique products
- Higher margins

**Disadvantages:**
- Higher MOQs
- Tooling costs
- Longer lead times
- More risk

**Best For:**
- Unique product ideas
- Brand differentiation
- High-volume products
- Long-term portfolio

**Cost Structure:**

| Cost Component | Percentage |
|----------------|------------|
| Tooling/molds | 10-20% (amortized) |
| Product cost | 55-65% |
| Freight | 8-12% |
| Duty | 5-10% |
| Other fees | 5-10% |

### 8. ODM Manufacturing

**Definition:** Manufacturer provides design, you brand it.

**Advantages:**
- Fast time to market
- No tooling cost
- Lower MOQs
- Lower risk

**Disadvantages:**
- Less customization
- Supplier owns design
- More competition
- Limited differentiation

**Best For:**
- Fast launch
- Limited budget
- Market testing
- Standard products

**Cost Structure:**

| Cost Component | Percentage |
|----------------|------------|
| Product cost | 60-70% |
| Branding | 3-5% |
| Freight | 8-12% |
| Duty | 5-10% |
| Other fees | 5-10% |

## Complete Import Process Breakdown

Importing products from international suppliers involves a systematic 10-step process that, when followed correctly, minimizes risk and maximizes profit margins.

### Step 1: Market Research and Product Validation
Before contacting any supplier, validate market demand using data-driven methods:
- **Google Trends**: Check if search interest is stable or growing (target: 50+ score)
- **Amazon BSR Analysis**: Look for products ranked #1000-#10000 (proven demand, manageable competition)
- **TikTok/Instagram Trends**: Identify viral products 2-4 weeks before they peak
- **1688 Trending**: Chinese domestic trends often precede Western markets by 3-6 weeks

### Step 2: Supplier Discovery and Shortlisting
Use multiple platforms to find and compare suppliers:

| Platform | Price Level | MOQ | Buyer Protection | Best For |
|----------|-------------|-----|------------------|----------|
| 1688 | Cheapest (15-25% below Alibaba) | Low (50+) | Limited (needs agent) | Bulk orders, experienced buyers |
| Alibaba | Mid-range | Medium (100+) | Trade Assurance | International buyers, beginners |
| AliExpress | Budget | None | Basic | Single items, testing |
| Global Sources | Factory-level | High (5000+) | Verified | Large enterprises |

### Step 3: Sample Evaluation Protocol
Always order 2-3 samples ($10-50 each) before committing to bulk:
1. Order from 2-3 different suppliers
2. Test product quality, packaging, and functionality
3. Measure actual weight and dimensions (verify shipping costs)
4. Test with target audience if possible
5. Document differences between samples

### Step 4: Supplier Verification Checklist
- [ ] Business license verified (look for "manufacturing" scope)
- [ ] 3+ years on platform
- [ ] 100+ completed transactions
- [ ] 4.5+ buyer rating
- [ ] Live video audit completed
- [ ] Trade Assurance or escrow available

### Step 5-10: Negotiation, Payment, QC, Shipping, Customs, Receiving
Each step has specific best practices that can make or break your import business. The key is following a rigid operational sequence rather than rushing to place orders.

## Import Cost Calculator

Understanding total landed cost is critical for maintaining profitable margins. Here is a detailed breakdown:

### Cost Components

| Cost Component | Percentage of FOB | Notes |
|----------------|-------------------|-------|
| Product (FOB price) | 100% | Base cost from supplier |
| Shipping (sea freight) | 15-25% | $2-8/kg depending on volume |
| Import duties | 0-15% | Varies by HS code and country |
| VAT/Tax | 0-23% | Country-specific |
| Agent fees | 3-8% | If using sourcing agent |
| Quality inspection | 1-2% | $100-300 per inspection |
| **Total landed cost** | **130-150%** | **Budget 1.3-1.5x FOB price** |

### Real-World Example
Product: Bluetooth Earbuds
- FOB Price (1688): $1.75/unit
- Shipping (sea, 1000 units): $2.50/unit
- Import duty (2%): $0.04/unit
- Agent fee (5%): $0.09/unit
- **Total landed: $4.38/unit**
- **Amazon selling price: $24.99**
- **Profit margin: 82%**

### Common Cost Mistakes
1. **Ignoring shipping weight** — Always get actual weight, not estimated
2. **Forgetting import duties** — Check HTS codes before ordering
3. **Underestimating packaging** — Custom packaging adds $0.20-1.00/unit
4. **Not accounting for returns** — Budget 5-10% for returns/refunds
5. **Currency fluctuation** — Lock in exchange rates for large orders
